 DIGEST OF INTRODUCED BILL 
 Citations Affected: IC 35-31.5-2; IC 35-45-4-9. 
 Synopsis: Digital sexual image abuse. Defines &quot;digital sexual image&quot; as a digital, computer generated image or video that is created or modified to depict: (1) sexual intercourse; (2) other sexual conduct; or (3) exhibition of certain body parts; of a quality, characteristic, or condition, such that it appears to depict an individual. Establishes the offenses of possession of a digital sexual image and distribution of a digital sexual image. 
 Effective: July 1, 2026.

 A BILL FOR AN ACT to amend the Indiana Code concerning criminal law and procedure. 
 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Indiana: 
 
 
 SECTION 1. IC 35-31.5-2-94.5, IS ADDED TO THE INDIANA CODE AS A NEW SECTION TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 94.5. &quot;Digital sexual image&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-45-4-9, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-45-4-9. 
 
 SECTION 2. IC 35-31.5-2-100, AS AMENDED BY P.L.186-2025, SECTION 204, IS AMENDED TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 100. (a) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-45-4-8 and IC 35-45-4-9, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-45-4-8. 
 (b) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-46-1-10, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-46-1-10(f). 
 (c) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-46-1-10.2, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-46-1-10.2(g). 
 (d) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-47.5, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-47.5-2-6. 
 (e) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-48, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-48-1.1-13. 
 (f) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-49, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-49-1-2. 
 SECTION 3. IC 35-45-4-9 IS ADDED TO THE INDIANA CODE AS A NEW SECTION TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 9. (a) This section does not apply to: 
 
 
 (1) a digital image or video that is distributed: 
 
 
 (A) to report a possible criminal act; 
 
 
 (B) in connection with a criminal investigation; 
 
 
 (C) under a court order; or 
 
 
 (D) by a news reporting or an entertainment medium (as defined in IC 32-36-1-4); 
 
 
 (2) a newspaper or news service that publishes news related information through a website; 
 
 
 (3) a cloud service provider; or 
 
 
 (4) an Internet provider, an affiliate or subsidiary of an Internet provider, or a search engine that: 
 
 
 (A) solely provides access or connection to a website or other Internet content that is not under the control of that Internet service provider, affiliate or subsidiary, or search engine; and 
 
 
 (B) is not responsible for creating or publishing the content that constitutes material harmful to minors. 
 
 
 (b) As used in this section, &quot;digital sexual image&quot; means a digital, computer generated image or video that is created or modified by means of artificial intelligence, a computer software program, an application, or other editing tools that depicts: 
 
 
 (1) sexual intercourse; 
 
 
 (2) other sexual conduct (as defined in IC 35-31.5-2-221.5); or 
 
 
 (3) exhibition of the uncovered buttocks, genitals, or female breast; 
 
 
 of a quality, characteristic, or condition such that it appears to depict an individual. 
 
 
 (c) As used in this section, &quot;distribute&quot; has the meaning set forth in section 8(b) of this chapter. 
 
 
 (d) A person who: 
 
 
 (1) creates; or 
 
 
 (2) possesses; 
 
 
 a digital sexual image without the consent of the individual depicted in the digital sexual image commits digital sexual image abuse, a Class B misdemeanor. 
 
 
 (e) An offense under subsection (d) is a: 
 
 
 (1) Class A misdemeanor if the person has one (1) prior unrelated conviction under subsection (d); or 
 
 
 (2) Level 6 felony if the person has more than one (1) prior unrelated conviction under subsection (d). 
 
 
 (f) A person who distributes a digital sexual image without the consent of the individual depicted in the digital sexual image commits distribution of a digital sexual image, a Class A misdemeanor. However, the offense is a Level 6 felony if the person has a prior unrelated conviction under this subsection.
