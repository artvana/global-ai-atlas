# New York Senate Bill 8420-A — Disclosure Requirements for AI-Generated Synthetic Performers in Advertising

**Senate Bill No. 8420-A**

*New York — December 11, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 8420-A requires advertisements that feature AI-generated "synthetic performers" — digital creations that realistically depict human beings in performances — to include a conspicuous disclosure that the performer is artificially generated.

### Definition of Synthetic Performer

A "synthetic performer" is defined as an AI-generated digital creation that realistically depicts the appearance, voice, or likeness of a human being in an expressive performance. The term covers fully synthetic digital humans and does not require the use of any real person's likeness.

### Disclosure Requirement

Any advertisement using a synthetic performer must include a conspicuous disclosure, in a form reasonably calculated to be noticed by a typical viewer or listener, that the performer is artificially generated. The disclosure must be present in the advertisement itself, not merely in accompanying fine print or terms.

### Civil Penalties

- First violation: $1,000 civil penalty
- Subsequent violations: $5,000 civil penalty per violation

### Exemption for Expressive Works

The bill exempts advertising for expressive works — such as films, television programs, or musical recordings — where a synthetic performer is used in advertising consistent with their use in the underlying expressive work itself.

### Effective Date

The act takes effect June 9, 2026 (180 days after enactment on December 11, 2025).
