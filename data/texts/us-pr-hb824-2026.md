TO THE SENATE OF PUERTO RICO

The Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate of Puerto Rico, after due study and consideration of House Bill 824, recommends to this High Body its approval, without amendments.

SCOPE OF THE MEASURE

To amend Articles 1, 4, and 9 of Act 40-2024, known as the "Cybersecurity Act of the Government of Puerto Rico"; for the purpose of having the virtual program and the continuing education course on cybersecurity provided for in the referenced Act include the topic of the use of artificial intelligence; to make technical amendments; and for other related purposes.

INTRODUCTION

The Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate of Puerto Rico submits for the consideration of this High Body the present Positive Report on House Bill 824, after having conducted a detailed analysis of the measure and evaluated its impact within the framework of the public policy on cybersecurity, virtual education, and the continuing education course on cybersecurity administered by the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), recognizing the challenges of technological innovation and digital transformation of the Government of Puerto Rico.

The measure proposes to amend Act 40-2024 for the purpose of expressly incorporating the topic of the use of artificial intelligence within the continuing education program, in light of the emerging challenges that this technology presents for the protection of information systems and data privacy.

As part of the legislative evaluation process, the Committee considered the explanatory memoranda submitted by the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), the Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly (OPAL), and the Office of Government Ethics of Puerto Rico, originally submitted to the House of Representatives. From those memoranda, relevant considerations emerge regarding the pertinence of the measure, its operational viability, and its fiscal impact.

In that context, the Committee concludes that House Bill 824 advances legitimate public policy objectives aimed at strengthening cybersecurity education, promoting the responsible use of artificial intelligence, and improving the capacity of the Government of Puerto Rico to face contemporary technological challenges. Consequently, it recommends its approval.

ANALYSIS OF THE MEASURE

In addressing its responsibility and ministerial duty in the study and evaluation of the present Bill, the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate of Puerto Rico analyzed the explanatory memoranda submitted to the Government Committee of the House of Representatives of Puerto Rico by the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service, the Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly, and the Office of Government Ethics of Puerto Rico, from which the position of the consulted instrumentalities can be discerned:

PUERTO RICO INNOVATION AND TECHNOLOGY SERVICE

The Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) submitted an explanatory memorandum regarding House Bill 824 in which it endorses the measure. The agency recognizes that the inclusion of the topic of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity education programs responds to the need to keep public policy up to date in light of the constant changes in information technologies. Likewise, it highlights that artificial intelligence is already part of multiple systems used in public administration and that its use entails risks in terms of security, privacy, and data management. PRITS notes that it has the institutional capacity and the legal framework necessary to implement the measure, since the measure naturally integrates with its existing functions of education, training, and development of public policies in technological matters.

OFFICE OF GOVERNMENT ETHICS

The Office of Government Ethics submitted an explanatory memorandum regarding House Bill 824 in which it expresses a favorable position on the measure. The entity highlights that the incorporation of the topic of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity education programs constitutes an important step to strengthen the capabilities of governmental personnel in the responsible, ethical, and safe use of these technologies. Likewise, it emphasizes the challenges associated with data privacy, system security, and the integrity of public information. The Office also underscores its collaboration with PRITS on continuing training to address the emerging challenges in this area.

OFFICE OF THE BUDGET OF THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY

The Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly (OPAL) submitted a report indicating that the measure does not impose a significant fiscal impact on the General Fund, since it is limited to adding content to existing educational programs. Nevertheless, it notes that, in the event additional resources were required for its implementation, the impact would be minimal and could be absorbed with available resources. Likewise, it indicates that any educational outreach initiative could be carried out through collaborations with public or private entities, without entailing significant costs.

FISCAL IMPACT

In compliance with Article 1.007 of Act 107-2020, as amended, known as the "Municipal Code of Puerto Rico," the undersigned Committee certifies that the measure that is the subject of this report does not impose an economic obligation on the budget of the municipal governments.

CONCLUSION

After examining House Bill 824 and considering the explanatory memoranda submitted by the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service, the Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly, and the Office of Government Ethics, this Committee concludes that the measure constitutes a legislative effort aimed at strengthening the cybersecurity public policy of the Government of Puerto Rico through the integration of artificial intelligence as an essential component in education and training processes.

The analysis conducted demonstrates that the bill directly addresses the emerging challenges associated with the use of artificial intelligence, promoting more informed, responsible public administration aligned with contemporary technological demands. Likewise, the measure strengthens the Government's capacity to prevent risks, protect information, and ensure the continuity of services through updated and pertinent continuing education.

From the memoranda evaluated, uniform support for the measure emerges, with its operational viability, its consistency with the existing legal framework, and its neutral fiscal impact standing out. In that sense, the Committee understands that the measure constitutes an affirmative step toward the modernization of public administration and the strengthening of digital security in the Government of Puerto Rico.

FOR ALL OF THE FOREGOING, the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate of Puerto Rico, after due study and consideration, hereby presents before this High Body the Positive Report on House Bill 824, recommending its approval without amendments.
