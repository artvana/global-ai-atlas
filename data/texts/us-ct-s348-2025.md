      January Session, 2025                LCO No. 732


    Referred to Committee on JUDICIARY

    Introduced by:
     SEN. HWANG, 28th Dist.




 AN ACT CONCERNING UNLAWFUL DISSEMINATION OF INTIMATE
 IMAGES THAT ARE DIGITALLY ALTERED OR CREATED THROUGH
 THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to criminalize unauthorized
2      dissemination of intimate images that are digitally altered or created
3      through the use of artificial intelligence.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To criminalize unauthorized dissemination of intimate images that are
       digitally altered or created through the use of artificial intelligence.
