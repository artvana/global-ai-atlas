# Shanghai Municipality Regulations on the Promotion and Development of the Artificial Intelligence Industry

**Adopted by:** Shanghai Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee  
**Date of Adoption:** September 25, 2022 (at the 44th Meeting of the 15th Shanghai Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee)  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2022  

---

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1:** These Regulations are formulated in order to promote the development of the artificial intelligence industry, drive innovation in AI technology and applications, strengthen AI governance, and build Shanghai into an international hub for AI innovation and development, in accordance with relevant national laws and regulations and in combination with the actual conditions of this Municipality.

**Article 2:** These Regulations apply to entities and individuals within the administrative area of this Municipality that are engaged in:

(1) Research and development of AI technology;

(2) AI product design, development, manufacturing, and sale;

(3) Development, deployment, and operation of AI applications and services;

(4) AI industry-related talent cultivation, investment, financing, and support services.

**Article 3:** The Municipality shall promote the development of the artificial intelligence industry in accordance with the principles of government guidance, market operation, open innovation, and safe governance, adhering to the approach of promoting development and governance in tandem, fostering international competitiveness, and enabling AI to serve the high-quality development of the economy and improvement of people's livelihoods.

**Article 4:** The Shanghai Municipal Development and Reform Commission, together with the Municipal Science and Technology Commission, the Municipal Commission of Economy and Informatization, and other relevant departments, shall be responsible for coordinating and promoting the overall development of the AI industry. Each relevant functional department shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of AI activities within their respective scope of responsibilities.

District-level people's governments shall strengthen support and promotion of AI industry development within their respective districts, and shall coordinate the cultivation, attraction, and service of AI enterprises.

**Article 5:** The Municipality shall incorporate the development of the AI industry into the relevant plans for economic and social development, formulate special plans for AI industry development, and establish coordination mechanisms for major AI industry development issues.

---

## Chapter II: Technology Innovation

**Article 6:** The Municipality shall support the undertaking of original AI research that targets key frontier technology challenges, and shall promote the integration of AI with other strategic technologies including semiconductors, advanced manufacturing, biopharmaceuticals, and new energy. The Municipality shall establish a sustained funding mechanism for basic AI research.

**Article 7:** The Municipality shall support the building of major AI technology infrastructure, including:

(1) Open computing power platforms and AI cloud infrastructure to support AI research and development activities;

(2) Public training data resource platforms and data exchange platforms;

(3) AI open innovation platforms and testing and evaluation environments;

(4) AI model repositories, algorithm libraries, and tool libraries.

**Article 8:** The Municipality shall encourage and support the establishment of artificial intelligence research institutions, including national key laboratories, technology innovation centers, engineering research centers, and industry-university-research joint research institutions. The Municipality shall provide preferential support to qualified AI research institutions in terms of land use, facility construction, and operating support.

**Article 9:** The Municipality shall promote the establishment of major AI technology innovation bases, including:

(1) AI-focused science parks, innovation clusters, and demonstration zones;

(2) AI application innovation centers in key industries such as automotive, healthcare, finance, and manufacturing;

(3) AI-focused incubators and accelerators for start-up enterprises.

**Article 10:** The Municipality shall establish mechanisms to promote the conversion of AI research results into commercial applications. The Municipality shall support AI enterprises in applying for and obtaining domestic and international intellectual property rights, and shall provide guidance and support for the protection of AI-related trade secrets and data assets.

---

## Chapter III: Computing Power and Data Resources

**Article 11:** The Municipality shall prioritize the development of computing power infrastructure for AI, including data centers, intelligent computing centers, and edge computing facilities. The Municipality shall support the construction of a unified computing power scheduling platform to improve the utilization efficiency of computing resources.

**Article 12:** The Municipality shall promote the orderly opening of public data resources for AI training and application purposes, in accordance with data classification and grading standards, and subject to security review requirements. Priority shall be given to opening data resources in fields with high public value and significant AI application potential.

**Article 13:** Enterprises, scientific research institutions, and other organizations that hold high-quality data resources relevant to AI development are encouraged to participate in data sharing and exchange. The Municipality shall establish a data exchange mechanism to facilitate compliant trading of AI training data under data security and personal information protection requirements.

**Article 14:** The Municipality shall promote the formulation and adoption of standards for AI data, including data collection standards, data quality standards, data labelling standards, and data security standards, to facilitate the use and exchange of AI-related data resources.

**Article 15:** The Municipality shall promote AI applications that enable the integration of data across industries, departments, and regions, and shall facilitate the construction of sector-specific data platforms in key fields such as smart city management, medical and health data, traffic data, and financial data, subject to the applicable legal requirements for data security and personal information protection.

---

## Chapter IV: Industry Applications

**Article 16:** The Municipality shall prioritize the deep integration of AI with the following key application domains:

(1) **Advanced manufacturing**: Support AI applications in industrial design, predictive maintenance, quality control, supply chain optimization, and smart factory management;

(2) **Life sciences and healthcare**: Support AI applications in medical imaging analysis, drug discovery, precision medicine, hospital management, and public health monitoring;

(3) **Finance**: Support AI applications in risk management, fraud detection, intelligent customer service, wealth management, and regulatory compliance technology;

(4) **Transportation and smart city**: Support AI applications in autonomous driving, traffic signal control, port and logistics management, and urban infrastructure monitoring;

(5) **Cultural and creative industries**: Support AI applications in content creation, cultural heritage digitization, and creative design.

**Article 17:** The Municipality shall implement an "AI+" initiative to promote the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries through the application of AI technology. The Municipality shall support industry associations and AI enterprises in formulating sector-specific AI application standards and best practices.

**Article 18:** The Municipality shall promote the application of AI in public services and government administration, including AI-assisted government service hotlines, intelligent administrative approval systems, AI applications in education, elderly care, and social security management, and AI-enhanced emergency management systems. Government AI applications shall be implemented in a manner that ensures equity, transparency, and human oversight.

**Article 19:** The Municipality shall support the construction of AI innovation and application demonstration zones, and shall promote experience-sharing and model replication of successful AI application practices. The Municipality shall support the development of AI-enabled smart city infrastructure, including smart traffic management, smart energy management, and smart public security systems, subject to applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

---

## Chapter V: Market Environment and Investment

**Article 20:** The Municipality shall work to create a market environment that is conducive to AI industry development, including:

(1) Reducing market access barriers for AI enterprises in fields that do not involve statutory licensing requirements;

(2) Implementing a government procurement policy that supports domestically developed AI products and services;

(3) Establishing a fair and transparent market environment that prevents monopolistic behavior and unfair competition in the AI sector;

(4) Streamlining administrative procedures for AI enterprise registration, project approvals, and regulatory compliance.

**Article 21:** The Municipality shall promote the development of AI finance and investment, encouraging venture capital, government guidance funds, industry investment funds, and other financial instruments to invest in AI enterprises and projects. The Municipality shall support qualified AI enterprises in accessing capital markets, including domestic and international stock exchanges.

**Article 22:** The Municipality shall establish a dedicated AI Industry Development Guidance Fund to support AI enterprises in areas including:

(1) Research and development of core AI technologies;

(2) Construction of major AI technology infrastructure;

(3) AI industry application and demonstration projects;

(4) Attraction and cultivation of AI enterprises and talent.

**Article 23:** The Municipality shall support AI enterprises in participating in international technology cooperation and investment, and shall facilitate the establishment of international AI research and development centers, joint laboratories, and innovation hubs in Shanghai. The Municipality shall provide facilitation services for AI enterprises seeking to expand internationally.

---

## Chapter VI: Talent Development

**Article 24:** The Municipality shall implement a comprehensive strategy for AI talent development, encompassing the attraction of internationally leading AI talent, the cultivation of domestic AI talent, and the development of AI-enabling skills in the broader workforce. The Municipality shall implement preferential policies for the attraction and retention of AI talent including in housing, residency registration, children's education, and healthcare.

**Article 25:** The Municipality shall support universities and vocational training institutions in establishing AI-related courses and programs, and shall encourage enterprises to provide industry-oriented AI training opportunities. The Municipality shall support the development of AI-focused interdisciplinary education programs that combine AI with other fields such as life sciences, finance, and manufacturing.

**Article 26:** The Municipality shall promote the development of a diversified AI talent supply system, including:

(1) Supporting leading universities in building world-class AI research and education programs;

(2) Supporting vocational institutions in developing AI technical skills training programs;

(3) Supporting enterprises in establishing AI training centers and apprenticeship programs;

(4) Supporting AI talent re-skilling and upskilling programs for workers in industries undergoing AI-driven transformation.

**Article 27:** The Municipality shall optimize the management of foreign AI talent, including streamlining work permit and residency permit procedures for internationally recruited AI researchers and engineers, and providing facilitation services for AI talent seeking to move between the Municipality and other regions.

---

## Chapter VII: AI Governance

**Article 28:** The Municipality shall promote responsible AI development and deployment, guided by principles of humanity-centered AI, fairness, transparency, accountability, and safety. AI developers and deployers are encouraged to adopt ethical guidelines for AI development and to integrate ethics review processes into their AI product and application development cycles.

**Article 29:** Entities that develop and deploy AI applications in high-stakes domains, including healthcare diagnosis, judicial assistance, financial decisions, and public security, shall:

(1) Conduct algorithmic impact assessments before deployment;

(2) Establish mechanisms for human oversight and review of AI-assisted decisions that materially affect individuals' rights or interests;

(3) Ensure that affected individuals have access to explanations of AI-assisted decisions and channels to contest such decisions;

(4) Regularly audit AI systems for accuracy, fairness, and compliance with applicable laws and standards.

**Article 30:** The Municipality shall establish an AI application testing and evaluation system, providing public testing and evaluation services to support enterprises in assessing the safety, reliability, and compliance of AI products and applications before deployment.

**Article 31:** The Municipality shall promote the establishment of industry self-regulatory mechanisms for the AI sector, supporting industry associations in developing codes of conduct, technical standards, and best practice guidelines for responsible AI development and deployment.

**Article 32:** The Municipality shall establish mechanisms for monitoring potential social impacts of AI applications, including labor market impacts, social equity concerns, and data security risks. The Municipality shall work to ensure that the benefits of AI development are broadly shared and that adverse impacts on vulnerable groups are identified and addressed.

---

## Chapter VIII: Support Policies

**Article 33:** The Municipality shall provide the following support policies to promote AI industry development:

(1) Tax incentives for qualified AI research and development expenditures in accordance with national and municipal tax regulations;

(2) Land use policies that prioritize AI technology parks and innovation clusters;

(3) Priority access to electricity and network infrastructure for AI data centers and computing power facilities;

(4) Streamlined environmental approval procedures for AI facilities;

(5) Fee reductions or exemptions for government services provided to AI start-up enterprises.

**Article 34:** The Municipality shall provide financial support for the construction of AI infrastructure and the demonstration and promotion of AI applications through government investment. The Municipality shall establish clear criteria and transparent procedures for the allocation of financial support.

**Article 35:** The Municipality shall support the establishment of AI professional associations and industry alliances, and shall support their activities in promoting industry standards, talent development, market development, and international cooperation.

---

## Chapter IX: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 36:** For the purposes of these Regulations, "artificial intelligence" refers to the theory, method, technology, and application system that uses computers or computer-controlled machines to simulate, extend, and expand human intelligence, including perception, learning, reasoning, and decision-making capabilities.

**Article 37:** These Regulations shall take effect on October 1, 2022. The Shanghai Municipality Regulations on Promoting the Development of the AI Industry (2019) are simultaneously repealed.
