	Assembly		Session

SENATE OF PUERTO RICO
Senate Bill No. 68
  January 2, 2025
Presented by Mr. Rivera Schatz
Co-authored by Messrs. Matías Rosario, Reyes Berríos, and Santiago Rivera
Referred to the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence
ACT

To create the "Artificial Intelligence Act of the Government of Puerto Rico"; to create the position of Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, attached to the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), and establish its duties; to create the Artificial Intelligence Committee of the Government of Puerto Rico and establish its duties; to order the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) to create and develop the Public Policy of the Government of Puerto Rico regarding the implementation of Artificial Intelligence through governmental agencies and instrumentalities; and for other related purposes.

STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
On February 11, 2019, the President of the United States issued Executive Order 13859,1 which seeks to maintain the United States as the leader in the development, use, and research of Artificial Intelligence. To this end, said executive order established certain principles, by which the United States government must:
    a) guide technological advances in Artificial Intelligence through the federal government, industry, and academia to promote scientific discovery, economic competitiveness, and national security;
    b) drive the development of appropriate standards and reduce barriers to the safe deployment of Artificial Intelligence technology in order to enable the creation of new related industries;
    c) train present and future generations of workers with the skills necessary to develop and apply artificial intelligence technologies that help them understand the current economy and the jobs of the future;
    d) foster public confidence in Artificial Intelligence, protecting civil rights and privacy in its application, in order to fully harness the potential of artificial intelligence.2
In addition, Executive Order 13960,3 seeks to promote the use of trustworthy Artificial Intelligence across federal government agencies. It establishes the principles for the use of Artificial Intelligence in the federal government, promulgates a public policy to implement it, orders agencies to record the use of Artificial Intelligence, and asks the General Services Administration (GSA) and the Office of Personnel Management to improve expertise in implementing Artificial Intelligence at the agencies.4
 As a consequence of these Executive Orders, in January 2021, through bipartisan legislation, the United States Congress approved the "National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020,"5 which establishes a coordinated program across the federal government to accelerate the research and application of Artificial Intelligence with the goal of promoting economic prosperity and national security. The mission of the "National Artificial Intelligence Initiative" is to ensure United States leadership in the research and development of this technology, as well as to prepare the present and future workforce for its integration.
The "National Artificial Intelligence Initiative" provides a general framework for strengthening and coordinating Artificial Intelligence research, development, demonstration, and education activities across all instrumentalities of the United States government, in cooperation with academic, industrial, non-profit, and civil society organizations. The work of this initiative is organized into six (6) strategic pillars: innovation, advancement of trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, education, training, infrastructure, and international cooperation.
In light of this, various states, such as California, Connecticut, and Illinois, have introduced legislative initiatives to promote the development and use of Artificial Intelligence, as well as to limit its scope. Therefore, this Legislative Assembly considers it necessary that the Government of Puerto Rico keep pace with the times we live in. To that end, the position of Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, the Artificial Intelligence Advisory Committee, are created, and PRITS, together with the Artificial Intelligence Officer, is ordered to establish, recommend, and develop the Public Policy of the Government of Puerto Rico regarding this type of technology.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:
Article 1.- Title
The Artificial Intelligence Act of the Government of Puerto Rico is created.
Article 2.- Definitions
For purposes of this Act, the following terms shall have the meaning expressed below:
    (1) Agency - shall mean any department, office, bureau, commission, board, administration, authority, public corporation, including its affiliates and subsidiaries, or any other entity of the Executive Branch;
(2) Committee shall mean the Artificial Intelligence Advisory Committee established in Article 5 of this Act;
 (3) Artificial Intelligence Officer - shall mean the person designated by the Chief Innovation and Information Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, as provided in Article 3 of this Act;
(4) PRITS – shall mean the Puerto Rico Innovation Technology and Service;
(5) Automated systems procedures - shall mean the procedures developed and adopted in accordance with this Act;
(6) Automated decision-making system - shall mean any computerized process that collects data, analyzes data, or uses artificial intelligence to make, modify, or be a factor in decision-making with or without human intervention.
Article 3.- The position of Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, attached to the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service, is created.
The Chief Innovation and Information Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico shall appoint an Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, no later than ninety (90) days after the approval of this Act, who possesses the following minimum requirements:
    1) knowledge of trends, technology, development, and terminology of automated decision-making systems and Artificial Intelligence analysis; and
    2) experience in administration, planning, public policy development, project management, and service coordination.
The Artificial Intelligence Officer shall be in charge of developing the Public Policy of the Government of Puerto Rico regarding the use, study, and development of Artificial Intelligence through governmental agencies and instrumentalities.
No later than July 1, 2025, and every two (2) years thereafter, the Artificial Intelligence Officer shall, in consultation with the Chief Innovation and Information Officer of the Government, develop and adopt procedures for automated systems that guide the agencies' use in the development, acquisition, and use of automated decision-making systems.
Article 4.- Duties of the Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico.
In the development of the automated decision-making systems procedures established in the preceding Article, the Artificial Intelligence Officer shall impose the following safeguards to mitigate risk:
(1) ensure that state agencies develop, acquire, and use automated systems in a manner consistent with national and international standards;
(2) ensure that agencies develop, acquire, and use automated systems that comply with the constitution, and state and federal laws;
(3) ensure that the automated system does not discriminate against any individual or group of individuals on the basis of age, color, race, creed, religion, origin, ancestry, sex, marital status, pregnancy, veteran status, disability, source of income, or any other cause established by law;
(4) ensure that any benefit an agency receives from using an automated system outweighs any inherent risk of using such system;
(5) ensure that each automated system is applied and used in a consistent manner for the purpose for which it was created, thereby ensuring the accuracy, reliability, and effectiveness of such system;
(6) ensure that each automated system is secure, protected, and resilient to circumstances in which such automated system faces any systematic vulnerability, manipulation, or malicious exploitation;
 (7) ensure that operations and outcomes generated by an automated system are sufficiently understandable to subject-matter experts and users; document its use and implementation;
 (8) ensure that the design, development, acquisition, and monitoring of an automated system are appropriately transparent to the public, under uniform protocols; and
 (9) ensure that each governmental entity using an automated system:
 (a) examines the automated system, at least once every two (2) years, to ensure compliance with the procedures of such automated systems,
(b) replaces, disconnects, and deactivates any application of the automated system that demonstrates performance inconsistent with the provisions of this Act;
 (c) implements safeguards to ensure that the automated system is properly applied and used; and
 (d) provides adequate training to all personnel responsible for developing, acquiring, or using the automated system;
(10) develop and adopt procedures for automated systems for use by the Government's agencies and instrumentalities;
(11) beginning June 30, 2026, render an annual report to the Legislative Assembly on advances, findings, studies, and recommendations regarding the use of Artificial Intelligence in the various governmental entities.
Article 5.- The Artificial Intelligence Advisory Committee of the Government of Puerto Rico is created.
The Artificial Intelligence Advisory Committee of the Government of Puerto Rico shall be composed of:
1) the Chief Innovation and Information Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico or a representative who may not be the Artificial Intelligence Officer;
 (2) the Administrator of the General Services Administration and Chief Procurement Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico or a representative;
 (3) the Secretary of the Department of Economic Development or a representative;
 (4) the Executive Director of the Office of Human Resources Administration and Transformation or a representative;
Article 6.- Duties of the Committee
The Committee shall advise the Governor of Puerto Rico, and the Artificial Intelligence Officer of Puerto Rico, on matters related to the use of Artificial Intelligence, including recommendations on:
(1) best practices for the use of artificial intelligence and automated systems at the agencies;
(2) the current state of Puerto Rico in relation to competitiveness in artificial intelligence, including the scope and scale of Puerto Rico's investments in artificial intelligence research and development;
(3) scientific advances regarding artificial intelligence;
(4) matters related to the use of artificial intelligence to improve the workforce in Puerto Rico, including using this type of technology to train the workforce in positions that lead to economic self-sufficiency; the Office of Human Resources Administration and Transformation shall submit reports on personnel and contracting whenever a state agency makes any transition of services to Artificial Intelligence, pursuant to the Act for the Administration and Transformation of Human Resources in the Government of Puerto Rico, Act 8-2017, as amended. In such a scenario, the Office of Human Resources Administration and Transformation of the Government shall additionally notify the Legislative Assembly, with no less than ninety (90) days advance notice of the entry into force of the administrative changes.
(5) how to leverage resources to optimize and improve operations in various areas of governmental operations, including medical services, cybersecurity, infrastructure, and natural disaster recovery;
(6) whether proposals are aligned with the best industry standards and practices.
(7) opportunities for international cooperation in artificial intelligence research activities, standards development, and regulations.
Article 7.- Mandate
No later than July 1, 2025, and every two (2) years thereafter, the Artificial Intelligence Officer shall present a draft of automated systems procedures for the Artificial Intelligence of the Government of Puerto Rico to the Committee.
The Committee, no later than 30 days after having received said draft, shall submit to the Artificial Intelligence Officer its suggestions or recommendations regarding the same.
After receiving the suggestions or recommendations, the Artificial Intelligence Officer shall finalize the automated systems procedures and submit such final procedures to the Committee.
 Maintain an internal registry of agencies with exclusive access by PRITS and members of the committee.
Nothing in this section shall be interpreted as:
(1) requiring the disclosure of any trade secret;
(2) overriding any work product protection; or
(3) restricting the access of the Artificial Intelligence Officer or any state agency to:
(a) conduct any internal research aimed at developing, improving, or repairing any product, service, or technology,
(b) prevent, detect, protect, respond to, investigate, report any person responsible for any security incident, identity theft, fraud, harassment, malicious or deceptive activity, or illegal activity, or
(c) preserve the integrity or security of any system.
Article 8.- Order
The Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), together with the Artificial Intelligence Officer of the Government of Puerto Rico, is ordered to develop the Government's Public Policy regarding the use of Artificial Intelligence by the agencies of the Government of Puerto Rico, to promote the use and development of Artificial Intelligence through the Agencies of the Government of Puerto Rico in a safe and effective manner.
For the development of the Public Policy on Artificial Intelligence, PRITS, together with the Artificial Intelligence Officer, shall seek the cooperation of academic, industrial, non-profit, and civil society organizations that study or develop Artificial Intelligence.
Article 9.- Prohibitions
 No Agency may use or develop Artificial Intelligence languages and codes without first submitting its proposal to PRITS and the Artificial Intelligence Officer for evaluation and approval.
 Article 10.- Severability Clause
If any clause, paragraph, section, or part of this Act is declared unconstitutional by a court of competent jurisdiction, the judgment to that effect shall not affect or invalidate the remaining provisions of this Act. The effect of such judgment shall be limited to the clause, paragraph, section, or part of the Act that has been declared unconstitutional.
Article 11 – Provision on Fiscal Impact
To ensure the fiscally responsible implementation of this Act, the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) shall identify and secure the funding sources necessary to cover the associated costs, either through the reprogramming of existing funds within its budget or the identification of new revenues, so that the implementation of this Act complies with the Principle of Fiscal Neutrality and does not generate an adverse fiscal impact on the General Fund.
Article 11 12.- Effectiveness
This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
