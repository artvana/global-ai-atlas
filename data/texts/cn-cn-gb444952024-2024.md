# China GB 44495-2024: Vehicle Cybersecurity Standard

## Overview

GB 44495-2024 is China's mandatory national standard on vehicle cybersecurity (汽车整车信息安全技术要求), which came into effect in 2024. It establishes cybersecurity technical requirements for motor vehicles, including requirements related to AI-enabled automotive systems.

## Key Scope

The standard covers:
- Cybersecurity management requirements for vehicle manufacturers
- Technical requirements for cybersecurity in vehicle electronic and electrical systems
- Requirements for protection against cyber attacks on vehicle systems
- Security requirements for vehicle software updates (OTA)
- Requirements for data security and privacy protection in connected vehicles
- Compliance testing and certification requirements

## Relationship to AI

The standard addresses AI-related cybersecurity concerns in the context of connected and autonomous vehicles, including:
- Security of AI-enabled automated driving systems
- Protection of AI model data used in vehicle systems
- Requirements for detecting and responding to adversarial attacks on AI systems
- Logging and audit trail requirements for AI-driven vehicle decisions

## Status

This is a mandatory (强制性) national standard. Vehicle manufacturers selling vehicles in China are required to comply with GB 44495-2024.
