# OAS Press Release E-100/24: VII Meeting of Ministers of Science and Technology Adopts Groundbreaking Declaration and Launches AI Governance Initiative

Organization of American States (OAS)
Date: December 13, 2024

## Summary

Ministers and high-level authorities of science and technology of the Americas, convened by the Organization of American States (OAS), adopted a Declaration and Plan of Action focused on the safe, secure, and trustworthy development and deployment of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the Americas.

## Event Details

**Meeting**: VII Meeting of Ministers and High-Level Authorities of Science and Technology of the Americas
**Theme**: "Towards the Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Deployment of AI in the Americas: The Importance of Governance, Regulatory, and Institutional Frameworks"
**Date**: December 12-13, 2024
**Convened by**: Organization of American States (OAS)

## Declaration and Plan of Action

The Declaration was adopted by Ministers and high-level authorities of science and technology from OAS Member States. The declaration emphasizes:

- The critical need for sound governance and institutional frameworks to ensure effective, efficient, and responsible deployment of AI
- The importance of safe, secure, and trustworthy AI development in the Americas
- Commitment to developing national AI policies, programs, and practices across the Western Hemisphere

## AI Governance Initiative

During the meeting on December 12, 2024, the United States Mission to the OAS launched a new project called "Developing an Artificial Intelligence Policy Framework for the Americas," supported by over $1.1 million in grant funding from the U.S. government.

The initiative aims to advance the development of safe, secure, and trustworthy AI policy frameworks and ecosystems by OAS member states across the Western Hemisphere.

## Participation

The meeting included over 150 participants, including:
- AI experts
- Executives from leading technology firms
- Academia representatives
- Ministers and high-level government officials

Participants presented a variety of perspectives and good practices to help Member States shape their own AI policies, programs, and practices.

## Context

This declaration represents a regional multilateral commitment by OAS Member States to develop harmonized AI governance frameworks in the Americas. The Inter-American Framework on Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence (MIGDIA) provides the broader institutional context for these efforts.

- U.S. Mission initiative on AI Policy Framework for the Americas (launched December 12, 2024)
