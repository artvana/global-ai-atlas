# South Dakota Senate Bill 164 — Prohibition on Unlabeled AI-Generated Deepfakes to Influence Elections

**South Dakota 100th Legislative Session, 2025**
**Enacted:** March 31, 2025 | **Effective:** July 1, 2025

**Citation**
*South Dakota — March 31, 2025*

---

## Overview

South Dakota SB 164 prohibits the use of unlabeled AI-generated deepfakes to influence an election within 90 days before an election. The law requires a clear and conspicuous disclaimer when AI-generated content depicts real persons in a political context intended to influence voters. Violation of the labeling requirement is a Class 1 misdemeanor. The law exempts satire, parody, and media broadcasters accurately reporting on political events. It applies to both digital and traditional media distributed in South Dakota.

---

## Key Provisions

### Prohibition on Unlabeled Election Deepfakes
- Within **90 days before an election**, it is unlawful to use, distribute, publish, or broadcast AI-generated deepfakes depicting real persons in a manner intended to influence the outcome of an election **without a clear and conspicuous disclaimer** disclosing that the content is AI-generated.
- Applies to images, video, and audio content created using artificial intelligence, deepfake technology, or digital manipulation that depicts a real, identifiable individual.

### Disclaimer Requirement
- AI-generated content depicting real persons in a political context must include a disclaimer stating that the content is AI-generated or artificially created.
- The disclaimer must be:
  - **Clear and conspicuous** — readily visible or audible to a reasonable viewer or listener;
  - Displayed throughout any video content containing the AI-generated depiction;
  - Spoken aloud at the beginning and end of audio content.

### 90-Day Election Window
- The prohibition and disclaimer requirement apply during the 90-day period immediately preceding any state, county, or municipal election in South Dakota.
- Applies to primary and general elections, ballot measure campaigns, and recall elections.

### Penalty
- Violation is a **Class 1 misdemeanor** under South Dakota law.
- Class 1 misdemeanor carries a maximum penalty of 1 year imprisonment and/or a $2,000 fine (SDCL § 22-6-2).

### Exemptions
- **Satire and parody** — Content that is clearly satirical or parodic in nature and would be understood by a reasonable person as satire or parody is exempt, provided it does not falsely represent a real candidate's genuine statements or actions as authentic.
- **Media broadcasters** — Broadcasters, newspapers, and other media entities that accurately report on political events, candidates, or public officials in a journalistic capacity are exempt when the AI-generated content is the subject of factual reporting, not promotion.
- **Incidental use** — AI tools used for minor editing, captioning, or production purposes that do not generate new impersonating content of real persons are not covered.

### Scope of Media
- Applies to digital media (websites, social media platforms, email, online video);
- Applies to traditional media (television broadcasts, radio, print ads);
- Applies regardless of whether the content is paid or organic/unpaid distribution.

---

## Enforcement

Criminal enforcement by county state's attorneys. The South Dakota Attorney General may also exercise concurrent enforcement authority for statewide election integrity matters.
