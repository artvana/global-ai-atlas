# COMELEC Resolution No. 11064

## Commission on Elections, Republic of the Philippines

**Subject:** Resolution on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in the 2024 National and Local Elections

**Issuing body:** Commission on Elections (COMELEC), Republic of the Philippines

**Context:** Resolution No. 11064 addresses the use of artificial intelligence (AI) technologies in the context of Philippine elections, specifically the 2024 National and Local Elections. The COMELEC has been proactive in regulating emerging technologies in the electoral process.

**Note:** The full text of this resolution was not accessible due to Cloudflare bot protection on the COMELEC website. The official document can be accessed at: https://comelec.gov.ph/?r=2024NationalandLocalElections/Resolution11064

Key provisions of COMELEC resolutions on AI in elections typically address:
- Requirements for disclosure when AI-generated or AI-altered content is used in political advertising
- Prohibitions on deepfakes and other AI-generated disinformation in election campaigns
- Requirements for political candidates and parties regarding AI-generated campaign materials
- Penalties for violations of AI-related election rules
- Reporting requirements for platforms hosting political advertising
