================================================================================
UAE NATIONAL STRATEGY FOR ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE 2031
National Program for Artificial Intelligence
Version 2018
================================================================================

Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence: Omar Sultan Al Olama
Website: www.ai.gov.ae


MINISTERIAL FOREWORD
================================================================================

"We want the UAE to become the world's most prepared country for Artificial Intelligence."
— His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, UAE Vice President and
Prime Minister and Ruler of Dubai

The UAE government knows its strength is in combining a strong vision with active
involvement — investment, legislation and testbeds — for technological innovation.
Therefore, for this nation, being the most prepared country means a lot more than
developing legislation that responds to changes in the world. Instead, it means
proactively changing the world first.

The UAE will build an AI economy, not wait for one.

The National AI Strategy — UAI — from AI ready to AI leader.

The UAE has a vision to become one of the leading nations in AI by 2031 in alignment
with the UAE Centennial 2071, creating new economic, educational, and social
opportunities for citizens, governments and businesses and generating up to AED 335
billion in extra growth.

At the annual World Government Summit Meeting in February 2018, the UAE announced
key elements of its strategy — a welcoming destination for developing AI products, new
education programs and championing good governance.


EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

The UAE sets a clear vision through its AI Strategy, to become the world leader in AI by
2031. The AI Strategy aligns with the UAE Centennial 2071, to make the UAE the best
country in the world by 2071.

The UAE AI and Blockchain Council will overlook the implementation of the AI Strategy
throughout all emirates. Implementation will be a multi-stakeholders effort and
cooperation from different local and federal entities in the UAE.

Eight strategic objectives:
1. Build a reputation as an AI destination
2. Increase the UAE competitive assets in priority sectors through deployment of AI
3. Develop a fertile ecosystem for AI
4. Adopt AI across customer services to improve lives and government
5. Attract and train talent for future jobs enabled by AI
6. Bring world-leading research capability to work with target industries
7. Provide the data and supporting infrastructure essential to become a test bed for AI
8. Ensure strong governance and effective regulation

Economic projections:
- AI to contribute AED 353 billion to GDP by 2030 (13.6% of GDP) — PwC estimate
- Potential gain of AED 335 billion in increased economic output (26% increase)
- Annual AI spending in Middle East and Africa projected at AED 419.54 million by 2021


WHERE THE UAE HAS OPPORTUNITIES TO LEAD
================================================================================

The UAE will focus on four foundational areas:

1. Industry Assets & Emerging Sectors
Priority sectors:
- Resources & Energy: 5th largest oil exporter; transitioning to renewables; AED 91 billion
  in resources and utilities
- Logistics & Transport: Dubai Airport (60M passengers/year), Abu Dhabi Airport
  (26M/year), Jebel Ali port (largest in Middle East); AED 19 billion in logistics
- Tourism & Hospitality: integrated tourist services, AI-driven personalization; AED 136
  billion gain in services and trade sectors
- Healthcare: Dubai Genomics program, population-scale whole-genome sequencing,
  rare disease diagnostics; small sector with world-leading potential
- Cybersecurity: strategic imperative; cybercrime projected at USD 6 trillion damage
  annually by 2021; USD 1 trillion in global security spending over 5 years

Estimated AI output increase by industry (AED billion):
Finance: 103 | Resources: 86 | Construction: 39 | Retail Trade: 39 | Logistics: 33 |
Manufacturing: 19 | ICT: 18 | Government: 14 | Utilities: 13 | Hospitality: 05 | Other: 03/02
Total: AED 335 billion

2. Smart Government
UAE already applying AI in: dynamic transport timetables, smart traffic sensors, facial
recognition for driver fatigue monitoring, chatbots for customer service.

3. Data Sharing and Governance
Combining hands-on experience with new technologies and global policy development.
UAE has 537 open datasets (vs Turkey's 1,280 and Canada's 10,000+). Ambition to
become a leader in AI-ready open data by leveraging 200+ nationalities residing in UAE.

4. New Generation of Regional Talent
Youth unemployment in Arab countries at 30.6% in 2016. UAE has 22% STEM graduate
share (vs 16% US). Strong segment of professionals with operational, IT and engineering
skills in MENA. 21% of business analyst skills in region associated with business
intelligence software; 20% with data operations; 8% with ML and statistical modeling.
40% of UAE workforce has good digital skills (vs 56% UK).


EIGHT STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE 1: BUILD A REPUTATION AS AN AI DESTINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

UAI Brand:
The UAE is developing a "UAI" brand to attract talent and business globally.
- UAI mark recognising high quality, ethical AI companies
- UAI Seal of Approval for safe, efficient, verified AI technology
- Four levels of approval: Public Sector Level, Private Sector Level, Institutional Level,
  Product Level
- Certification based on highest level of worldwide standards
- UAE to host key international conferences and forums on AI


OBJECTIVE 2: INCREASE THE UAE COMPETITIVE ASSETS IN PRIORITY SECTORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Existing priority sectors and initiatives:

a. Resources & Energy:
Proof-of-concept to utilize AI systems for internal energy saving decisions and global
supply/demand understanding for oil. AI for smart grids and water recycling innovation.

b. Logistics & Transport:
Demonstrator projects for air traffic management, baggage handling, airplane boarding
using physical and digital assets at Dubai and Abu Dhabi airports and Jebel Ali port.

c. Tourism & Hospitality:
Partnerships to integrate tourist attraction and in-UAE services; AI to predict tourist
needs and provide customized services; automated assistants during stays.

Emerging sectors:

a. Healthcare:
Access for companies and researchers to hospital and national databases; AI algorithm
for detecting Tuberculosis via X-ray diagnosis (piloted at UN World Data Forum 2018);
partnership models for quantum computing in health diagnostics.

b. Cybersecurity:
Supporting pilots for new cybersecurity approaches; community and skills-building
programs for SMEs and local talent.

Proof-of-Concept Support:
UAE government to fund or broker pilot projects within priority sectors. Funding
dependent on mapping to sector reasoning. Working with private sector companies on
quantum computing for health diagnostics and global energy supply management.


OBJECTIVE 3: DEVELOP A FERTILE ECOSYSTEM FOR AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI Network:
Network of researchers, industry experts and policy experts from across the UAE.
Collaboration with Mohammed bin Rashid Innovation Fund (AED 2 billion to support
local innovators) and UAE Artificial Intelligence and Blockchain Council.

Applied AI Accelerator:
Support for domestic AI startup and product development through incubator funds,
mentoring, and publication of shared knowledge. Modelled on programmes like
Zeroth.AI (Hong Kong) and AI Singapore's 100 Experiments project (up to USD 250,000
for industry-specific AI solutions).

AI Incentive Scheme for Overseas Companies:
Incentives for UAE firms to partner with global AI technology firms; motivate
international companies to set up regional offices or relocate to UAE; support
technology transfer and FDI.

Business Support for UAE AI Firms:
Guidance, financial support, and coordination to develop AI solutions and strengthen
connections to international markets; increasing exports and growth.


OBJECTIVE 4: ADOPT AI ACROSS CUSTOMER SERVICES TO IMPROVE LIVES AND GOVERNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

National AI Challenges:
Program to support best ideas from government, universities and private sector to
solve UAE's most pressing challenges using AI (obesity, heart disease, traffic fatalities,
poor air quality, education outcomes). Modelled on Australia's National Missions
Program.

UAE Artificial Intelligence and Blockchain Council:
Representatives from all emirates on federal and local levels. Main objective: identify
how and where AI can be incorporated in government and what supporting
infrastructure it requires. Target: move key government services (tax filings, applications,
regulatory compliance checks, payment of fines) to interoperable digital platforms.


OBJECTIVE 5: ATTRACT AND TRAIN TALENT FOR FUTURE JOBS ENABLED BY AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Context:
- 43% of existing UAE work activities potentially automatable based on current technologies
- ~300,000 UAE jobs in administrative/government sector may be impacted
- ~125,000 of these held by UAE nationals
- 70% of Emiratis employed in public sector; 54% of Emirati workers ideally wanted
  administrative roles in 2016 survey

Public AI Training:
- UAE AI Summer Camp (summer 2018): 5,000+ UAE residents trained in AI fundamentals
- Free courses for UAE residents to raise awareness

Upskilling Students:
- Upskill 1/3 of UAE's STEM graduates per year (2,000 students)
- Specialist courses for STEM graduates to increase AI expert pipeline
- Target: 1,000 government-supported PhD places in AI and related disciplines by 2025

Government Training:
- Advanced courses for Government employees from Q4 2018
- Target: 100% of senior government leadership (Director-General, Ministerial,
  Senior-Ministerial levels) trained and versed in AI
- AI Experts (ambassadors) programme with capstone projects

Professional Upskilling:
Specialist training, secondments and study tours overseas for professionals with expert
digital and analytic skills.

Employment Transition Support:
Career advice and training services for those at risk from automation (60% of workforce
with low digital skills).

Skills tiers:
- Workers with low digital skills (60%): career advice, basic digital/computational courses
- Professionals with digital competence (35%): project-based learning, computational concepts
- Professionals with expert digital skills (5%): short courses on specific techniques,
  overseas secondments
- AI experts (0.2%): PhD scholarships, summer courses


OBJECTIVE 6: BRING WORLD-LEADING RESEARCH CAPABILITY TO WORK WITH TARGET INDUSTRIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Context:
UAE ranked 35th in world for overall R&D investment. 0.19% of population working as
full-time researchers in science, technology and innovation (vs Korea 0.69%, UK 0.44%).

National Virtual AI Institute:
Survey of current local R&D capacity followed by launch of National Virtual AI Institute
with stakeholder partners to aggregate best local and global expertise, encourage R&D
activity, collaboration and commercialisation.

Key Thinkers Program:
Programme to attract key AI thinkers to visit UAE. Participation in workshops and
lectures with local universities and businesses; incentives to run research projects in
partnership with local bodies. Award for programmes with outstanding governance
frameworks or greatest social impact.

AI Library:
Open-access digital library of research and papers in both English and Arabic — a
first-of-a-kind initiative for the region. Accessible summaries of UAE government-funded
AI research. Joint collaboration between academia, industry and government.


OBJECTIVE 7: PROVIDE THE DATA AND SUPPORTING INFRASTRUCTURE ESSENTIAL TO BECOME A TEST BED FOR AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data Sharing:
UAE ambition to create a data-sharing programme providing shared open and
standardised AI-ready data collected through a consistent data standard. Modelled on
Estonia's X-Road platform. UAE has 537 open datasets vs Turkey's 1,280 and Canada's
10,000+.

Secure Data Infrastructure:
Investment in single AI data infrastructure to facilitate data sharing, manage privacy
concerns, and support access for research and product development. Modelled on
Australia's SURE (Secure User Research Environment). Address data protection and
authentication as part of good corporate practice. Reference to Europe's GDPR as an
opportunity to re-consider how consumer data is handled.


OBJECTIVE 8: ENSURE STRONG GOVERNANCE AND EFFECTIVE REGULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

National Governance Review:
UAE Artificial Intelligence and Blockchain Council to review national approaches to
data management, ethics and cybersecurity; review latest international best practices
in legislation and global AI risks; oversee implementation of the AI Strategy in the UAE.

Second Global Governance of Artificial Intelligence Roundtable:
Working with UNESCO, OECD, IEEE and the Council on Extended Intelligence for the
2019 World Government Summit. High Level Ministerial Panel of foremost Ministers
of Digital, Technology and ICT responsible for AI in their countries.

Intergovernmental Panel on Artificial Intelligence:
UAE aims to work with France and other governments to create the foundations for
an "IPCC for Artificial Intelligence" — an intergovernmental body providing a mechanism
for governments and private companies to understand AI and its impact on societies and
give a solid framework for future regulation. Announced in parallel with France's AI
strategy launch (President Macron, March 2018).


CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The UAE is among the first movers in paving the path for AI nationally. The AI National
Strategy is a cornerstone of the UAE Centennial 2071 and is a major variable in the
overall equation. It will bring transformation to a new level by 2031 and set the
foundations for future generations in the UAE.

Goal: to catalyze the responsible development of AI within the UAE, in order to help
reach the UAE Centennial 2071 — and to act as an inspiration for other nations to
harness this technology for the betterment of humankind.
