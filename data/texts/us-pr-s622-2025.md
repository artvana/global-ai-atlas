GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO
20th Legislative Assembly      3rd Regular
            Session
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
Senate Bill No. 622
SECOND POSITIVE REPORT
March 13, 2026
TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PUERTO RICO:
    The Government Committee of the House of Representatives, after study and consideration, is pleased to render this Second Positive Report on Senate Bill 622, recommending to this High Body its approval without amendments.
SCOPE OF THE MEASURE
Senate Bill 622, approved by the Senate on June 24, 2025, proposes to create the "Transparency and Right to Human Interaction in Public Services Assisted by Artificial Intelligence Act," for the purposes of requiring the agencies and public corporations of the Government of Puerto Rico to inform citizens when they are interacting with automated systems or artificial intelligence, and to guarantee the right to request the intervention of a human being in the provision of public services.
The measure further establishes that the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) shall be the entity responsible for developing the regulations necessary for the implementation of this Act within a term not exceeding one hundred and eighty (180) days from its approval.
Likewise, it provides for the imposition of administrative sanctions in case of non-compliance by the public agencies or corporations, and incorporates provisions aimed at ensuring that citizens may exercise their right to receive human attention at any stage of the provision of public services assisted by automated systems.
Analysis of the Measure
It is widely known that the advancement of artificial intelligence technologies has transformed the way in which public and private institutions provide services to citizens. In that context, governments around the world have begun to adopt automated tools for the purpose of improving administrative efficiency, expediting the provision of services, and broadening the channels of interaction with citizens.
However, and as set forth in the Statement of Motives, the use of these technologies also poses important challenges related to transparency, accessibility, and the protection of citizens' rights, particularly with regard to ensuring that persons can understand when they are interacting with automated systems and that they have the possibility of requesting the intervention of a human being when they so require.
In view of this, the measure seeks to address these concerns through the establishment of rules that ensure transparency in the use of artificial intelligence systems within governmental services, while safeguarding the right of citizens to receive human attention when they deem it necessary.
According to the analysis of this Committee, the proposed legislation constitutes an appropriate tool to harmonize the technological development of the Government with the protection of citizens' rights, promoting a model of digital transformation that is inclusive, transparent, and centered on public service.
SCOPE OF THE REPORT
For the study and evaluation of Senate Bill 622, the Government Committee of the House of Representatives requested explanatory memoranda from various agencies and entities, among them:
Autoridad de Asesoría Financiera y Agencia Fiscal (AAFAF)
Comisión de Derechos Civiles (CDC)
Departamento de Justicia
Defensoría de las Personas con Impedimentos (DPI)
Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto (OGP)
Oficina de Presupuesto de la Asamblea Legislativa (OPAL)
Oficina del Procurador de las Personas de Edad Avanzada
Likewise, this Committee examined the memoranda submitted before the Senate's Committee on Science, Technology and Artificial Intelligence by the Departamento de Asuntos del Consumidor (DACO), the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), and the entity Soy Un Grinch, LLC.
The following were received and considered:
Autoridad de Asesoría Financiera y Agencia Fiscal (AAFAF)
The AAFAF expressed that the Government Program of this Administration contemplates the use of artificial intelligence as a tool to improve government services and the citizen experience, through the adoption of a digital transformation and artificial intelligence (AI) plan.
To that end, it noted that said program provides for the need to evaluate the digital maturity of the agencies that provide essential services, define a short- and medium-term digital vision, and establish strategic initiatives aimed at closing the gap between the current situation and the projected goals. Likewise, it indicated that it is essential to set specific goals for each agency and to drive the digital integration of government services, in order to offer a more agile and coordinated experience to the citizen.
Finally, it recommended that the Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly (OPAL) be requested to evaluate the fiscal impact of the measure. The AAFAF supported approval of the measure.
Comisión de Derechos Civiles
The Civil Rights Commission held that artificial intelligence represents a far-reaching technological transformation that significantly impacts daily life and social interactions. It further noted that, although these technologies bring efficiency, they should be conceived as a complement to human interaction and not as a substitute for it, which is why it expressed its support for the approval of the measure.
Defensoría de las Personas con Impedimentos
The Defensoría de las Personas con Impedimentos indicated that it endorses the public policy contained in the project, particularly with regard to the creation of alternatives to automated systems that facilitate equitable access to government services by persons with disabilities. In support of its position, it highlighted its function as the agency in charge of safeguarding the protection of the rights of persons who face discrimination on the basis of disability, and therefore it expressed its support for the legislative intent of the project.
Departamento de Asuntos del Consumidor (DACO)
DACO reaffirmed in its testimony its commitment to the development of an efficient, agile public administration oriented to the protection of consumers, particularly the most vulnerable sectors. It indicated that Senate Bill 622 promotes rules that foster the trust of users when interacting with artificial intelligence tools within the governmental sphere.
Furthermore, it maintains that it is a government agency that offers direct services to citizens, and its employees are in direct contact with persons who visit our five regional offices. DACO argues that they have seen first-hand how the number of senior citizens visiting our offices in search of services has been increasing, as well as persons with disabilities, low levels of schooling, and poor digital literacy. For this reason, they understand it indispensable that rules such as the one provided in Article 4 of S.B. 622 be enacted to prevent the most disadvantaged persons from being discriminated against and deprived of services. They conclude by pointing out that every government agency must have available the alternative of offering the service through a human being with sensitivity and empathy.
Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS)
PRITS specified that the measure aligns with internationally recognized principles for the responsible use of artificial intelligence. It noted that various jurisdictions in the United States have adopted legislative initiatives with similar purposes and expressed that the approval of this legislation would strengthen its capacity to lead the technical and regulatory implementation of governmental technological solutions, ensuring compliance with standards of efficiency, accessibility, and transparency.
Likewise, it maintained that the approval of the measure would represent a necessary step to strengthen the legitimacy of the use of AI in the public sector, incorporating ethical and legal safeguards that inspire trust and protect citizens' rights. Finally, it endorsed the project, since it is in keeping with the best international practices, and it responds to regulatory needs not yet addressed by the current legal framework and promotes a fair balance between technological efficiency and respect for citizen's rights.
Soy Un Grinch, LLC.
Soy Un Grinch, LLC expressed that, as a Puerto Rican entity dedicated to training and consulting in artificial intelligence, it has worked directly with agencies, companies and organizations in promoting the ethical adoption of these emerging technologies. It maintained that the measure has the potential to position Puerto Rico as a leader in responsible regulation of artificial intelligence, and therefore endorsed its approval.
FISCAL IMPACT
In accordance with the analysis carried out by the Office of the Budget of the Legislative Assembly (OPAL), the approval of Senate Bill 622 does not represent an additional fiscal impact for the agencies and public corporations of the Government of Puerto Rico.
OPAL indicated that the requirements established in the measure may be integrated within the existing or future technological platforms that use artificial intelligence, in a manner similar to the citizen guidance mechanisms already required in digital governmental services.
Likewise, it noted that the alternative of human attention forms part of the ordinary functions of public service, and therefore does not entail the creation of new positions or additional budgetary allocations.
CONCLUSION
The Government Committee considers that Senate Bill 622 constitutes a public policy measure aimed at strengthening transparency, accessibility, and citizen trust in the provision of governmental services in the context of the State's digital transformation.
Having examined the positions presented by the agencies and entities concerned, this Committee concludes that the recommendations made were duly considered during the legislative procedure in the Senate and incorporated into the approved text, particularly with regard to the clarification of technical definitions, the delimitation of the regulatory responsibilities of PRITS, and the establishment of a reasonable and proportional penalty scheme.
Likewise, this Committee recognizes that the measure addresses legitimate concerns related to the possible digital exclusion of vulnerable populations, such as the elderly, persons with disabilities, and citizens with limited technological skills, by guaranteeing clear mechanisms that allow access to human attention within automated processes.
Likewise, we emphasize that the measure does not limit technological development, but rather establishes reasonable safeguards aimed at promoting transparency, accountability, and inclusion in the Government's digital transformation process. The assignment of responsibilities to PRITS for its implementation also ensures coherence and uniformity in the application of technological standards within the public administration.
Therefore, we conclude that the legislation establishes reasonable safeguards to ensure that the use of automated systems and artificial intelligence in the government is carried out responsibly, while always maintaining the availability of human intervention when so required by the citizen.
For the foregoing reasons, this Committee recommends the approval of Senate Bill 622 without amendments.
