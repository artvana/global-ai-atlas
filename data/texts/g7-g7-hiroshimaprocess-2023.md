# Hiroshima Process International Guiding Principles for Organizations Developing Advanced AI Systems
**G7 — October 30, 2023**

Also accompanied by: Hiroshima Process International Code of Conduct for Organizations Developing Advanced AI Systems

## Overview
On October 30, 2023, the G7 countries (Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, UK, US and the EU) reached agreement on 11 International Guiding Principles for AI and a Code of Conduct for AI developers. The framework provides voluntary guidance for organizations developing advanced AI systems including foundation models and generative AI.

## The 11 Guiding Principles

### 1. Risk Identification and Mitigation Throughout Lifecycle
Take appropriate measures throughout the development of advanced AI systems to identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks, including testing, red-teaming, and ensuring traceability of datasets and processes.

### 2. Post-Deployment Vulnerability Management
Monitor for vulnerabilities and misuse after deployment, facilitate third-party reporting, and maintain incident documentation.

### 3. Public Transparency Reporting
"Publicly report advanced AI systems' capabilities, limitations and domains of appropriate and inappropriate use" through transparency reports for significant releases.

### 4. Responsible Information Sharing
Share evaluation reports, security risks, and dangerous capabilities responsibly across industry, government, civil society, and academia.

### 5. AI Governance and Risk Management Policies
Establish and disclose governance frameworks grounded in risk-based approaches, including privacy policies and accountability mechanisms.

### 6. Robust Security Controls
Implement physical security, cybersecurity, and insider threat safeguards protecting model weights, algorithms, servers, and datasets.

### 7. Content Authentication and Provenance
Deploy watermarking or similar techniques enabling users to identify AI-generated content and verify its origin.

### 8. Safety Research Investment
Prioritize research addressing societal, safety, and security risks with appropriate mitigation tools.

### 9. Global Challenge Focus
Direct development toward addressing climate, health, and education challenges aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

### 10. International Technical Standards
Contribute to developing and adopting international standards, including watermarking specifications.

### 11. Data Input Protections
Manage training data quality, mitigate harmful biases, ensure dataset transparency, and comply with applicable legal frameworks.

## Nature
Voluntary, non-binding guidance framework for AI developers.
