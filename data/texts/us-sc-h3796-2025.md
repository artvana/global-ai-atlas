 South Carolina General Assembly 
 
 126th Session, 2025-2026 
 
 H. 3796 
 
 
 
 
 A BILL 
 
 TO AMEND THE SOUTH CAROLINA CODE OF LAWS BY ADDING ARTICLE 29 TO CHAPTER 1, TITLE 1 SO AS TO PROHIBIT A GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY FROM GRANTING OR RECOGNIZING LEGAL PERSONHOOD IN CERTAIN ITEMS. 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: 
 
 SECTION 1. Chapter 1, Title 1 of the S.C. Code is amended by adding: 
 Article 29 
 Legal Personhood 
 Section 1-1-1910. (A) As used in this article: 
 (1) &quot;Body of water&quot; means any natural or man-made accumulation of water, regardless of whether the accumulation of water is static or subject to a force that causes a hydrological current. 
 (2) &quot;Governmental entity&quot; means: 
 (a) a court; 
 (b) the General Assembly; 
 (c) the governing body of a political subdivision; or 
 (d) another entity of the State or a political subdivision, if the entity has adjudicatory or rulemaking authority. 
 (3) &quot;Human being&quot; means a member of the species classified as Homo sapiens; 
 (4) &quot;Land&quot; means the solid terrestrial surface or subsurface of the earth. 
 (5) &quot;Legal personhood&quot; means: 
 (a) the legal rights and obligations of an individual under the laws of this State; or 
 (b) the legal rights and obligations of a person other than an individual under the laws of this State. 
 (6) &quot;Political subdivision&quot; means the same as that term is defined in Section 2-7-76. 
 (7) &quot;Real property&quot; means any building, fixture, improvement, appurtenance, structure, or other development that is affixed permanently to land. 
 (8) &quot;State&quot; means the same as that term is defined in Section 1-3-490. 
 (B) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a governmental entity may not grant legal personhood to, nor recognize legal personhood in: 
 (1) artificial intelligence; 
 (2) an inanimate object; 
 (3) a body of water; 
 (4) land; 
 (5) real property; 
 (6) atmospheric gases; 
 (7) an astronomical object; 
 (8) weather; 
 (9) a plant; 
 (10) a nonhuman animal; or 
 (11) any other member of a taxonomic domain that is not a human being. 
 SECTION 2. This act takes upon approval by the Governor.
