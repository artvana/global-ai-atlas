# California Assembly Bill 853 — AI Transparency Act Technical Corrections

**Assembly Bill No. 853**

*California — October 13, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

AB 853 amends the California AI Transparency Act (SB 942, enacted 2024) to delay the compliance date for content provenance and AI origin disclosure requirements from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026, and to make technical corrections to the disclosure framework.

### Implementation Delay

The bill pushes back the operative date of SB 942's AI detection tool and manifest/latent disclosure requirements from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026. This delay was intended to provide large AI providers (those with 1 million or more monthly users) additional time to implement the required technical infrastructure for watermarking and detection tools.

### Technical Corrections

AB 853 makes technical corrections to SB 942's disclosure requirements, clarifying definitions and procedural obligations for covered providers. The core requirement that large AI providers make available a free, publicly accessible AI detection tool — enabling users to determine whether image, video, or audio content was generated or substantially altered by a generative AI system — is maintained without substantive change.

### Enforcement

Enforcement authority and civil penalty structure from SB 942 ($5,000 per violation, collected by the Attorney General, city attorneys, or county counsel) are carried forward without modification.
