 AN ACT CONCERNING UNAUTHORIZED DISSEMINATION OF
 INTIMATE IMAGES THAT ARE DIGITALLY ALTERED OR CREATED
 THROUGH THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         Section 1. Section 53a-189c of the general statutes is repealed and the
2      following is substituted in lieu thereof (Effective October 1, 2025):

 3        (a) A person is guilty of unlawful dissemination of an intimate image
 4     when (1) such person intentionally disseminates by electronic or other
 5     means a photograph, film, videotape or other recorded image, including
 6     any digitally altered image or image created through the use of artificial
 7     intelligence that depicts the likeness of a person, of (A) the genitals,
 8     pubic area or buttocks of another person with less than a fully opaque
 9     covering of such body part, or the breast of such other person who is
10     female with less than a fully opaque covering of any portion of such
11     breast below the top of the nipple, or (B) another person engaged in
12     sexual intercourse, as defined in section 53a-193, (2) such person
13     disseminates such image without the consent of such other person,
14     knowing that such other person understood that the image would not

17     (b) For purposes of this [subsection, "disseminate"] section:

18      (1) "Disseminate" means to sell, give, provide, lend, trade, mail,
19   deliver, transfer, publish, distribute, circulate, present, exhibit, advertise

21     (2) "Harm" includes, but is not limited to, subjecting such other

25   living, identifiable person; and

26      (4) "Artificial intelligence" means any technology, including, but not
27   limited to, machine learning, that uses data to train an algorithm or
28   predictive model for the purpose of enabling a computer system or
29   service to autonomously perform any task, including, but not limited to,
30   visual perception, language processing or speech recognition, that is
31   normally associated with human intelligence or perception.

33   shall not apply to:

34      (1) Any image described in subsection (a) of this section of such other
35   person if such image resulted from voluntary exposure or engagement
36   in sexual intercourse by such other person, in a public place, as defined
37   in section 53a-181, or in a commercial setting;

38      (2) Any image described in subsection (a) of this section of such other
39   person, if such other person is not clearly identifiable, unless other
40   personally identifying information is associated with or accompanies
41   the image; or
