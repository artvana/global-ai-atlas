General Policy for the Safe and Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems
Sultanate of Oman
April 1, 2025

Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology (MTCIT)

Categories: Artificial Intelligence, Policies and Governance

OVERVIEW

The National AI Policy 2025 establishes a comprehensive strategic framework for the responsible, safe, and effective adoption of artificial intelligence technologies in Oman. Published April 1, 2025, this policy supersedes or supplements the 2024 draft AI Policy, providing an updated framework aligned with Oman Vision 2040.

The policy aims to govern the use of advanced AI technologies in a manner that aligns with human values and national principles, and enhances the Sultanate's competitiveness in areas of digital transformation and technological innovation.

KEY FOCUS AREAS

The policy addresses the following key areas:
- Fostering innovation in AI development and deployment
- Supporting economic and developmental sectors through AI adoption
- Building national AI capabilities and human resources
- Ensuring integration between legislation and technology for ethical and sustainable use
- Infrastructure development for AI systems
- Investment stimulation in the AI sector
- Research and innovation encouragement in AI

ALIGNMENT WITH NATIONAL STRATEGY

The policy contributes to Oman Vision 2040 objectives and aims to strengthen the Sultanate's global standing in artificial intelligence. It builds on:
- The 2024 National AI Policy
- The National Program for Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Digital Technologies (September 2024)
- Oman's broader digital transformation agenda

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

The policy establishes governance requirements for both public and private sector entities. Key elements include:

Responsible Use of AI:
The policy emphasizes the need for responsible and transparent use of AI systems, including generative AI, within environments that respect individual privacy and support sustainable development.

Regulatory Alignment:
The Ministry called on all regulatory bodies to align their internal systems with the content of the policy and circulate it among their affiliated entities, to ensure consistency in the regulatory framework across the Sultanate.

Human-Centered AI:
AI deployment must maintain human oversight and ensure that technology serves human needs rather than the reverse.

SAFE AND ETHICAL PRINCIPLES

The 2025 policy reaffirms and expands on principles from the 2024 draft:

1. Safety — AI systems must be designed and operated to ensure physical and operational safety. Impact assessments required before high-risk deployments.

2. Ethics — AI must comply with ethical standards aligned with Omani values and Islamic principles where applicable.

3. Privacy — Compliance with Personal Data Protection Law mandatory. Data minimization and purpose limitation required.

4. Transparency — AI decision-making processes must be explainable to users and regulators.

5. Accountability — Clear responsibility structures for AI systems mandatory.

6. Fairness — AI must not perpetuate discrimination or bias.

7. Reliability — AI systems must perform consistently and accurately.

8. Environmental Responsibility — AI deployment must consider environmental impact.

RESPONSIBLE AI SCOPE

The policy specifically addresses:
- Generative AI systems used by public and private entities
- Automated decision-making systems affecting individuals
- AI systems used in critical infrastructure and public services
- AI applications in government services

APPLICATION TO GOVERNMENT ENTITIES

Government entities are required to:
- Adopt the policy framework for all AI deployments
- Align procurement processes with responsible AI requirements
- Establish internal governance for AI systems
- Report on AI use to MTCIT as required
