 AN ACT REQUIRING A STUDY OF THE EFFECT OF ARTIFICIAL
 INTELLIGENCE ON THE TRADES INDUSTRY.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to require a study of the effect
2      of artificial intelligence on trade workers. Such study shall include, but
3      not be limited to, job displacement and the curriculum of the Technical
4      Education and Career System.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To study the effect of artificial intelligence on the trades industry.
