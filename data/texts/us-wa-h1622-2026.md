 NEW SECTION. Sec. 5. A new section is added to chapter 41.56 RCW to read as follows: 
 (1) An employer shall bargain over the decision to adopt artificial intelligence technology or modify the current uses of artificial intelligence technology if the adoption or modification affects employees&#39; wages or performance evaluations. An employer is not required to bargain over the implementation or modification of artificial intelligence technology if the implementation or modification is part of an update made by a third party to technology already in use by employees. 
 (2) For the purposes of this section: 
 (i) &quot;Artificial intelligence&quot; has the same meaning as defined in RCW 41.80.005 ; 
 (ii) &quot;Third party&quot; means an individual or entity that provides services such as technology services, digital services, equipment, or software, but does not have an employment or a coemployment relationship with the employer. 
 
 NEW SECTION. Sec. 6. A new section is added to chapter 41.80 RCW to read as follows: 
 (1) An employer shall bargain over the decision to adopt artificial intelligence technology or modify the current uses of artificial intelligence technology if the adoption or modification affects employees&#39; wages or performance evaluations. An employer is not required to bargain over the implementation or modification of artificial intelligence technology if the implementation or modification is part of an update made by a third party to technology already in use by employees. 
 (2) For the purposes of this section, &quot;third party&quot; means an individual or entity that provides services such as technology services, digital services, equipment, or software, but does not have an employment or a coemployment relationship with the employer. 
 
 NEW SECTION. Sec. 7. Contracts in effect prior to the effective date of this section remain unaffected by sections 5 and 6 of this act until the contract expires or is renewed or reopened. 
 
 NEW SECTION. Sec. 8. If specific funding for the purposes of this act, referencing this act by bill or chapter number, is not provided by June 30, 2026, in the omnibus appropriations act, this act is null and void.
