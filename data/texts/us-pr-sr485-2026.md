GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO

20th Legislative Assembly                                          3rd Regular Session

SENATE OF PUERTO RICO

S.R. 485

April 16, 2026

Introduced by Mr. Reyes Berríos

RESOLUTION

To amend Section 3 of S.R. 133, which directs the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence to conduct a thorough investigation for the purpose of investigating and learning about the technological advances implemented by the State Elections Commission, as well as its strategic and technological projection in anticipation of future electoral events.

BE IT RESOLVED BY THE SENATE OF PUERTO RICO:

Section 1.- Section 3 of Senate Resolution 133 is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Section 3.- The Committee shall submit a report with its findings, recommendations, and conclusions to the Senate of Puerto Rico, on or before the conclusion of the 3rd Regular Session of the Twentieth Legislative Assembly."

Section 2.- This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
