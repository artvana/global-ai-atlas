 STATE OF OKLAHOMA 
 2nd Session of the 60th Legislature (2026) 
 HOUSE BILL 3176 
 By: Archer 
 AS INTRODUCED 
 
 
 An Act relating to state development; creating the Oklahoma Gas, Artificial Intelligence, and Space Research Hub (GAS Hub); establishing the GAS Hub as the State&#39;s central coordinating entity for recruitment, development, and hosting of a National Laboratory; clarifying that the GAS Hub shall function as a state-federal partnership platform; providing the purposes of the GAS Hub; directing the Oklahoma Department of Commerce in coordination with other state agencies to administer the GAS Hub; authorizing the GAS Hub to enter public-private partnerships for certain purposes; directing the GAS Hub to prepare and maintain formal strategies; directing the GAS Hub to submit an annual report; establishing the Oklahoma National Laboratory Development Program; directing the GAS Hub to administer the program; granting the Oklahoma Department of Commerce rulemaking authority; providing for codification; and providing an effective date. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA: 
 
 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 5003.20 of Title 74, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows: 
 There is hereby created the Oklahoma Gas, Artificial Intelligence, and Space Research Hub (GAS Hub) to serve as the State of Oklahoma&#39;s central coordinating entity for the recruitment, development, and hosting of a United States National Laboratory. The GAS Hub shall function as a state-federal partnership platform to attract, secure, and support federal laboratory designation, funding, and operations. 
 SECTION 2. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 5003.20a of Title 74, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows: 
 A. The purposes of the Oklahoma Gas, Artificial Intelligence, and Space Research Hub (GAS Hub) are to: 
 1. Position Oklahoma as a leading national center for: 
 a. advanced oil and gas research, 
 b. artificial intelligence and high-performance computing, and 
 c. aerospace, space launch systems, and orbital technologies; 
 2. Prepare and maintain federal-ready laboratory sites; 
 3. Coordinate workforce pipelines; 
 4. Aggregate state incentives and infrastructure; and 
 5. Serve as the formal applicant and liaison to the U.S. Department of Energy, the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, Department of War, and other federal agencies. 
 B. The GAS Hub shall be administered by the Oklahoma Department of Commerce, and shall provide necessary staffing for the GAS Hub, in coordination with: 
 1. The Oklahoma Corporation Commission; 
 2. The Oklahoma Space Industry Development Authority (OSIDA); 
 3. The State Regents for Higher Education; 
 4. The Oklahoma Department of Transportation; and 
 5. The Office of Management and Enterprise Services. 
 C. The GAS Hub may enter into public-private partnerships to: 
 1. Develop research parks; 
 2. Construct laboratory campuses; 
 3. Build high-performance computing facilities; and 
 4. Construct spaceport and test range infrastructure. 
 D. The GAS Hub shall prepare and maintain a formal strategy for: 
 1. U.S. Department of Energy National Lab designation; 
 2. U.S. National Aeronautics and Space Administration center designation; 
 3. U.S. Department of War research campus designation; and 
 4. Federally Funded Research and Development Center status. 
 E. The GAS Hub shall submit an annual report to the Governor, President Pro Tempore of the Oklahoma Senate, and the Speaker of the Oklahoma House of Representatives detailing: 
 1. Federal engagement; 
 2. Site readiness; 
 3. Funds expended; 
 4. Workforce pipeline development; and 
 5. Federal designation progress. 
 SECTION 3. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 5003.20b of Title 74, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows: 
 A. There is hereby created the Oklahoma National Laboratory Development Program to: 
 1. Identify and certify federal-ready laboratory sites; 
 2. Pre-permit utility, water, rail, and highway access; 
 3. Aggregate state tax incentives; 
 4. Coordinate land acquisition and site assembly; 
 5. Maintain pre-approved environmental compliance documents; and 
 6. Prepare formal federal applications. 
 B. This program shall be administered by the Oklahoma Gas, Artificial Intelligence, and Space Research Hub in coordination with other state agencies. 
 SECTION 4. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 5003.20c of Title 74, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows: 
 The Oklahoma Department of Commerce shall promulgate rules to implement this act. 
 SECTION 5. This act shall become effective November 1, 2026.
