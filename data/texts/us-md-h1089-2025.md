 TITLE 7.3. DATA BROKER REGISTRY AND GROSS INCOME TAX. 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 1. DEFINITIONS. 
 
 
 7.3-101. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS TITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;APPORTIONABLE INCOME&quot; MEANS GROSS INCOME OF A DATA BROKER GENERATED FROM ENGAGING IN DATA BROKERING, INCLUDING INCOME RECEIVED FROM DATA BROKERING PERFORMED OUTSIDE THE STATE IF THE INCOME WOULD BE TAXABLE UNDER THIS TITLE IF RECEIVED FROM DATA BROKERING IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (C) (1) &quot;BIOMETRIC INFORMATION&quot; MEANS A RECORD OF ONE OR MORE MEASURABLE BIOLOGICAL OR BEHAVIORAL CHARACTERISTICS THAT CAN BE USED ALONE OR IN COMBINATION WITH EACH OTHER OR WITH OTHER INFORMATION FOR AUTOMATED RECOGNITION OF A KNOWN OR UNKNOWN INDIVIDUAL. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;BIOMETRIC INFORMATION&quot; INCLUDES: 
 
 
 (I) FINGERPRINTS; 
 
 
 (II) RETINA AND IRIS PATTERNS; 
 
 
 (III) VOICEPRINTS; 
 
 
 (IV) DNA SEQUENCE; 
 
 
 (V) FACIAL CHARACTERISTICS; 
 
 
 (VI) GAIT; 
 
 
 (VII) HANDWRITING; 
 
 
 (VIII) KEYSTROKE DYNAMICS; AND 
 
 
 (IX) MOUSE MOVEMENTS. 
 
 
 (3) &quot;BIOMETRIC INFORMATION&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) WRITING SAMPLES; 
 
 
 (II) WRITTEN SIGNATURES; 
 
 
 (III) HUMAN BIOLOGICAL SAMPLES USED FOR VALID SCIENTIFIC TESTING OR SCREENING; 
 
 
 (IV) DEMOGRAPHIC DATA; 
 
 
 (V) TATTOO DESCRIPTIONS; 
 
 
 (VI) PHYSICAL DESCRIPTIONS, SUCH AS HEIGHT, WEIGHT, HAIR COLOR, OR EYE COLOR; OR 
 
 
 (VII) DONATED ORGANS, TISSUES, OR PARTS, OR BLOOD OR SERUM STORED ON BEHALF OF RECIPIENTS OR POTENTIAL RECIPIENTS OF LIVING OR CADAVERIC TRANSPLANTS AND OBTAINED OR STORED BY A FEDERALLY DESIGNATED ORGAN PROCUREMENT AGENCY. 
 
 
 (D) &quot;BROKERED PERSONAL DATA&quot; MEANS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING COMPUTERIZED DATA ELEMENTS ABOUT A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL, IF CATEGORIZED OR ORGANIZED FOR SALE OR LICENSING TO ANOTHER ENTITY: 
 
 
 (1) THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S NAME OR THE NAME OF A MEMBER OF THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S IMMEDIATE FAMILY OR HOUSEHOLD; 
 
 
 (2) THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39; S ADDRESS OR AN ADDRESS FOR A MEMBER OF THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S IMMEDIATE FAMILY OR HOUSEHOLD; 
 
 
 (3) THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S DATE OF BIRTH OR PLACE OF BIRTH; 
 
 
 (4) THE MAIDEN NAME OF THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S MOTHER; 
 
 
 (5) BIOMETRIC INFORMATION ABOUT THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL; 
 
 
 (6) PERSONAL DATA ABOUT THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL; 
 
 
 (7) THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S SOCIAL SECURITY NUMBER OR THE NUMBER OF ANY OTHER GOVERNMENT-ISSUED IDENTIFICATION FOR THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL; OR 
 
 
 (8) OTHER INFORMATION THAT, ALONE OR IN COMBINATION WITH OTHER INFORMATION THAT IS SOLD OR LICENSED, CAN REASONABLY BE ASSOCIATED WITH THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL. 
 
 
 (E) (1) &quot;BUSINESS ENTITY&quot; MEANS: 
 
 
 (I) A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL WHO REGULARLY ENGAGES IN COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY FOR THE PURPOSE OF GENERATING INCOME; 
 
 
 (II) A CORPORATION OR NONPROFIT CORPORATION, LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY, PARTNERSHIP OR LIMITED LIABILITY PARTNERSHIP, BUSINESS TRUST, JOINT VENTURE, OR ANY OTHER FORM OF BUSINESS ORGANIZATION THE CONSTITUENT PARTS OF WHICH SHARE A COMMON ECONOMIC INTEREST; OR 
 
 
 (III) AN INDIVIDUAL THAT CONTROLS, IS CONTROLLED BY, OR IS UNDER COMMON CONTROL WITH A PERSON DESCRIBED IN ITEM (I) OR (II) OF THIS PARAGRAPH. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;BUSINESS ENTITY&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE THE STATE OR A UNIT OF THE STATE, A LOCAL GOVERNMENT, OR A BUSINESS ENTITY OR OTHER PERSON DURING A PERIOD IN WHICH THE BUSINESS ENTITY OR PERSON IS ACTING SOLELY ON BEHALF OF AND AT THE DIRECTION OF THE STATE, A UNIT OF THE STATE, OR A LOCAL GOVERNMENT. 
 
 
 (F) (1) &quot;DATA BROKER&quot; MEANS ANY BUSINESS ENTITY THAT ENGAGES IN DATA BROKERING. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;DATA BROKER&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) A CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCY, AS DEFINED IN 15 U.S.C. &sect; 1681 A, A PERSON THAT FURNISHES INFORMATION TO A CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCY, AS PROVIDED IN 15 U.S.C. &sect; 1681 S-2, OR A USER OF A CONSUMER REPORT, AS DEFINED IN 15 U.S.C. &sect; 1681 A, TO THE EXTENT THAT THE CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCY, THE PERSON THAT FURNISHES INFORMATION TO A CONSUMER REPORTING AGENCY, OR THE USER OF A CONSUMER REPORT ENGAGES IN ACTIVITIES THAT ARE SUBJECT TO REGULATION UNDER THE FEDERAL FAIR CREDIT REPORTING ACT, 15 U.S.C. &sect; 1681; AND 
 
 
 (II) A FINANCIAL INSTITUTION, AN AFFILIATE, OR A NONAFFILIATED THIRD PARTY, AS THOSE TERMS ARE DEFINED IN 15 U.S.C. &sect; 6809, TO THE EXTENT THAT THE FINANCIAL INSTITUTION, AFFILIATE, OR NONAFFILIATED THIRD PARTY IS SUBJECT TO REGULATION UNDER TITLE V OF THE GRAMM-L EACH-B LILEY ACT, 15 U.S.C. &sect;&sect; 6801 THROUGH 6809 AND REGULATIONS ADOPTED UNDER TITLE V OF THE GRAMM-LEACH-B LILEY ACT. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;DATA BROKERING&quot; MEANS THE ACT OF COLLECTING, AGGREGATING, ANALYZING, BUYING, SELLING, AND SHARING BROKERED PERSONAL DATA. 
 
 
 (H) &quot;DE-IDENTIFIED DATA&quot; MEANS DATA THAT CANNOT REASONABLY BE USED TO INFER INFORMATION ABOUT OR OTHERWISE BE LINKED TO AN IDENTIFIED OR IDENTIFIABLE INDIVIDUAL OR A DEVICE LINKED TO THAT INDIVIDUAL, PROVIDED THAT THE PERSON IN CONTROL OF THE DATA: 
 
 
 (1) TAKES REASONABLE MEASURES TO ENSURE THAT THE DATA CANNOT BE ASSOCIATED WITH A NATURAL PERSON; 
 
 
 (2) PUBLICLY COMMITS TO MAINTAIN AND USE THE DATA ONLY IN A DE-IDENTIFIED FASHION AND NOT ATTEMPT TO RE-IDENTIFY THE DATA; AND 
 
 
 (3) CONTRACTUALLY OBLIGATES ANY RECIPIENTS OF THE INFORMATION TO COMPLY WITH ALL PROVISIONS OF THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (I) &quot;GROSS INCOME&quot; MEANS THE VALUE PROCEEDING OR ACCRUING BY REASON OF COLLECTING, AGGREGATING, ANALYZING, BUYING, SELLING, AND SHARING BROKERED PERSONAL DATA WITHOUT ANY DEDUCTION ON ACCOUNT OF THE COST OF TANGIBLE PROPERTY SOLD, THE COST OF MATERIALS USED, LABOR COSTS, INTEREST, DISCOUNT, DELIVERY COSTS, TAXES, OR ANY OTHER EXPENSE PAID OR ACCRUED AND WITHOUT ANY DEDUCTION ON ACCOUNT OF LOSSES. 
 
 
 (J) (1) &quot;PERSONAL DATA&quot; MEANS ANY INFORMATION THAT IS LINKED OR REASONABLY LINKED TO AN IDENTIFIED OR IDENTIFIABLE NATURAL PERSON. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;PERSONAL DATA&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE DE-IDENTIFIED DATA OR PUBLICLY AVAILABLE INFORMATION. 
 
 
 (K) &quot;RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&quot; MEANS A PERSON WHO RESIDES IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 2. DATA BROKER REGISTRY. 
 
 
 7.3-201. 
 
 
 (A) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, A DATA BROKER SHALL REGISTER WITH THE COMPTROLLER AS PROVIDED IN &sect; 7.3-202 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (B) A DATA BROKER IS NOT REQUIRED TO REGISTER WITH THE COMPTROLLER IF THE BROKERED PERSONAL DATA INVOLVES ANY OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 (1) PROVIDING PUBLICLY AVAILABLE INFORMATION THAT IS RELATED TO A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S BUSINESS OR PROFESSION; 
 
 
 (2) PROVIDING PUBLICLY AVAILABLE INFORMATION AS PART OF A SERVICE THAT PROVIDES ALERTS FOR HEALTH OR SAFETY PURPOSES; 
 
 
 (3) PROVIDING DIRECTORY ASSISTANCE OR DIRECTORY INFORMATION SERVICES AS OR ON BEHALF OF A TELECOMMUNICATIONS CARRIER; OR 
 
 
 (4) SELLING THE ASSETS OF A BUSINESS ENTITY OR A PART OF A BUSINESS ENTITY A SINGLE TIME OR ONLY OCCASIONALLY AS PART OF A TRANSFER OF CONTROL OVER THE ASSETS THAT IS NOT PART OF THE ORDINARY CONDUCT OF THE BUSINESS ENTITY OR A PART OF THE BUSINESS ENTITY. 
 
 
 7.3-202. 
 
 
 (A) (1) ON OR BEFORE JANUARY 31 EACH YEAR, FOLLOWING A YEAR IN WHICH A BUSINESS ENTITY MEETS THE DEFINITION OF A DATA BROKER, A DATA BROKER SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) SUBMIT ON A FORM AND IN A FORMAT THE COMPTROLLER SPECIFIES: 
 
 
 1. THE NAME OF THE DATA BROKER; 
 
 
 2. THE STREET ADDRESS AND TELEPHONE NUMBER OF THE DATA BROKER; AND 
 
 
 3. THE DATA BROKER&#39;S PRIMARY WEBSITE AND E-MAIL ADDRESS;

 (III) INCLUDE WITH THE REGISTRATION FORM A DECLARATION IN WHICH THE DATA BROKER: 
 
 
 1. STATES WHETHER A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S PRECISE GEOLOCATION INFORMATION IS A PART OF THE DATA BROKER&#39;S DATA BROKERING ACTIVITY; 
 
 
 2. STATES WHETHER RESIDENT INDIVIDUALS&#39; CONSUMER HEALTH DATA IS A PART OF THE DATA BROKER&#39;S DATA BROKERING ACTIVITY; 
 
 
 3. STATES WHETHER RESIDENT INDIVIDUALS MAY OPT OUT OF ALL OR A PORTION OF THE DATA BROKER&#39;S USE OF THEIR BROKERED PERSONAL DATA; 
 
 
 4. A. IDENTIFIES WHICH OF THE DATA BROKER&#39;S ACTIVITIES A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL MAY OPT OUT OF; AND 
 
 
 B. IDENTIFIES WHICH PORTION OF THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S BROKERED PERSONAL DATA THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL MAY OPT OUT FROM PROVIDING OR PERMITTING THE DATA BROKER TO USE; 
 
 
 5. DESCRIBES THE METHOD BY WHICH A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL MAY EXERCISE THE CHOICES DESCRIBED IN ITEMS 3 AND 4 OF THIS ITEM; AND 
 
 
 6. STATES WHETHER A RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL MAY AUTHORIZE AN INDIVIDUAL TO EXERCISE THE OPTIONS DESCRIBED IN ITEMS 3 AND 4 OF THIS ITEM ON THE RESIDENT INDIVIDUAL&#39;S BEHALF AND IF SO, THE APPROPRIATE PROCESS FOR THE AUTHORIZATION. 
 
 
 (2) (I) THE COMPTROLLER SHALL ESTABLISH THE FEE REQUIRED UNDER PARAGRAPH (1)(II) OF THIS SUBSECTION IN AN AMOUNT THAT IS SUFFICIENT TO PAY THE COSTS OF ADMINISTERING THE REGISTRATION PROGRAM ESTABLISHED UNDER THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (II) THE FEES COLLECTED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION SHALL BE DEPOSITED IN THE GENERAL FUND. 
 
 
 (B) (1) IF A DATA BROKER COMPLIES WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SECTION, THE COMPTROLLER SHALL APPROVE THE REGISTRATION.

 SUBTITLE 3. DATA BROKER GROSS INCOME TAX. 
 
 
 7.3-301. 
 
 
 A TAX IS IMPOSED ON THE ANNUAL GROSS INCOME OF A DATA BROKER. 
 
 
 7.3-302. 
 
 
 THE DATA BROKERING TAX RATE IS 6% OF THE GROSS INCOME OF A DATA BROKER APPORTIONED UNDER &sect; 7.3-303 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 7.3-303. 
 
 
 (A) THE APPORTIONABLE INCOME OF A DATA BROKER IS APPORTIONED TO THE STATE BY MULTIPLYING ITS APPORTIONABLE INCOME BY THE RECEIPTS FACTOR DETERMINED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE NUMERATOR OF THE RECEIPTS FACTOR IS THE TOTAL GROSS INCOME OF THE DATA BROKER ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE STATE DURING THE TAXABLE YEAR FROM ENGAGING IN DATA BROKERING DURING THE TAXABLE YEAR. 
 
 
 (2) THE DENOMINATOR OF THE RECEIPTS FACTOR IS THE TOTAL GROSS INCOME OF THE DATA BROKER FROM ENGAGING IN DATA BROKERING EVERYWHERE IN THE WORLD DURING THE TAXABLE YEAR. 
 
 
 (C) (1) (I) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION AND SUBJECT TO SUBPARAGRAPH (II) OF THIS PARAGRAPH, FOR PURPOSES OF COMPUTING THE RECEIPTS FACTOR UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, GROSS INCOME OF A DATA BROKER GENERATING SALES OR EXCHANGE FOR CONSIDERATION OF PERSONAL DATA IS ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE STATE IN THE RATIO, EXPRESSED AS A PERCENTAGE, THAT THE NUMBER OF MARYLAND ADDRESSES IN THE PERSONAL DATA BEARS TO ALL ADDRESSES IN THE PERSONAL DATA. 
 
 
 (II) ONLY PERSONAL DATA USED TO GENERATE THE GROSS INCOME OF THE BUSINESS TO BE ATTRIBUTED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION IS APPLICABLE IN CALCULATING THE RATIO DESCRIBED UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I) OF THIS PARAGRAPH. 
 
 
 (2) IF THE DATA BROKER IS UNABLE TO ATTRIBUTE GROSS INCOME UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION, GROSS INCOME OF THE DATA BROKER GENERATED FROM MAKING SALES, OR EXCHANGING FOR CONSIDERATION, OF PERSONAL DATA IS ATTRIBUTED TO THE STATE: 
 
 
 (I) IN THE RATIO, EXPRESSED AS A PERCENTAGE, OF THE POPULATION OF THE STATE TO ALL THE STATES OF THE UNITED STATES IN THE DATA BROKERS&#39; MARKET; OR 
 
 
 (II) BY ANY OTHER REASONABLE METHODS OF ATTRIBUTION ALLOWED BY THE COMPTROLLER. 
 
 
 (D) (1) (I) A DATA BROKER MAY CALCULATE THE RECEIPTS FACTOR FOR THE CURRENT TAXABLE YEAR BASED ON THE MOST RECENT CALENDAR YEAR FOR WHICH INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE FOR THE FULL CALENDAR YEAR. 
 
 
 (II) IF A DATA BROKER DOES NOT CALCULATE THE RECEIPTS FACTOR FOR THE CURRENT TAXABLE YEAR BASED ON PREVIOUS CALENDAR YEAR INFORMATION, THE DATA BROKER SHALL USE THE CURRENT YEAR INFORMATION TO CALCULATE THE RECEIPTS FACTOR FOR THE CURRENT TAXABLE YEAR. 
 
 
 (2) A DATA BROKER SHALL CORRECT THE REPORTING FOR THE CURRENT TAXABLE YEAR WHEN COMPLETE INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE TO CALCULATE THE RECEIPTS FACTOR FOR THAT YEAR, BUT NOT LATER THAN OCTOBER 31 OF THE FOLLOWING TAXABLE YEAR. 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 4. RETURNS. 
 
 
 7.3-401. 
 
 
 (A) EACH DATA BROKER THAT, IN A CALENDAR YEAR, HAS GROSS INCOME DERIVED FROM DATA BROKERING SHALL COMPLETE, UNDER OATH, AND FILE WITH THE COMPTROLLER A RETURN ON OR BEFORE APRIL 15 THE NEXT YEAR.
