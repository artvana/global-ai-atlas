VISION OF THE DIGITAL
MOROCCO 2030
STRATEGY

The enlightened vision of His Majesty the King, May God Assist Him, places
digital technology at the heart of national priorities. This Royal Vision,
ratified by the New Development Model, establishes digital technology
as areal driver of social and economic development for the Kingdom.
Following the example of the New Development Model, the Ministry
delegated to the Head of Government in charge of Digital Transition and
Administration Reform has adopted a participatory approach based
on listening, national consultation and co-construction to develop this
strategy. Thus civil society, the private sector and the public sector
have all been involved.

This strategy aims to:

« Make Morocco a Digital
Hub to accelerate the
Kingdom's social and

economic development »
