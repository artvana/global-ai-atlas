Maroc IA 2030 — Feuille de Route Nationale pour l'Intelligence Artificielle
(National Artificial Intelligence Roadmap — Morocco IA 2030)
Ministère de la Transition Numérique et de la Réforme de l'Administration, Royaume du Maroc
MOROCCO | 2026

Source: https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/01/274765/morocco-launches-maroc-ia-2030-roadmap-to-drive-ai-innovation

Non-binding national AI roadmap officially launched by Morocco's Ministry of Digital Transition and Administrative Reform in January 2026. The roadmap establishes Morocco's strategic vision for artificial intelligence development through 2030, building on the country's existing digital transformation agenda and the New Development Model (Nouveau Modèle de Développement) adopted in 2021.

Key Elements (based on publicly reported information):

1. Vision and Economic Targets
   Morocco's Maroc IA 2030 roadmap aims to:
   - Achieve a 100 billion dirham (approximately $10 billion USD) contribution of AI to GDP by 2030
   - Create 50,000 AI-related jobs across the economy
   - Establish Morocco as a regional AI hub for Africa and the Arab world
   - Develop an "AI Made in Morocco" brand to promote locally developed AI solutions

2. Digital Sovereignty
   A central theme of the roadmap is reducing Morocco's dependence on foreign AI technology and building genuine domestic AI capabilities. This includes:
   - Developing large language models (LLMs) trained on Moroccan and Arabic/Amazigh language data
   - Building national AI infrastructure including computing capacity
   - Supporting domestic AI companies and startups

3. Priority Sectors
   The roadmap identifies sectors for priority AI deployment:
   - Healthcare: AI-assisted diagnostics, telemedicine, disease surveillance
   - Agriculture: Precision farming, water management, crop optimization
   - Education: Personalized learning, administrative efficiency
   - Finance and banking: Fraud detection, credit scoring, regulatory compliance
   - Tourism: Personalized experiences, demand forecasting
   - Industry and manufacturing: Predictive maintenance, quality control
   - Public administration: E-government, smart city services

4. Governance and Ethics
   The roadmap includes provisions for responsible AI governance:
   - Establishing an AI governance framework with clear regulatory oversight
   - Developing ethical guidelines for AI development and deployment
   - Alignment with international AI ethics frameworks (OECD, UNESCO)
   - Data protection in compliance with Law 09-08 and planned updates

5. Human Capital
   Major investment in AI skills development:
   - Integration of AI into school and university curricula
   - Training 10,000 AI specialists and engineers
   - Supporting AI research centers at Moroccan universities
   - Attracting Moroccan diaspora talent

6. Infrastructure
   Infrastructure development to support AI:
   - Expansion of national data center capacity
   - Improved connectivity and cloud infrastructure
   - Open data initiatives and national data repositories

7. Ecosystem and Financing
   - Dedicated financing mechanisms for AI startups and SMEs
   - Public-private partnership models for AI development
   - Attracting foreign investment in Morocco's AI sector
   - Regional cooperation frameworks with African partners
