# Texas Senate Bill 1188 — Healthcare AI Disclosure Act

**Senate Bill No. 1188**

*Texas — June 20, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 1188 requires healthcare practitioners in Texas who use artificial intelligence for diagnostic purposes to disclose that use to patients, and prohibits offshoring of electronic medical records.

### AI Disclosure Requirement

Healthcare practitioners — including physicians, nurses, and other licensed clinical providers — who use AI in the course of a clinical encounter for diagnostic purposes must disclose that use to the patient verbally or in writing before or at the time of the encounter. Covered uses include:
- AI-assisted diagnosis
- AI-generated treatment recommendations
- AI interpretation of medical imaging (radiology, pathology, etc.)

The disclosure must be made in a manner that allows the patient to understand that AI tools are being used in their care.

### Electronic Medical Records Offshoring Prohibition

The bill includes a prohibition on offshoring electronic medical records — patient records may not be transmitted to or stored in servers outside the United States, subject to limited exceptions for approved cross-border care contexts.

### Enforcement

Enforcement is by the Texas Medical Board, which may impose disciplinary action against practitioners who fail to comply with the disclosure requirement. Sanctions may include license suspension or revocation in cases of repeated or willful violation.

### Effective Date

The act takes effect September 1, 2025.
