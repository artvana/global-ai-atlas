    Introduced by:
     REP. CANINO, 65th Dist.




 AN ACT PROHIBITING THE CREATION AND DISSEMINATION OF
 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-GENERATED PORNOGRAPHIC IMAGES
 OF A PERSON WITHOUT CONSENT.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to establish a crime prohibiting
2      the creation and dissemination of artificial intelligence-generated
3      pornographic images of a person without such person's consent.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To establish a criminal offense for the creation and dissemination of
       artificial intelligence-generated pornography.
