 HOUSE BILL NO. 251 
 
 IN THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF ALASKA 
 THIRTY-FOURTH LEGISLATURE - FIRST SESSION 
 
 
 
 A BILL 
 
 
 FOR AN ACT ENTITLED 
 
 
 &quot;An Act relating to the definition of &rsquo;person&rsquo; in Alaska Statutes; relating to criminal law definitions; and providing for an effective date.&quot; 
 
 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF ALASKA: 
 
 
 
 * Section 1. AS 01.10.060(a)(8) is amended to read: 
 (8) &quot;person&quot; includes a corporation, company, partnership, firm, association, organization, business trust, or society, as well as a natural person; or a human entity that has the moral right of self-determination; &quot;person&quot; does not include an environmental element, artificial intelligence, animal, or inanimate object; 
 
 
 * Sec. 2. AS 11.81.900(b)(48) is amended to read: 
 (48) &quot;person&quot; means a natural person or a human entity that has the moral right of self-determination and, when appropriate, an organization, government, or governmental instrumentality; &quot;person&quot; does not include an environmental element, artificial intelligence, animal, or inanimate object; 
 
 
 * Sec. 3. AS 11.81.900(b) is amended by adding a new paragraph to read: 
 (71) &quot;life&quot; means the property or quality that distinguishes a living human organism from a dead organism or inanimate matter and that is manifested in the function of metabolism, growth, or reproduction, a response to stimuli, or adaptation to the environment, each of which originates within the organism. 
 
 * Sec. 4. The uncodified law of the State of Alaska is amended by adding a new section to read: 
 APPLICABILITY. AS 11.81.900(b)(48), as amended by sec. 2 of this Act, and AS 11.81.900(b)(71), enacted by sec. 3 of this Act, apply to offenses committed on or after the effective date of this Act. 
 
 * Sec. 5. This Act takes effect July 1, 2026.
