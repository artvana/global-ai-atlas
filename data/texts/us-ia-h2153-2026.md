 A BILL FOR 
 An Act requiring community colleges, school districts, and institutions under the control of the state board of regents to adopt policies related to the use of artificial intelligence by students and employees. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF IOWA: 
 
 Section 1. Section 256.9, Code 2026, is amended by adding the following new subsection: 
 
 NEW SUBSECTION. 80. Publish on the department&rsquo;s internet site the policies related to artificial intelligence received from all of the following: 
 a. Community colleges pursuant to section 260C.14, subsection 27. 
 b. School districts pursuant to section 279.89. 
 Sec. 2. Section 260C.14, Code 2026, is amended by adding the following new subsection: 
 
 NEW SUBSECTION. 27. a. (1) On or before July 1, 2028, adopt a policy related to artificial intelligence that describes all of the following: 
 (a) When a student attending the community college may utilize artificial intelligence during classroom instructional time and during the completion of course or program work. 
 (b) When a faculty or staff member of the community college may utilize artificial intelligence during the completion of work-related duties. 
 (c) Prohibitions related to the use of artificial intelligence by students attending the community college, faculty members of the community college, and staff members of the community college. 
 (2) Publish the policy on the community college&rsquo;s internet site. 
 (3) Provide a copy of the policy to the director of the department of education. 
 b. For purposes of this subsection, &ldquo;artificial intelligence&rdquo; means any machine-based system that, for any explicit or implicit objective, infers from the inputs the system receives to generate outputs, including content, decisions, predictions, or recommendations, that can influence physical or virtual environments. 
 Sec. 3. Section 262.9, Code 2026, is amended by adding the following new subsection: 
 
 NEW SUBSECTION. 43. a. (1) Direct the institutions of higher education under its control to, on or before July 1, 2028, adopt a policy related to artificial intelligence that describes all of the following: 
 (a) When a student may utilize artificial intelligence during classroom instructional time and during the completion of course or program work. 
 (b) When a faculty or staff member may utilize artificial intelligence during the completion of work-related duties. 
 (c) Prohibitions related to the use of artificial intelligence by students, faculty members, and staff members. 
 (2) Publish the policy on the institution of higher education&rsquo;s internet site. 
 (3) Provide a copy of the policy to the board. 
 b. Publish the policies received pursuant to paragraph &ldquo;a&rdquo;, subparagraph (3), on the board&rsquo;s internet site. 
 c. For purposes of this subsection, &ldquo;artificial intelligence&rdquo; means any machine-based system that, for any explicit or implicit objective, infers from the inputs the system receives to generate outputs, including content, decisions, predictions, or recommendations, that can influence physical or virtual environments. 
 Sec. 4. NEW SECTION. 279.89 Artificial intelligence policies. 
 1. The board of directors of each school district shall do all of the following: 
 a. On or before July 1, 2028, adopt a policy related to artificial intelligence that describes all of the following: 
 (1) When a student enrolled in the school district may utilize artificial intelligence during classroom instructional time and during the completion of school work. 
 (2) When an employee of the school district may utilize artificial intelligence during the completion of work-related duties. 
 (3) Prohibitions related to the use of artificial intelligence by students enrolled in the school district and by employees of the school district. 
 b. Publish the policy on the school district&rsquo;s internet site. 
 c. Provide a copy of the policy to the director of the department of education. 
 2. For purposes of this section, &ldquo;artificial intelligence&rdquo; means any machine-based system that, for any explicit or implicit objective, infers from the inputs the system receives to generate outputs, including content, decisions, predictions, or recommendations, that can influence physical or virtual environments. 
 
 
 EXPLANATION 
 The inclusion of this explanation does not constitute agreement withthe explanation&rsquo;s substance by the members of the general assembly. 
 This bill requires community colleges, school districts, and institutions under the control of the state board of regents to adopt policies related to the use of artificial intelligence by students and employees. 
 The bill requires the board of directors of each community college and the board of directors of each school district to, on or before July 1, 2028, adopt a policy related to artificial intelligence that describes when a student may utilize artificial intelligence during classroom instructional time and during the completion of course or program work, when employees may utilize artificial intelligence during the completion of work-related duties, and prohibitions related to the use of artificial intelligence by students and employees. Community colleges and school districts are required to publish the policy on their respective internet sites and provide a copy of the policy to the director of the department of education (DE). The director of DE is required to publish the policies on DE&rsquo;s internet site. 
 The bill requires the state board of regents to direct the institutions of higher education under its control to, on or before July 1, 2028, adopt similar policies. The institutions are required to publish the policies on the institutions&rsquo; respective internet sites and provide copies of the policies to the state board of regents. In addition, the state board of regents is required to publish such policies on the state board of regents&rsquo; internet site. 
 The bill defines &ldquo;artificial intelligence&rdquo; to mean any machine-based system that, for any explicit or implicit objective, infers from the inputs the system receives to generate outputs, including content, decisions, predictions, or recommendations, that can influence physical or virtual environments.
