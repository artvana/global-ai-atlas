# Indiana SB 10 — Deceptive AI Political Content Prohibition Act

**Enacted March 11, 2024 | Indiana Code IC 3-14-16**

---

## Purpose

Indiana SB 10 prohibits the creation and distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated content depicting candidates for public office in the 30-day period preceding an election, and requires disclosures on AI-generated political advertising.

---

## Definitions

**"Artificial intelligence-generated content"** (IC 3-14-16-1): means any synthetic audio, image, or video generated using AI or machine learning systems that depicts a real individual but was not captured from a real-world event.

**"Candidate"** (IC 3-14-16-2): means an individual who has filed a declaration of candidacy or certificate of nomination for any Indiana state or local office, or who is a candidate for federal office.

**"Materially deceptive"**: means that a reasonable person who views or hears the content would believe it depicts something the real candidate actually said or did, when in fact the content was fabricated or substantially altered.

**"Political advertisement"**: means any communication made in support of or in opposition to any candidate, or for or against any political party, within 90 days before a primary, general, or special election.

---

## IC 3-14-16-3 — Prohibition

**(a) Prohibition.** A person may not knowingly create, publish, distribute, or cause to be published or distributed, through any means, a political advertisement that contains materially deceptive AI-generated content depicting a candidate or public official within **30 days** before a primary or general election, unless the political advertisement contains the required disclosure.

**(b) Required disclosure.** A political advertisement containing AI-generated content must include a clear and conspicuous statement: **"This advertisement contains artificial intelligence generated content."**

The disclosure:
- In video: must appear in text on screen for the duration of the AI-generated portion and be spoken aloud at the beginning of the advertisement;
- In audio: must be spoken aloud at the beginning and end of the advertisement;
- In print or digital images: must appear in text that is clearly readable, in a font at least 12 points in size or the equivalent.

**(c) Exceptions.** This section does not apply to:
(1) A news organization or journalist reporting on news of public concern
(2) Satire or parody identified as such
(3) Archival, documentary, historical, or educational content

---

## IC 3-14-16-4 — Civil Action

A candidate for public office who is depicted in AI-generated content in violation of this chapter may bring a civil action for injunctive relief, actual damages, and attorney's fees.

Courts hearing civil actions under this chapter in the 30-day period before an election must provide expedited proceedings. A court finding a likely violation and irreparable harm may grant a temporary restraining order within 24 hours.

---

## IC 3-14-16-5 — Election Law Violations

A violation of IC 3-14-16-3 constitutes a Level 6 felony if the violation was committed intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly for the purpose of influencing an election.

---

## Effective Date

This Act applies to political advertisements created or distributed on or after March 11, 2024.
