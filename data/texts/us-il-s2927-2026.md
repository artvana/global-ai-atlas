 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY 
 
 
 State of Illinois

 SB2927 
 
 Introduced 1/27/2026, by Sen. Mike Simmons 
 
 SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 
 
 20 ILCS 605/605-1119 new 
 Amends the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity Law of the Civil Administrative Code of Illinois. Provides that the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity shall conduct, in consultation with the Department of Labor, a comprehensive study to identify strategies to protect jobs that are at risk of loss due to artificial intelligence. Provides that the study shall include specified recommendations and analysis. Effective immediately. 
 
 A BILL FOR 
 
 
 
 AN ACT concerning State government. 
 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, represented in the General Assembly: 
 
 
 Section 5. The Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity Law of the Civil Administrative Code of Illinois is amended by adding Section 605-1119 as follows: 
 (20 ILCS 605/605-1119 new) 
 
 Sec. 605-1119. Study on strategies to protect jobs that are at risk of loss due to automation. 
 
 
 (a) The Department, in consultation with the Department of Labor, shall conduct a comprehensive study to identify strategies to protect jobs that are at risk of loss due to artificial intelligence, as defined in Section 2-101 of the Illinois Human Rights Act. 
 
 
 (b) The study shall examine the impact of artificial intelligence on the Illinois workforce and propose recommendations to protect workers from job displacement due to artificial intelligence. 
 
 
 (c) The study shall include, but shall not be limited to, the following: 
 
 
 (1) an analysis of the current state of artificial intelligence and its likely impact on the State&#39;s labor market conditions, including data on past and projected job displacement; 
 
 
 (2) an analysis of how the labor market impact might reshape the State&#39;s key industries, occupations, and foundational skill sets; 
 
 
 (3) proposed initiatives to stimulate economic growth, create jobs, and enhance talent development in the face of artificial intelligence-related changes; 
 
 
 (4) recommendations for legal regulations or policy changes needed to ensure the responsible and ethical use of artificial intelligence in the State while protecting the rights of Illinois citizens; 
 
 
 (5) recommendations on ways to encourage artificial intelligence innovation and entrepreneurship and strategies to support workers displaced by artificial intelligence; 
 
 
 (6) proposed policy changes related to workforce development, educational systems, and research opportunities for colleges and universities in the State; 
 
 
 (7) recommendations of ways to promote transparency and accountability for the use of artificial intelligence technologies by measuring, monitoring, and reporting outcomes and progress; and 
 
 
 (8) data and recommendations regarding job displacement of workers from disproportionately impacted communities. 
 
 
 (d) The Department shall solicit input from labor leaders, technology experts, industry leaders, and gig workers. 
 
 
 (e) The Department shall submit a final report to the General Assembly and the Governor no later than December 31, 2026. The report shall include findings, recommendations, and potential statutory language to update Illinois law. The Department shall make the report available to the public on its website. 
 
 
 (f) This Section is repealed on December 31, 2027. 
 
 Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect upon becoming law.
