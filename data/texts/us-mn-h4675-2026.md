 HF 4675 
 Introduction - 94th Legislature (2025 - 2026) 
 Posted on 03/25/2026 
 
 
 A bill for an act relating to human services; establishing a Human Services Systems Steering Committee; requiring reports; appropriating money; proposing coding for new law in Minnesota Statutes, chapter 256. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF MINNESOTA: 
 
 Section 1. 
 [256.0141] HUMAN SERVICES SYSTEMS STEERING COMMITTEE. 
 
 
 
 Subdivision 1. Establishment. The Human Services Systems Steering Committee is established to provide recommendations to the commissioners of human services, information technology services, and children, youth, and families on the development, administration, and business operations of human services information technology systems. For the purposes of this section, &quot;human services systems&quot; means any information technology system used by counties or the commissioner of human services or children, youth, and families. 
 
 
 Subd. 2. Membership; costs. (a) The steering committee must be composed of the following members: 
 
 
 (1) two members appointed by the commissioner of human services; 
 
 
 (2) two members appointed by the commissioner of children, youth, and families; 
 
 
 (3) six members appointed jointly by the Association of Minnesota Counties, the Minnesota Inter-County Association, and the Minnesota Association of County Social Service Administrators; and 
 
 
 (4) two nonvoting members appointed by the commissioner of information technology services. 
 
 
 (b) One member appointed under paragraph (a), clause (3), and one member appointed under paragraph (a), clause (4), must serve as co-chairpersons for the steering committee. 
 
 
 (c) Steering committee costs must be paid from the budgets of the Department of Human Services, the Department of Information Technology Services, and the Department of Children, Youth, and Families. 
 
 
 Subd. 3. Duties. (a) The steering committee must provide recommendations on the administration and business operations of current human services systems and the development of any new human services systems. 
 
 
 (b) For each human services system, the steering committee must make recommendations on setting system goals and priorities, allocating system resources, making major system decisions, and tracking total system funding and expenditures from all sources. 
 
 
 Subd. 4. Meetings. (a) The steering committee must meet at least quarterly. 
 
 
 (b) As part of every steering committee meeting, the steering committee must provide the opportunity for oral and written public testimony and comments on steering committee recommendations. 
 
 
 (c) All votes of the steering committee must be recorded, with each member&#39;s vote identified. 
 
 
 Subd. 5. Implementation of changes; new systems. (a) The commissioners of human services and children, youth, and families must not implement any new major changes to current human services systems, or implement a new human services system, prior to receiving recommendations from the steering committee and consulting with counties on the changes or development. 
 
 
 (b) The commissioners of human services and children, youth, and families must implement changes to human services systems recommended and passed by at least seven members of the steering committee. 
 
 
 Subd. 6. Report. (a) Beginning January 30, 2027, and each January 30 thereafter, the steering committee must report to the chairs and ranking minority members of the legislative committees with jurisdiction over human services systems. The report must include: 
 
 
 (1) for each human services system, system funding and expenditures, including amounts received in the previous calendar year by funding source and expenditures made in the previous calendar year by funding source; 
 
 
 (2) recommendations made by the steering committee under subdivision 3; and 
 
 
 (3) a list of projects needed to improve human services systems. 
 
 
 (b) The legislative committees with jurisdiction over human services systems must hold a public hearing on the report during the regular legislative session in the year in which the report was submitted. 
 
 Sec. 2. 
 APPROPRIATION; COUNTY INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS UPDATES. 
 
 
 
 Subdivision 1. Information technology changes; system focused. (a) $....... in fiscal year 2027 is appropriated from the general fund to the commissioner of information technology systems to update county information technology systems. This appropriation must be used, in collaboration with counties, to fund the following projects: 
 
 
 (1) expand Data Depot to all interested Minnesota counties and Tribal Nations; 
 
 
 (2) expand BlueZone Scripting Collaborative to all interested Minnesota counties and Tribal Nations; 
 
 
 (3) expand piloted artificial intelligence tools for human services to all interested Minnesota counties and Tribal Nations; 
 
 
 (4) develop new user interfaces to replace character-based green screens for the MAXIS and PRISM computer systems through the integration layer; 
 
 
 (5) complete the renewal self-service portal in the Minnesota Eligibility Technology System (METS); 
 
 
 (6) copy all state human services systems policies and procedures into the data warehouse and keep the policies and procedures regularly updated; 
 
 
 (7) develop the ability for METS to note when a recipient has a second pregnancy; and 
 
 
 (8) determine, in collaboration with Minnesota Management and Budget, the dollar amount needed to fund the integration layer tool and the amount needed to allow full implementation for both the MAXIS and PRISM computer systems. 
 
 
 (b) Of the amount appropriated under paragraph (a), $....... is for fully funding the work verification vendor system for child support, health care, and public assistance programs. 
 
 
 (c) This is a onetime appropriation. 
 
 
 Subd. 2. Information technology changes; policy focused. (a) $....... in fiscal year 2027 is appropriated from the general fund to the commissioner of information technology systems to update county information technology systems to conform with legislative changes. This appropriation must be used, in collaboration with counties, to fund the following projects: 
 
 
 (1) develop an automated process in the MAXIS computer system to confirm the eligibility for health care program enrollees; 
 
 
 (2) eliminate the duplicative data entry by county eligibility workers, including integrating MNBenefits with the MAXIS computer system and developing a solution to allow recipients to provide updated information through electronic means; 
 
 
 (3) update the MAXIS computer system to require a county eligibility worker to update the shelter and utility panels in MAXIS every time the address panel is updated for Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) applications; 
 
 
 (4) update the MAXIS computer system to allow the state to move to a simplified reporting method for SNAP so that all recipients will report changes on a six-month timeline; 
 
 
 (5) update the MAXIS computer system with correct coding for time-limited recipients receiving SNAP benefits and integrate the coding with other panels in MAXIS; 
 
 
 (6) update the MAXIS computer system eligibility results to reflect the most recent noncitizen eligibility policy for health care programs and SNAP; 
 
 
 (7) update the MAXIS computer system to eliminate the need for county eligibility workers to enter and approve general assistance and SNAP applications separately within the same system, including the ability for MAXIS to translate information between general assistance and SNAP; 
 
 
 (8) initiate a review and modernization of client notices generated by the MAXIS computer system and METS to ensure the notices are clear, accurate, and compliant with all current federal and state requirements and enhance system functionality so the notices automatically populate correct and complete information based on case actions; and 
 
 
 (9) update the MAXIS computer system to allow automatic case noting. 
 
 
 (b) This is a onetime appropriation.
