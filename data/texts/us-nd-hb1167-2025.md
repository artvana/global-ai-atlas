# North Dakota House Bill 1167 — Artificial Intelligence in Political Communications Disclosure Act

**North Dakota 69th Legislative Assembly, 2025 Regular Session**
**Enacted:** April 11, 2025 | **Effective:** August 1, 2025

**Citation**
*North Dakota — April 11, 2025*

---

## Overview

North Dakota HB 1167 requires political communications and advertisements that are created wholly or in part by artificial intelligence to carry a prominent disclaimer disclosing AI involvement. The law applies to paid political ads and mass communications distributed in North Dakota. Violations constitute a Class B misdemeanor. The law is part of a broader national trend of state-level AI political disclosure mandates, consistent with similar laws enacted in Wisconsin (2023 Act 123), California (AB 2839), and Minnesota (HF 4114).

---

## Key Provisions

### Disclosure Requirement
- Any political communication or advertisement created wholly or in part by artificial intelligence must include a clear and prominent disclaimer stating: **"This content generated by artificial intelligence."**
- The disclaimer must be displayed in a manner that is readily visible or audible to the audience depending on the medium (print, broadcast, digital, audio).

### Scope of Application
- Applies to paid political advertisements targeting North Dakota voters.
- Applies to mass communications sent to a large number of recipients that contain AI-generated content and are intended to influence a political opinion, candidate, or ballot measure.
- Covers audio, video, images, and text-based communications that incorporate AI-generated material.

### Exemptions
- Tools used solely for **spell-check, autocorrect, grammar correction**, or minor editing functions that do not generate new content or impersonate human speech or likeness are exempt.
- **Satire and parody** clearly and conspicuously labeled as such are exempt from the disclaimer requirement.
- Factual news reporting and journalism on political matters is not subject to the Act.

### Criminal Penalty
- Failure to include the required disclaimer is a **Class B misdemeanor** under North Dakota law.
- Class B misdemeanor carries a maximum penalty of 30 days imprisonment and/or a $1,500 fine under NDCC § 12.1-32-01.

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement is through North Dakota's existing criminal misdemeanor framework. The County State's Attorney in the county where the violation occurred has jurisdiction to prosecute violations.
