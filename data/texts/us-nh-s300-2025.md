 AMENDED ANALYSIS 
 This bill makes it a class B felony and a tier II registrable offense for a person to knowingly create, produce, manufacture, or direct an intimate visual representation of a child for prurient purposes. 
 
 
 STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE 
 
 In the Year of Our Lord Two Thousand Twenty Five 
 
 AN ACT criminalizing the creation of child intimate visual representations. 
 
 
 
 
 Be it Enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court convened: 
 
 
 259:1 New Section; Child Sexual Abuse Images; Creation of Child Intimate Visual Representations. Amend RSA 649-A by inserting after section 3-b the following new section: 
 
 649-A:3-c Creation of Child Intimate Visual Representations. 
 I. No person shall knowingly create, produce, manufacture, or direct an intimate visual representation of a child for prurient purposes when that child is identifiable from the intimate visual representation itself or information displayed in connection with the intimate visual representation. 
 II. For purposes of this section, &quot;intimate visual representation&quot; means any visual depiction, including any photograph, film, video, digital image, synthetic image, picture, or computer or computer-generated image or picture, whether made or produced by electronic, mechanical, or other means, in which a child&rsquo;s intimate parts are depicted. 
 III. For purposes of this section, &ldquo;intimate parts&rdquo; means the fully unclothed, partially unclothed, or transparently clothed genitals, pubic area, or anus, or, if the person is female, a partially or fully exposed nipple, including exposure through transparent clothing. 
 IV. For purposes of this section, &ldquo;synthetic image&rdquo; means an image that has been altered or created depicting an individual&rsquo;s image in a realistic but false representation of the individual. 
 V. For purposes of this section, &ldquo;prurient purposes&rdquo; means for the purpose of sexual gratification or sexual arousal from viewing the intimate visual representation prohibited by this section. 
 VI. An offense under this section shall be a class B felony.
