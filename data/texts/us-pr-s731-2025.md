                                              ACT

To create the "Public Bidding Auctions with Artificial Intelligence and
     Governmental Transparency Act of Puerto Rico"; for the purposes of centralizing
     all purchases, acquisitions and public auctions under the General Services
     Administration of the Government of Puerto Rico (ASG), to provide for the
     mandatory use of the JEDI Joint E-procurement Digital Intelligence (J.E.D.I.) platform
     in all agencies, departments, public corporations and other instrumentalities
     of the Government of Puerto Rico every Governmental Entity as defined
     by Act 73-2019, as amended, known as the "General Services Administration
     for the Centralization of the Purchases of the Government of Puerto Rico Act of 2019";
     to establish the incorporation of artificial intelligence systems and large language
     models (LLMs) in the procurement processes; to create a Public Registry of Algorithms;
     to set forth the duties of the ASG, the Office of Innovation and Technology Services of the
     Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) and of the agencies; to establish
     sanctions for non-compliance; and to repeal conflicting legal provisions, for
     other related purposes.

                               STATEMENT OF MOTIVES

       Public contracting constitutes one of the most sensitive areas of governmental
administration, since it involves the use of public funds belonging to the people of
Puerto Rico. The lack of transparency, the dispersion of processes, and the
                                                2


    absence of uniformity have resulted in a fragmented system, vulnerable to
    corruption, with duplication of efforts and limitations in fiscal supervision.

           Act 73-2019, known as the "General Services Administration for the Centralization
    of the Purchases of the Government of Puerto Rico Act of 2019", laid the foundation
    for ASG to assume a leading role in the organization of public acquisitions
    and biddings. Notwithstanding, reports and statistics have demonstrated that
    many public agencies and corporations continued to operate outside such centralization,
    with multiple parallel processes that detract from efficiency and
    foster discretion.

           At present, Puerto Rico has the JEDI J.E.D.I. (Joint E-
    procurement Digital Intelligence) platform, created to standardize and digitize public
    auctions. This tool has proven to be useful, but its implementation has been partial.
    In parallel, the evolution of artificial intelligence and large language models
    has transformed best practices in public contracting at the
    international level, allowing automatic detection of anomalies, validation of
    documents, demand forecasts, and real-time audits.

           It is the public policy of the Government of Puerto Rico to advance toward the
    complete centralization of all acquisitions in ASG, ensuring uniform,
    efficient and transparent processes, supported by state-of-the-art technological tools.
    The present act consolidates this framework and endows the ASG with express
    and exclusive powers over the entirety of auctions and purchases.

13         Article 6. – Implementation of artificial intelligence

14         The ASG, in coordination with the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service

15   (PRITS), shall implement modules of artificial intelligence and large language

16   models on the JEDI platform for the following:

17         a) Automatic validation of documents submitted in auctions.

18         b) Detection and alert of possible irregularities, anomalies and fraud.

19         c) Generation of forecasts of prices and demand for goods and services.

20         d) Issuance of summaries of files and audits in real time.

21         e) Creation of virtual assistants to guide bidders and providers.

22         f) Evaluation of the performance of bidders.

 1           Article 7. – Public Registry of Algorithms

 5           Article 8. – Regulations and training

 6           The ASG, in       coordination     with       PRITS, shall adopt regulations for the

 9           Article 9. – Sanctions

10           Non-compliance with the provisions of this act shall entail the nullity of the

11   contracts awarded outside JEDI J.E.D.I., the administrative sanctions against the

12   responsible entity in accordance with Act 73-2019, as amended, known as the

13   "General Services Administration for the Centralization of the Purchases of the

14   Government of Puerto Rico Act of 2019" and the withholding of operational funds until

15   the integration provided is complied with.

16           Article 10. – Reports

17           The ASG shall render an annual report to the Legislative Assembly on the degree of

18   compliance of the agencies and           corporations, the savings generated, the

19   irregularities detected, and the improvements implemented through the use of artificial

20   intelligence.
