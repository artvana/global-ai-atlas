    104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY

    State of Illinois

    2025 and 2026

    SB2780

   Introduced 1/13/2026, by Sen. Lakesia Collins

    SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED:

   Appropriates the amount of $250,000 from the General Revenue Fund to the Department of Central Management Services for the purpose of making a grant to Amp AI, doing business as Nozma, to conduct a procurement modernization and artificial intelligence pilot program. Effective July 1, 2026.

   SB 2780

   AN ACT concerning appropriations.

     Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, represented in the General Assembly:

   Section 5. The sum of $250,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is appropriated from the General Revenue Fund to the Department of Central Management Services for the purpose of making a grant to Amp AI, doing business as Nozma, to conduct a procurement modernization and artificial intelligence pilot program.

   Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect July 1, 2026.
