OMB Memorandum M-24-18: Advancing the Responsible Acquisition of Artificial Intelligence in Government

Date: September 24, 2024
Issuing Authority: Office of Management and Budget (OMB)
Status: RESCINDED by OMB M-25-22 (April 2025)

[Full text unavailable. The document was removed from whitehouse.gov following the change of administration in January 2025 and has not been preserved on any accessible official government server. The document was also not published in the Federal Register.]

SUMMARY (from database record):

OMB Memorandum M-24-18 directed federal agencies on responsible AI acquisition principles under the Biden administration, establishing guidance on AI vendor due diligence, contractual safeguards for government AI purchases, and responsible AI principles that agencies must incorporate into procurement. The memo operationalized the AI acquisition aspects of EO 14110 for the federal contracting process. It was rescinded and superseded by OMB M-25-22 in April 2025.

KEY PROVISIONS (as described in secondary sources):
- Directed federal agencies to apply responsible AI acquisition principles in all significant AI procurements
- Required AI vendor due diligence for significant AI acquisitions, covering safety, reliability, and civil rights considerations
- Mandated contractual safeguards in federal AI contracts
- Required agencies to designate AI acquisition points of contact
- Directed agencies to assess AI risks before procurement
- Implemented equity and civil rights considerations in AI procurement processes
- Required agencies to incorporate responsible AI principles into existing acquisition frameworks

The memo was issued pursuant to Executive Order 14110 (Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence, October 2023) and complemented OMB M-24-10 (Advancing Governance, Innovation, and Risk Management for Agency Use of Artificial Intelligence).
