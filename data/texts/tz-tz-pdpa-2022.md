THE PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION ACT
An Act to provide for principles of protection of personal data so as to
establish minimum requirements for the collection and processing of
personal data; to provide for establishment of Personal Data Protection
Commission; to provide for improvement of protection of personal
data processed by public and private bodies; and to provide for matters
connected therewith.
