# Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (Tanzania)
**Act No. 11 of 2022**
*Tanzania — November 1, 2022 (effective May 1, 2023)*

---

## Overview

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act 2022 is the country's first comprehensive personal data protection law, establishing a modern data protection framework aligned with global standards. The Act establishes the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) as the independent supervisory authority responsible for overseeing and enforcing data protection requirements. The Act applies to any organization processing personal data of Tanzania residents, whether the processing takes place in Tanzania or elsewhere. The law draws on international best practices including the GDPR and African Union Data Policy Framework, and incorporates contemporary concepts such as data portability and data protection officers. Note: Commission establishment and implementing regulations were in progress as of 2026; some provisions may have staggered implementation.

---

## Key Provisions

### Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
- Independent regulatory authority established under the Act
- Registers and oversees data controllers and processors
- Investigates complaints, conducts audits, and takes enforcement action
- Issues guidance, codes of practice, and binding orders
- Cooperates with regional and international data protection authorities
- PDPC establishment and full operationalization subject to implementing regulations

### Data Protection Principles
The Act enshrines nine core principles:
- Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability of the data controller
- Cross-border transfer with safeguards
- Rights-based processing

### Data Subject Rights
- Right of access to personal data held by controllers
- Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") where grounds are met
- Right to restriction of processing in certain circumstances
- Right to object to processing for direct marketing or legitimate interests
- Right to data portability where technically feasible
- Right to lodge complaints with the PDPC

### Sensitive Personal Data
- Special categories requiring heightened protection: health and medical data, genetic data, biometric data, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, criminal record
- Processing requires explicit consent or specific statutory justification

### Data Protection Officer (DPO) Requirements
- Mandatory DPO appointment for public authorities and organizations conducting large-scale or high-risk processing
- DPO must have expert knowledge of data protection law
- DPO acts as point of contact for data subjects and PDPC

### Breach Notification
- Data controllers must notify the PDPC within a specified period of a personal data breach likely to cause harm
- Notification to affected data subjects required where there is a high risk to their rights and freedoms

### Cross-Border Data Transfers
- Transfers outside Tanzania restricted unless the recipient country provides adequate protection or appropriate safeguards are in place (standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, or individual consent)
- PDPC may issue adequacy determinations for countries or regions

### Impact Assessments
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) required for high-risk processing activities
- Processing involving new technologies, large-scale profiling, or sensitive data categories triggers DPIA obligation

## Enforcement & Penalties

- PDPC may issue compliance orders, enforcement notices, and formal warnings
- Administrative fines for violations: up to TZS 50 million (approximately $19,000 USD)
- Criminal penalties: up to 5 years imprisonment for serious violations
- Civil liability: data subjects may seek compensation through Tanzanian courts
- Processing suspension may be ordered as an interim or final remedy
- Note: Penalty amounts subject to implementing regulations which were being finalized as of 2026
