 HOUSE BILL 1085 
 
 By: Delegate Miller 
 
 Introduced and read first time: February 10, 2026 
 Assigned to: Government, Labor, and Elections 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Maryland Office of the Inspector General - Establishment 
 
 
 (Maryland Government Efficiency and Accountability Act of 2026) 
 
 FOR the purpose of establishing the Maryland Office of the Inspector General; establishing the State Transparency Commission to appoint and provide oversight for the Inspector General of the State; providing for the qualifications, salary, and term of office of the Inspector General; providing for the employment of certain staff by the Inspector General; establishing provisions related to the powers and duties of the Inspector General, the Chief Deputy Inspector General, and the Office; requiring certain units to cooperate fully with the Inspector General in certain investigations, audits, and reviews; protecting certain records prepared or obtained by the Inspector General from disclosure under the Public Information Act; extending certain whistleblower protections to certain State employees who make a complaint or provide information to the Inspector General or staff of the Office; authorizing the Governor to transfer certain positions and funds to the Office; providing certain protections for employees who are transferred to the Office; and generally relating to the Maryland Office of the Inspector General. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - State Government 
 Section 7.5-101 through 7.5-112 to be under the new title &quot;Title 7.5. Inspector General&quot; 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 BY repealing and reenacting, with amendments, 
 Article - State Personnel and Pensions 
 Section 5-305, 5-306, and 5-313 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2024 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 
 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - State Government 
 
 
 TITLE 7.5. INSPECTOR GENERAL. 
 
 
 7.5-101. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS TITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW&quot; MEANS A HOLISTIC REVIEW OF A UNIT&#39;S ORGANIZATION AND OPERATIONS TO IDENTIFY INEFFICIENCIES AND AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT. 
 
 
 (C) &quot;INSPECTOR GENERAL&quot; MEANS THE INSPECTOR GENERAL OF THE STATE. 
 
 
 (D) &quot;KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS&quot; MEANS QUANTIFIABLE METRICS DESIGNED TO MEASURE HOW EFFECTIVELY A UNIT IS ACHIEVING OBJECTIVES RELATED TO EFFICIENCY AND PRODUCTIVITY. 
 
 
 (E) &quot;OFFICE&quot; MEANS THE MARYLAND OFFICE OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL. 
 
 
 (F) &quot;TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION&quot; MEANS THE STATE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;UNIT&quot; MEANS AN AGENCY OR A UNIT OF THE EXECUTIVE BRANCH OF STATE GOVERNMENT. 
 
 
 7.5-102. 
 
 
 (A) THERE IS A STATE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION. 
 
 
 (B) THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION IS COMPOSED OF THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS: 
 
 
 (1) THE GOVERNOR; 
 
 
 (2) THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE; AND 
 
 
 (3) THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE. 
 
 
 (C) ALL MEMBERS OF THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION ARE REQUIRED FOR THE TRANSACTION OF BUSINESS BY THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION. 
 
 
 (D) (1) THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION SHALL APPOINT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL WITHOUT REGARD TO POLITICAL AFFILIATION ON THE AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF NOT LESS THAN TWO MEMBERS. 
 
 
 (2) THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION MAY REMOVE THE INSPECTOR GENERAL ON THE AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF NOT LESS THAN TWO MEMBERS FOR: 
 
 
 (I) MISCONDUCT IN OFFICE; 
 
 
 (II) PERSISTENT FAILURE TO PERFORM THE DUTIES OF THE OFFICE; OR 
 
 
 (III) CONDUCT PREJUDICIAL TO THE PROPER ADMINISTRATION OF JUSTICE. 
 
 
 (E) (1) (I) IN THE EVENT THAT THE POSITION OF INSPECTOR GENERAL REMAINS VACANT FOR A PERIOD IN EXCESS OF 180 DAYS, THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION SHALL SUBMIT A REPORT, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THIS ARTICLE, TO THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY ON THE 181 ST DAY AND EVERY 60 TH DAY THEREAFTER UNTIL THE VACANCY IS FILLED. 
 
 
 (II) FOR THE PURPOSE OF CALCULATING TIME UNDER THIS SUBSECTION, THE POSITION OF INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL BE DEEMED VACANT FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 1. THE RESIGNATION OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL; 
 
 
 2. THE REMOVAL OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL UNDER SUBSECTION (D)(2) OF THIS SECTION; OR 
 
 
 3. THE END OF THE TERM FOR THE INSPECTOR GENERAL, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE INSPECTOR GENERAL CONTINUES TO SERVE UNDER &sect; 7.5-103(B)(3) OF THIS TITLE. 
 
 
 (2) THE REPORT SHALL INCLUDE A REVIEW OF: 
 
 
 (I) THE ONGOING EFFORTS TO APPOINT A NEW INSPECTOR GENERAL; AND 
 
 
 (II) THE ACTIVITIES OF THE OFFICE DURING THE VACANCY. 
 
 
 7.5-103. 
 
 
 (A) THERE IS AN INSPECTOR GENERAL OF THE STATE. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL BE APPOINTED BY THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION. 
 
 
 (2) THE TERM OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS 6 YEARS, BEGINNING JULY 1 AFTER THE APPOINTMENT OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL. 
 
 
 (3) AT THE END OF A TERM, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL CONTINUE TO SERVE UNTIL A SUCCESSOR IS APPOINTED. 
 
 
 (C) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MUST BE PROFESSIONALLY QUALIFIED THROUGH EXPERIENCE OR EDUCATION IN AT LEAST ONE OF THE FOLLOWING AREAS: 
 
 
 (I) AUDITING; 
 
 
 (II) FINANCIAL ANALYSIS; 
 
 
 (III) BUSINESS MANAGEMENT; 
 
 
 (IV) PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION; 
 
 
 (V) CRIMINAL JUSTICE; OR 
 
 
 (VI) LAW. 
 
 
 (2) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MUST BE CERTIFIED BY THE ASSOCIATION OF INSPECTORS GENERAL AS A CERTIFIED INSPECTOR GENERAL OR SHALL OBTAIN CERTIFICATION WITHIN 6 MONTHS AFTER APPOINTMENT. 
 
 
 (D) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS ENTITLED TO: 
 
 
 (1) THE SALARY PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET; AND 
 
 
 (2) REIMBURSEMENT FOR TRAVEL AND OTHER EXPENSES THAT ARE CONNECTED WITH THE DUTIES OF THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 7.5-104. 
 
 
 (A) THERE IS A MARYLAND OFFICE OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL. 
 
 
 (B) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL SUPERVISE AND DIRECT THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 (C) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY ESTABLISH OFFICE SPACE THAT THE STATE SHALL MAINTAIN AND EQUIP. 
 
 
 (2) FUNDING FOR THE OFFICE SHALL BE AS PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET. 
 
 
 7.5-105. 
 
 
 (A) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY EMPLOY A STAFF IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE STATE BUDGET. 
 
 
 (2) STAFF EMPLOYED UNDER THIS SECTION: 
 
 
 (I) SHALL: 
 
 
 1. PERFORM THE DUTIES THAT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL ASSIGNS; AND 
 
 
 2. CARRY IDENTIFICATION CARDS THAT IDENTIFY THE STAFF AS EMPLOYEES OF THE OFFICE; 
 
 
 (II) SERVE AT THE PLEASURE OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL; AND 
 
 
 (III) ARE ENTITLED TO: 
 
 
 1. COMPENSATION AS PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET; AND 
 
 
 2. REIMBURSEMENT FOR EXPENSES UNDER THE STANDARD STATE TRAVEL REGULATIONS, AS PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET. 
 
 
 (B) FROM AMONG THE EMPLOYEES ON THE STAFF, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY DESIGNATE ONE CHIEF DEPUTY INSPECTOR GENERAL AND ONE OR MORE DEPUTY INSPECTORS GENERAL. 
 
 
 7.5-106. 
 
 
 (A) THE CHIEF DEPUTY INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL SERVE AS ACTING INSPECTOR GENERAL IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS TEMPORARILY UNABLE OR UNAVAILABLE TO CARRY OUT THE DUTIES OF THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 (B) IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL GIVES THE CHIEF DEPUTY INSPECTOR GENERAL WRITTEN NOTICE OF A TEMPORARY INABILITY OR UNAVAILABILITY: 
 
 
 (1) THE ACTING INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL SERVE ON AND AFTER THE DATE THAT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SETS IN THE NOTICE AND UNTIL THE INSPECTOR GENERAL GIVES THE ACTING INSPECTOR GENERAL WRITTEN NOTICE THAT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS ABLE TO CARRY OUT THE DUTIES OF THE OFFICE; AND 
 
 
 (2) THE POSITION OF INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY NOT BE DEEMED VACANT. 
 
 
 7.5-107. 
 
 
 (A) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) RECEIVE COMPLAINTS OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION IN THE UNITS; AND 
 
 
 (II) ESTABLISH A TOLL-FREE HOTLINE NUMBER AND WEBSITE THROUGH WHICH ANONYMOUS COMPLAINTS MAY BE FILED. 
 
 
 (2) IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL RECEIVES A COMPLAINT UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL DETERMINE WHETHER THE COMPLAINT SHOULD BE INVESTIGATED. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL IMPLEMENT TECHNOLOGY POWERED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO AUTOMATICALLY FLAG POTENTIAL INSTANCES OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION WITHIN THE UNITS IN REAL TIME, INCLUDING UNUSUAL TRANSACTIONS, DUPLICATE BILLING ERRORS, AND PROCUREMENT ANOMALIES. 
 
 
 (2) IF THE TECHNOLOGY IMPLEMENTED UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION FLAGS A POTENTIAL INSTANCE OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL DETERMINE WHETHER THE INSTANCE SHOULD BE INVESTIGATED. 
 
 
 (C) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL ESTABLISH: 
 
 
 (I) KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS FOR EACH UNIT; AND 
 
 
 (II) ANNUAL GOALS FOR EACH UNIT TO MEET BASED ON THE KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS. 
 
 
 (2) IF A UNIT FAILS TO MEET THE GOALS ESTABLISHED UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION FOR 2 CONSECUTIVE YEARS, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) CONDUCT AN EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW OF THE UNIT; AND 
 
 
 (II) MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS TO THE TRANSPARENCY COMMISSION REGARDING MEASURES THAT COULD BE TAKEN TO ADDRESS THE EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW&#39;S FINDINGS. 
 
 
 (D) IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL DISCOVERS EVIDENCE OF CRIMINAL ACTIVITY IN THE COURSE OF CARRYING OUT THE DUTIES UNDER THIS SECTION, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL NOTIFY THE APPROPRIATE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY. 
 
 
 7.5-108. 
 
 
 (A) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL: 
 
 
 (1) MAY INVESTIGATE: 
 
 
 (I) COMPLAINTS OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION IN THE UNITS; AND 
 
 
 (II) POTENTIAL INSTANCES OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION FLAGGED BY TECHNOLOGY IMPLEMENTED BY THE INSPECTOR GENERAL; 
 
 
 (2) MAY CONDUCT AUDITS AND EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEWS OF THE UNITS; 
 
 
 (3) MAY PROCURE AND IMPLEMENT TECHNOLOGY POWERED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO FLAG POTENTIAL INSTANCES OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION WITHIN THE UNITS IN REAL TIME; 
 
 
 (4) SHALL COOPERATE WITH INVESTIGATIONS BY THE UNITS OR OTHER STATE OR FEDERAL AGENCIES AND, IF A PRELIMINARY INVESTIGATION OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION ESTABLISHES A SUFFICIENT BASIS TO WARRANT REFERRAL, SHALL REFER THE MATTER TO THE APPROPRIATE STATE OR FEDERAL ENFORCEMENT OFFICIALS; AND 
 
 
 (5) IN CARRYING OUT THE DUTIES UNDER ITEMS (1) THROUGH (4) OF THIS SUBSECTION, SHALL TAKE STEPS TO ENSURE THAT A PERSON OR UNIT SUBJECT TO THE JURISDICTION OF THE OFFICE IS NOT SUBJECT TO DUPLICATIVE INVESTIGATIONS OR AUDITS. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY NOT DISCLOSE THE IDENTITY OF THE SOURCE OF A COMPLAINT OR INFORMATION PROVIDED UNDER &sect; 7.5-107 OF THIS TITLE UNLESS THE INSPECTOR GENERAL: 
 
 
 (I) OBTAINS THE WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOURCE; OR 
 
 
 (II) DETERMINES THAT DISCLOSURE OF THE IDENTITY OF THE SOURCE IS NECESSARY AND UNAVOIDABLE DURING THE COURSE OF THE INVESTIGATION. 
 
 
 (2) IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL DETERMINES THAT DISCLOSURE OF THE IDENTITY OF A SOURCE IS NECESSARY AND UNAVOIDABLE, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL NOTIFY THE SOURCE IN WRITING AT LEAST 7 DAYS BEFORE DISCLOSURE. 
 
 
 (C) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL PERIODICALLY SHALL: 
 
 
 (1) REVIEW THE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES OF THE UNITS; AND 
 
 
 (2) MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS TO IMPROVE THE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES OF THE UNITS. 
 
 
 (D) AS TATE EMPLOYEE WHO MAKES A COMPLAINT OR PROVIDES INFORMATION UNDER &sect; 7.5-107 OF THIS TITLE SHALL BE SUBJECT TO THE PROTECTIONS ESTABLISHED UNDER TITLE 5, SUBTITLE 3 OF THE STATE PERSONNEL AND PENSIONS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 7.5-109. 
 
 
 (A) A UNIT SHALL COOPERATE FULLY WITH THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IN AN INVESTIGATION, AUDIT, OR EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW THAT IS CONDUCTED BY THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 (B) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL HAVE ACCESS TO ALL RECORDS, DATA, REPORTS, CONTRACTS, CORRESPONDENCE, OR OTHER DOCUMENTS OF A UNIT DURING AN INVESTIGATION, AUDIT, OR EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW CONDUCTED BY THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 (C) IN THE PERFORMANCE OF THE DUTIES OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL OR THE INSPECTOR GENERAL&#39;S DESIGNEE MAY: 
 
 
 (1) SEEK AND OBTAIN SWORN TESTIMONY; 
 
 
 (2) COMPEL THE ATTENDANCE OF WITNESSES TO BE DEPOSED BY THE OFFICE; AND 
 
 
 (3) COMPEL THE PRODUCTION OF RECORDS BY ISSUING A SUBPOENA IN ACCORDANCE WITH SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (D) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY ISSUE A SUBPOENA OR SUBPOENA DUCES TECUM ONLY WITH THE APPROVAL OF A JUDGE OF THE CIRCUIT COURT FOR THE COUNTY IN WHICH THE OFFICE IS LOCATED. 
 
 
 (2) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL SUBMIT A WRITTEN APPLICATION FOR THE APPROVAL SOUGHT UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (3) A CIRCUIT COURT SHALL ISSUE A WRITTEN DECISION ON AN APPLICATION SUBMITTED UNDER PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION WITHIN 72 HOURS AFTER THE RECEIPT OF THE APPLICATION. 
 
 
 (4) A SUBPOENA ISSUED BY THE INSPECTOR GENERAL UNDER THIS SUBSECTION MAY BE SERVED IN THE SAME MANNER AS A SUBPOENA ISSUED BY A CIRCUIT COURT. 
 
 
 (E) IF A PERSON REFUSES TO OBEY A SUBPOENA ISSUED BY THE INSPECTOR GENERAL UNDER SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION, THE COURT THAT APPROVED THE ISSUANCE OF THE SUBPOENA UNDER SUBSECTION (D) OF THIS SECTION MAY: 
 
 
 (1) ISSUE AN ORDER TO THE PERSON REQUIRING THE PERSON TO APPEAR BEFORE THE COURT TO SHOW CAUSE; AND 
 
 
 (2) AFTER CONDUCTING A HEARING, GRANT APPROPRIATE RELIEF. 
 
 
 7.5-110. 
 
 
 (A) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL MAY ADOPT REGULATIONS TO CARRY OUT THIS TITLE. 
 
 
 (B) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL: 
 
 
 (1) DEVELOP AN OPERATIONS MANUAL THAT CONFORMS TO THE STANDARDS ADOPTED BY THE ASSOCIATION OF INSPECTORS GENERAL; 
 
 
 (2) MAKE THE OPERATIONS MANUAL AVAILABLE TO THE PUBLIC ON THE OFFICE&#39;S WEBSITE; AND 
 
 
 (3) PROVIDE TRAINING AND EDUCATION FOR THE UNITS. 
 
 
 7.5-111. 
 
 
 (A) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, RECORDS PREPARED OR OBTAINED BY THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IN CONNECTION WITH AN INVESTIGATION, AUDIT, OR EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW CONDUCTED BY THE OFFICE ARE CONFIDENTIAL AND NOT SUBJECT TO DISCLOSURE UNDER THE PUBLIC INFORMATION ACT UNTIL THE FINAL REPORT ON THE INVESTIGATION OR AUDIT IS ISSUED UNDER &sect; 7.5-112(B) OF THIS TITLE. 
 
 
 (B) IF AN INDIVIDUAL REQUESTS A PUBLIC RECORD UNDER THE PUBLIC INFORMATION ACT AND THE ONLY COPY OF THE PUBLIC RECORD HAS BEEN OBTAINED BY THE INSPECTOR GENERAL, THE CUSTODIAN OF THE PUBLIC RECORD SHALL CERTIFY THAT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS IN POSSESSION OF THE ONLY COPY OF THE RECORD AND THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL ALLOW THE INDIVIDUAL TO EXAMINE AND COPY THE RECORD. 
 
 
 (C) AN INDIVIDUAL WHO DISCLOSES A RECORD PROTECTED FROM DISCLOSURE UNDER THIS SECTION IS GUILTY OF A MISDEMEANOR AND IS SUBJECT TO A FINE NOT TO EXCEED $2,000 OR IMPRISONMENT NOT TO EXCEED 1 YEAR OR BOTH. 
 
 
 7.5-112. 
 
 
 (A) (1) AT THE END OF EACH FISCAL YEAR, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL SUBMIT AN ANNUAL REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR AND, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THIS ARTICLE, THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY. 
 
 
 (2) THE ANNUAL REPORT SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) DESCRIBE THE BUSINESS AND PROCEEDINGS OF THE OFFICE DURING THE PRECEDING FISCAL YEAR; AND 
 
 
 (II) INCLUDE ANY RECOMMENDATIONS REGARDING THE ACTIVITIES OF THE UNITS THAT THE INSPECTOR GENERAL CONSIDERS APPROPRIATE. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL ISSUE A FINAL REPORT ON AN INVESTIGATION, AUDIT, OR EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW COMPLETED BY THE OFFICE. 
 
 
 (2) THE FINAL REPORT ISSUED UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION SHALL BE SUBMITTED TO THE GOVERNOR AND, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THIS ARTICLE, THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY. 
 
 
 (3) (I) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBPARAGRAPH (II) OF THIS PARAGRAPH, BEFORE RELEASING A FINAL REPORT UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL SHALL PROVIDE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR THE UNIT THAT WAS THE SUBJECT OF THE INVESTIGATION, AUDIT, OR EFFICIENCY RECONSTRUCTION REVIEW TO RESPOND TO THE REPORT. 
 
 
 (II) THE INSPECTOR GENERAL IS NOT REQUIRED TO PROVIDE AN OPPORTUNITY FOR RESPONSE UNDER SUBPARAGRAPH (I) OF THIS PARAGRAPH IF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL, IN CONJUNCTION WITH A UNITED STATES ATTORNEY, AN ATTORNEY GENERAL, A STATE&#39;S ATTORNEY, OR ANY OTHER APPROPRIATE PROSECUTORIAL AGENCY, DETERMINES THAT ALLOWING THE UNIT TO RESPOND WOULD JEOPARDIZE A PENDING OR POTENTIAL CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION. 
 
 
 (C) A REPORT ISSUED UNDER SUBSECTION (A) OR (B) OF THIS SECTION SHALL BE POSTED TO THE OFFICE&#39;S WEBSITE. 
 
 
 Article - State Personnel and Pensions 
 
 5-305. 
 Subject to the limitations of &sect; 5-306 of this subtitle, a supervisor, appointing authority, or the head of a principal unit may not take or refuse to take any personnel action as a reprisal against: 
 (1) an employee who discloses information that the employee reasonably believes evidences: 
 (i) an abuse of authority, gross mismanagement, or gross waste of money; 
 (ii) a substantial and specific danger to public health or safety; or 
 (iii) a violation of law; 
 (2) an employee of the Department of Juvenile Services who discloses information to the Director of Juvenile Justice Monitoring or staff of the Office of the Correctional Ombudsman, including the Juvenile Justice Monitoring Unit relating to the Unit&#39;s duties under &sect; 9-4014 of the State Government Article; 
 (3) an employee of the Department of Public Safety and Correctional Services who discloses information to the Correctional Ombudsman or staff of the Office of the Correctional Ombudsman relating to the Office&#39;s duties under &sect; 9-4004 of the State Government Article; or 
 
 (4) AN EMPLOYEE WHO MAKES A COMPLAINT OR PROVIDES INFORMATION TO THE INSPECTOR GENERAL OF THE STATE OR STAFF OF THE OFFICE OF THE INSPECTOR GENERAL RELATING TO WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION; 
 
 
 (5) an employee who, following a disclosure under item (1), (2), or (3) , OR (4) of this section, seeks a remedy provided under this subtitle or any other law or policy governing the employee&#39;s unit. 
 5-306. 
 Section 5-305 of this subtitle applies to a disclosure that is specifically prohibited by law only if that disclosure is made exclusively to the Attorney General OR THE INSPECTOR GENERAL OF THE STATE in the manner allowed in &sect; 5-313 of this subtitle. 
 5-313. 
 
 (A) For purposes of this subtitle, the Attorney General shall: 
 (1) designate an assistant Attorney General to receive from applicants and employees any information the disclosure of which is otherwise protected by law; 
 (2) investigate each allegation of illegality or impropriety; 
 (3) take appropriate legal action; and 
 (4) if the investigation concerns an allegation of illegality or impropriety in the Executive Branch, submit a confidential report to the Governor that describes the content of the disclosure. 
 
 (B) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBTITLE, THE INSPECTOR GENERAL OF THE STATE SHALL, IN ACCORDANCE WITH TITLE 7.5 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE: 
 
 
 (1) INVESTIGATE EACH COMPLAINT OF WASTE, MISMANAGEMENT, MISCONDUCT, ABUSE, FRAUD, OR CORRUPTION MADE BY AN EMPLOYEE; AND 
 
 
 (2) TAKE APPROPRIATE LEGAL OR ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION. 
 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That the Governor may transfer positions and funds appropriated for the positions from agencies, departments, or units of the Executive Branch of State government to the Maryland Office of the Inspector General established under Section 1 of this Act. 
 SECTION 3. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That all employees who are transferred to the Maryland Office of the Inspector General as a result of this Act shall be transferred without diminution of their rights, benefits, employment, or retirement status. 
 SECTION 4. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect October 1, 2026.
