PUBLISHED ON 29 APRIL 2025 IN THE OFFICIAL GAZETTE EL PERUANO


LAW THAT AMENDS THE CRIMINAL CODE, LEGISLATIVE DECREE 635, AND LAW
30096, THE COMPUTER CRIMES LAW, TO INCLUDE THE USE OF
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN THE COMMISSION OF CRIMES


                                          LAW 32314


THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC
WHEREAS:
THE CONGRESS OF THE REPUBLIC;
Has issued the following Law:


LAW THAT AMENDS THE CRIMINAL CODE, LEGISLATIVE DECREE 635, AND LAW
30096, THE COMPUTER CRIMES LAW, TO INCLUDE THE USE OF
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN THE COMMISSION OF CRIMES

Article 1. Amendment of articles 46, 129-M, 132, 196-A, 217, 218, 219, 220, 220-A, 220-B and 220-C of the Criminal Code, Legislative Decree 635

Articles 46, letter e) of numeral 2 are amended and letter ñ) is added to that numeral; 129-M, first paragraph; 132, third paragraph; 196-A, to add numeral 7; 217, first paragraph; 218, letters a) and d); 219; 220, to add letter f); 220-A; 220-B and 220-C of the Criminal Code, Legislative Decree 635, are amended in the following terms:

       "Article 46. Mitigating and aggravating circumstances
       […]
       2. The following are aggravating circumstances, provided that they are not specifically prescribed for the punishment of the offence and are not constituent elements of the punishable act:
       […]
       e) Employing in the execution of the punishable conduct means whose use may result in common danger, as well as the improper use of artificial intelligence or of similar or analogous technologies;
       […]
       ñ) When artificial intelligence or similar or analogous technologies are used to carry out the punishable conduct.

       Article 129-M. Child pornography
       Whoever possesses, promotes, manufactures, distributes, exhibits, offers, markets, advertises, publishes, imports, exports or manipulates by any means objects, books, writings, images, videos or audios, or uses technologies based on artificial intelligence, including deep falsifications ('deepfakes') or any multimedia content generated by artificial intelligence, for purposes related to child pornography, or stages live shows of a sexual nature in which minors under eighteen years of age participate, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than six and not more than ten years and with one hundred and twenty to three hundred and sixty-five day-fines.
       […].

       Article 132. Defamation
       […]
       If the offence is committed by means of a book, the press or another social communication medium, or through artificial intelligence technologies, deep falsifications ('deepfakes') or other content generated by means of artificial intelligence that disseminate false or denigrating information that causes harm to reputation or image, the penalty shall be a custodial sentence of not less than one nor more than three years and from one hundred and twenty to three hundred and sixty-five day-fines.

       Article 196-A. Aggravated fraud
       The penalty shall be a custodial sentence of not less than four and not more than eight years and ninety to two hundred day-fines, when the fraud:
       […]
       7. Is carried out through the manipulation of the voice, image, audio or bodily movement of third parties, using artificial intelligence or analogous technologies in such a way as to cause economic harm to the victim.

       Article 217. Reproduction, dissemination, distribution and circulation of a work without the author's authorization
       Whoever, with respect to a work, an artistic interpretation or performance, a phonogram or a broadcast or radio transmission, or an audiovisual recording or a photographic image, of human authorship, expressed in any form, performs any of the following acts without the prior written authorization of the author or rights holder shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than two and not more than six years and thirty to ninety day-fines:
       […].

       Article 218. Aggravated forms
       The penalty shall be a custodial sentence of not less than four and not more than eight years and ninety to one hundred and eighty day-fines when:
       a. A work of human authorship, unpublished or undisclosed, that has been received in confidence from the holder of the copyright or from someone on his behalf, is made known to the public without the consent of the rights holder.
       […]
       d. Devices, tangible or intangible systems, schemes or equipment capable of circumventing another device intended to prevent or restrict the making of copies of works of human authorship, or to impair the quality of the copies made, or capable of allowing or encouraging the reception of an encoded program, broadcast or otherwise communicated to the public, by those who are not authorized to do so, are manufactured, assembled, imported, exported, modified, sold, rented, offered for sale or rental, or otherwise put into circulation.
       […].

       Article 219. Plagiarism
       Whoever, with respect to a work of human authorship, disseminates it as his own, in whole or in part, copying or reproducing it textually, or attempting to disguise the copy by means of certain alterations, attributing to himself or to another the authorship or ownership belonging to a third party, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than three and not more than six years and ninety to one hundred and eighty day-fines.

       Article 220. Aggravated forms
       Whoever:
       […]
       f. The agent obtains a pecuniary advantage derived from the exploitation of the work that is the object of the conduct described in article 219.
       Shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than four and not more than eight years and ninety to three hundred and sixty-five day-fines.

       Article 220-A. Circumvention of an effective technological measure
       Whoever, for purposes of commercialization or other type of economic advantage, circumvents without authorization any effective technological measure used by phonogram producers, performing artists, performers, or by the authors of any work protected by intellectual property rights, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than two and not more than four years and from sixty to one hundred and twenty day-fines.

       Article 220-B. Products intended for the circumvention of technological measures
       Whoever, for purposes of commercialization or other type of economic advantage, manufactures, imports, distributes, offers to the public, supplies or in any way commercializes devices, products or components principally intended to circumvent a technological measure used by phonogram producers, performing artists or performers, or by the authors of any work protected by intellectual property rights, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than two and not more than four years and from sixty to one hundred and twenty day-fines.

       Article 220-C. Services intended for the circumvention of technological measures
       Whoever, for purposes of commercialization or other type of economic advantage, provides or offers services to the public principally intended to circumvent an effective technological measure used by phonogram producers, performing artists or performers, or by the authors of any work protected by intellectual property rights, shall be punished with a custodial sentence of not less than two and not more than four years and from sixty to one hundred and twenty day-fines."


Article 2. Amendment of article 11 of Law 30096, Computer Crimes Law

Numeral 5 is added to article 11 of Law 30096, Computer Crimes Law, in the following terms:

       "Article 11. Aggravating circumstances
       The judge increases the custodial sentence by up to one third above the maximum legal limit established for any of the offences provided for in this Law when:
       […]
       5. The agent commits the offence using artificial intelligence or similar or analogous technologies."


                       SOLE FINAL COMPLEMENTARY PROVISION

UNIQUE. Additional regulations
The Executive Branch, through the Presidency of the Council of Ministers and the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights, shall issue the additional regulations necessary for the application of the amendments provided for in this law, within a period of sixty calendar days counted from its entry into force.
