 HOUSE BILL NO. 4537 
 
 
 June 03, 2025, Introduced by Reps. Rheingans, Coffia, Price, Byrnes, Morgan, Wegela, Tsernoglou, Breen, Young, Wilson and MacDonell and referred to Committee on Communications and Technology. 
 
 
 
 A bill to amend 1939 PA 280, entitled 
 &quot;The social welfare act,&quot; 
 (MCL 400.1 to 400.119b) by adding section 107b. 
 
 
 
 
 THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT: 
 
 
 
 Sec. 107b. The department or a contracted health plan shall not deny, modify, or delay a claim under the medical assistance program based on a review using artificial intelligence.
