General Assembly                      Proposed Bill No. 5587
      January Session, 2025                LCO No. 1276


    Referred to Committee on INSURANCE AND REAL ESTATE

    Introduced by:
     REP. MENAPACE, 37th Dist.




 AN ACT PROHIBITING HEALTH INSURERS FROM USING ARTIFICIAL
 INTELLIGENCE AS THE PRIMARY METHOD TO DENY HEALTH
 INSURANCE CLAIMS.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to prohibit any health insurer
2      from using artificial intelligence as the primary method to deny health
3      insurance claims.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To prohibit any health insurer from using artificial intelligence as the
       primary method to deny health insurance claims.
