# ASEAN Responsible AI Roadmap 2025-2030

**Issuing Body:** Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN)
**Document Type:** Regional Policy Roadmap (non-binding)
**Adopted:** March 5, 2025 (during ASEAN Digital Ministers Meeting, Brunei Darussalam)
**Scope:** All 10 ASEAN Member States
**Official URL:** https://asean.org/book/asean-responsible-ai-roadmap-2025-2030/
**Full PDF:** https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ASEAN-Responsible-AI-Roadmap-Final.docx.pdf

---

## Overview

The ASEAN Responsible AI Roadmap 2025-2030 is a five-year strategic framework providing customised, step-by-step guidance for ASEAN governments to prioritise and operationalise responsible AI in an integrated and interoperable manner across the region. It was adopted by all ten ASEAN member states on March 5, 2025.

The Roadmap aligns with the ASEAN Digital Masterplan 2025 and the provisions of the ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA). It is designed to be flexible, allowing each member state to apply guidance according to its own capacity level, priorities, and national context.

---

## Primary Objectives

The Roadmap pursues four overarching goals:

1. **Regional coordination**: Support ASEAN Member States in developing a regionally concerted approach to responsible AI enablement and operationalisation
2. **Actionable guidance**: Provide practical steps for lawmakers, policymakers, and regulators in ASEAN to leverage responsible AI in a meaningful, inclusive, and sustainable manner by 2030
3. **Country-level empowerment**: Enable ASEAN Member States and stakeholders to drive and operationalise responsible AI in ways that fit each country's specific needs, priorities, and capabilities
4. **Alignment with ASEAN digitalization**: Facilitate alignment of Member States' national AI efforts with ASEAN's broader digitalization commitments

---

## Strategic Framework

### Self-Assessment Model

Member states employ a self-assessment framework that categorises their AI development stage as:
- **Emerging**: Early-stage capacity, building foundational governance structures
- **Promising**: Developing capacity with partial frameworks in place
- **Advanced**: Mature capacity with established governance and implementation

This tiered model allows the Roadmap to deliver customised actions appropriate to each country's actual readiness.

### Dual Strategy Approach

The Roadmap employs two complementary strategic streams:

1. **Cross-Cutting Pillars**: ASEAN-wide initiatives addressing skills development, fairness in AI systems, regional governance structures, and cooperation mechanisms applicable to all member states regardless of maturity level

2. **Targeted Pillars**: Country-specific actions customised to local capacity levels, particularly in the areas of governance frameworks, AI risk management, and stakeholder engagement

---

## Eight Strategic Priorities Through 2030

1. **Build public-sector AI capacity**: Boost government AI skills and institutional capability across member state administrations
2. **Strengthen AI start-up ecosystems**: Support the growth of responsible AI innovation and entrepreneurship across the region
3. **Expand digital infrastructure**: Improve connectivity, computing capacity, and digital foundations required for AI development
4. **Promote inclusive community engagement**: Ensure AI governance processes include diverse communities and perspectives
5. **Enable trusted cross-border data sharing**: Create secure, privacy-respecting data-sharing frameworks enabling regional AI collaboration
6. **Foster multi-stakeholder governance**: Encourage open discussions on AI governance involving government, industry, civil society, and academia
7. **Scale regional AI collaboration**: Build and strengthen regional partnerships on AI research, development, and governance
8. **Engage with global AI governance bodies**: Align ASEAN approaches with international AI governance frameworks and standards

## Intellectual Property Framework

The Roadmap addresses emerging IP challenges created by AI development across three key fronts:

### Patents
Traditional examination standards struggle to address AI inventions built on model training and probabilistic reasoning. The Roadmap positions ASEAN to develop regional patent guidelines permitting early filings with iterative claim modifications, drawing from EPO, USPTO, and WIPO best practices.

### Copyright
Questions around ownership arise as AI systems increasingly co-create content. Policymakers are encouraged to explore hybrid models where human-directed prompts qualify for copyright protection, while autonomous AI creations may require specialised rights frameworks.

### Data Licensing
Quality datasets form the foundation of responsible AI development. The Roadmap proposes harmonised ASEAN approaches modelled on Creative Commons frameworks for training datasets, establishing legal certainty and cross-border trust.

### Proposed ASEAN IP Initiatives
- **Model Guidelines for Patenting AI**: Drawing from international best practices
- **Data Use Charters**: Enabling privacy-respecting cross-border data flows
- **Creative Rights Task Force**: A multi-stakeholder forum piloting copyright approaches for AI-assisted works

---

## Member States

The Roadmap applies to all 10 ASEAN member states:
Brunei Darussalam, Cambodia, Indonesia, Lao PDR, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam.

---

## Context and Background

The Roadmap was developed in line with the priorities set by the ASEAN Digital Masterplan 2025 and supports implementation of the upcoming ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA). It positions intellectual property frameworks as "a crucial enabler" for aligning legal infrastructure, innovation policy, and IP frameworks throughout the decade, fostering trust-based ecosystems that support ethical AI growth across Southeast Asia.
