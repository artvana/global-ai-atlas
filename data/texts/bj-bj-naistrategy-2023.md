Stratégie Nationale pour l'Intelligence Artificielle et les Mégadonnées du Bénin 2023–2027
(National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence and Big Data of Benin 2023–2027)
Ministère du Numérique et de la Digitalisation, République du Bénin
BENIN | 2023

Source: https://numerique.gouv.bj/assets/documents/national-artificial-intelligence-and-data-strategy-2023-2027.pdf

Non-binding national AI and big data strategy adopted by Benin's Council of Ministers on 18 January 2023, under the leadership of the Ministry of Digital Affairs and Digitalization. The strategy covers the period 2023–2027 and establishes Benin's framework for harnessing AI and big data for national development under the Government Action Programme (PAG) and the national digital transformation strategy.

Key Elements (based on publicly reported information):

1. Vision
   Benin aims to become a digital hub in West Africa, using AI and big data to accelerate economic development, modernize public services, and position Benin as a regional leader in digital innovation.

2. Four Strategic Programs

   Program 1 — High-Impact AI Solutions
   - Deploying AI in priority sectors including agriculture, health, education, public administration, and financial services
   - Supporting the development of AI applications addressing Benin's specific development challenges
   - Scaling successful AI pilots across government and private sector

   Program 2 — Human Capacity Development
   - Building a talent pipeline through AI education at universities and technical institutions
   - Training civil servants and government officials in AI literacy and governance
   - Attracting Beninese AI talent from abroad and supporting diaspora engagement
   - Supporting coding schools and digital skills programs

   Program 3 — Research and Innovation Support
   - Establishing AI research centers and innovation labs
   - Supporting startups working on AI solutions
   - Funding AI research partnerships between academia and industry
   - Creating an enabling environment for AI experimentation and testing

   Program 4 — AI and Big Data Governance Framework
   - Developing legal and regulatory frameworks for AI and data governance
   - Establishing data management policies and standards
   - Addressing ethical issues in AI including fairness, transparency, and accountability
   - Strengthening data protection and cybersecurity

3. Governance and Implementation
   The strategy is overseen by the Ministry of Digital Affairs and Digitalization, with coordination across relevant line ministries and the presidency. Implementation is monitored through annual reviews.

4. Financing
   The strategy identifies multiple financing sources including government budget allocations, development partners, private sector investment, and regional funding mechanisms.

5. Regional and International Context
   Benin's strategy is aligned with the African Union's Continental AI Strategy and ECOWAS regional digital initiatives, and aims to contribute to pan-African AI development efforts.
