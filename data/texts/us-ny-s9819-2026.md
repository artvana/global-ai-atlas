 Introduced by Sen. MATTERA -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Labor 
 
 
 AN ACT directing the department of labor to conduct a study on the effects of artificial intelligence on job losses in the state 
 
 
 
 
 The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assembly, do enact as follows: 
 
 
 Section 1. The department of labor, in conjunction with the office of information technology services, shall conduct a study and prepare a report on the effects of artificial intelligence on job losses in the state. 
 Section 2. Within one year of the effective date of this act, the department of labor shall issue a report of the findings of the study to the governor, the temporary president of the senate and the speaker of the assembly. 
 Section 3. This act shall take effect immediately.
