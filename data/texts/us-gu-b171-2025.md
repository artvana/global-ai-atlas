AN ACT TO ADD A NEW CHAPTER 28A TO TITLE 9, GUAM
CODE ANNOTATED; TO ADD A NEW PARAGRAPH (12) TO §
25.10(a) AND TO ADD A NEW § 25.50 TO CHAPTER 25, TITLE 9,
GCA; AND TO ADD A NEW ARTICLE 7 TO CHAPTER 24, TITLE
7, GCA, RELATIVE TO PROHIBITING THE CREATION,
POSSESSION, DISCLOSURE, OR THREATENED DISCLOSURE
OF NONCONSENSUAL INTIMATE DEPICTIONS, WHETHER
AUTHENTIC OR TECHNOLOGICALLY MANIPULATED
(“DEEPFAKES”), AND TO PROVIDE CRIMINAL PENALTIES,
CIVIL REMEDIES, FORFEITURE, VICTIM-PRIVACY
SAFEGUARDS, AND EXPANDED JURISDICTION.
1 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF GUAM:
2 Section 1. Legislative Findings and Intent. I Liheslaturan Guåhan finds
3 that advances in artificial intelligence and digital technologies now allow
4 perpetrators to create or alter intimate images, audio, and video in ways that are often
5 indistinguishable from reality. The nonconsensual disclosure of such material causes
6 serious and lasting harm to survivors, including impacts to mental health, personal
7 safety, reputation, and economic well-being.
8 Current Guam law addresses certain forms of sexual abuse and indecent
9 exposure, but it does not fully address the severity and scope of modern manipulated
10 depictions, often referred to as "deepfakes." These materials are increasingly used
11 as tools for harassment, coercion, and abuse.
1

1 I Liheslaturan Guåhan further finds that this Act is intended to modernize
2 Guam’s criminal and civil laws to respond to this new form of digital sexual abuse.
3 It ensures that both authentic and technologically manipulated depictions are treated
4 equally under the law. It further provides survivors with clear, enforceable legal
5 rights to pursue justice, remove unlawful material, and safeguard their privacy
6 through civil and criminal remedies.
7 Section 2. A new Chapter 28A is added to Title 9, Guam Code Annotated, to
8 Read:
9 "CHAPTER 28A
10 NONCONSENSUAL INTIMATE DEPICTIONS ACT OF 2025
11 § 28A101. Short Title.
12 § 28A102. Definitions.
13 § 28A103. Offense: Possession, Disclosure, or Threatened Disclosure.
14 § 28A104. Forfeiture.
15 § 28A105. Civil Cause of Action.
16 § 28A106. Victim Privacy.
17 § 28A107. Limitations.
18 § 28A108. Exceptions.
19 § 28A109. Construction.
20
21 § 28A101. Short Title. This Chapter shall be known and cited as the
22 “Nonconsensual Intimate Depictions Act of 2025.”
23 § 28A102. Definitions.
24 For the purposes of this Chapter:
25 (a)“Depiction” means any voice, audio, image, or visual
26 representation that is created, generated, or altered by any means, including
2

1 software, machine learning, or artificial intelligence, whether authentic or
2 manipulated.
3 (b)“Disclose” means to publish, distribute, transfer, transmit, or
4 otherwise make a depiction accessible to another by any method now known
5 or developed in the future.
6 (c) “Identifiable Individual” means a person who appears in whole or
7 in part in a depiction and can be recognized from facial likeness, voice,
8 distinguishing features, or contextual information.
9 (d)“Intimate Conduct” means:
10 (1)exposure of genitals, pubic area, anus, or female breast,
11 (2)transfer or display of sexual bodily fluids, or
12 (3)sexually explicit conduct including intercourse, bestiality,
13 masturbation, or sadomasochistic abuse.
14 (e) “Nonconsensual Intimate Depiction” means a depiction of intimate
15 conduct of an identifiable individual that is created, possessed, or disclosed
16 without that individual’s consent, regardless of whether the depicted conduct
17 actually occurred.
18 § 28A103. Offense: Possession, Disclosure, or Threatened Disclosure.
19 (a)A person commits the offense of Nonconsensual Intimate Depiction if
20 that person knowingly or recklessly possesses, discloses, or threatens to disclose a
21 Nonconsensual Intimate Depiction of an Identifiable Individual:
22 (1)with intent to harass, annoy, threaten, alarm, or cause physical,
23 psychological, emotional, financial, or reputational harm, or
24 (2)with knowledge that such possession, disclosure, or threat will
25 cause such harm.
26 (b)Grading.
27 (1)The offense is a felony of the third degree.
3

1 (2)If the depicted individual is a minor, or the offense is committed
2 for financial gain, in the course of extortion, or against five (5) or more
3 separate persons, it is a felony of the second degree.
4 (3)If the depiction is used to facilitate, conceal, or commit another
5 felony, it is a felony of the first degree.
6 (c) A person may be convicted under this Section regardless of whether the
7 threatened disclosure ultimately occurs.
8 § 28A104. Forfeiture.
9 Upon conviction, the court shall order the offender to forfeit to the victim, or
10 to the government of Guam if the victim so elects, all rights in the unlawful
11 material, any proceeds derived from it, and any device used to create or
12 disseminate the material.
13 § 28A105. Civil Cause of Action.
14 (a)An Identifiable Individual, or a parent or guardian on behalf of a minor,
15 may bring a civil action in the Superior Court of Guam for:
16 (1)actual damages,
17 (2)statutory damages up to Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000) per
18 depiction,
19 (3)injunctive relief, including takedown or non-disclosure orders, and
20 (4)reasonable attorney’s fees and costs.
21 (b)Venue lies in the Superior Court of Guam regardless of the location of
22 the defendant or the platform used to publish the depiction.
23 § 28A106. Victim Privacy.
24 Courts shall permit pseudonyms, in-camera review, and sealing of court
25 records to protect the identity and dignity of victims.
26 § 28A107. Limitations.
4

1 The statute of limitations begins upon discovery by the victim or written
2 notice from law enforcement. It applies separately to each depiction. No limitation
3 shall apply if the depicted individual was a minor at the time of the offense.
4 § 28A108. Exceptions.
5 This Chapter does not apply to disclosures made reasonably and in good
6 faith:
7 (a)to a law enforcement agency in the course of reporting or
8 investigating unlawful activity,
9 (b)as part of a legal proceeding, or
10 (c) with the sole purpose of assisting the Identifiable Individual and at
11 that individual’s request.
12 (d)A disclaimer stating the material is unauthorized, manipulated, or
13 fake shall not constitute a defense.
14 § 28A109. Construction. Nothing in this Chapter shall be construed to
15 impair constitutionally protected speech or expression, provided that such
16 expression is not made with intent to harm, harass, or abuse an identifiable
17 individual.
18 Section 3. A new paragraph (12) is added to Subsection (a) of § 25.10,
19 Chapter 25, Title 9, Guam Code Annotated, to read:
20 “(12) “Manipulated child sexual depiction” means any depiction, wholly or
21 partially computer-generated, that portrays a minor engaged in intimate conduct as
22 defined in § 28A102(d), Chapter 28A of this Title, whether or not the minor’s actual
23 intimate parts are depicted.”
24 Section 4. A new § 25.50 is added to Chapter 25, Title 9, Guam Code
25 Annotated, to read:
26 “§ 25.50. Possession or Dissemination of Manipulated Child Sexual
27 Depictions.
5

1 Any person who knowingly possesses or disseminates a manipulated child
2 sexual depiction, as defined in § 25.10(a)(12), shall be punished in the same manner
3 as for possession or dissemination of authentic child sexual abuse material under
4 applicable Guam law.”
5 Section 5. A new Article 7 is added to Chapter 24, Title 7, Guam Code
6 Annotated to hereby read as follows:
7 “Article 7
8 Victim Anonymity and Privacy in Civil Proceedings
9 § 24701. Victim Pseudonyms and Record Sealing.
10
11 § 24701. Victim Pseudonyms and Record Sealing.
12 The Superior Court shall adopt rules allowing an Identifiable Individual
13 bringing a civil action under Chapter 28A of Title 9, Guam Code Annotated, to
14 proceed under a pseudonym upon a showing that public disclosure of the
15 individual’s identity would cause further harm. The court shall also permit the
16 sealing of court records as necessary to protect the victim’s privacy.”
17 Section 6. Effective Date. This Act shall be effective upon enactment.
