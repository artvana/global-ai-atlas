 CS/SB 202: Municipal Water and Sewer Utility Rates 

 
 GENERAL BILL
 by 
 
Rules ; Jones ; (CO-INTRODUCERS) 
 Davis 
 
 Municipal Water and Sewer Utility Rates; Requiring a municipality to charge customers receiving its utility services in another municipality the same rates, fees, and charges as it charges consumers within its municipal boundaries under certain circumstances, etc.
