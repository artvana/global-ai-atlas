Co-authors Ms. Pérez Soto; and Messrs. Rosa Ramos and Santos Ortiz

JOINT RESOLUTION
To order the Department of Health of Puerto Rico, in coordination with the Medical Services Administration (ASEM), the Puerto Rico Science, Technology and Research Trust, the Medical Sciences Campus of the University of Puerto Rico, and the accredited medical schools on the Island, to conduct a thorough investigation on the feasibility of establishing a Precision Medicine Program supported by Artificial Intelligence, including its integration into diagnostic processes; and to present to the Legislative Assembly an implementation plan with phases, estimated budget, and evaluation metrics; while the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) shall serve as technical advisor in this investigation; and for other related purposes.

STATEMENT OF MOTIVES

The Legislative Assembly of Puerto Rico recognizes the importance of scientific and technological innovation as an engine of economic and social development, as well as its direct impact on the quality of life of the citizenry. Precision medicine, understood as a model of care that takes into account the genetic, environmental, and lifestyle variability of each individual, constitutes one of the most significant advances in contemporary medical practice. This modality allows for the optimization of diagnoses and treatments, reducing risks and increasing the effectiveness of clinical interventions.
The establishment of a Precision Medicine Program supported by the use of Artificial Intelligence represents a strategic opportunity for Puerto Rico. The integration of these technologies has the potential to transform the health system through more accurate diagnostic processes, the early detection of diseases, the prediction of risks in specific populations, and the design of personalized therapies. In addition, by automating and streamlining routine processes of clinical analysis and evaluation, these tools represent a tangible relief to the workload that doctors in Puerto Rico face today. In view of the reality of the migration and shortage of health professionals on the Island, precision medicine supported by artificial intelligence is shaping up to be an essential tool to maximize efficiency, allowing doctors to dedicate more time to the direct attention of their patients and to critical clinical decision-making.
Puerto Rico has first-rate institutions, such as the Department of Health, the Medical Services Administration, the Medical Sciences Campus of the University of Puerto Rico, and the various accredited medical schools on the Island, which possess the experience and knowledge necessary to lead projects of this magnitude. Likewise, entities such as the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service and the Science, Technology and Research Trust contribute the technological infrastructure, the regulatory framework, and the research capacity to insert Puerto Rico into this cutting-edge model.
The collaboration between these institutions would allow for a thorough investigation of the feasibility of implementing a Precision Medicine Program supported by Artificial Intelligence. Such effort must contemplate a detailed analysis of the technical, regulatory, and budgetary requirements, as well as the metrics necessary to measure its effectiveness. It is equally indispensable to design an implementation plan with clearly defined phases, a realistic estimated budget, and evaluation mechanisms that ensure the sustainability of the program over time.
The Legislative Assembly has the duty to drive initiatives that advance the well-being of the population and strengthen Puerto Rico's position as a reference in the field of health and biomedical research. The adoption of cutting-edge technologies such as Artificial Intelligence in precision medicine not only represents an opportunity to improve patient care, but also a firm step toward the transformation of the public health system on the Island. By virtue of the foregoing, this Joint Resolution is presented with the purpose of ordering the coordination of the necessary institutional efforts and ensuring that Puerto Rico advances strategically and responsibly in the integration of precision medicine in its health processes.
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:
 Section 1.- The Department of Health of Puerto Rico is hereby ordered, in coordination with the Medical Services Administration, the Science and Technology Trust, the Medical Sciences Campus of the University of Puerto Rico, the accredited medical schools, and the entities of the private health sector that voluntarily wish to provide support, to conduct a thorough investigation on the feasibility, scope, limitations, and legal requirements of establishing a Precision Medicine Program supported by Artificial Intelligence, including its application in diagnostic and clinical support processes.
It is provided that the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) shall serve as technical advisor in this investigation in those aspects that link governmental information systems, interoperability, or digital security standards in state platforms.
Section 2.- The investigation shall include, without being limited to:
a) Current use cases and projections of AI in oncology, chronic diseases, and pharmacogenetics.
b) The technological, biomedical, and hospital infrastructure necessary.
c) Available human and academic resources, including bioinformatics and genomics.
d) Legal and regulatory challenges around data protection and medical liability.
e) Estimated costs, implementation phases, and federal and local sources of financing.
f) Strategies to ensure equitable access to the population.
Section 3.- The required report shall be delivered to the Legislative Assembly of Puerto Rico on or before two hundred seventy (270) days from the approval of this Joint Resolution.
Section 4.- This Joint Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
