# Singapore Elections (Integrity of Online Advertising) (Amendment) Act 2024
**Passed: October 15, 2024 | Singapore Parliament**

## Overview
The Elections (Integrity of Online Advertising) (Amendment) Bill was introduced in Parliament on 9 September 2024 and passed on 15 October 2024. It is the first Singapore legislation to explicitly prohibit AI-generated or AI-manipulated online election content. The Act amends existing elections law to ban deepfake election advertising during the election period (from Writ of Election to close of polling).

## Scope of Prohibition
During the election period (from issuance of the Writ to the close of polling on Polling Day), the Act prohibits:
- Publication, sharing, promoting, or reposting of deepfake content featuring election candidates
- Creating or disseminating election advertising containing content that realistically depicts a candidate saying or doing something they did not in fact say or do
- Content that falsely represents the identity, views or actions of any candidate or political party
- Content that falsely represents the relationship or association of any candidate or political party with any other person or entity
- Content likely to cause enmity, hatred, ill-will or hostility between different groups in Singapore

## Enforcement Mechanisms
- The Returning Officer has power to issue corrective directions to take down offending content or disable Singapore user access
- Corrective directions cover: individuals who publish content, social media services, and Internet Access Service Providers

## Penalties
- Fines of up to **SGD 50,000** and/or **up to 5 years imprisonment** for creating or disseminating false/misleading content
- Doubled penalties if the content is likely to affect the election outcome

## Applicability
Applies during Singapore general elections and by-elections; covers all online platforms accessible in Singapore.
