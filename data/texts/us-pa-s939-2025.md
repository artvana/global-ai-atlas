22 Section 301. High impact data center.
23 (a) Notification to office.--
24 (1) The owner of a data center operating in this
25 Commonwealth shall notify the office if the data center
26 becomes a high impact data center. The notification must be
27 sent to the office:
28 (i) not later than 30 days after the data center
29 becomes a high impact data center; or
30 (ii) if the owner reasonably anticipates that the
20250SB0939PN1048 - 4 -

1 data center will, at some future date, become a high
2 impact data center, prior to the future date.
3 (2) The notification shall include known or expected
4 power consumption of the data center and other information as
5 required by the office. If the notification is sent under
6 paragraph (1)(ii), the notification shall include the future
7 date.
8 (b) Confirmation by office.--
9 (1) The office shall identify and designate high impact
10 data centers in this Commonwealth.
11 (2) The office shall review a notification submitted by
12 an owner under subsection (a). If the office determines that
13 the data center is or will become a high impact data center,
14 the office shall issue confirmation to the owner not later
15 than 14 days after receipt of the notification.
16 (c) Recordkeeping.--Any information provided under this
17 section by an owner that the owner identifies as confidential
18 business information shall be exempt from inspection and
19 duplication under the act of February 14, 2008 (P.L.6, No.3),
20 known as the Right-to-Know Law.
21 Section 302. Uniformity of local ordinances.
22 (a) Requirements.--A local ordinance regulating high impact
23 data centers shall allow for the reasonable development of high
24 impact data centers.
25 (b) Reasonable development.--In order to allow for the
26 reasonable development of high impact data centers, a local
27 ordinance:
28 (1) May not impose conditions, requirements or
29 limitations on the construction of high impact data centers
30 that are more stringent than conditions, requirements or
20250SB0939PN1048 - 5 -

1 limitations imposed on construction activities for other
2 industrial uses within the geographic boundaries of the local
3 government.
4 (2) May not impose conditions, requirements or
5 limitations on the heights of structures, screening and
6 fencing, lighting or noise relating to permanent high impact
7 data centers that are more stringent than the conditions,
8 requirements or limitations imposed on other industrial uses
9 or other land development within the particular zoning
10 district where the high impact data centers are situated
11 within the local government.
12 (3) Shall include a review period for permitted uses
13 that does not exceed 30 days for complete submissions or 120
14 days for conditional uses.
15 (4) May impose restrictions on vehicular access routes
16 for overweight vehicles only as authorized under 75 Pa.C.S.
17 (relating to vehicles) or the act of July 31, 1968 (P.L.805,
18 No.247), known as the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning
19 Code.
20 (5) May not impose limits or conditions on operations or
21 hours of operation of the high impact data center as it
22 relates to normal data processing activity.

5 (a) Establishment.--The Artificial Intelligence, Data Center
