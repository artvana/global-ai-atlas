# Mississippi Senate Bill 2426 — Mississippi Artificial Intelligence Regulation Task Force Act

**SB 2426 | Mississippi Legislature, 2025 Regular Session**
*Mississippi — Enacted March 18, 2025 | Effective March 18, 2025*

---

## Overview

Mississippi Senate Bill 2426 establishes the Mississippi Artificial Intelligence Regulation (AIR) Task Force — a legislative study body charged with examining the benefits and risks of artificial intelligence, reviewing national AI policy developments, and recommending legislation to the Mississippi Legislature. The Task Force is required to report annually through December 2027 and has no independent regulatory or enforcement powers.

The legislation reflects Mississippi's deliberate, study-first approach to AI governance, consistent with the approach taken by a number of other states that have chosen to assess the AI regulatory landscape before enacting binding requirements on the private sector. The AIR Task Force is explicitly modeled as a multi-stakeholder body including legislators, executive branch officials, and industry representatives.

## Key Provisions

### Establishment of the AIR Task Force

- Creates the Mississippi Artificial Intelligence Regulation (AIR) Task Force as a formal legislative advisory body.
- Membership composition: legislators from both chambers of the Mississippi Legislature, appointees of the Governor representing state executive agencies (including the Mississippi Department of Information Technology Services), and representatives from the private sector with expertise in artificial intelligence, data science, and technology law.
- Task Force meets at least quarterly, or more frequently as needed to complete its work.
- Members serve without additional compensation; state employees serve as part of their official duties.

### Mandate and Scope

- Study the current and anticipated uses of artificial intelligence in Mississippi's economy, government operations, and society.
- Assess the potential risks and harms of AI systems, including risks related to bias, privacy, security, and economic displacement.
- Review AI regulatory frameworks adopted by other states, the federal government, and international bodies.
- Evaluate the potential economic impact of various AI regulatory approaches on Mississippi businesses and industries, including agriculture, healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing.
- Develop specific, actionable legislative recommendations for the Mississippi Legislature addressing AI governance across sectors.

### Annual Reporting

- Task Force must submit written reports to the Mississippi Legislature and the Governor by December 1 of each year through December 2027.
- Reports must include: findings on AI risks and benefits, a summary of regulatory developments in other jurisdictions, specific legislative recommendations, and any minority views.
- Reports are public records.

### Industry and Stakeholder Engagement

- Task Force is directed to hold public hearings and solicit testimony from AI developers, deployers, affected communities, civil rights organizations, and academic experts.
- Industry representatives on the Task Force are intended to provide practical insight into implementation challenges and economic impacts of proposed regulations.

### Limitations

- The Task Force has no authority to promulgate binding regulations or issue enforcement actions.
- All recommendations require separate legislative action to become law.
- The Act imposes no obligations on private AI developers or deployers operating in Mississippi.
- Reporting obligation expires automatically after the December 2027 report; the Task Force itself may be extended or dissolved by subsequent legislation.
