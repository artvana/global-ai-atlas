 AN ACT 
 
 
 RELATING TO COMMERCIAL LAW -- GENERAL REGULATORY PROVISIONS -- INTERNET ACCESS AND ADVERTISING BY FACSIMILE 
 
 
 
 
 
 Introduced By: 
 Representatives Voas, Potter, O&#39;Brien, Slater, and Read

 Referred To: 
 House Innovation, Internet, &amp; Technology 
 
 
 
 It is enacted by the General Assembly as follows: 
 
 SECTION 1. Chapter 6-47 of the General Laws entitled &quot;Internet Access and Advertising by Facsimile&quot; is hereby amended by adding thereto the following section: 
 
 
 
 6-47-2.1. Accessibility and ability to determine artificial intelligence generated images. 
 
 
 
 
 (a) Any and all video, image, or photography generated by artificial intelligence or &quot;AI&quot; that is uploaded, created, distributed or originated from within the State of Rhode Island and is posted on or to a public platform or that is posted from outside of the State of Rhode Island and is viewable in Rhode Island shall contain a marking disclosing that the video image or photograph has been generated by AI. 
 
 
 (b) As used in this section &quot;artificial intelligence&quot; or &quot;AI&quot; means a machine-based system that undertakes analysis, reasoning and problem-solving, and that can be used to generate predictions, recommendations, photographs, videos, or other content. 
 
 
 (c) Any person or entity violating the provisions of subsection (a) of this section for profit, or for trade as defined in &sect; 6-13.1-1, shall be deemed to have engaged in a deceptive trade practice in violation of chapter 13.1 of title 6 and shall be subject to a civil penalty pursuant to the provisions of &sect;6-13.1-8. 
 
 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon passage. 
 
 
 
 EXPLANATION 
 
 
 BY THE LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL 
 
 
 OF 
 
 
 AN ACT 
 
 
 RELATING TO COMMERCIAL LAW -- GENERAL REGULATORY PROVISIONS -- INTERNET ACCESS AND ADVERTISING BY FACSIMILE 
 
 This act would require that video or photography generated by artificial intelligence or &quot;AI&quot; that is posted on a public platform and that is viewable in Rhode Island shall contain a marking disclosing that the video or photo has been generated by AI. A violation of this provision by an entity for profit would constitute a deceptive trade practice. 
 This act would take effect upon passage.
