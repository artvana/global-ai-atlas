# Texas Senate Bill 441 — Unlawful Production or Distribution of Intimate Deepfake Images

**Senate Bill No. 441**

*Texas — June 20, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 441 updates the Texas Penal Code to criminalize the production and distribution of AI-generated intimate deepfake images without the depicted person's consent, and criminalizes the use of such threats for sexual coercion.

### Base Criminal Offense

It is a Class A misdemeanor (punishable by up to 1 year in jail and a $4,000 fine) to knowingly:
- Produce an AI-generated or AI-manipulated intimate image depicting an identifiable real person without that person's consent
- Distribute, promote, or disseminate such an image without the depicted person's consent

The offense applies to both wholly synthetic AI-generated intimate imagery and AI-manipulated images that superimpose or digitally alter a real person's likeness onto intimate content.

### Enhanced Penalty for Minors

If the depicted person in the nonconsensual AI-generated intimate image is a minor, the offense is elevated to a state jail felony, carrying a sentence of 180 days to 2 years in a state jail facility and a fine of up to $10,000.

### Sexual Coercion Offense

The bill also criminalizes threatening to produce or distribute nonconsensual AI-generated intimate imagery as a form of sexual coercion — i.e., using the threat of creating or releasing deepfake intimate images to coerce another person into sexual conduct or to extort them. This offense is separately classified and prosecutable.

### Effective Date

The act takes effect September 1, 2025.
