 LONG TITLE 
 
 
 General Description: 
 
 This bill addresses sexual crimes. 
 
 
 
 Highlighted Provisions: 
 
 This bill: 
 &bull; provides a definition of a female breast for certain sexual and sexual exploitation offenses that includes a breast that is undeveloped or partially developed; 
 &bull; expands the definition of &quot;child sexual abuse material&quot; to include a minor observing or being used for sexually explicit conduct; 
 &bull; expands the definition of &quot;sexually explicit conduct&quot; concerning certain bodily functions for certain offenses; 
 &bull; changes the mental state necessary to commit the offense of sexual exploitation of a minor; 
 &bull; expands the offense of sexual exploitation of a minor to include when an actor accesses child sexual abuse material with the intent to view; 
 &bull; provides a safe harbor provision for certain employees and independent contractors who are required to access or view child sexual abuse material within the scope of employment; 
 &bull; changes the mental state necessary to commit the offense of aggravated sexual exploitation of a minor; 
 &bull; amends the offense of aggravated sexual exploitation of a minor to remove a lesser penalty for circumstances when the child sexual abuse material depicts an individual who is 14 years old or older or pubescent; and 
 &bull; makes technical and conforming changes.

 76-5b-201. Sexual exploitation of a minor -- Offenses. 
 
 (1) Terms defined in Section 76-1-101.5 apply to this section.
