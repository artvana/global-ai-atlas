 CHAPTER ______ 
 
 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Information Technology - Modernizing Data Governance - Office of Enterprise Data and Council for Open Data Advancement 
 
 FOR the purpose of establishing the position and responsibilities of the State Chief Data Officer; establishing the Office of Enterprise Data in the Department of Information Technology and the responsibilities of the Office; requiring each unit of State government to appoint an Agency Data Officer; renaming the Council on Open Data to be the Council for Open Data Advancement; altering the membership, member terms, and responsibilities of the Council; and generally relating to ensuring the efficient, secure, and ethical use of data in support of State goals and initiatives. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 Section 3.5-319 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2024 Supplement) 
 BY repealing and reenacting, with amendments, 
 Article - State Government 
 Section 10-1501, 10-1503, and 10-1504 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2024 Supplement) 
 
 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 
 
 3.5-319. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SECTION, &quot;OFFICE&quot; MEANS THE OFFICE OF ENTERPRISE DATA. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THERE IS A STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER IN THE DEPARTMENT. 
 
 
 (2) THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER SHALL BE APPOINTED BY THE GOVERNOR AND SUPERVISED BY THE SECRETARY. 
 
 
 (3) THE RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) DIRECTING, COORDINATING, AND IMPLEMENTING THE STATEWIDE DATA STRATEGY AND POLICIES FOR UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT TO ENSURE RESPONSIBLE GOVERNANCE AND MANAGEMENT OF STATE DATA AND TO PROMOTE STANDARDIZATION, COLLABORATION, AND ESTABLISHMENT OF EFFICIENT DATA PRACTICES; 
 
 
 (II) PROVIDING ADVICE, RECOMMENDATIONS, AND CONSULTATIONS TO THE GOVERNOR AND ANY UNIT OF STATE GOVERNMENT CONCERNING DATA POLICIES, PROCEDURES, AND STANDARDS; 
 
 
 (III) OVERSEEING THE USE, MANAGEMENT, AND SHARING OF STATE DATA BY UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT TO ENSURE COMPLIANCE WITH ALL REQUIREMENTS, PROHIBITIONS, AND RESTRICTIONS ON DISCLOSURE OR RELEASE OF THE DATA APPLICABLE TO THE UNIT SHARING IT; 
 
 
 (IV) COMPILING AN AUTHORITATIVE INVENTORY OF DATA COLLECTED, CREATED, OR MAINTAINED BY UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT AS REQUIRED BY &sect; 3.5-318 OF THIS SUBTITLE; 
 
 
 (V) EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE PROVIDED IN LAW, DESIGNATING AND FACILITATING ACCESS TO AUTHORITATIVE DATA SOURCES; AND 
 
 
 (VI) OVERSEEING THE DATA LITERACY OF STATE EMPLOYEES TO ENSURE STATE DATA ARE RESPONSIBLY UNDERSTOOD, ANALYZED, AND COMMUNICATED. 
 
 
 (C) (1) THERE IS AN OFFICE OF ENTERPRISE DATA IN THE DEPARTMENT. 
 
 
 (2) THE HEAD OF THE OFFICE IS THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER. 
 
 
 (3) THE OFFICE SHALL ESTABLISH A STATEWIDE DATA GOVERNANCE PROGRAM TO: 
 
 
 (I) ESTABLISH A STATEWIDE FRAMEWORK BY WHICH THE MANAGEMENT, USE, AND SHARING OF DATA WITHIN AND BETWEEN UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT IS GOVERNED; 
 
 
 (II) ESTABLISH STANDARDS BY WHICH DATA ARE MANAGED IN COMPLIANCE WITH DEFINED POLICIES; 
 
 
 (III) DEVELOP GUIDANCE ON THE EFFECTIVE USE, MANAGEMENT, AND SHARING OF DATA WITHIN AND BETWEEN UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT AND WITH THIRD PARTIES; 
 
 
 (IV) PROVIDE SERVICES AND ADVISE UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT ON HOW TO MATURE THEIR DATA PROGRAMS; AND 
 
 
 (V) ENSURE UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT ARE LEVERAGING STATE DATA AS A STRATEGIC ASSET. 
 
 
 (4) THE OFFICE SHALL ESTABLISH A DATA SHARING PROGRAM TO: 
 
 
 (I) ESTABLISH A STATEWIDE FRAMEWORK THAT PROMOTES DATA SHARING AND IMPROVES ACCESS TO STATE DATA; 
 
 
 (II) ESTABLISH STANDARDS BY WHICH DATA ARE SHARED IN COMPLIANCE WITH EXISTING LAWS AND DEFINED POLICIES; 
 
 
 (III) DEVELOP PROCESSES FOR UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT TO ACHIEVE AND MAINTAIN DATA READINESS FOR ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; 
 
 
 (IV) ADMINISTER A SECURE, MULTISTAKEHOLDER DATA EXCHANGE AND ANALYTICS PLATFORM APPLYING COMMON RULES FOR DATA SECURITY, PRIVACY, AND CONFIDENTIALITY; AND 
 
 
 (V) PROVIDE SERVICES AND ADVISE UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT ON HOW TO MAXIMIZE THE VALUE OF DATA THROUGH COLLECTIVE INVESTMENT AND EFFECTIVE INTEROPERABILITY OF DATA SOURCES. 
 
 
 (5) THE OFFICE SHALL ESTABLISH A DATA LITERACY PROGRAM TO: 
 
 
 (I) ESTABLISH A STATEWIDE FRAMEWORK TO FOSTER A CULTURE OF ANALYTICAL THINKING AND EVIDENCE-BASED DECISION MAKING WITHIN UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT; 
 
 
 (II) ADMINISTER A SECURE, MULTISTAKEHOLDER DATA ACADEMY PLATFORM WITH CURATED CONTENT; 
 
 
 (III) DEVELOP GUIDANCE ON THE EFFECTIVE UNDERSTANDING, ANALYSIS, PRIVACY SAFEGUARDING, AND PROTECTION OF STATE DATA FOR ALL APPROPRIATE EMPLOYEES OF UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT; 
 
 
 (IV) MANAGE DATA TRAINING FOR ALL APPROPRIATE EMPLOYEES OF UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT; AND 
 
 
 (V) ESTABLISH A LIST OF DEFINED DATA TERMS AND CREATE A UNIFIED DATA LANGUAGE IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (D) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE THE OFFICE WITH SUFFICIENT STAFF TO PERFORM THE FUNCTIONS REQUIRED UNDER THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (E) (1) EACH UNIT OF STATE GOVERNMENT SHALL APPOINT AN AGENCY DATA OFFICER WHO SHALL REPORT DIRECTLY TO THE UNIT HEAD OR AN APPROPRIATE SENIOR OFFICIAL WITHIN THE UNIT OF STATE GOVERNMENT. 
 
 
 (2) EACH AGENCY DATA OFFICER SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR: 
 
 
 (I) COLLABORATING WITH THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER TO ENSURE THAT DATA INITIATIVES ARE CONSISTENT WITH THE STATEWIDE DATA STRATEGY; 
 
 
 (II) DEVELOPING AND IMPLEMENTING DATA GOVERNANCE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES, INCLUDING THOSE PERTAINING TO DATA PRIVACY, WITHIN THE AGENCY DATA OFFICER&#39;S UNIT OF STATE GOVERNMENT THAT ENSURE COMPLIANCE WITH THE POLICIES ESTABLISHED BY THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER AND WITH LAWS, REGULATIONS, AND POLICIES; 
 
 
 (III) OVERSEEING DATA QUALITY INITIATIVES AND ENSURING DATA ACCURACY, RELIABILITY, ACCESSIBILITY, AND RETENTION OF STATE DATA THAT ARE CONSISTENT WITH STANDARDS AND GUIDANCE ESTABLISHED BY THE OFFICE AND IN COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS, REGULATIONS, AND POLICIES; 
 
 
 (IV) FACILITATING DATA SHARING WITHIN AND BETWEEN UNITS OF STATE GOVERNMENT AND WITH EXTERNAL PARTNERS THAT IS CONSISTENT WITH STANDARDS AND GUIDANCE ESTABLISHED BY THE OFFICE AND IN COMPLIANCE WITH EXISTING LAWS, REGULATIONS, AND POLICIES; 
 
 
 (V) ASSISTING WITH FACILITATING PARTICIPATION OF STATE EMPLOYEES IN DATA TRAINING PROVIDED BY THE OFFICE; 
 
 
 (VI) ADOPTING A DATA PRIVACY GOVERNANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT PROGRAM AND IMPLEMENTATION OF REASONABLE PRIVACY PROCEDURES AND PRACTICES, CONSISTENT WITH EXISTING LAWS, REGULATIONS, AND POLICIES, AND BEST PRACTICES ESTABLISHED BY THE OFFICE, TO ENSURE THAT CONFIDENTIALITY, INTEGRITY, AND AVAILABILITY OF ALL PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION ARE MAINTAINED; 
 
 
 (VII) REDUCING THE AMOUNT OF PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION COLLECTED AND RETAINED TO ONLY THAT NECESSARY FOR THE PROPER PERFORMANCE OF THE AUTHORIZED FUNCTIONS OF THE UNIT OF STATE GOVERNMENT; 
 
 
 (VIII) REPORTING TO THE OFFICE AT INTERVALS ESTABLISHED BY THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER ON DATA - RELATED ACTIVITIES, INCLUDING COMPLIANCE WITH STATEWIDE DATA STRATEGY AND POLICIES, USING QUANTIFIABLE METRICS; AND 
 
 
 (IX) MAKING AVAILABLE TO THE STATE CHIEF DATA OFFICER ANY DATA OR METADATA REQUESTED.

 (i) (1) &quot;Open data&quot; means data that, consistent with any applicable laws, rules, regulations, ordinances, resolutions, policies, or other restrictions including requirements or rights associated with the data, a State entity: 
 (i) has collected; and 
 (ii) is permitted, required, or able to make available to the public. 
 (2) &quot;Open data&quot; includes contractual or other legal orders, restrictions, or requirements. 
 (3) &quot;Open data&quot; does not include data that if made public would: 
 (i) violate another law or regulation that prohibits the data from being made public; 
 (ii) endanger the public health, safety, or welfare; 
 (iii) hinder the operation of government, including criminal and civil investigations; 
 (iv) impose an undue financial, operational, or administrative burden on a State entity; or 
 (v) disclose proprietary or confidential information. 
 (j) &quot;Open data portal&quot; means a mapping and geographic information systems portal or data portal. 
 (k) &quot;State entity&quot; means a department, a board, a commission, an agency, or a subunit in the Executive Branch of State government.
