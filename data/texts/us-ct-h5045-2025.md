 AN ACT CONCERNING UNLAWFUL DISSEMINATION OF INTIMATE IMAGES THAT ARE DIGITALLY ALTERED OR CREATED THROUGH THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE. 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened: 
 
 That titles 53 and 53a of the general statutes be amended to criminalize the unauthorized creation and distribution of digitally manipulated intimate images. 
 
 Statement of Purpose: 
 
 To criminalize the nonconsensual creation and dissemination of &quot;deep fake&quot; pornography.
