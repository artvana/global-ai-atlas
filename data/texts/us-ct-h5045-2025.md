General Assembly                    Proposed Bill No. 5045
      January Session, 2025              LCO No. 865


    Referred to Committee on JUDICIARY

    Introduced by:
     REP. CARPINO, 32nd Dist.




 AN ACT CONCERNING UNLAWFUL DISSEMINATION OF INTIMATE
 IMAGES THAT ARE DIGITALLY ALTERED OR CREATED THROUGH
 THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That titles 53 and 53a of the general statutes be amended to
2      criminalize the unauthorized creation and distribution of digitally
3      manipulated intimate images.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To criminalize the nonconsensual creation and dissemination of "deep
       fake" pornography.
