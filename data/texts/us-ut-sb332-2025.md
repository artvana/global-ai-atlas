 Artificial Intelligence Revisions 
 
 2025 GENERAL SESSION 
 STATE OF UTAH 
 
 Chief Sponsor: Kirk A. Cullimore

 LONG TITLE 
 
 
 General Description: 
 
 This bill extends the repeal date of the Artificial Intelligence Policy Act. 
 
 
 
 Highlighted Provisions: 
 
 This bill: 
 &bull;extends the repeal date of the Artificial Intelligence Policy Act to July 1, 2027. 
 
 Money Appropriated in this Bill: 
 
 None 
 
 Other Special Clauses: 
 
 This bill provides a special effective date. 
 
 Utah Code Sections Affected: 
 
 AMENDS: 
 
 63I-2-213 , as last amended by Laws of Utah 2024, Third Special Session, Chapter 5

 63I-2-213. Repeal dates: Title 13.

 Section 2. Effective Date. 
 
 
 This bill takes effect: 
 
 
 (1) except as provided in Subsection (2), May 7, 2025; or
