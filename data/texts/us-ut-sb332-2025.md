# Utah Senate Bill 332 — Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act Amendments 2025

**Senate Bill No. 332**

*Utah — March 25, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 332 amends the Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act (UAIPA), originally established by SB 149 (2024) and previously amended by HB 452 (2024), making further refinements to mental health chatbot regulation, updating generative AI disclosure requirements, and extending the UAIPA's sunset date.

### Mental Health Chatbot Amendments

The bill further amends UAIPA provisions governing conversational AI systems used in mental health contexts. These amendments refine the definition of covered mental health AI services and clarify obligations for operators of AI chatbots that provide mental health support or guidance, including disclosure requirements and safety protocol mandates.

### Generative AI Disclosure Revisions

SB 332 revises the UAIPA's disclosure requirements for generative AI in consumer transactions, updating definitions and the scope of when disclosures are required. The amendments provide additional clarity on what constitutes a "consumer transaction" involving generative AI and what disclosure language is sufficient.

### Sunset Date Extension

The bill extends the UAIPA's repeal/sunset date by two additional years, giving the Utah Legislature additional time to evaluate the law's effectiveness and develop a permanent, more comprehensive AI governance framework before the temporary law expires.

### Updated Definitions and Scope

SB 332 includes scope clarifications and updated definitions for key terms in the UAIPA, including refinements to the definitions of "generative AI," "conversational AI," and "operator," to address interpretive ambiguities that arose in the first year of the law's operation.

### Effective Date

The act takes effect May 7, 2025.
