 HOUSE BILL 712 
 
 By: Delegate Grammer 
 
 Introduced and read first time: February 2, 2026 
 Assigned to: Judiciary 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Civil Actions - Product Liability - Artificial Intelligence Systems 
 
 FOR the purpose of establishing a cause of action against a developer of a certain artificial intelligence system for defective design, failure to provide adequate instruction or warning, and breach of express warranty; establishing that a deployer of a certain artificial intelligence system may be sued in lieu of a developer under certain circumstances; establishing a certain rebuttable presumption that an artificial intelligence system is not dangerous or defective if a developer engaged in certain testing and evaluation procedures, mitigated and disclosed foreseeable risks, and, under certain circumstances, considered and mitigated risks to minors; establishing a certain rebuttable presumption in an action against a deployer that an artificial intelligence system is not dangerous or defective if the deployer implemented and adhered to a certain risk management policy; authorizing the Attorney General to bring an action against a developer or deployer of an artificial intelligence system for harm caused by a dangerous or defective product; establishing that contributory negligence is not a defense to an action under this Act and that recovery by a plaintiff shall be reduced by the percentage of fault attributable to the plaintiff; establishing that a developer and a deployer may be held jointly and severally liable in an action under this Act; and generally relating to artificial intelligence system product liability. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - Courts and Judicial Proceedings 
 Section 3-2701 through 3-2706 to be under the new subtitle &quot;Subtitle 27. Artificial Intelligence Systems Product Liability&quot; 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2020 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 
 
 Preamble 
 WHEREAS, Artificial intelligence systems are products that shift decision-making power and responsibility away from persons to software-based systems, often without direct human oversight; and 
 WHEREAS, While this technology provides great benefits, the deployment of these products has resulted in measurable harm to individuals and businesses; and 
 WHEREAS, Developers of high-impact artificial intelligence systems have an obligation to make such products safe when used in reasonably foreseeable ways; and 
 WHEREAS, Deployers of these products also have an obligation to ensure that these products are used in a way that does not materially affect an individual&#39;s rights; and 
 WHEREAS, Maryland&#39;s laws on artificial intelligence systems need to be expanded to better protect Marylanders from harm; now, therefore, 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Courts and Judicial Proceedings 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 27. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS PRODUCT LIABILITY. 
 
 
 3-2701. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM&quot; MEANS AN ENGINEERED OR MACHINE-BASED SYSTEM WITH VARYING LEVELS OF AUTONOMY THAT CAN, FOR EXPLICIT OR IMPLICIT OBJECTIVES, INFER FROM THE INPUTS IT RECEIVES HOW TO GENERATE OUTPUTS THAT CAN INFLUENCE PHYSICAL OR VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS. 
 
 
 (C) &quot;CONSEQUENTIAL DECISION&quot; MEANS A DECISION THAT HAS A LEGAL OR SIMILARLY SIGNIFICANT EFFECT ON AN INDIVIDUAL&#39;S ACCESS TO THE CRIMINAL JUSTICE SYSTEM, HOUSING, EMPLOYMENT, CREDIT, EDUCATION, HEALTH CARE, OR INSURANCE. 
 
 
 (D) (1) &quot;DEPLOYER&quot; MEANS A PERSON, INCLUDING A DEVELOPER, THAT USES OR OPERATES AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM FOR USE BY THE DEPLOYER OR THIRD PARTIES. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;DEPLOYER&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE AN INDIVIDUAL OR SMALL BUSINESS THAT HAS FEWER THAN: 
 
 
 (I) 20 EMPLOYEES; OR 
 
 
 (II) 10,000 USERS OF ITS PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (E) (1) &quot;DEVELOPER&quot; MEANS A PERSON THAT DESIGNS, CODES, PRODUCES, OWNS, OR SUBSTANTIALLY MODIFIES AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM FOR USE BY A DEVELOPER OR FOR USE BY THIRD PARTIES. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;DEVELOPER&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE A PERSON THAT USES AN OPEN SOURCE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM AND DOES NOT SUBSTANTIALLY MODIFY THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM. 
 
 
 (F) &quot;DESIGN&quot; MEANS THE INTENDED OR KNOWN PHYSICAL AND MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS OF A PRODUCT, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 (1) ANY INTENDED OR KNOWN FORMULATION OR CONTENT OF THE PRODUCT; AND 
 
 
 (2) THE USUAL RESULT OF THE INTENDED METHOD OF DEVELOPMENT OR OTHER PROCESS USED TO PRODUCE THE PRODUCT, INCLUDING UNEXPECTED SKILLS OR BEHAVIORS THAT APPEAR IN A PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;EXPRESS WARRANTY&quot; MEANS ANY MATERIAL, POSITIVE STATEMENT, AFFIRMATION OF FACT, PROMISE, OR DESCRIPTION RELATING TO A PRODUCT, INCLUDING A SAMPLE OR MODEL OF A PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (H) &quot;GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM&quot; MEANS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM THAT CAN GENERATE DERIVED SYNTHETIC CONTENT, SUCH AS TEXT, IMAGES, VIDEO, AND AUDIO, THAT EMULATES THE STRUCTURE AND CHARACTERISTICS OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM&#39;S TRAINING DATA. 
 
 
 (I) &quot;HARM&quot; MEANS: 
 
 
 (1) DAMAGE TO PROPERTY OTHER THAN THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM ITSELF; 
 
 
 (2) PERSONAL PHYSICAL, FINANCIAL, OR REPUTATIONAL INJURY, ILLNESS, OR DEATH; 
 
 
 (3) MENTAL OR PHYSIOLOGICAL ANGUISH, EMOTIONAL HARM, OR DISTORTION OF A PERSON&#39;S BEHAVIOR THAT WOULD BE HIGHLY OFFENSIVE TO A REASONABLE PERSON; OR 
 
 
 (4) ANY LOSS OF CONSORTIUM OR SERVICES, OR OTHER LOSS DERIVING FROM ANY TYPE OF HARM DESCRIBED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (J) &quot;HIGH-IMPACT ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM&quot; MEANS ANY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM, REGARDLESS OF THE NUMBER OF PARAMETERS AND SUPERVISION STRUCTURE, THAT: 
 
 
 (1) IS USED, OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLY MAY BE USED, AS A CONTROLLING FACTOR IN MAKING CONSEQUENTIAL DECISIONS; 
 
 
 (2) IS USED, OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLY MAY BE USED, TO CATEGORIZE GROUPS OF PERSONS BY PROTECTED CHARACTERISTICS, SUCH AS RACE, ETHNIC ORIGIN, OR RELIGIOUS BELIEF; 
 
 
 (3) IS USED, OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLY MAY BE USED, IN THE DIRECT MANAGEMENT OR OPERATION OF CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE; 
 
 
 (4) IS USED, OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLY MAY BE USED, IN A VEHICLE, IN A MEDICAL DEVICE, OR IN THE SAFETY SYSTEM OF A VEHICLE OR MEDICAL DEVICE; 
 
 
 (5) IS USED, OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLY MAY BE USED, TO ENGAGE IN A SYNTHETIC RELATIONSHIP; OR 
 
 
 (6) EXHIBITS, OR COULD BE EASILY MODIFIED TO EXHIBIT, HIGH LEVELS OF PERFORMANCE AT TASKS THAT POSE A SERIOUS RISK TO ECONOMIC SECURITY OR PUBLIC HEALTH OR SAFETY. 
 
 
 (K) (1) &quot;MATERIAL FACT&quot; MEANS ANY SPECIFIC CHARACTERISTIC OR QUALITY OF THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;MATERIAL FACT&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE A GENERAL OPINION ABOUT THE PRODUCT OR ITS QUALITY. 
 
 
 (L) &quot;METHOD OF DEVELOPMENT&quot; MEANS THE SELECTION OF TRAINING DATA FOR THE PRODUCT, INCLUDING THE TRAINING, TESTING, AUDITING, AND FINE-TUNING OF THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (M) &quot;PERSON&quot; MEANS ANY INDIVIDUAL, CORPORATION, COMPANY, ASSOCIATION, FIRM, PARTNERSHIP, SOCIETY, JOINT STOCK COMPANY, OR ANY OTHER ENTITY, INCLUDING ANY GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY OR UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATION OF PERSONS. 
 
 
 (N) &quot;PRODUCT&quot; MEANS A HIGH -IMPACT ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM OR A GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM. 
 
 
 (O) &quot;SYNTHETIC RELATIONSHIP&quot; MEANS A SERIES OF INTERACTIONS BETWEEN AN INDIVIDUAL AND AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM THAT MIMICS HUMAN INTERACTION AND EMOTIONAL RESPONSES. 
 
 
 3-2702. 
 
 
 THIS SUBTITLE DOES NOT APPLY TO ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS USED ONLY FOR PEER-REVIEWED SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH. 
 
 
 3-2703. 
 
 
 (A) A PERSON MAY BRING AN ACTION IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS SECTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER OR DEPLOYER FOR HARM CAUSED BY A PRODUCT THAT IS DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE. 
 
 
 (B) IN AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER ALLEGING HARM CAUSED BY A DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCT DUE TO THE DEFECTIVE DESIGN OF THE PRODUCT, THE PLAINTIFF SHALL PROVE BY A PREPONDERANCE OF THE EVIDENCE THAT THE HARM WAS PROXIMATELY CAUSED BY THE FAILURE OF THE DEVELOPER TO EXERCISE REASONABLE CARE AND THAT, AT THE TIME THE PRODUCT LEFT THE DEVELOPER&#39;S CONTROL: 
 
 
 (1) THE DEVELOPER KNEW OR REASONABLY SHOULD HAVE KNOWN OF THE DESIGN DEFECT THAT CAUSED THE HARM; 
 
 
 (2) THE DEVELOPER ACCOUNTED FOR INTENDED USES AND REASONABLY FORESEEABLE UNINTENDED USES OF THE PRODUCT; AND 
 
 
 (3) A TECHNOLOGICALLY FEASIBLE AND PRACTICAL ALTERNATIVE DESIGN EXISTED THAT WOULD HAVE MITIGATED OR AVOIDED THE FORESEEABLE RISK OF HARM WITHOUT SIGNIFICANTLY IMPAIRING THE INTENDED USE OF THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (C) (1) IN THIS SUBSECTION, &quot;ADEQUATE INSTRUCTION OR WARNING&quot; MEANS AN INSTRUCTION OR A WARNING REGARDING A DANGER THAT WOULD SUFFICIENTLY INFORM A REASONABLY PRUDENT PERSON ON THE SAFE USE AND DANGERS OF A PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (2) IN AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER ALLEGING HARM CAUSED BY A DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCT DUE TO A FAILURE TO PROVIDE ADEQUATE INSTRUCTION OR WARNING, A PLAINTIFF SHALL PROVE BY A PREPONDERANCE OF THE EVIDENCE THAT THE HARM WAS PROXIMATELY CAUSED BY THE FAILURE TO PROVIDE ADEQUATE INSTRUCTION OR WARNING AND THAT, AT THE TIME THE PRODUCT LEFT THE DEVELOPER&#39;S CONTROL, THE DEVELOPER KNEW OR REASONABLY SHOULD HAVE KNOWN OF THE DANGER POSED BY THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (3) IT IS A DEFENSE TO AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER FOR HARM CAUSED BY A FAILURE TO INSTRUCT OR WARN THAT: 
 
 
 (I) THE USER OR CONSUMER OF THE PRODUCT WAS AT LEAST 17 YEARS OLD; AND 
 
 
 (II) THE PRODUCT DANGER WAS KNOWN OR OPEN AND OBVIOUS TO THE USER OR CONSUMER OF THE PRODUCT, OR SHOULD HAVE BEEN KNOWN OR OPEN AND OBVIOUS TO THE USER OR CONSUMER OF THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (D) IN AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER ALLEGING HARM CAUSED BY A DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCT DUE TO THE FAILURE OF THE PRODUCT TO CONFORM TO AN EXPRESS WARRANTY, THE PLAINTIFF SHALL PROVE BY A PREPONDERANCE OF THE EVIDENCE THAT THE HARM WAS PROXIMATELY CAUSED BY THE FAILURE OF THE DEVELOPER TO EXERCISE REASONABLE CARE AND THAT: 
 
 
 (1) THE PLAINTIFF REASONABLY RELIED ON AN EXPRESS WARRANTY MADE BY A DEVELOPER ABOUT A MATERIAL FACT CONCERNING THE SAFETY OF THE PRODUCT; AND 
 
 
 (2) (I) 1. THE PRODUCT FAILED TO CONFORM TO THE EXPRESS WARRANTY; AND 
 
 
 2. THE FAILURE OF THE PRODUCT TO CONFORM TO THE EXPRESS WARRANTY RESULTED IN THE HARM; OR 
 
 
 (II) 1. THE EXPRESS WARRANTY WAS UNTRUE; AND 
 
 
 2. HAD THE EXPRESS WARRANTY BEEN TRUE, THE HARM WOULD NOT HAVE OCCURRED. 
 
 
 (E) (1) A DEPLOYER MAY BE HELD LIABLE IN PLACE OF A DEVELOPER IN AN ACTION UNDER THIS SECTION IF THE DEPLOYER: 
 
 
 (I) MADE A MATERIAL AND SUBSTANTIAL CHANGE TO THE PRODUCT; OR 
 
 
 (II) INTENTIONALLY MISUSED THE PRODUCT CONTRARY TO AN EXPRESS WARRANTY AND THAT MISUSE WAS THE PROXIMATE CAUSE OF HARM SUFFERED BY A PLAINTIFF. 
 
 
 (2) (I) USE OF A PRODUCT THAT IS INTENDED BY THE DEVELOPER OF THE PRODUCT DOES NOT CONSTITUTE A MISUSE OR MATERIAL OR SUBSTANTIAL CHANGE OF THE PRODUCT UNDER THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (II) IF A DEVELOPER DOES NOT SPECIFY AN INTENDED USE FOR THE PRODUCT, INTENDED USE MAY BE INFERRED BY THE TARGETED MARKET AND MANNER OF DISTRIBUTION OF THE PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (3) A DEPLOYER LICENSING A PRODUCT MAY NOT BE HELD LIABLE TO A PLAINTIFF FOR A VIOLATION UNDER THIS SECTION SOLELY BY REASON OF OWNERSHIP OR USE OF A PRODUCT. 
 
 
 (F) IN AN ACTION BROUGHT UNDER THIS SECTION, THERE IS A REBUTTABLE PRESUMPTION THAT A PRODUCT IS NOT DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE IF: 
 
 
 (1) IN AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER, THE DEVELOPER: 
 
 
 (I) CONDUCTED A DOCUMENTED TESTING, EVALUATION, VERIFICATION, VALIDATION, AND AUDITING OF THE PRODUCT CONSISTENT WITH INDUSTRY BEST PRACTICES; 
 
 
 (II) MITIGATED FORESEEABLE RISKS TO THE EXTENT POSSIBLE; 
 
 
 (III) DISCLOSED FORESEEABLE RISKS AND MITIGATION TACTICS DIRECTLY TO DEPLOYERS AND CONSUMERS; AND 
 
 
 (IV) IF THE PRODUCT IS DESIGNED FOR OR REASONABLY LIKELY TO BE USED BY MINORS, CONSIDERED AND MITIGATED FORESEEABLE RISKS TO MINORS USING THE PRODUCT, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 1. THE IMPACT OF THE PRODUCT ON COGNITIVE AND EMOTIONAL DEVELOPMENT; 
 
 
 2. THE IMPLEMENTATION OF AGE AND CONTENT RESTRICTIONS; AND 
 
 
 3. PROVIDING CLEAR, ACCESSIBLE DISCLOSURES TO DEPLOYERS, CONSUMERS, AND GUARDIANS ABOUT POTENTIAL RISKS; OR 
 
 
 (2) IN AN ACTION AGAINST A DEPLOYER, THE DEPLOYER IMPLEMENTED AND ADHERED TO A RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY THAT: 
 
 
 (I) SPECIFIES THE POLICY BY WHICH THE DEPLOYER WILL IDENTIFY, DOCUMENT, AND MITIGATE ANY RISK, ESPECIALLY TO MINOR USERS, THAT IS REASONABLY FORESEEABLE; 
 
 
 (II) IS CONSISTENT WITH INDUSTRY BEST PRACTICES; 
 
 
 (III) IS REASONABLE IN LIGHT OF: 
 
 
 1. THE SIZE AND COMPLEXITY OF THE DEPLOYER; 
 
 
 2. THE NATURE AND SCOPE OF THE PRODUCT, INCLUDING THE INTENDED USES AND UNINTENDED USES, AS WELL AS ANY MODIFICATION MADE TO THE SYSTEM BY THE DEPLOYER; AND 
 
 
 3. THE DATA THAT THE SYSTEM, ONCE DEPLOYED, PROCESSES AS INPUTS; AND 
 
 
 (IV) IS ELECTRONICALLY AVAILABLE TO ITS EMPLOYEES AND TO THE ATTORNEY GENERAL ON REQUEST. 
 
 
 3-2704. 
 
 
 (A) THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY BRING AN ACTION AGAINST A DEVELOPER OR A DEPLOYER FOR HARM CAUSED BY A DANGEROUS OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCT DESCRIBED IN &sect; 3-2703 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (B) (1) IN AN ACTION BROUGHT UNDER THIS SECTION, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY OBTAIN: 
 
 
 (I) INJUNCTIVE RELIEF; 
 
 
 (II) A CIVIL PENALTY; 
 
 
 (III) DAMAGES, RESTITUTION, OR OTHER COMPENSATION ON BEHALF OF AN INDIVIDUAL WHO HAS BEEN HARMED BY AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM; AND 
 
 
 (IV) ANY OTHER RELIEF THAT THE ATTORNEY GENERAL CONSIDERS APPROPRIATE. 
 
 
 (2) IF THE USE OF THE SOFTWARE CAUSES PERSONAL INJURY OR DEATH, A CIVIL PENALTY IMPOSED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION MAY NOT EXCEED THE APPLICABLE LIMITATION ON NONECONOMIC DAMAGES UNDER &sect; 3-2A-09 OF THIS TITLE. 
 
 
 (C) IN AN ACTION BROUGHT BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL UNDER THIS SECTION, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL IS ENTITLED TO RECOVER THE COSTS OF THE ACTION FOR THE USE OF THE STATE. 
 
 
 (D) BEFORE BRINGING AN ACTION UNDER THIS SECTION SEEKING INJUNCTIVE RELIEF, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL PROVIDE WRITTEN NOTICE TO A DEVELOPER OR DEPLOYER AT LEAST 30 DAYS BEFORE COMMENCEMENT OF THE ACTION THAT INCLUDES A DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE BASIS OF THE CLAIM AND, WHEN POSSIBLE, THE OPPORTUNITY TO CURE ANY CONDITION GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM. 
 
 
 3-2705. 
 
 
 (A) (1) CONTRIBUTORY NEGLIGENCE IS NOT A DEFENSE TO AN ACTION BROUGHT UNDER THIS SUBTITLE AND RECOVERY BY A PLAINTIFF MAY NOT BE BARRED REGARDLESS OF THE PLAINTIFF&#39;S DEGREE OF FAULT. 
 
 
 (2) RECOVERY BY A PLAINTIFF UNDER THIS SUBTITLE SHALL BE REDUCED IN PROPORTION TO THE PERCENTAGE OF FAULT ATTRIBUTABLE TO THE PLAINTIFF. 
 
 
 (B) A DEVELOPER AND A DEPLOYER MAY BE HELD JOINTLY AND SEVERALLY LIABLE FOR THE PORTION OF HARM TO THE PLAINTIFF ATTRIBUTED TO THE DEVELOPER AND DEPLOYER. 
 
 
 (C) IT IS NOT A DEFENSE TO AN ACTION BROUGHT UNDER THIS SUBTITLE THAT AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM AUTONOMOUSLY CAUSED THE HARM TO THE PLAINTIFF. 
 
 
 3-2706. 
 
 
 THIS SUBTITLE: 
 
 
 (1) SUPPLEMENTS ANY COMMON LAW TORT LIABILITY CAUSE OF ACTION AND ANY STATE PRODUCT LIABILITY LAWS; AND 
 
 
 (2) MAY NOT BE INTERPRETED TO PROHIBIT ANY PRODUCT LIABILITY CAUSE OF ACTION INVOLVING A PRODUCT BROUGHT UNDER A DIFFERENT CLAIM UNDER PRODUCT LIABILITY COMMON LAW OR STATUTE. 
 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect October 1, 2026.
