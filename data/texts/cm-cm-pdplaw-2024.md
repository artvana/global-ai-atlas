PART VII
MISCELLANEOUS, TRANSITIONAL AND FINAL PROVISIONS

SECTION 72: The processing of personal data relating, in particular, to security, defence,
health, justice and civil status shall, as and when necessary, be governed, by separate
instruments.

SECTION 73: Natural or legal persons responsible for the processing of personal data
shall have 18 (eighteen) months from the date of enactment of this law to comply with its
provisions.

SECTION 74: The conditions for the implementation of this law shall be laid down, as and
when necessary, in separate instruments.
