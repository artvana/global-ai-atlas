 A BILL TO BE ENTITLED 
 AN ACT 
 relating to sexually explicit visual material. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS: 
 
 SECTION 1. The heading to Section 21.165, Penal Code, is amended to read as follows: 
 Sec. 21.165. UNLAWFUL PRODUCTION OR DISTRIBUTION OF CERTAIN SEXUALLY EXPLICIT VISUAL MATERIAL 
 VIDEOS . 
 SECTION 2. Sections 21.165 (a) and (b), Penal Code, are amended to read as follows: 
 (a) In this section: 
 (1) &quot;Deep fake visual material 
 video &quot; means visual material 
 a video , created with the intent to deceive, that appears to depict a real person performing an action that did not occur in reality. 
 (2) &quot;Intimate parts&quot; and &quot;sexual conduct&quot; have the meanings assigned by Section 21.16. 
 
 (3) &quot;Visual material&quot; has the meaning assigned by Section 43.26(b)(3), Penal Code. 
 
 (b) A person commits an offense if, without the effective consent of the person appearing to be depicted, the person knowingly produces or distributes by electronic means a deep fake visual material 
 video that appears to depict the person with the person&#39;s intimate parts exposed or engaged in sexual conduct. 
 SECTION 3. This Act takes effect September 1, 2025.
