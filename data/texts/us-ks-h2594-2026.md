As Amended by House Committee
     Session of 2026

                            HOUSE BILL No. 2594
                              By Committee on Judiciary
      Requested by Steve Kearney on behalf of Kansas County and District Attorneys
                                     Association
                                         1-29


 1   AN ACT concerning crimes, punishment and criminal procedure; relating
 2     to blackmail; modifying the elements related to threatened
 3     dissemination of any videotape, photograph, film or image of another
 4     identifiable person who is nude or engaged in sexual activity by
 5     removing changing the connection to breach of privacy and describing
 6     the content of the videotape, photograph, film or image; amending
 7     K.S.A. 21-5428 and repealing the existing section.
 8
 9   Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Kansas:
10      Section 1. K.S.A. 21-5428 is hereby amended to read as follows: 21-
11   5428. (a) Blackmail is intentionally gaining or attempting to gain anything
12   of value or compelling or attempting to compel another to act against such
13   person's will, excluding sexual contact, sexual intercourse or conduct
14   that is of a sexual nature, by threatening to:
15      (1) Communicate accusations or statements about any person that
16   would subject such person or any other person to public ridicule, contempt
17   or degradation; or
18      (2) disseminate any videotape, photograph, film or image obtained in
19   violation of K.S.A. 21-6101(a)(6) or (a)(8), and amendments thereto,
20   video or other recording:
21      (A) Obtained in violation of K.S.A. 21-6101(a)(6), and
22   amendments thereto; or
23      (B) of another identifiable person who is nude or engaged in sexual
24   activity, including, but not limited to, any videotape, photograph, film or
25   image, video or other recording that has been created, in whole or in
26   part, altered or modified by artificial intelligence or any digital means to
27   appear to depict or purport to depict such identifiable person, regardless
28   of whether such identifiable person was involved in the creation of the
29   original image, video or other recording.
30      (b) Blackmail as defined in:
31      (1) Subsection (a)(1) is a severity level 7, nonperson felony; and
32      (2) subsection (a)(2) is a severity level 4, person felony.
33      Sec. 2. K.S.A. 21-5428 is hereby repealed.
34      Sec. 3. This act shall take effect and be in force from and after its
