# Shenzhen Special Economic Zone Artificial Intelligence Industry Promotion Regulation

**Adopted by:** Shenzhen Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee  
**Date of Adoption:** September 7, 2022 (at the 14th Meeting of the 7th Shenzhen Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee)  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2022  

---

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1:** These Regulations are formulated in order to promote the development of the artificial intelligence industry in the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone, accelerate the establishment of Shenzhen as a globally influential AI innovation and development hub, and support the construction of a pilot demonstration zone for socialism with Chinese characteristics, in accordance with relevant national laws and regulations and in combination with the actual conditions of this city.

**Article 2:** These Regulations apply to the research and development, product and service provision, talent development, and industry promotion activities related to artificial intelligence carried out within the administrative area of this city.

**Article 3:** The development of the AI industry shall follow the principles of innovation-driven development, integration and application, talent priority, safe and orderly development, and open cooperation.

The promotion of AI industry development shall adhere to the combination of government guidance and market operation, encourage open and inclusive competition, and prevent monopoly and unfair competition.

**Article 4:** The municipal people's government shall strengthen leadership over the development of the AI industry, incorporate AI development into the city's overall planning for economic and social development and scientific and technological innovation, establish cross-department coordination mechanisms for major AI development matters, and formulate and implement special AI industry development plans.

The municipal development and reform department shall be responsible for organizing the implementation of these Regulations, coordinating the promotion of AI industry development, and supervising and administering the implementation of AI industry policies. The municipal science and technology, industry and information technology, market supervision, human resources, and other relevant departments shall fulfill their respective responsibilities in the promotion and governance of the AI industry.

**Article 5:** District people's governments shall vigorously support and promote the development of the AI industry within their respective districts according to the requirements of the municipal people's government's overall plans and policy directions, and shall specifically support the construction of AI industry parks, innovation clusters, and application demonstration projects.

---

## Chapter II: Technology Innovation

**Article 6:** The municipal people's government shall increase support for basic AI research and frontier technology exploration, and shall implement major AI technology research programs to address key core technologies and "bottleneck" challenges, including:

(1) Core algorithms, including reinforcement learning, federated learning, neural architecture search, and interpretable AI;

(2) AI chips and specialized computing hardware;

(3) Open-source AI software frameworks and development tools;

(4) AI security and trustworthiness technologies;

(5) AI combined with frontier technologies such as quantum computing and advanced sensing.

**Article 7:** The city shall support enterprises, universities, and research institutions in building high-level AI research platforms, including national-level and municipal-level key laboratories, technology innovation centers, and engineering research centers. The city shall implement a project management mechanism that allows major AI research institutions greater autonomy in using research funding.

**Article 8:** The city shall promote open innovation in the AI industry by:

(1) Supporting leading enterprises in opening AI platforms, tools, and computing resources to external developers, start-ups, and researchers;

(2) Building AI open-source communities and supporting AI developers in contributing to international open-source AI projects;

(3) Establishing AI innovation competitions, hackathons, and application challenges to identify and support promising AI innovations;

(4) Promoting sharing and exchange of AI models, datasets, and development tools among enterprises and research institutions.

**Article 9:** The city shall protect AI-related intellectual property and encourage innovation in AI intellectual property protection, including:

(1) Supporting AI enterprises in applying for patents, including software patents and technical trade secrets, both domestically and internationally;

(2) Providing guidance and legal support services to AI enterprises for IP protection;

(3) Actively participating in the development of AI-related intellectual property standards at the national and international levels.

---

## Chapter III: Data Resources

**Article 10:** The city shall establish an AI data resource development system, promoting the aggregation, sharing, and application of high-quality data resources for AI development.

The city shall formulate a catalogue of public data resources that may be opened for AI purposes, and shall promote the orderly opening of public data resources from government agencies, public utilities, and state-owned enterprises in accordance with data security and personal information protection requirements.

**Article 11:** The city shall establish a data exchange and trading platform, providing services for the compliant trading of data and data products. Data trading shall comply with applicable laws and regulations, including the Data Security Law, the Personal Information Protection Law, and relevant national standards for data security.

The city shall develop and promote data element rights certification standards, exploring models for quantifying and protecting data rights to support the development of a healthy data market.

**Article 12:** The city shall promote the development of high-quality AI training datasets. The city shall support the construction of public AI training data sets in key domains such as smart city management, industrial manufacturing, medical health, autonomous transportation, and financial services, and shall make such datasets available to qualified enterprises and research institutions under appropriate conditions.

**Article 13:** Enterprises and institutions that contribute high-quality data to public AI data platforms and participate in data exchange activities shall be given corresponding recognition and support by the city, in accordance with relevant regulations.

**Article 14:** The city shall promote the development of federated learning and privacy-preserving computation technologies to enable AI model training on distributed data without requiring the pooling of raw data, and shall support the application of such technologies in regulated sectors where data sharing is subject to legal constraints.

---

## Chapter IV: Computing Power Infrastructure

**Article 15:** The city shall prioritize the construction of advanced AI computing infrastructure, including:

(1) Large-scale intelligent computing centers equipped with AI-optimized hardware;

(2) Distributed edge computing infrastructure to support real-time AI inference applications;

(3) A unified computing resource scheduling and management platform to improve the utilization efficiency of computing resources across the city.

**Article 16:** The city shall support the research and development and industrialization of domestic AI chips and computing hardware, and shall implement policies to support the development of the AI hardware supply chain. The city shall establish a procurement preference policy to encourage public institutions and state-owned enterprises to prioritize domestically developed AI hardware.

**Article 17:** The city shall optimize utility and infrastructure policies for AI computing facilities, including:

(1) Streamlining approval procedures for the construction of AI data centers;

(2) Providing priority access to power grid connections for large AI computing facilities;

(3) Encouraging the construction of energy-efficient AI data centers and incentivizing the adoption of green energy sources.

---

## Chapter V: Application and Demonstration

**Article 18:** The city shall promote the deep integration of AI in key application domains, including:

(1) **Smart city governance**: AI applications in traffic management, urban planning, emergency response, environmental monitoring, and government service delivery;

(2) **Industrial intelligence**: AI applications in intelligent manufacturing, predictive maintenance, quality inspection, logistics optimization, and supply chain management;

(3) **Digital finance**: AI applications in risk control, anti-fraud, intelligent customer service, credit assessment, and regulatory technology;

(4) **Smart healthcare**: AI applications in medical imaging, disease prediction, drug discovery, telemedicine, and smart hospital management;

(5) **Smart transportation**: AI applications in autonomous driving, intelligent transportation systems, smart ports, and logistics coordination;

(6) **Education and cultural industries**: AI applications in personalized education, intelligent tutoring systems, and creative content generation.

**Article 19:** The city shall establish AI industry application demonstration zones and shall designate specific areas for AI innovation testing, where policies and regulations may be applied flexibly in support of AI application experimentation under appropriate regulatory supervision, subject to national laws.

**Article 20:** The city shall promote cooperation between AI enterprises and traditional industry enterprises in AI application projects, and shall encourage the implementation of AI transformation projects in key manufacturing sectors. The city shall provide support including technical guidance, talent matching, and financing assistance to enterprises undertaking AI transformation.

**Article 21:** The city shall promote AI applications in public services, including education, medical care, elderly services, and community management. Government departments that deploy AI applications in public services shall ensure the equity, transparency, and fairness of such applications, and shall provide channels for affected individuals to seek explanations and contest AI-assisted decisions.

---

## Chapter VI: Open Cooperation

**Article 22:** The city shall actively promote international cooperation in AI research, development, and application, and shall encourage enterprises, universities, and research institutions to engage in joint research projects, talent exchange, and technical standards cooperation with international counterparts.

**Article 23:** The city shall support the hosting of international AI conferences, forums, and exhibitions, and shall build Shenzhen into a major venue for international AI exchange and collaboration. The city shall encourage AI enterprises to participate in international AI governance discussions and the development of international AI standards.

**Article 24:** The city shall optimize business environment policies to attract international AI enterprises, research institutions, and talent to establish operations in Shenzhen. The city shall establish dedicated facilitation services for internationally recruited AI talent and enterprises.

**Article 25:** The city shall support qualified AI enterprises in establishing overseas research and development centers, innovation outposts, and market development offices, and shall provide information, legal, and financial support services for AI enterprises seeking international expansion.

---

## Chapter VII: AI Ethics and Governance

**Article 26:** The city shall promote the responsible and safe development of the AI industry, and shall implement AI ethics and safety governance policies consistent with national requirements.

AI developers and deployers operating in the city are encouraged to:

(1) Adopt and implement AI ethics guidelines as part of their corporate governance frameworks;

(2) Establish ethics review processes for AI products and applications in high-stakes domains;

(3) Promote transparency and explainability in AI systems to the extent technically feasible;

(4) Implement fairness testing and bias mitigation procedures in AI development.

**Article 27:** The city shall support the development of AI safety and evaluation technologies, including:

(1) AI security testing methodologies and tools;

(2) AI robustness and reliability evaluation standards;

(3) AI fairness and bias detection tools;

(4) Trustworthy AI certification and evaluation services.

**Article 28:** AI developers and deployers in the city shall implement appropriate security measures to protect AI systems from unauthorized access, adversarial attacks, and data poisoning, and shall establish incident response mechanisms for AI security events.

**Article 29:** The city shall establish an AI industry complaint and dispute resolution mechanism. Individuals and enterprises that have disputes arising from AI product or service quality, AI-assisted decisions, or other AI-related matters may seek resolution through this mechanism.

---

## Chapter VIII: Support Policies

**Article 30:** The city shall provide financial support for the development of the AI industry through the following means:

(1) AI-specific items in the city's industrial development funds to support AI research and development, talent development, and application demonstration;

(2) Subsidies or grants for qualified AI enterprises for major AI research projects, platform construction, and international expansion;

(3) Tax preferences for AI enterprises in accordance with national and local tax regulations;

(4) Subsidized financing and loan guarantees for qualified small and medium-sized AI enterprises.

**Article 31:** The city shall implement talent attraction and development policies for the AI field, including:

(1) Offering residency registration facilitation for AI talent meeting specified qualifications;

(2) Providing housing subsidies, rental support, or priority access to talent housing for qualified AI professionals;

(3) Providing education support for the children of AI talent, including priority access to high-quality schools;

(4) Establishing AI talent recognition awards and other recognition mechanisms.

**Article 32:** The city shall support AI enterprises in accessing public procurement markets, including:

(1) Implementing preferential policies for domestically developed AI products in government procurement;

(2) Establishing demonstration programs for AI applications in public services;

(3) Supporting AI enterprises in participating in procurement tenders for smart city projects.

---

## Chapter IX: Legal Liability and Supervision

**Article 33:** Regulatory authorities shall conduct supervision and inspection of entities engaged in AI activities within the city in accordance with their respective responsibilities, and may take measures including requesting information, conducting on-site inspections, and issuing rectification orders for non-compliance.

**Article 34:** Where entities or individuals violate the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant regulatory authority shall impose penalties in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Where violations constitute crimes, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

---

## Chapter X: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 35:** For the purposes of these Regulations, "artificial intelligence" refers to theories, methods, technologies, and application systems that use computers or computer-controlled machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, including perception, learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, and language understanding.

**Article 36:** These Regulations take effect on November 1, 2022.
