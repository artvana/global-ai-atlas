# Jordan Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023
**Published in Official Gazette: September 17, 2023 | In force: March 17, 2024**

## Overview
Law No. 24 of 2023 regarding personal data protection in Jordan (PDPL) is the first data protection law of its kind in Jordan. The law consists of 25 articles and came into effect six months after its publication on March 17, 2024.

## Scope and Application
The provisions of the PDPL apply to all personal data within Jordan, regardless of when it was collected or processed, including data collected and processed before the PDPL's enactment.

## Consent Requirements
Processing of personal data is only permitted with the prior consent of the data subject, unless otherwise permitted by law. Consent must be:
- Clear and in writing
- With a specified period and purpose
- In an intelligible and easily accessible form using clear and plain language

## Data Controller Obligations
The data controller is required to implement appropriate measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and security of personal data in its possession or received from others.

## Data Subject Rights
Data subjects have the following rights:
- Right of information, review, and access to their personal data
- Right to erasure of data
- Right to object to processing and profiling if such activities are no longer necessary or are discriminatory

## Automated Decision-Making
Data subjects have the right to object to profiling if such activities are discriminatory.

## Enforcement
The law establishes a body within the Council of Ministers to oversee the PDPL's enforcement and imposes severe financial penalties on entities found to violate its provisions.
