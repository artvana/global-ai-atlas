# Measures for Labelling of Artificial Intelligence-Generated Synthetic Content

**Promulgating Entities:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC); Ministry of Industry and Information Technology; Ministry of Public Security; National Radio and Television Administration; State Film Administration  
**Promulgation Date:** 2025-03-14  
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01  
**Companion Standard:** Mandatory National Standard GB 45438-2025 on Technical Requirements for AI-Generated Content Labelling  

---

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1:** These Measures are formulated in accordance with the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Interim Measures for the Administration of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, for the purpose of regulating the labelling of artificial intelligence-generated synthetic content (hereinafter "AI-generated content"), protecting the lawful rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, and promoting the healthy development of AI technology applications.

**Article 2:** These Measures apply to the activities of organizations and individuals within the territory of the People's Republic of China that use artificial intelligence technology to generate or synthesize text, images, audio, video, virtual scenes, or other information content, and that provide such content to the public (hereinafter "service providers").

Where the state has separate provisions on labelling requirements for specific categories of information content, such as news and information services, film and television productions, and online audio-visual programs, those provisions shall also apply.

**Article 3:** For the purposes of these Measures, "AI-generated content" means content in the form of text, images, audio, video, virtual scenes, or combinations thereof that is generated, synthesized, edited, or enhanced in a material way by artificial intelligence technology, including but not limited to:

(1) Text content generated by large language models or other natural language processing technologies;

(2) Images generated or significantly altered by generative AI, image synthesis, or style transfer technologies;

(3) Audio content generated by voice synthesis, voice cloning, or other speech generation technologies;

(4) Video content generated by video synthesis, deepfake, or AI animation technologies;

(5) Virtual scenes and interactive content generated by AI-driven rendering or world simulation technologies.

**Article 4:** Service providers shall label AI-generated content in accordance with these Measures and the relevant requirements of mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025. Service providers bear the primary responsibility for ensuring that AI-generated content they make available to users is properly labelled.

**Article 5:** The state encourages the development of technically robust, interoperable, and user-friendly labelling technologies for AI-generated content, and promotes the establishment of unified technical standards and labelling systems that are compatible with international norms.

**Article 6:** The Cyberspace Administration of China is responsible for the overall coordination of the implementation and supervision of these Measures. Relevant departments including the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security, National Radio and Television Administration, and State Film Administration shall, within their respective scope of responsibilities, carry out supervision and administration of AI-generated content labelling activities.

---

## Chapter II: Explicit Labelling Requirements

**Article 7:** Service providers shall adopt explicit labelling (明示标识) for AI-generated content, ensuring that users can clearly and promptly identify whether the content was generated or significantly processed by artificial intelligence. Explicit labelling shall be:

(1) Prominently displayed — the label shall be visible and legible, placed in a position where users are likely to see it before engaging with the content;

(2) Clear in meaning — the label shall use plain language and standardized expressions such as "AI generated" (人工智能生成), "AI synthesized" (人工智能合成), or other expressions prescribed in GB 45438-2025;

(3) Accurate and not misleading — labels shall not be designed, placed, or worded in a manner that obscures or understates the AI-generated nature of the content.

**Article 8:** For text content generated by AI, the following labelling requirements apply:

(1) Short-form text of one hundred Chinese characters or fewer that is entirely AI-generated shall bear a clear label at the beginning or end of the text;

(2) Long-form text that is substantially AI-generated shall bear a label at the beginning, and may additionally include section-level labels where appropriate;

(3) AI-assisted editing that materially alters the meaning or factual content of original human-authored text shall also require labelling.

**Article 9:** For image content generated or substantially altered by AI, the following labelling requirements apply:

(1) The label shall appear as an overlay or caption attached to the image in a manner that is clearly visible under normal viewing conditions;

(2) Where AI-generated images are embedded in articles, web pages, or multimedia content, labels shall be attached to each individual image;

(3) AI-generated images that realistically depict real persons, real events, or real locations shall be subject to enhanced labelling requirements, including a visible watermark or persistent on-screen label.

**Article 10:** For audio content generated or significantly altered by AI, including voice synthesis and voice cloning, the following labelling requirements apply:

(1) An explicit audio announcement or caption at the beginning of the audio content;

(2) Where the AI-generated voice closely resembles or is intended to mimic the voice of a specific real person, enhanced labelling and disclosure requirements apply.

**Article 11:** For video content generated or significantly altered by AI, including deepfakes and AI animation, the following labelling requirements apply:

(1) A persistent on-screen label or watermark for the duration during which AI-generated or AI-altered elements appear;

(2) A prominent label in the title, description, or introductory segment of the video;

(3) AI-generated video content that depicts real persons in realistic scenarios shall bear enhanced labels, including a watermark that cannot be easily removed or obscured.

## Chapter III: Implicit Labelling Requirements (Metadata)

**Article 12:** In addition to explicit labelling, service providers shall embed metadata in AI-generated content to facilitate automated detection and platform-level verification. Implicit labelling through metadata shall comply with the technical specifications set out in GB 45438-2025 and shall include:

(1) A standardized content provenance identifier indicating that the content is AI-generated;

(2) The category of AI-generation technology used (text generation, image synthesis, voice synthesis, video synthesis, etc.);

(3) A timestamp indicating when the content was generated;

(4) A unique identifier for the service provider and the specific AI system used;

(5) A content integrity value (such as a hash) to enable detection of post-generation tampering.

**Article 13:** Service providers shall adopt technical measures to ensure the persistence and integrity of metadata labels. Metadata shall not be stripped from AI-generated content when it is downloaded, shared, or transmitted through the service provider's platform. Service providers shall take reasonable technical measures to maintain metadata integrity even when content is transferred to third-party platforms.

**Article 14:** Service providers shall, where technically feasible, implement C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) or equivalent open, internationally recognized content provenance standards for the purpose of cross-platform interoperability of AI-generated content labels.

---

## Chapter IV: Platform Obligations

**Article 15:** Online information content platforms (hereinafter "platforms") that allow users to upload, publish, or distribute content shall establish mechanisms to:

(1) Detect AI-generated content uploaded by users using automated content detection tools and prompt users who appear to have uploaded unlabelled AI-generated content to add appropriate labels;

(2) Provide users with convenient tools to add AI-generated content labels when uploading content;

(3) Require users to indicate whether uploaded content is AI-generated as part of the content submission process.

**Article 16:** Platforms shall not allow users to remove, conceal, or deliberately obscure labels that have been applied to AI-generated content by service providers or that are required under these Measures. Platforms shall implement technical safeguards to prevent the unauthorized removal of AI-generated content labels.

**Article 17:** Platforms that have the capability to generate or recommend AI-generated content shall apply required labels to such content before it is displayed to users, and shall not display AI-generated content to users without the required labels.

**Article 18:** Platforms shall maintain records of the labelling status of AI-generated content distributed on their platforms and make such records available to regulatory authorities upon request. Platforms shall report annually to the relevant regulatory authority on the implementation of AI-generated content labelling requirements.

---

## Chapter V: Special Requirements for High-Risk AI-Generated Content

**Article 19:** For AI-generated content that involves or depicts:

(1) Real natural persons in realistic scenarios, especially content that depicts real persons saying or doing things they have not said or done;

(2) Real news events, public figures, or official announcements;

(3) Content with the potential to influence public opinion on matters of political, social, or public health significance;

the service provider shall apply enhanced labelling measures, including persistent watermarks that are technically resistant to removal, and shall clearly disclose the AI-generated nature of the content in the title or introductory materials.

**Article 20:** Service providers that generate synthetic media depicting real persons shall ensure that they have obtained the lawful consent of the depicted person or have a lawful basis for such depiction, and shall ensure that the labelling clearly identifies the content as AI-generated to prevent misuse.

**Article 21:** Service providers and platforms shall not assist users in generating or distributing AI-generated content that is deliberately designed to deceive audiences about its AI-generated origin, including AI-generated content that is structured to evade detection by content moderation systems.

---

## Chapter VI: Technical Standards and Interoperability

**Article 22:** The mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025, "Technical Requirements for the Labelling of Artificial Intelligence-Generated Content," sets out the detailed technical specifications for both explicit and implicit labelling of AI-generated content. Service providers shall ensure that their labelling practices comply with GB 45438-2025.

**Article 23:** The Standardization Administration of China, in coordination with the relevant regulatory departments, shall regularly review and update GB 45438-2025 to reflect developments in AI technology and content generation methods. Service providers shall update their labelling practices within the period prescribed after any revision to the technical standard.

**Article 24:** The state encourages and supports Chinese enterprises and research institutions to participate in the development of international technical standards for AI-generated content identification and labelling, and to promote the mutual recognition of Chinese labelling standards with those of other countries and international organizations.

---

## Chapter VII: Supervision, Inspection, and Legal Liability

**Article 25:** Cybersecurity and informatization departments and other relevant regulatory authorities shall conduct regular supervision and inspection of compliance with AI-generated content labelling requirements, and may take the following measures:

(1) Require service providers and platforms to submit labelling compliance reports;

(2) Conduct technical inspections and audits of AI-generated content labelling systems;

(3) Issue orders for rectification of non-compliant labelling practices within a specified period;

(4) Make public findings of significant non-compliance where appropriate.

**Article 26:** Where service providers fail to label AI-generated content in accordance with these Measures, the relevant regulatory authority shall order corrections and may impose warnings. For serious violations, fines shall be imposed in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws including the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law, and the Personal Information Protection Law. Unlawful income derived from non-compliant labelling activities shall be confiscated.

**Article 27:** Where platforms fail to implement their obligations under Chapter IV of these Measures, the relevant regulatory authority shall order corrections within a specified time period. For platforms that repeatedly fail to comply after correction orders, the relevant regulatory authority may impose fines, order the suspension of relevant services, or report to the competent authority for revocation of relevant licenses.

**Article 28:** Where any organization or individual deliberately removes, falsifies, or circumvents required AI-generated content labels, the relevant regulatory authority shall order corrections, confiscate unlawful income, and impose fines. Where the conduct constitutes a crime, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

**Article 29:** Individuals who find that AI-generated content labels do not comply with the requirements of these Measures have the right to report and complain to the relevant regulatory authority. The regulatory authority shall process reports and complaints in a timely manner and notify the reporter of the outcome.

---

## Chapter VIII: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 30:** For the purposes of these Measures:

(1) "Artificial intelligence-generated content" means content generated, substantially synthesized, or materially altered by artificial intelligence technology, as defined in Article 3 of these Measures.

(2) "Service provider" means organizations and individuals that use AI technology to generate content and make it available to the public, including through applications, websites, APIs, and other means.

(3) "Platform" means an online information content service platform that allows users to upload, publish, or distribute content.

(4) "Explicit label" means a label that is directly visible to users in normal use and includes text, icons, or other visual or audio indicators identifying content as AI-generated.

(5) "Implicit label" means metadata embedded in the content file or transmission data that identifies the content as AI-generated, in accordance with technical standards.

**Article 31:** Where the labelling of AI-generated content in specific sectors such as broadcasting, film, news information services, and advertising is regulated by sector-specific laws and regulations that impose stricter requirements, those requirements shall apply in addition to these Measures.

**Article 32:** These Measures take effect on September 1, 2025.
