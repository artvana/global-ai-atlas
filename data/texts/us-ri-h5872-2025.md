# Rhode Island H5872 / S0816 — Prohibition on Deceptive AI Campaign Communications

**Enacted July 2, 2025 | R.I. Gen. Laws Chapter 17-23.2**

---

## Overview

Rhode Island H5872 (companion: S0816) prohibits the creation and distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated media in campaign communications within a specified period before elections. The law requires clear and conspicuous disclosures when AI-generated content is included in political advertising, and creates civil and criminal penalties for violations.

---

## Definitions

**"Materially deceptive AI-generated media"** means any image, video, audio recording, or digital content that:
(a) was created or substantially altered using artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar computational methods;
(b) depicts a clearly identified candidate or officeholder appearing to do or say something they did not in fact do or say; and
(c) would be believed by a reasonable person to authentically depict the candidate or officeholder.

**"Campaign communication"** means any communication made or distributed to Rhode Island voters in connection with a primary, general, or special election for Rhode Island state or local office, or federal office representing Rhode Island voters.

**"Candidate"** means any person who has filed for nomination to or election to any elective office in Rhode Island, or who is publicly known to be seeking such an office.

---

## R.I. Gen. Laws § 17-23.2-1 — Prohibition

**(a) Prohibition.** No person may create, publish, distribute, or cause to be distributed any campaign communication that contains materially deceptive AI-generated media within **90 days** of any election without including the required disclosure.

**(b) Required disclosure.** Campaign communications containing AI-generated content must include a clear and conspicuous disclosure: **"This advertisement contains content created or substantially altered using artificial intelligence."**

For video content: the disclosure must appear as text on screen throughout the AI-generated segments, in a font size of at least 4% of the frame height.

For audio content: the disclosure must be spoken clearly at both the beginning and end of the communication.

For still images or print materials: the disclosure must appear in text of not less than 12-point font in a location that is clearly visible.

**(c) Exceptions.** This section does not apply to:
(1) Bona fide news reporting, journalism, or commentary on matters of public concern
(2) Satire or parody clearly labeled as such
(3) Archival, documentary, or educational content
(4) Content where the AI-generated nature is made obvious by context and a reasonable person would not mistake it for authentic

---

## R.I. Gen. Laws § 17-23.2-2 — Civil Cause of Action

A candidate depicted in materially deceptive AI-generated media in violation of this chapter has a cause of action for:
- Preliminary and permanent injunctive relief
- Actual damages
- Statutory damages of not less than $1,000 and not more than $15,000 per violation
- Reasonable attorney's fees and costs

Courts shall provide expedited proceedings for actions filed within 60 days before an election.

---

## R.I. Gen. Laws § 17-23.2-3 — Criminal Penalties

Knowing violations of the prohibition constitute a misdemeanor, punishable by a fine of not less than $500 and not more than $5,000. A second or subsequent violation within 4 years is a felony punishable by up to 2 years imprisonment.

---

## R.I. Gen. Laws § 17-23.2-4 — Enforcement by Board of Elections

The Rhode Island Board of Elections may investigate complaints of violations of this chapter, issue cease-and-desist orders, and impose civil penalties of up to $10,000 per violation.

---

## Effective Date

This Act takes effect July 2, 2025.
