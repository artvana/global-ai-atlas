 SF 1879 
 as introduced - 94th Legislature (2025 - 2026) Posted on 02/24/2025 
 
 
 A bill for an act relating to energy; exempting sales to cryptocurrency and artificial intelligence data centers from certain energy savings goals; amending Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 216B.2402, subdivision 3a. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF MINNESOTA: 
 
 Section 1. Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 216B.2402, subdivision 3a, is amended to read: 
 Subd. 3a. Data mining facility. &quot;Data mining facility&quot; means all buildings, structures, equipment, and installations at a single site where electricity is used primarily by computers to process transactions involving digital currency that is not issued by a central authority , including cryptocurrency and artificial intelligence .
