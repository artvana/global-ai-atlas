# Colorado Senate Bill 25B-004 — Consumer AI Protections Act Effective Date Amendment

**Senate Bill No. 25B-004**

*Colorado — August 28, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 25B-004 was enacted during a special legislative session to delay the compliance date of Colorado SB 24-205 (the Consumer AI Protections Act, Colorado's comprehensive AI law) from February 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026.

### Effective Date Delay

The bill amends the operative/compliance date of SB 24-205 from February 1, 2026 to June 30, 2026, giving businesses subject to the comprehensive AI law additional time to prepare compliance programs. The delay was enacted during a special session of the Colorado General Assembly.

### Substantive Law Unchanged

SB 25B-004 makes no substantive changes to the requirements of SB 24-205. The underlying law continues to require high-risk AI system developers and deployers to conduct impact assessments, provide disclosures, implement risk management programs, and offer human review and opt-out rights.

### Federal Court Litigation Context

As of early 2026, SB 24-205 faces a preliminary injunction issued by a federal district court in a challenge brought by the United States Department of Justice on First Amendment grounds. The outcome of that litigation may substantially alter or preclude the law's enforcement even after the delayed effective date.

### Effective Date

The bill took effect upon the Governor's signature on August 28, 2025, as emergency legislation enacted during special session.
