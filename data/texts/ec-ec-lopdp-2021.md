# Ecuador Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales (LOPDP)
**(Organic Law on the Protection of Personal Data)**
**Published: May 26, 2021**

## Overview
The Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales is Ecuador's fundamental legal framework regulating the processing of personal data. It guarantees the exercise of the fundamental right to personal data protection, establishing limits and obligations for data processors and effective protection mechanisms for data subjects.

## Main Objective
Guaranteeing the exercise of the fundamental right to personal data protection, establishing:
- Limits and obligations for those who process data
- Effective protection mechanisms for data subjects
- International adequacy-level safeguards

## Key Rights

### Right to Data Portability
The data subject has the right to receive their personal data from the data controller in a compatible, updated, structured, common, interoperable and machine-readable format, or to transmit them to other controllers.

### Other Data Subject Rights
- Right of access
- Right of rectification
- Right of erasure (right to be forgotten)
- Right to object
- Right to restriction of processing

## Lawful Basis for Processing
Legitimate treatment of personal data requires:
- Consent from the data subject, or
- Compliance with other established legal conditions (contract performance, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests)

## Data Security
Chapter VI addresses data security with control measures focused on protecting data through:
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
- Obligation to notify security events that compromise personal data (breach notification)

## Supervisory Authority
**Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales** — responsible for enforcement and oversight.

## Adequacy and International Transfers
The law establishes mechanisms for international data transfers with adequate protection requirements.
