                    GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO
20th Legislative Assembly                                                  3rd Regular Session

                     SENATE OF PUERTO RICO
                              S. B. 1203
                                   April 16, 2026

                          Introduced by Mr. Reyes Berríos

                                        Referred to

                                           ACT

To add a new subsection (dd) to Article 5 of Act 83-2025, as amended,
      known as the "Puerto Rico Police Act," for the purpose of requiring the
      development and implementation of educational campaigns on the consequences
      legal of false reports, including those made through
      artificial intelligence; and for other related purposes.

                             STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
       The Government of Puerto Rico has the non-delegable responsibility of guaranteeing
public safety and the proper use of resources allocated to the protection of life
and property. In this context, the filing of false reports or the simulation of
crimes represents a significant threat to the efficient functioning of the agencies
of public order, by diverting essential resources and potentially placing the lives
of citizens and law enforcement agents at risk.
       The current criminal legal system criminalizes the act of providing
false information or simulating the commission of a crime knowingly. However, the
accelerated development of emerging technologies, particularly artificial intelligence,
has facilitated the creation and dissemination of false or manipulated content, including
images, videos, and audio that may mislead the authorities and provoke
unnecessary responses from the public safety apparatus.

                                            1
            This reality demands a proactive response that is not limited to the punitive sphere,
     but that also incorporates prevention strategies through citizen education.
     It is indispensable to orient the citizenry on the legal consequences of these
     conducts, as well as to foster the capacity to identify false or manipulated content
     that may affect public safety.
            Likewise, the integration of the municipalities, through the commissioners of the
     Municipal Police, is essential to maximize the reach of these educational
     initiatives, promoting a coordinated and effective strategy at the community level.
            In view of the foregoing, this Legislative Assembly understands it necessary to amend
     the Puerto Rico Police Act to establish as a duty of the Superintendent the
     development and implementation of educational campaigns aimed at preventing the filing
     of false reports and the misuse of emerging technologies to the detriment of
     public safety.

 1          Section 1. - A new subsection (dd) is added to Article 5 of Act 83-2025, as

 2   amended, known as the Puerto Rico Police Act, to read as follows:

 3          Article 5.- Superintendent; Powers and Duties.

 4          The Superintendent, as administrator and director of the Police, shall have the

 5   following powers and duties:

 6          (a) ...

 7          ...

 8          "(dd) Develop and implement, in coordination with the commissioners of the Municipal

 9   Police of the municipalities of Puerto Rico, continuous educational campaigns directed at the

10   citizenry on the proper use of emergency systems and the filing of complaints,

 1   including the legal consequences of providing false information or simulating the commission of a

 2   crime knowingly, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Penal Code of Puerto Rico.

 3          Said campaigns shall include orientation on the identification and responsible handling

 4   of false or manipulated information, including content generated through artificial intelligence,

 5   such as images, videos, audio, or other technological means, that may mislead the

 6   authorities or provoke the improper activation of public safety resources.

 7          For the fulfillment of this subsection, the Superintendent shall use digital media, social

 8   networks, audiovisual material, and any other communication platform deemed pertinent,

 9   and shall foster the active participation of the municipalities to maximize the reach of the campaigns.

10          The Superintendent shall submit an annual report to the Governor of Puerto Rico and to the

11   Legislative Assembly on the implementation, scope, and effectiveness of these educational

12   campaigns."

13          Section 2.- Regulation

14          The Police of Puerto Rico shall adopt or amend the necessary regulations to

15   comply with this Act.
