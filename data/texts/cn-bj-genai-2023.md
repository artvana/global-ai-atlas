# Beijing Measures for the Administration of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services (Trial)

*Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission and Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park Management Committee*
*Issued: October 8, 2023*
*Effective: October 8, 2023*

---

## Preface and Context

These Measures implement, within Beijing Municipality, the national-level **Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services** (Sheng Cheng Shi Ren Gong Zhi Neng Fu Wu Guan Li Zan Xing Ban Fa) issued jointly by the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC), the National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the People's Bank of China, and the State Intellectual Property Office, which took effect on August 15, 2023.

These Beijing-level implementing rules complement the national-level Measures by establishing local operational requirements for generative AI service providers operating in Beijing, including Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park — China's leading technology and innovation hub and home to major AI companies including Baidu, Zhihu, Megvii, and other prominent generative AI developers.

## National Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (August 15, 2023)

*The following is the text of the national-level Interim Measures, which form the regulatory foundation implemented by the Beijing rules.*

---

### Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1 — Purpose**

These Measures are formulated to promote the healthy development and standardized application of generative artificial intelligence, safeguard national security and societal public interests, and protect the legitimate rights and interests of citizens, legal persons, and other organizations, pursuant to the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Science and Technology Progress Law of the People's Republic of China, and other laws and administrative regulations.

**Article 2 — Scope of Application**

These Measures apply to the use of generative AI technology to provide text, pictures, audio, video, and other content to the public within the People's Republic of China (generative AI services).

Where the use of generative AI technology to develop products or provide services does not involve the provision of generative AI services to the public within the territory of China, these Measures do not apply.

**Article 3 — Definition of Generative AI**

For purposes of these Measures, "generative AI technology" refers to models and related technologies that use deep learning, large language models, and similar technological approaches to generate text, pictures, audio, video, programming code, and other content based on prompts submitted by users.

"Generative AI services" refers to the provision to the public of content generated through generative AI technology by means of a programmatic interface (API), internet website, client application, or other form.

"Generative AI service providers" refers to organizations and individuals that use generative AI technology to provide generative AI services to the public within the territory of China.

**Article 4 — Dual Development and Safety Mandate**

The State encourages the innovative development of generative AI and supports the development of the generative AI industry. The State implements management measures that promote the development and application of generative AI technology while simultaneously ensuring safety.

Generative AI service providers shall fulfill their principal responsibilities, shall take effective measures to ensure the safety and quality of generated content, and shall promote the application of generative AI technology in an orderly manner in accordance with law.

**Article 5 — Core Principles for Generative AI Services**

Generative AI services shall adhere to the following principles:

(1) Adhering to core socialist values, and not generating content that incites subversion of state power or the socialist system; endangers national security or interests; harms the national image; incites secession or undermines national unity and social stability; promotes terrorism or extremism; promotes ethnic hatred; promotes violence, obscenity, or pornography; spreads false information; or is prohibited by law or administrative regulations;

(2) Taking effective measures during the development stages of generative AI model training, including data collection and annotating, to enhance model quality, improve model safety, and prevent discriminatory content;

(3) Respecting intellectual property rights, business ethics, and trade secrets; not engaging in unfair competition; not infringing upon the legitimate rights and interests of others in the course of developing and providing generative AI services;

(4) Respecting the legitimate rights and interests of others, and not endangering the physical and mental health of others, and not infringing upon portrait rights, reputation rights, honor rights, privacy rights, and personal information rights of others.

**Article 6 — Regulatory Framework**

The Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) and competent authorities for telecommunications, public security, education, science and technology, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, culture and tourism, health, radio and television, press and publication, and other departments shall, within the scope of their respective functions and responsibilities, take charge of the supervision and administration of generative AI services.

### Chapter II: Technology Development and Training Data

**Article 7 — Lawful Data Collection for Training**

Generative AI service providers using training data shall comply with the following requirements:

(1) Use data that has been legally obtained and does not infringe upon the intellectual property rights of others;

(2) Data involving personal information shall be processed in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law; generative AI service providers shall obtain personal information in compliance with the law, and shall protect the legitimate rights and interests of personal information subjects;

(3) Take effective measures to improve data quality and truthfulness, and enhance the authenticity, accuracy, objectivity, and diversity of data;

(4) Data involving national security shall be handled in accordance with national security laws and regulations; data involving state secrets shall be handled in accordance with relevant confidentiality laws and regulations.

**Article 8 — Prohibition on Illegal Data Sources**

Generative AI service providers shall not use data obtained through internet scraping or other methods that violate laws and administrative regulations for training. Data collected for training shall not include personal information that natural persons have not consented to be used for AI training purposes, except where the law provides a basis for such use.

**Article 9 — Data Annotation Standards**

When providing manual annotation services, generative AI service providers shall:

(1) Formulate clear, specific, and feasible annotation rules;

(2) Carry out systematic training of annotators to ensure they understand annotation rules;

(3) Conduct data sampling inspection of annotation outcomes; and

(4) Ensure that annotations are accurate and that the annotation process does not introduce prejudice or discrimination into the data.

---

### Chapter III: Service Provision and User Obligations

**Article 10 — Registration and Real-Name Verification**

Generative AI service providers shall, in accordance with relevant national regulations, implement real-name registration for users and shall not provide services to users who refuse to register under their real names.

Generative AI service providers shall not provide services anonymously or using fake identities.

**Article 11 — Service Agreements and User Protections**

Generative AI service providers shall enter into service agreements with users and shall inform users in a clear and conspicuous manner of the following:

(1) The scope, function, and limitations of the service;

(2) The rights and obligations of both parties;

(3) Rules on the use of content generated by the service;

(4) The mechanism for handling user complaints and reports;

(5) Other matters required by laws and administrative regulations.

Generative AI service providers shall not use service agreements to improperly limit users' rights, transfer risks to users, or evade their own obligations.

**Article 12 — Prohibition on Providing Services to Minors for Age-Restricted Content**

Generative AI service providers shall not provide generative AI services that contain pornographic or obscene content to minors. Providers shall take technical and administrative measures to prevent minors from accessing age-restricted generative AI services.

**Article 13 — Watermarking and Synthetic Content Identification**

Generative AI service providers shall add identification to generated content (including text, pictures, audio, and video) in accordance with the Provisions on Deep Synthesis Technology in Internet Information Services and other relevant regulations. The identification shall be added in a manner that allows identification of the generated content and its source.

Generative AI service providers shall not help users evade identification of AI-generated content.

**Article 14 — Content Safety Requirements**

Generative AI service providers shall establish and improve the following content safety management mechanisms:

(1) Pre-generation review mechanisms: Implement keyword filtering, content classification, and safety review of inputs before content is generated where technically feasible;

(2) Post-generation review mechanisms: Conduct sampling inspection and review of generated content on an ongoing basis to identify and remove harmful content;

(3) User complaint and report mechanisms: Establish channels for users to report harmful content generated by the service; respond to reports in a timely manner;

(4) Emergency response mechanisms: When harmful content is found, take emergency measures to stop its dissemination and report to the competent authority.

**Article 15 — Handling of Illegal and Harmful Content**

When generative AI service providers discover that generated content contains or may contain content that is illegal or harmful, they shall:

(1) Immediately stop generating, transmitting, or disseminating such content;

(2) Delete stored illegal content;

(3) Preserve relevant logs; and

(4) Report to the relevant authorities in accordance with the law.

Generative AI service providers shall not provide technical support for the generation, transmission, or dissemination of illegal content.

---

### Chapter IV: Obligations Regarding Intellectual Property Rights

**Article 16 — Respect for Copyright and Related Rights**

Generative AI service providers shall respect intellectual property rights and shall take measures to:

(1) Avoid generating content that infringes upon the copyright, patent rights, trademark rights, or other intellectual property rights of others;

(2) Avoid reproducing or distributing third-party copyrighted works in generated content without authorization;

(3) Implement measures requested by rights holders to filter known infringing content from generation outputs, where technically feasible.

**Article 17 — User-Submitted Content**

Where users upload or input third-party content to generative AI services (for example, asking the AI to summarize, translate, or otherwise process third-party works), generative AI service providers shall provide users with clear notice that the users bear responsibility for ensuring they have the right to submit such content.

---

### Chapter V: Data Security and Personal Information Protection

**Article 18 — Personal Information Processing Requirements**

Generative AI service providers that process personal information shall:

(1) Obtain personal information through lawful means;

(2) Inform personal information subjects of the purposes and methods of processing personal information, the types of personal information processed, and the period for which it will be retained;

(3) Implement technical measures such as de-identification and encryption to protect personal information security;

(4) Not use personal information for other purposes beyond the scope of the service without the consent of the personal information subject;

(5) Provide mechanisms for personal information subjects to access, correct, delete, and transfer their personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law.

**Article 19 — User Dialogue Data Security**

User inputs (prompts) submitted to generative AI services constitute personal information and shall be handled in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law. Generative AI service providers shall:

(1) Not retain user inputs for longer than necessary for the provision of the service, unless required by law;

(2) Not share user inputs with third parties without user consent, except where required by law;

(3) Not use user inputs to train AI models without the separate, explicit consent of users;

(4) Protect user inputs from unauthorized access or disclosure.

**Article 20 — Cross-Border Data Transfer**

Generative AI service providers that transfer data generated by services offered in China to recipients outside of China shall comply with applicable cross-border data transfer requirements, including security assessment and certification requirements under the Data Security Law and the Personal Information Protection Law.

---

### Chapter VI: Ethical Requirements

**Article 21 — Non-Discrimination in AI Systems**

Generative AI service providers shall take measures to prevent their services from generating content that discriminates against individuals on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, age, disability, religious belief, or other characteristics.

Providers shall conduct regular assessments of their generative AI systems for discriminatory outputs and shall take corrective action when discriminatory patterns are identified.

**Article 22 — Prohibition on Using AI to Spread Disinformation**

Generative AI service providers shall take measures to prevent their services from being used to generate and disseminate false information, including fabricated news, false statements attributed to real individuals, and other content intended to deceive.

Providers shall mark generated content in ways that enable users to understand that the content was produced by AI.

**Article 23 — Prohibition on Impersonation of Government Authorities**

Generative AI service providers shall take technical measures to prevent their services from generating content that impersonates government authorities, official documents, or communications from government agencies.

---

### Chapter VII: Oversight and Administration

**Article 24 — Security Assessment and Filing**

Generative AI services with public opinion properties or social mobilization capabilities shall, prior to launch, complete a security assessment (an Algorithmic Recommendation Service Filing) in accordance with the Provisions on the Administration of Algorithmic Recommendations in Internet Information Services, and complete the filing requirements for internet information services with generative AI features.

**Article 25 — Ongoing Compliance Obligations**

Generative AI service providers shall:

(1) Develop and implement data security and personal information protection systems;

(2) Establish cybersecurity protection measures;

(3) Develop policies for protecting the rights and interests of intellectual property rights holders;

(4) Establish systems for protecting the legitimate rights and interests of users;

(5) Regularly audit the above systems and policies and report on compliance to the competent authority.

**Article 26 — Reporting and Incident Notification**

Generative AI service providers shall, within 24 hours of discovering any of the following, report to the Cyberspace Administration and other relevant authorities:

(1) The dissemination of illegal content through their services at significant scale;

(2) Security incidents affecting the data security or personal information of a large number of users;

(3) Other significant security incidents as defined by the competent authority.

**Article 27 — Regulatory Inspection Rights**

Regulatory authorities shall have the right to:

(1) Inspect documents and data held by generative AI service providers related to their compliance with these Measures;

(2) Inquire about generative AI service providers' technical architecture, training data, safety measures, and security assessment records;

(3) Conduct technical testing of generative AI services to assess compliance.

Generative AI service providers shall cooperate with regulatory inspection and shall not obstruct, delay, or refuse regulatory inspection.

**Article 28 — Third-Party Service Providers**

Where generative AI service providers use the products or services of other technology providers (including foundation model providers, cloud services, data annotation services, or other AI infrastructure providers), they shall take responsibility for the compliance of their services with these Measures. Generative AI service providers shall conduct due diligence on their technology partners and shall include compliance requirements in relevant contracts.

---

### Chapter VIII: Legal Liability

**Article 29 — Administrative Penalties**

Where generative AI service providers violate these Measures, the Cyberspace Administration or other competent authorities shall, in accordance with the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law, the Personal Information Protection Law, and other applicable laws and administrative regulations, order correction, issue warnings, confiscate illegal gains, impose fines, order suspension of operations, revoke operating licenses, or take other penalties as appropriate.

**Article 30 — Criminal Liability**

Where violations of these Measures constitute a criminal offense, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

**Article 31 — Civil Liability**

Where violations of these Measures infringe upon the legitimate rights and interests of organizations or individuals, civil liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

---

### Chapter IX: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 32 — Application to Overseas Providers**

Where organizations and individuals outside the territory of China use generative AI technology to provide generative AI services to users within the territory of China, they shall comply with the requirements of these Measures. Where overseas providers do not comply with these Measures, the Cyberspace Administration may restrict or prohibit the provision of their services in China.

**Article 33 — Relation to Other Regulations**

These Measures supplement and do not replace other applicable regulations governing internet information services, algorithmic recommendations, deep synthesis technology, cybersecurity, data security, and personal information protection. Generative AI service providers shall comply with all applicable regulations.

**Article 34 — Effective Date**

These Measures take effect on August 15, 2023.

## Beijing Implementing Measures: Specific Provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose

These Measures are issued by the Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission and the Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park Management Committee to implement the national-level Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services within Beijing Municipality and to support the responsible development of the generative AI industry in Beijing.

### Article 2 — Scope

These Measures apply to generative AI service providers registered or operating within Beijing Municipality and to generative AI services provided to users within Beijing. Major generative AI service providers with their principal place of business in Beijing shall be subject to these Measures in addition to any requirements applicable under the national Measures.

### Article 3 — Innovation Support Alongside Compliance

Beijing Municipality recognizes the importance of generative AI to Beijing's position as a national technology innovation hub. The Zhongguancun Science and Technology Park Management Committee shall establish a regulatory sandbox mechanism to allow eligible generative AI companies to test innovative products and services under controlled conditions before full public deployment, allowing compliance challenges to be identified and addressed.

### Article 4 — Zhongguancun AI Governance Working Group

The Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission shall establish a Zhongguancun AI Governance Working Group (the "Working Group") composed of representatives from:

(a) The Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission;
(b) The Beijing Cyberspace Administration;
(c) Major generative AI companies in Beijing;
(d) Academic and research institutions;
(e) Legal and technical experts.

The Working Group shall:

(1) Develop technical standards and implementation guidelines for the Beijing AI industry consistent with national regulations;
(2) Provide compliance guidance to generative AI companies in Beijing;
(3) Report regularly to the national Cyberspace Administration and other competent authorities on implementation of the national Measures in Beijing;
(4) Identify regulatory gaps or uncertainties and propose solutions.

### Article 5 — Enhanced Content Security Review for High-Risk Applications

Generative AI services deployed within Beijing in connection with the following high-risk applications shall be subject to enhanced content security review by the Working Group prior to launch:

(a) Generative AI services used in medical diagnosis or clinical decision support;
(b) Generative AI services used in educational instruction to minors;
(c) Generative AI services used in financial advice or investment information;
(d) Generative AI services used in public safety or law enforcement contexts.

Enhanced review includes a structured risk assessment, testing of the service for potential safety failures, and review of safety measures by the Working Group.

### Article 6 — Beijing AI Filing Registry

All generative AI service providers subject to these Measures shall register their services with the Beijing Municipal filing registry within 30 days of service launch. The filing shall include:

(a) Company name and registration information;
(b) Name and description of the generative AI service;
(c) Description of the foundation model(s) used;
(d) Summary of safety assessment results;
(e) Contact information for the company's AI safety officer.

The Beijing filing registry will be coordinated with national-level filing requirements to avoid duplication.

### Article 7 — Coordination with National Regulators

The Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission shall cooperate with the national Cyberspace Administration of China and other competent national authorities in the supervision of generative AI services, including sharing of:

(a) Safety assessment results for services subject to Beijing oversight;
(b) Incident reports and enforcement actions taken against Beijing-based providers;
(c) Technical standards and implementation experience.

### Article 8 — Effective Date

These Beijing-level implementing measures take effect on October 8, 2023.

## Annex: Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Generative AI | AI systems that generate new content (text, images, audio, video, code) based on user prompts or other inputs |
| Foundation model | A large AI model trained on broad data that can be adapted for multiple downstream generative AI applications |
| Provider | An organization or individual that provides generative AI services to the public within China |
| Training data | Data used to train or fine-tune a generative AI model |
| Security assessment | A pre-launch review of an AI service's safety measures, content controls, and compliance with applicable regulations |
| Deep synthesis | AI technologies used to generate or significantly alter realistic-seeming audio, video, or other content, including deepfake technologies |
| Algorithmic recommendation | AI systems that push or recommend content, products, or information to users based on analysis of user behavior and preferences |
