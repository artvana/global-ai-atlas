 104TH GENERAL ASSEMBLY 
 
 
 State of Illinois 
 
 
 2025 and 2026 
 
 
 HB4350 
 
 Introduced, by Rep. Martin McLaughlin 
 
 SYNOPSIS AS INTRODUCED: 
 
 35 ILCS 5/246 new 
 Amends the Illinois Income Tax Act. Creates an income tax credit in an amount equal to 100%, but in no event more than $250,000 per taxpayer in any taxable year, of qualified business expenses paid by a qualified small business during the taxable year. Effective immediately. 
 
 A BILL FOR 
 
 
 
 AN ACT concerning revenue. 
 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the People of the State of Illinois, represented in the General Assembly: 
 
 
 Section 5. The Illinois Income Tax Act is amended by adding Section 246 as follows: 
 (35 ILCS 5/246 new) 
 
 Sec. 246. Small business credit. 
 
 
 (a) For taxable years that end on or after December 31, 2026 and end on or before December 31, 2030, each qualified small business is entitled to a credit against the taxes imposed by subsections (a) and (b) of Section 201 in an amount equal to 100%, but in no event more than $250,000 per taxpayer in any taxable year, of qualified business expenses paid by the qualified small business during the taxable year. 
 
 
 (b) In no event shall a credit under this Section reduce the taxpayer&#39;s liability to less than zero. If the amount of the credit exceeds the tax liability for the year, the excess may be carried forward and applied to the tax liability of the 5 taxable years following the excess credit year. The tax credit under this Section shall be applied to the earliest year for which there is a tax liability. If there are credits for more than one year that are available to offset a liability, the earlier credit shall be applied first. 
 
 
 (c) If the taxpayer is a partnership or Subchapter S corporation, the credit is allowed to pass through to the partners and shareholders as provided in Section 251. 
 
 
 (d) The Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity, in cooperation with the Department of Revenue, shall adopt rules for the administration of this Section. The Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity shall cooperate with the Department of Revenue to certify qualified small businesses under this Section. The Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity shall publish annual reports on its website concerning the use and impact of the credit under this Section. 
 
 
 (e) As used in this Section: 
 
 
 &quot;Qualified business expense&quot; means the purchase of a good or service used exclusively to further the success of the business, including, but not limited to, the following: 
 
 
 (1) a capital good that is reasonably appropriate for the needs of the small business, depending on the enterprise, including, but not limited to: office equipment; tools or machinery used in making or crafting a product sold to consumers; vehicles used exclusively for business operations, including delivery vehicles; technology used to benefit the business, including costs related to artificial intelligence; or building costs used for expansion of the business; 
 
 
 (2) branding art or design services used exclusively to improve the business&#39;s appeal to customers, either through decor or logo improvements, if the expense is used to improve the business&#39;s branding; and 
 
 
 (3) expenses for employee training, whether the employee is a part-time employee or a full-time employee, consisting of course fees, materials, or technology used exclusively for training the employee. 
 
 
 &quot;Qualified business expense&quot; does not include raw materials, advertising, travel expenses, rent or mortgage payments, government licensing or permits, charitable donations, loans, insurance, legal fees, food or perishable items, subscriptions that are not used for training or are not essential to the business&#39;s operations, or any other purchase that is not specifically classified as a qualified business expense. 
 
 
 &quot;Qualified small business&quot; means a business that has been certified by the Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity as a qualified small business for the purposes of this Section and that: 
 
 
 (1) is a sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, joint venture, association, or cooperative; 
 
 
 (2) has conducted business in Illinois for at least one year prior to the taxable year for which the credit is claimed; 
 
 
 (3) employs fewer than 100 employees in the State during the taxable year; and 
 
 
 (4) is current with all of its State and federal tax obligations as of the last day of the taxable year for which the credit is claimed. 
 
 Section 99. Effective date. This Act takes effect upon becoming law.
