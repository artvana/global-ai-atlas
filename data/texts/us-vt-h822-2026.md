 Subject: Commerce and trade; consumer protection; generative artificialintelligence 
 Statement of purpose of bill as introduced: This bill proposes to require providers of generative artificial intelligence systems to provide consumers with a tool that would determine whether content was created or altered by the provider&#39;s system. This bill also proposes to require that providers of generative artificial intelligence systems give users of the system an option to add a watermark to media that was generated by artificial intelligence.
