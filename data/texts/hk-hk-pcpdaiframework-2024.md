# Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework
**Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD), Hong Kong**
**Published: June 11, 2024**

## Overview
The PCPD issued the "Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework" to help organizations use AI systems while protecting personal data under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO). This is the PCPD's first guidance document targeted at organisations procuring, implementing and using artificial intelligence systems. It aligns with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and its six Data Protection Principles (DPP).

## Four Core Areas

### 1. Establish AI Strategy and Governance
- Develop internal AI governance strategies with defined purposes for procurement
- Create an AI governance committee reporting to the board with adequate resources and expertise
- Implement policies for handling AI-generated outputs, including anonymization and content labeling
- Provide staff training on data protection compliance, cybersecurity, and AI technology fundamentals

### 2. Conduct Risk Assessment and Human Oversight
- Perform comprehensive assessments examining data volume, sensitivity, security, and potential harm scenarios
- Adopt proportionate risk mitigation aligned with severity levels
- Determine appropriate human involvement levels:
  - "Human-out-of-the-loop" (fully automated)
  - "Human-in-command" (human can override)
  - "Human-in-the-loop" (human approves each decision)
- Balance competing requirements when implementing ethical principles

### 3. Customize and Implement AI Systems
- Use minimal personal data; validate models against privacy and ethical requirements
- Test for reliability, robustness, and fairness; conduct rigorous user acceptance testing
- Implement security measures including attack prevention and audit mechanisms
- Establish incident response protocols and conduct regular internal audits

### 4. Communicate with Stakeholders
- Maintain transparent dialogue with staff, suppliers, customers, and regulators
- Provide decision explanations and disclose AI system use and associated risks
- Use clear, accessible language in all stakeholder communications

## Legal Basis
Organizations must comply with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO) and adhere to the six Data Protection Principles (DPP) outlined in Schedule 1 during the procurement and implementation of AI systems.

## Status
Voluntary guidance framework; not legally binding legislation.
