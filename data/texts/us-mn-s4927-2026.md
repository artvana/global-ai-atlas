 SF 4927 
 Introduction - 94th Legislature (2025 - 2026) 
 Posted on 03/25/2026 
 
 
 A bill for an act relating to commerce; precluding delivery of professional services through artificial intelligence directly to consumers; providing for enforcement and penalties; proposing coding for new law in Minnesota Statutes, chapter 325F. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF MINNESOTA: 
 
 Section 1. 
 [325F.701] PROHIBITION ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PERFORMING LICENSED ACTIVITIES. 
 
 
 
 Subdivision 1. Definitions. (a) For purposes of this section, the following terms have the meanings given. 
 
 
 (b) &quot;Artificial intelligence model&quot; means a machine-based system that is capable of, for explicit or implicit objectives, inferring from the input the system receives how to generate outputs that are capable of influencing physical or virtual environments. 
 
 
 (c) &quot;Consumer&quot; means a natural person or business that is not a credentialed professional for professional services that the natural person or business is seeking. 
 
 
 (d) &quot;Credentialed&quot; means a natural person or a business that holds a current license, certificate, or registration to provide professional services. 
 
 
 (e) &quot;Professional services&quot; means services for which the state requires a license, certificate, or registration. 
 
 
 Subd. 2. Prohibition on artificial intelligence models performing professional services. (a) The owner of an artificial intelligence model is prohibited from allowing the model to provide a professional service to a consumer unless the model is operated, while the service is being provided, by a natural person who is credentialed to provide the professional services or by a natural person representing a business that is credentialed to provide the professional services. 
 
 
 (b) A business or a person is prohibited from using an artificial intelligence model to provide a professional service to a consumer unless the model is operated, while the service is being provided, by a natural person who is credentialed to provide the professional services or by a natural person representing a business that is credentialed to provide the professional services. 
 
 
 (c) This section does not prohibit a credentialed professional from using an artificial intelligence model as a tool to assist the credentialed professional in performing a professional service. 
 
 
 Subd. 3. Enforcement and penalties; attorney general. A violation of this section is subject to enforcement by the attorney general under section 8.31.
