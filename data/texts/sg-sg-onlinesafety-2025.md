Government officials will NEVER call you to transfer money, verify refunds, disclose bank log-in details or request for your personal information. For scam-related advice, please call the ScamShield Helpline at 1799 or go to [www.ScamShield.gov.sg](https://www.ScamShield.gov.sg).
