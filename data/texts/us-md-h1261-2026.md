 HOUSE BILL 1261 
 
 By: Delegates Coley, Amprey, Mireku-North, Pasteur, Roberts, Rogers, Ruff, Tomlinson, Wilkins, Wims, and Young 
 
 Introduced and read first time: February 12, 2026 
 Assigned to: Economic Matters 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Consumer Protection - Artificial Intelligence Toys 
 
 
 (Artificial Intelligence Toy Safety Act) 
 
 FOR the purpose of establishing certain child safety and data privacy requirements for certain toys featuring artificial intelligence; requiring an artificial intelligence toy to contain certain labeling; prohibiting the manufacturer of an artificial intelligence toy from using certain data for certain marketing; establishing the Artificial Intelligence Toy Safety Review Panel in the Consumer Protection Division of the Office of the Attorney General to monitor manufacturer compliance with this Act and conduct independent audits and safety assessments relating to artificial intelligence toys; establishing that a violation of this Act is an unfair, abusive, or deceptive trade practice subject to certain enforcement and penalty provisions under the Maryland Consumer Protection Act; and generally relating to artificial intelligence toys.

 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Commercial Law 
 
 13-301. 
 Unfair, abusive, or deceptive trade practices include any: 
 (14) Violation of a provision of: 
 (xlvii) Title 14, Subtitle 50 of this article; or 
 
 (xlviii) Section 13-411.1(c)(2) of the Transportation Article; or 
 
 (XLIX) TITLE 14, SUBTITLE 51 OF THIS ARTICLE; OR 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 51. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SAFETY ACT. 
 
 
 14-5101. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;ADAPTIVE CONTENT&quot; MEANS CONTENT OR RESPONSES GENERATED OR MODIFIED USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE THAT CHANGE BASED ON USER INPUT, STORED DATA, OR BEHAVIORAL PATTERNS. 
 
 
 (C) &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY&quot; MEANS ANY TOY, DEVICE, OR PRODUCT SOLD TO A CONSUMER IN THE STATE THAT: 
 
 
 (1) EMPLOYS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, MACHINE LEARNING, ADAPTIVE ALGORITHMS, CONVERSATIONAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, BEHAVIORAL MODELING, OR SIMILAR COMPUTATIONAL PROCESSES; AND 
 
 
 (2) IS MARKETED AND SOLD TO, OR PRIMARILY USED BY, A CHILD. 
 
 
 (D) (1) &quot;CHILD USER DATA&quot; MEANS INFORMATION COLLECTED OR INFERRED FROM A CHILD&#39;S INTERACTION WITH AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;CHILD USER DATA&quot; INCLUDES AUDIO, VIDEO, GEOLOCATION, BIOMETRIC DATA, TEXT, BEHAVIORAL PATTERNS, AND RECORDED SPEECH. 
 
 
 (E) &quot;CORE FUNCTIONALITY&quot; MEANS THE FEATURES OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY THAT HAVE BEEN DESIGNED AND MARKETED FOR USE BY A CHILD. 
 
 
 (F) &quot;MANUFACTURER&quot; MEANS A PERSON ENGAGED IN THE PRODUCTION, DISTRIBUTION, IMPORTATION, OR SALE OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;PARENTAL CONSENT&quot; MEANS VERIFIED PERMISSION FROM THE PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN OF A CHILD. 
 
 
 (H) &quot;REVIEW PANEL&quot; MEANS THE CHILD ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SAFETY REVIEW PANEL. 
 
 
 (I) (1) &quot;SAFE MODE&quot; MEANS AN AUTOMATIC SECURITY FEATURE THAT LOCKS OR DISABLES AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY OR FEATURES ON THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;SAFE MODE&quot; INCLUDES AUTOMATICALLY SHUTTING DOWN, RESETTING, REDIRECTING, OR LOCKING THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 14-5102. 
 
 
 (A) AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY PRODUCED, DISTRIBUTED, IMPORTED, OR SOLD IN THE STATE MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (1) GENERATE, DISPLAY, OR COMMUNICATE CONTENT THAT: 
 
 
 (I) IS SEXUAL, VIOLENT, DISCRIMINATORY, OR OTHERWISE INAPPROPRIATE FOR A CHILD; 
 
 
 (II) IS EMOTIONALLY MANIPULATIVE, INCLUDING BY ENCOURAGING ISOLATION, SECRECY, OVERDEPENDENCE, OR EMOTIONAL RELIANCE ON THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY; 
 
 
 (III) PROVIDES INSTRUCTION, ENCOURAGEMENT, OR ADVICE ABOUT CRIMINAL ACTIVITY, SELF - HARM, SUBSTANCE USE, OR SEXUAL BEHAVIOR; OR 
 
 
 (IV) INFERS, RECORDS, OR CATEGORIZES DEMOGRAPHIC OR OTHER PERSONAL ATTRIBUTES OF A CHILD, INCLUDING RACE, RELIGION, SEXUAL ORIENTATION, GENDER IDENTITY, OR POLITICAL AFFILIATION; OR 
 
 
 (2) ENCOURAGE A CHILD TO INTERACT WITH THIRD - PARTY ONLINE SYSTEMS, INCLUDING WEBSITES, APPLICATIONS, OR OTHER ONLINE DEVICES. 
 
 
 (B) AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY PRODUCED, DISTRIBUTED, IMPORTED, OR SOLD IN THE STATE SHALL INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (1) A MECHANISM FOR EASILY OBTAINING AND REVOKING PARENTAL CONSENT; 
 
 
 (2) A MECHANISM THAT DISABLES ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FEATURES AND DATA COLLECTION FUNCTIONS IF PARENTAL CONSENT IS NOT OBTAINED OR IS REVOKED; 
 
 
 (3) AGE - APPROPRIATE CONVERSATIONAL FILTERS TO BLOCK HARMFUL OR INAPPROPRIATE DIALOGUE; 
 
 
 (4) CONTENT - MODERATION MECHANISMS THAT PREVENT THE OUTPUT OF CONTENT PROHIBITED UNDER SUBSECTION (A) OF THIS SECTION; 
 
 
 (5) AN AUTOMATIC SAFE MODE THAT IS ACTIVATED WHEN THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY RECEIVES POTENTIALLY HARMFUL, UNKNOWN, OR PROHIBITED INPUTS; AND 
 
 
 (6) THE CAPABILITY TO SECURELY UPDATE SOFTWARE OR FIRMWARE TO ADDRESS VULNERABILITIES OR IMPROVE SAFETY. 
 
 
 14-5103. 
 
 
 (A) AMANUFACTURER SHALL CONDUCT A CHILD ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SAFETY ASSESSMENT FOR EACH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY PRODUCED, DISTRIBUTED, IMPORTED, OR SOLD IN THE STATE THAT: 
 
 
 (1) DESCRIBES FORESEEABLE RISKS OF OPERATING THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY; AND 
 
 
 (2) OUTLINES THE MITIGATION STRATEGIES FOR THE FORESEEABLE RISKS IDENTIFIED UNDER ITEM (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (B) AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SAFETY ASSESSMENT SHALL BE CONDUCTED: 
 
 
 (1) EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (C) OF THIS SECTION, BEFORE MARKETING OR SELLING AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY IN THE STATE; 
 
 
 (2) ON AN ANNUAL BASIS FOR EACH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY CONTINUING TO BE MARKETED OR SOLD IN THE STATE; AND 
 
 
 (3) FOLLOWING ANY SIGNIFICANT CHANGE MADE TO THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FEATURES OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 (C) AN INITIAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SAFETY ASSESSMENT OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SOLD OR MARKETED IN THE STATE ON OR BEFORE JULY 1, 2026, SHALL BE CONDUCTED NOT LATER THAN JANUARY 1, 2027. 
 
 
 14-5104. 
 
 
 (A) (1) SUBJECT TO SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, A MANUFACTURER MAY ONLY COLLECT THE MINIMUM CHILD USER DATA NECESSARY FOR THE OPERATION OF THE CORE FUNCTIONALITY OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 (2) CHILD USER DATA COLLECTED BY A MANUFACTURER SHALL BE ENCRYPTED. 
 
 
 (B) AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY MANUFACTURER MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (1) SELL, LEASE, OR OTHERWISE TRANSFER CHILD USER DATA TO A THIRD PARTY; 
 
 
 (2) USE CHILD USER DATA TO TRAIN UNRELATED ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MODELS OR SYSTEMS; 
 
 
 (3) USE CHILD USER DATA FOR TARGETED ADVERTISING; OR 
 
 
 (4) RETAIN CHILD USER DATA FOR MORE THAN 12 MONTHS UNLESS THE MANUFACTURER OBTAINS RENEWED PARENTAL CONSENT. 
 
 
 (C) A MANUFACTURER SHALL ENSURE THAT THE PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN OF A CHILD USER OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY HAS THE ABILITY TO: 
 
 
 (1) EASILY ACCESS, REVIEW, DOWNLOAD, AND DELETE ALL CHILD USER DATA COLLECTED BY AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY; AND 
 
 
 (2) EASILY DISABLE CHILD USER DATA COLLECTION WITHOUT DISABLING THE CORE FUNCTIONALITY OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 
 (D) IF A DATA BREACH OR SUSPECTED DATA BREACH INVOLVING CHILD USER DATA OCCURS, A MANUFACTURER SHALL NOTIFY EACH READILY IDENTIFIABLE PARENT OR GUARDIAN OF A CHILD ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY USER WITHIN 48 HOURS AFTER THE MANUFACTURER DISCOVERING THE DATA BREACH OR SUSPECTED DATA BREACH. 
 
 
 14-5105. 
 
 
 (A) AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SOLD OR DISTRIBUTED IN THE STATE SHALL INCLUDE A CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS LABEL: 
 
 
 (1) STATING THAT THE PRODUCT USES ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; AND 
 
 
 (2) DIRECTLY OR THROUGH THE USE OF A QUICK RESPONSE CODE: 
 
 
 (I) IDENTIFYING ANY TYPE OF DATA COLLECTED BY THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY; AND 
 
 
 (II) IF INTERACTIONS WITH THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY ARE STORED, TRANSMITTED, OR USED TO GENERATE ADAPTIVE CONTENT, PROVIDING INFORMATION ON HOW THE INTERACTIONS ARE STORED, TRANSMITTED, OR USED. 
 
 
 (B) A MANUFACTURER THAT PRODUCES, DISTRIBUTES, IMPORTS, OR SELLS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY IN THE STATE SHALL MAKE PUBLICLY AVAILABLE AN EASILY ACCESSIBLE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SAFETY SUMMARY REPORT USING PLAIN, EASILY UNDERSTANDABLE LANGUAGE IDENTIFYING: 
 
 
 (1) THE AGE RANGE THAT THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY IS INTENDED FOR; 
 
 
 (2) RISKS IDENTIFIED IN THE USE OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SAFETY ASSESSMENT COMPLETED UNDER &sect; 14-5103 OF THIS SUBTITLE; 
 
 
 (3) THE RESULTS OF ANY TESTING OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY; AND 
 
 
 (4) DIRECTIONS THAT THE PARENT OR GUARDIAN OF A CHILD USER MAY USE TO CONTROL OR LIMIT DATA COLLECTION OR ADAPTIVE BEHAVIORS. 
 
 
 14-5106. 
 
 
 A MANUFACTURER MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (1) MARKET OR ADVERTISE AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY AS AN EMOTIONAL COMPANION, PARENTAL SUBSTITUTE, OR PSYCHOLOGICAL COUNSELOR; 
 
 
 (2) USE A RECORDING OF OR GENERATED CONTENT FROM CHILD INTERACTIONS WITH THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY IN ANY MARKETING OR PROMOTIONAL MATERIALS WITHOUT OBTAINING EXPRESS PARENTAL CONSENT; OR 
 
 
 (3) USE DATA COLLECTED FROM A CHILD USER OF AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY TO DEVELOP AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY ADVERTISEMENT TARGETED AT CHILDREN. 
 
 
 14-5107. 
 
 
 (A) THERE IS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY SAFETY REVIEW PANEL IN THE CONSUMER PROTECTION DIVISION OF THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL. 
 
 
 (B) (1) THE REVIEW PANEL SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) REVIEW MANUFACTURER COMPLIANCE WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SUBTITLE; 
 
 
 (II) CONDUCT INDEPENDENT AUDITS OF ANY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY MARKETED, SOLD, OR DISTRIBUTED TO CHILDREN IN THE STATE; AND 
 
 
 (III) EVALUATE SAFETY STANDARDS AND MAKE RECOMMENDATIONS FOR STATUTORY CHANGES BASED ON INDUSTRY BEST PRACTICES. 
 
 
 (2) ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1, 2027, AND EACH DECEMBER 1 THEREAFTER, THE REVIEW PANEL SHALL REPORT TO THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, ON MANUFACTURER COMPLIANCE, THE RESULTS OF INDEPENDENT AUDITS, AND POTENTIAL STATUTORY CHANGES. 
 
 
 14-5108. 
 
 
 (A) A VIOLATION OF THIS SUBTITLE IS: 
 
 
 (1) AN UNFAIR, ABUSIVE, OR DECEPTIVE TRADE PRACTICE WITHIN THE MEANING OF TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE; AND 
 
 
 (2) SUBJECT TO THE ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTY PROVISIONS CONTAINED IN TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (B) IN ADDITION TO THE PENALTIES UNDER TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE, A MANUFACTURER THAT VIOLATES ANY PROVISION OF THIS SUBTITLE IS SUBJECT TO: 
 
 
 (1) A CIVIL PENALTY UP TO $50,000 PER VIOLATION; AND 
 
 
 (2) A MANDATORY RECALL OF A NONCOMPLIANT ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOY. 
 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
