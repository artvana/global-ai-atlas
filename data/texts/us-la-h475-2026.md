 HEALTH CARE/PROVIDERS: Requires a healthcare provider to obtain a patient&#39;s consent prior to recording a medical visit 
 
 
 AN ACT 
 To enact R.S. 37:22.1, relative to artificial intelligence; to require a healthcare professional to disclose to a patient the use of certain software; and to provide for related matters. 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the Legislature of Louisiana: 
 
 Section 1. R.S. 37:22.1 is hereby enacted to read as follows: 
 
 &sect; 22.1. Recordings; artificial intelligence; disclosure 
 
 
 A healthcare professional licensed by this Title shall verbally disclose the use of any recording device, software, or service to a patient before recording any part of an appointment or treatment to be transcribed by artificial intelligence.

 Berault 
 
 Abstract: Requires a healthcare professional to disclose to a patient the use of a recording device, software, or service to transcribe using artificial intelligence during an appointment or treatment. 
 
 Proposed law requires a healthcare professional licensed under present law to disclose to a patient, prior to an appointment or treatment, the use of any recording device, software, or service that utilizes artificial intelligence to transcribe communications between the professional and the patient. 
 (Adds R.S. 37:22.1) 
 
 Summary of Amendments Adopted by House 
 
 The Committee Amendments Proposed by House Committee on Health and Welfare to the original bill: 
 1. Remove the requirement to obtain patient consent to record for the purpose of transcribing a conversation between a healthcare professional and a patient using artificial intelligence. 
 2. Remove the option for the patient to opt out of being recorded during an appointment or treatment for the purpose of transcribing a conversation between a healthcare professional and a patient using artificial intelligence. 
 3. Make technical changes.
