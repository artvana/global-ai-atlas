 A bill to be entitled 
 An act relating to mandatory human reviews of insurance claim denials; creating s. 440.131, F.S.; defining terms; authorizing workers&#39; compensation carriers to use artificial intelligence systems and machine learning systems to assist in processing claims; prohibiting the use of artificial intelligence or machine learning systems as the sole basis for determining whether to reduce a claim payment or deny a claim or portion of a claim; requiring that carriers&#39; decisions to deny a claim or portion of a claim or reduce a claim be made by qualified human professionals; specifying the duties of qualified human professionals; requiring a carrier to maintain certain records; requiring carriers to include certain information in denial communications to claimants; requiring that certain carriers detail certain information in their claims-handling manual; authorizing the Department of Financial Services to conduct examinations and investigations under certain circumstances and to adopt rules; providing penalties; creating s. 627.4263, F.S.; defining terms; authorizing insurers to use artificial intelligence systems and machine learning systems to assist in processing claims; prohibiting the use of artificial intelligence or machine learning systems as the sole basis for determining whether to reduce a claim payment or deny a claim or portion of a claim; requiring that insurers&#39; decisions to deny a claim or portion of a claim or reduce a claim be made by qualified human professionals; specifying the duties of qualified human professionals; requiring an insurer to maintain certain records;  Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida: 
 
 
 Section 1. Section 440.131, Florida Statutes, is created to read: 
 
 
 440.131 Mandatory human reviews of claim denials.-- 
 
 
 (1) As used in this section, the term: 
 
 
 (a) &quot;Artificial intelligence system&quot; means a machine-based system that may have varying levels of autonomy and that can, for a given set of objectives, generate outputs, such as predictions, recommendations, or content, influencing decisions made in real or virtual environments. 
 
 
 (b) &quot;Machine learning system&quot; means an artificial intelligence system that has the ability to learn from provided data without being explicitly programmed. 
 
 
 (c) &quot;Qualified human professional&quot; means an individual who, under the Florida Insurance Code, has the authority to adjust or deny a claim or a portion of a claim and may exercise such authority over a particular claim. 
 
 
 (2) A carrier may use an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system to assist in processing claims, including generating recommendations to reduce a claim payment or to approve or deny a claim or a portion of a claim, in accordance with this section. 
 
 
 (3) A decision to reduce a claim payment or deny a claim or a portion of a claim may not be made solely on the basis of an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system. A carrier&#39;s decision to reduce a claim payment or deny a claim or a portion of a claim must be made by a qualified human professional. 
 
 
 (4) If an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system assists in processing a claim, the qualified human professional must do all of the following before reducing a claim payment or denying the claim or a portion of the claim: 
 
 
 (a) Analyze the facts of the claim and the terms of the insurance policy independently of any artificial intelligence system or machine learning system. 
 
 
 (b) Review the accuracy of any output generated by such a system. 
 
 
 (c) Determine that the claim or portion of the claim is not payable under the terms of the insurance policy and should be denied or that the claim payment should be reduced. 
 
 
 (5) A carrier shall maintain detailed records of the actions of qualified human professionals who are required to perform the actions under subsection (4), including: 
 
 
 (a) The name, the title, the business address, and the unique identifier associated with the name of the qualified human professional who made the decision to reduce the claim payment or deny the claim or a portion of the claim. 
 
 
 (b) The date and time of the qualified human professional&#39;s decision. 
 
 
 (c) Documentation of the basis for the reduction of the claim payment or denial of the claim or a portion of the claim, including any information provided by an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system. 
 
 
 (6) In all written denial communications to an injured employee, a carrier shall include: 
 
 
 (a) An e-mail address, a telephone number, a business address, and a unique identifier, in lieu of the name of the qualified human professional who made the decision to reduce the claim payment or deny the claim or a portion of the claim; and 
 
 
 (b) A written statement affirming that an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system did not serve as the sole basis for determining whether to reduce the claim payment or deny the claim or a portion of the claim. 
 
 
 (7) A carrier that uses an artificial intelligence system or a machine learning system as part of its claims-handling process shall detail in its claims-handling manual the manner in which such systems are to be used and the manner in which the carrier complies with this section. 
 
 
 (8) The department may conduct examinations and investigations it deems necessary to verify compliance with this section. 
 
 
 (9) The department may adopt rules to implement this section. 
 
 
 (10) Failure to comply with this section shall be considered a violation of this chapter and is subject to penalties as provided for in s. 440.525.
