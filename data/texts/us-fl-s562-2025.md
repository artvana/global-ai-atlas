Florida Senate - 2025                                     SB 562
By Senator Ingoglia
11-00409-25                                            2025562__
1                        A bill to be entitled
2         An act relating to the use of artificial intelligence
3         to detect firearms; creating s. 790.34, F.S.;
4         prohibiting the use of artificial intelligence to
5         detect firearms in public areas; providing criminal
6         penalties; providing exceptions; providing an
7         effective date.
8
9  Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida:
10
11         Section 1. Section 790.34, Florida Statutes, is created to
12  read:
13         790.34 Prohibited use of artificial intelligence to detect
14  firearms in public areas; penalties; exceptions.—
15         (1) Artificial intelligence may not be used, including, but
16  not limited to, in conjunction with any camera, video recording,
17  or live-streaming device, to detect firearms in public areas.
18         (2) A person who violates this section commits a
19  misdemeanor of the first degree, punishable as provided in s.
20  775.082 or s. 775.083.
21         (3) This section does not apply to:
22         (a) The grounds or facilities of a school as defined in s.
23  790.115.
24         (b) Any location listed in s. 790.06(12)(a).
25         Section 2. This act shall take effect October 1, 2025.
