# HB 1205 - 2025-26

Prohibiting the knowing distribution of a forged digital likeness.

Sponsors: [Ryu](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/15736), [Obras](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/35464), [Cortes](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/34028), [Callan](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/29092), [Pollet](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/16596), [Kloba](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/26168), [Timmons](https://leg.wa.gov/legislators/member/34037)
