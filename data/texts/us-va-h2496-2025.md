 HOUSE BILL NO. 2496 
 Offered January 8, 2025 
 Prefiled January 8, 2025 
 
 
 
 A BILL to amend the Code of Virginia by adding in Article 3 of Chapter 1 of Title 29.1 a section numbered 29.1-115, relating to Department of Wildlife Resources; use of a safety monitoring device; Fox Hill Boat Ramp. 
 
 Patron&mdash;Cordoza 
 Committee Referral Pending 
 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of Virginia: 
 
 
 
 1. That the Code of Virginia is amended by adding in Article 3 of Chapter 1 of Title 29.1 a section numbered 29.1-115 as follows: 
 
 
 
 &sect; 29.1-115. Use of a safety monitoring device; Fox Hill Boat Ramp. 
 
 
 
 The Department may place and operate a safety monitoring device at the Fox Hill Boat Ramp in the City of Hampton for the purpose of safety monitoring on state-owned property. For purposes of this section, &quot;safety monitoring device&quot; means a camera that uses machine learning to capture (i) license plate information or other visible characteristics of a vehicle and (ii) photographs, microphotographs, videotapes, or other recorded images.
