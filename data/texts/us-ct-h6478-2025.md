General Assembly                      Proposed Bill No. 6478
      January Session, 2025                LCO No. 3510


    Referred to Committee on GENERAL LAW

    Introduced by:
     REP. ROCHELLE, 104th Dist.




 AN ACT PROHIBITING THE USE OF CERTAIN SOFTWARE TO
 ESTABLISH OCCUPANCY LEVELS AND RENTAL RATES FOR THE
 PURPOSE OF INCREASING LANDLORD AND PROPERTY MANAGER
 PROFITS.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to prohibit the use of any
2      software that (1) is developed by a third party for the purpose of
3      providing services to one or more landlords or property managers, (2)
4      uses one or more algorithms to examine sensitive, proprietary or
5      publicly available data regarding the housing market, including, but not
6      limited to, data submitted by one or more landlords or property
7      managers, and (3) makes recommendations to one or more landlords or
8      property managers for the purpose of establishing occupancy levels and
9      rental rates that maximize landlord and property manager profits.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To prohibit the use of certain software that uses one or more algorithms
       to examine data for the purpose of increasing landlord and property
       manager profits by constraining housing supply and increasing rental
       rates.
