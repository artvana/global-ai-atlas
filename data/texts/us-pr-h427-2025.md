GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO
20th Legislative Assembly         1st Regular Session

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

H.B. 427

MARCH 20, 2025

Introduced by Representative Pérez Ramírez
and co-sponsored by Representative Aponte Hernández

Referred to the Committee on Education

LAW

To create the "Act for the Implementation of Artificial Intelligence in the English Program of the Public Education System in Puerto Rico," for the purpose of integrating artificial intelligence as a support tool in the teaching of English with emphasis on conversational English; to establish the Evaluation Committee; to set forth the legal framework; and to direct the Department of Education and the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) to implement the corresponding regulations.

STATEMENT OF MOTIVES

The Constitution of Puerto Rico enshrines the right of every person to an education that is conducive to the full development of their personality and to the strengthening of respect for the fundamental rights and freedoms of mankind. Likewise, it directs the government to establish a system of free public education, without any sectarian inclination, and free of charge at the primary and secondary levels, encompassing kindergarten through twelfth grade. Pursuant to that constitutional mandate, the school curriculum, school activities, and the departmental administrative environment must be governed.

On the other hand, Act No. 85-2018, as amended, known as the "Puerto Rico Educational Reform Act," declared, as public policy, that "[t]he student is the sole reason for the existence of the educational system," and that the objective is to develop the student to the maximum of their capacity, and to educate them "in an integral manner, attending to their interests and ensuring their particular needs are met." On the basis of these precepts, the goal is for all students to have a fair, equal, and meaningful opportunity to obtain a high-quality education and to achieve, at a minimum, proficiency in the basic subjects, including English.

Today, the command of conversational English is a fundamental skill for accessing better academic, employment, and professional opportunities. However, students in Puerto Rico's public schools face significant challenges in achieving fluency in that language.

Traditionally, the manner of learning a language in school requires the use of teaching methods that include: textbooks, recordings, and grammar exercises, among others. However, the rapid advance of technology has stimulated the emergence of new tools with the capacity to complement and even facilitate the traditional ones, so that the learning process is enjoyable, accessible, and varied. These technologies include interactive applications, online lessons and platforms, automatic translators, multimedia resources, and, recently, artificial intelligence (hereinafter, AI).

Platforms based on AI operate through natural language processing (NLP), which uses machine learning to process and interpret texts and data. These are dedicated to evaluating and adapting lessons according to the student's level. This, in turn, implies constantly optimizing the systems to improve the contents and teaching methods. In that sense, the integration of AI tools in the educational system offers, as never before, an innovative opportunity to personalize learning, reinforce linguistic skills, and improve educational outcomes.

Indeed, AI is transforming language learning by offering innovative tools that promise a more inclusive and personalized future for students of all levels, including those with special needs. Various research studies have demonstrated that artificial intelligence can provide immediate feedback, personalization of learning, and continuous interactive practice, which strengthens language acquisition. A study by the British Council underscores how AI can facilitate more adaptive and less invasive learning. This personalized approach is crucial to overcoming barriers in language learning, allowing students to progress at their own pace. See A. Edmett, N. Ichaporia, H. Crompton and R. Crichton, Artificial intelligence and English language teaching: Preparing for the future, July 2024.

With dizzying speed, the introduction of AI has caused and will continue to cause substantial changes in communication and creation that are linked to the teaching and learning processes. Therefore, this will require adjustments in the training of teachers and students in order to build and assimilate knowledge in an adequate manner. See M. Sánchez-Vera, La inteligencia artificial como recurso docente: usos y posibilidades para el profesorado, October 2023.

In view of all of the above, it is of utmost public interest to implement a pilot program that allows for the evaluation of the effectiveness of artificial intelligence as a support tool for the teaching of conversational English in the schools of the public education system in Puerto Rico. In addition, it is a step in the direction set by the "National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020" (NAIIA), approved by the Congress of the United States of America on January 1, 2021, which requires accelerating the research and application of artificial intelligence systems in both the public and private sectors. To that effect, this Act is approved.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:

Article 1.- Title.

This Act shall be known as the "Act for the Implementation of Artificial Intelligence in the English Program of the Public Education System in Puerto Rico."

Article 2.- Declaration of Public Policy.

It shall be the public policy of the Government of Puerto Rico to promote educational innovation through the use of emerging technologies that improve the effectiveness of the learning processes. Thus, the integration of artificial intelligence as a support tool seeks to strengthen linguistic competencies in conversational English, personalize the educational experience, and prepare students of the public education system to face the challenges of a globalized world.

The public policy will also seek to reduce educational gaps by attending to the most disadvantaged communities and those with the greatest academic lag, in order to ensure that all students in Puerto Rico's public schools have access to effective learning resources.

Article 3.- Definitions.

The following words and terms used in this Act shall have the meaning set forth below:

a) Committee – refers to the Pilot Project Evaluation Committee created under Article 5 of this Act.

b) Department – refers to the Department of Education of Puerto Rico.

c) AI Tool – Software or platform based on artificial intelligence that facilitates the learning and practice of conversational English through real-time interaction, personalized feedback, and analysis of the student's progress.

d) Artificial Intelligence (AI) – refers to the branch of computer science dedicated to creating systems and machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

e) Pilot Program – refers to the limited and supervised implementation project that seeks to evaluate the viability, effectiveness, and impact of the integration of artificial intelligence as a support tool in the teaching of conversational English in the public education system.

f) Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) – refers to the Office of the Executive Branch in charge of implementing, developing, and coordinating the government's public policy on innovation, information, and technology, created under Act No. 75-2019.

g) Secretary – refers to the Secretary of the Department of Education of Puerto Rico.

Article 4.- Purpose.

The purpose of this Act is to establish a pilot project to integrate an AI tool that helps to improve the learning of conversational English in the schools of the public education system in Puerto Rico; to evaluate the effectiveness of artificial intelligence in improving students' comprehension, fluency, pronunciation, and confidence in speaking English; to personalize the learning experience through immediate feedback and interactive practice adapted to each student's individual needs; to train teachers for the proper implementation and supervision of the use of AI tools in school facilities; and to analyze the impact of the Pilot Program on students' academic performance and conversational skills.

Article 5.- General principles that will govern the Pilot Program.

The Department will follow a policy on the use of artificial intelligence based on principles that guide its implementation in a responsible manner. These principles include the following:

a) Transparency in the use of artificial intelligence will ensure that the processes and criteria used are clear, understandable, and freely accessible to all participants in the Pilot Program.

b) The Department will assume responsibility for the development, implementation, and use of artificial intelligence, complying with applicable ethical and legal standards.

c) The privacy and confidentiality of information, both of the Department and of teachers and students, will be safeguarded through protocols and security measures.

d) The Department will promote adherence to ethical principles in the implementation of the Pilot Program.

e) The Department commits to respecting the fundamental rights of participants in the Pilot Program, as well as to avoiding all types of prohibited discrimination.

f) The Department will establish processes for evaluation and continuous improvement to monitor the performance of AI tools, in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

g) The Department will promote the collaboration and participation of teachers and students in the evaluation of the development and use of artificial intelligence in the Pilot Program, in such a way as to promote an inclusive approach in the design of educational tools.

h) The Department will foster what is established in these general principles, recognizing technological advances and the advantages that may derive from the ethical and responsible use of artificial intelligence in the educational function, to improve the efficiency and performance of students.

Article 6.- Creation of the Pilot Project; duration and scope.

In close collaboration with PRITS, the Department of Education will design, implement, and administer a Pilot Program for the purpose of integrating artificial intelligence as a support tool in the teaching of conversational English at the intermediate and upper levels in public schools.

The Pilot Program shall have a duration of three (3) school years from its implementation.

The Department will implement the project initially in twenty-five (25) public schools in twenty-one (21) schools, equivalent to three (3) schools per region, beginning in grades pre-K through 3rd grade, giving priority to school facilities most distant from urban centers, such as those in rural and hard-to-access areas. Likewise, it will give priority to those schools that, according to statistics, reflect a greater academic lag or low performance in the standardized English evaluations, as well as to students with language learning difficulties. Following this first phase, it will also include schools in urban areas to ensure representativeness. This Act will begin in those schools with facilities suitable for using Artificial Intelligence systems or Large Language Models.

Article 7.- Tools to be used; bidding process.

After the due process of evaluation and bidding, the Department will select those artificial intelligence platforms specialized in the teaching of conversational English, and will ensure that they meet, at a minimum, the following criteria:

a) Capacity for real-time interaction and personalization of learning.

b) Functions for immediate feedback on pronunciation, grammar, and fluency.

c) Compatibility with electronic devices available in public schools.

The Department may require additional requirements through the corresponding regulation. In addition, the AI tools adopted must comply with standards of privacy, ethics, accessibility, and pedagogical suitability.

Article 8.- Implementation of the Pilot Project.

a) Phases of implementation:

1. Phase I – First six (6) months.

i. Identification of the participating schools.

ii. Selection of the AI tools to be used.

iii. Training of teachers in the use of artificial intelligence platforms. The Department will guarantee the training of participating teachers in the use of the platforms and will ensure the effective integration of the technology into the English curriculum.

Both for the selection of the AI tools and for the training of teachers in the use of the platforms, the Department will consult and/or use the services and advice that PRITS may provide, in such a way as to ensure the best possible performance.

2. Phase II – Three (3)-year execution.

i. Regular use of AI tools in conversational English classes.

ii. Continuous monitoring of student performance.

3. Phase III – Evaluation of the Pilot Program.

i. The Department will conduct a quantitative and qualitative analysis of students' progress in conversational English.

ii. The Department will conduct perception surveys of students, teachers, and parents of participating students.

b) Technological access

The Department must ensure that the participating schools have the participating schools have the necessary technological infrastructure, including access to electronic devices and adequate internet connectivity.

Article 9.- Creation of the Evaluation Committee; evaluation and monitoring of the Pilot Program.

The Department will create an Evaluation Committee that will be composed of the following persons, to be designated by the Secretary:

a) An official of the Department.

b) An expert in educational technology.

c) A linguist specialized in the teaching of English as a second language.

d) A representative of the participating teachers.

e) A representative of the parents of participating students.

The Committee will create an evaluation and monitoring system that will include the following aspects:

a) Comparative analysis of students' grades and skills in the English language, before and after their participation in the Program.

b) Semiannual reports on the progress, challenges encountered, and results obtained.

c) Recommendations for the possible progressive implementation of the Program in all public schools.

Article 10.- Collaboration with the academic sector.

The Department, in addition to managing the collaboration of PRITS, may establish agreements and collaborative alliances with the academic sector for the development, testing, implementation, and improvement of AI tools that facilitate the learning and practice of conversational English.

Article 11.- Reports.

a) Progress reports.

The Department will submit an annual report to the Legislative Assembly and to the Governor of Puerto Rico that will include the following:

1. Quantitative and qualitative results of the conversational English learning of students participating in the Pilot Program.

2. Evaluations, from both teachers and students, regarding their learning experience with the AI tools.

3. Recommendations for the possible expansion of the Pilot Program to other public schools.

b) Final report.

No later than three (3) months after the culmination of the Pilot Program, the Department will submit a report to the Legislative Assembly detailing the following:

a) Impact of the Pilot Program on the level of conversational English of the students.

b) Evaluation of the effectiveness of the AI tools.

c) Viability and costs of implementing the Program at the state level.

Article 12.- Privacy and data protection.

The use of AI tools in the schools of the public education system must comply with all state and federal laws related to student privacy, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

Article 13.- Budget.

The funds necessary for the development and implementation of the Pilot Program created under Article 5 of this Act shall be appropriated in the line item of the General Budget of Expenditures corresponding to the Department of Education.

The Department may, in addition, request federal funds available for the financing of projects related to the implementation of technologies that use artificial intelligence.

Article 14.- Regulation.

The Department and PRITS shall create or adapt any regulation pertinent to what is established in this Act.

Article 15.- The provisions of this Act shall prevail over any other legal provision that is not in harmony with what is established herein.

Article 16.- Severability clause.

If any article, subsection, part, paragraph, or clause of this Act, or its application to any person or circumstance, is declared unconstitutional by a Court with jurisdiction, the judgment rendered shall not affect or invalidate the remaining provisions, but rather its effect shall be limited and shall extend to the subsection, part, paragraph, or clause of this Act, or its application, which has been declared unconstitutional.

Article 17.- Effectiveness.

This Act shall take effect on July 1, 2025, but the establishment of the Pilot Program must begin on or before January 31, 2026.
