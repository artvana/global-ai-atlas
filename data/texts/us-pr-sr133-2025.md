                                     RESOLUTION

To order the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate
      of Puerto Rico, to conduct a thorough investigation for the purpose of becoming aware of the
      technological advances implemented by the State Elections Commission, as
      well as its strategic and technological projection in view of future electoral events.

                             STATEMENT OF MOTIVES

       Technology plays a fundamental role in strengthening democratic systems.
At present, electoral processes must respond to standards of security, transparency,
efficiency, and citizen confidence. The technological modernization of electoral
bodies is not only desirable, but indispensable to guarantee the integrity of the vote
and the legitimacy of the electoral result.

       The State Elections Commission of Puerto Rico (CEE) is the entity
constitutionally entrusted with organizing and supervising the electoral processes on the
Island. This work requires a robust, reliable, and continuously updated technological
apparatus that allows for effectively addressing the logistical, operational, and
cybersecurity challenges faced by modern democracies.
                                                2


           In recent years, various situations have arisen that have generated
    concern regarding the technological preparedness of the CEE, including
    difficulties in handling voter databases and controversies related
    to electronic vote-counting equipment. These events have highlighted the
    need for a serious and proactive evaluation of the technological capabilities of the
    CEE.

           Likewise, it is necessary to know in advance the strategic and
    technological projection of the CEE in view of upcoming electoral events and other future
    elections. It is fundamental to identify the budgetary needs, the operational
    challenges, the implementation of new technologies, and the internal oversight
    processes that guarantee a modern, efficient, and secure electoral system.

           For these reasons, this Legislative Assembly understands it meritorious and urgent that the
    Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence of the Senate of Puerto Rico,
    carry out a thorough investigation on the technological advances that the
    State Elections Commission has implemented, as well as its strategic planning
    in this area. This investigation will allow the formulation of legislative recommendations,
    budgetary allocations, and possible collaborations aimed at strengthening the electoral
    system of Puerto Rico for the benefit of all citizens.
