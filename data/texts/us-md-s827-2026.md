 By: Senator Hester

 Assigned to: Finance 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Consumer Protection and Product Liability - Chatbots 
 
 FOR the purpose of regulating the manner in which a developer designs and creates and an operator makes available to users in the State a chatbot, including establishing safety and privacy protections for users, establishing an enhanced protection for users who are minors under a certain age, requiring the display of certain warnings when using a chatbot, and establishing reporting mechanisms and requirements; establishing that a violation of this Act is an unfair, abusive, or deceptive trade practice subject to enforcement and penalty provisions under the Maryland Consumer Protection Act and certain additional enforcement and penalty provisions; establishing that a chatbot is considered a product for certain product liability actions; applying certain requirements of this Act to units of State and local government; requiring the Behavioral Health Administration to develop educational materials that contain certain information relating to behavioral health care and services for individuals; and generally relating to the design, use, and operation of chatbots in the State.

 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Commercial Law 
 
 13-301. 
 Unfair, abusive, or deceptive trade practices include any: 
 (14) Violation of a provision of: 
 (xlvii) Title 14, Subtitle 50 of this article; or 
 
 Section 13-411.1(c)(2) of the Transportation Article; or 
 
 (XLIX) TITLE 14, SUBTITLE 51 OF THIS ARTICLE; OR 
 
 
 SUBTITLE 51. CHATBOTS. 
 
 
 14-5101. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;ADVERTISEMENT&quot; MEANS A WRITTEN STATEMENT, AN ORAL STATEMENT, AN ILLUSTRATION, OR A DEPICTION DISPLAYED IN EXCHANGE FOR MONEY OR OTHER CONSIDERATION, INCLUDING ACCESS TO DATA, THAT PROMOTES THE PURCHASE OR USE OF A GOOD OR A SERVICE OR INTEREST IN A BRAND, A GOOD, OR A SERVICE. 
 
 
 (C) &quot;AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT&quot; MEANS AN AFFIRMATIVE ACT SIGNIFYING A USER&#39;S FREELY GIVEN, UNAMBIGUOUS, AND INFORMED AUTHORIZATION IN RESPONSE TO A SPECIFIC REQUEST FROM A CHATBOT AND IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 14-5102 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (D) &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE&quot; OR &quot;AI&quot; MEANS AN ENGINEERED OR MACHINE-BASED SYSTEM THAT VARIES IN ITS LEVEL OF AUTONOMY AND THAT CAN, FOR EXPLICIT OR IMPLICIT OBJECTIVES, INFER FROM THE INPUT IT RECEIVES HOW TO GENERATE OUTPUTS THAT CAN INFLUENCE PHYSICAL OR VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS. 
 
 
 (E) &quot;CHATBOT&quot; MEANS A GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM WITH A NATURAL LANGUAGE INTERFACE THAT PROVIDES ADAPTIVE RESPONSES TO USER INPUTS. 
 
 
 (F) &quot;CHAT LOG&quot; MEANS A RECORD OF: 
 
 
 (1) INPUT DATA; AND 
 
 
 (2) SYNTHETIC CONTENT GENERATED BY A CHATBOT AND SHARED WITH THE USER. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;COLLECT&quot; MEANS TO CREATE, BUY, RENT, GATHER, OBTAIN, RECEIVE, ACCESS, OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRE PERSONAL DATA OR INPUT DATA BY ANY MEANS THROUGH A USER&#39;S INTERACTIONS WITH A CHATBOT. 
 
 
 (H) &quot;DE-IDENTIFIED DATA&quot; MEANS DATA HANDLED IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 14-5103 OF THIS SUBTITLE THAT CANNOT REASONABLY BE: 
 
 
 (1) USED TO INFER INFORMATION ABOUT A USER; OR 
 
 
 (2) LINKED TO AN IDENTIFIABLE USER. 
 
 
 (I) &quot;DEVELOPER&quot; MEANS A PERSON THAT DESIGNS AND CREATES A CHATBOT THAT AN OPERATOR MAKES AVAILABLE TO A USER IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (J) &quot;GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE&quot; MEANS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM THAT CAN GENERATE SYNTHETIC CONTENT INCLUDING TEXT, AN IMAGE, A VIDEO, OR AUDIO DERIVED FROM THE AI SYSTEM&#39;S TRAINING DATA. 
 
 
 (K) &quot;INPUT DATA&quot; MEANS INFORMATION AND CONTENT INCLUDING TEXT, AN IMAGE, A VIDEO, OR AUDIO THAT A USER MAKES AVAILABLE TO A CHATBOT. 
 
 
 (L) &quot;OPERATOR&quot; MEANS A PERSON, INCLUDING A DEVELOPER, THAT MAKES AVAILABLE A CHATBOT TO A USER IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (M) (1) &quot;PERSONAL DATA&quot; MEANS INFORMATION THAT IS LINKED OR MAY REASONABLY BE LINKED TO A USER. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;PERSONAL DATA&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) DE-IDENTIFIED DATA; OR 
 
 
 (II) INFORMATION THAT IS LAWFULLY AVAILABLE TO THE GENERAL PUBLIC: 
 
 
 1. AS A RESULT OF A DISCLOSURE BY OR THROUGH ANY UNIT OF GOVERNMENT IN ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE LAWS AND REGULATIONS; OR 
 
 
 2. AS WIDELY DISTRIBUTED INFORMATION. 
 
 
 (N) &quot;PROCESS&quot; MEANS THE USE, STORAGE, ANALYSIS, DISCLOSURE, DELETION, OR MODIFICATION OF INPUT DATA, INCLUDING PERSONAL DATA. 
 
 
 (O) (1) &quot;PROFILE&quot; MEANS PROCESSING PERFORMED ON INPUT DATA, INCLUDING PERSONAL DATA, TO DETECT AND CLASSIFY OR DESIGNATE PERSONALITY AND BEHAVIORAL CHARACTERISTICS OF A USER. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;PROFILE&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE PROCESSING CHAT LOGS FOR USER SAFETY PURPOSES OR AS REQUIRED BY LAW. 
 
 
 (P) &quot;SELL&quot; MEANS THE EXCHANGE OF INPUT DATA, INCLUDING PERSONAL DATA, FOR MONEY OR OTHER CONSIDERATION. 
 
 
 (Q) (1) &quot;TRAINING&quot; MEANS THE USE OF INPUT DATA AS TRAINING DATA TO ADJUST OR MODIFY A CHATBOT. 
 
 
 (2) &quot;TRAINING&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) TESTING FOR USER SAFETY PURPOSES, INCLUDING FOR RISKS OF HARM TO A USER; 
 
 
 (II) ADJUSTMENTS OR OTHER MODIFICATIONS TO ADDRESS IDENTIFIED RISKS OF HARM TO A USER; OR 
 
 
 (III) ADJUSTMENTS OR OTHER MODIFICATIONS REQUIRED BY LAW. 
 
 
 (R) &quot;USER&quot; MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO INTERACTS OR ENGAGES DIRECTLY WITH A CHATBOT. 
 
 
 14-5102. 
 
 
 (A) IF A PROVISION OF THIS SUBTITLE REQUIRES A PERSON TO OBTAIN A USER&#39;S AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT, THE REQUEST FOR AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT: 
 
 
 (1) SHALL: 
 
 
 (I) BE PROVIDED IN A CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS MANNER; 
 
 
 (II) BE REASONABLY ACCESSIBLE TO A USER WITH A DISABILITY; 
 
 
 (III) BE AVAILABLE TO A USER IN EACH LANGUAGE IN WHICH THE CHATBOT IS AVAILABLE TO THE USER; AND 
 
 
 (IV) CONTAIN AN OPTION TO REFUSE TO PROVIDE AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT THAT IS AT LEAST AS PROMINENT AS, AND CONSISTS OF NOT MORE THAN THE SAME NUMBER OF STEPS THAN, THE OPTION TO PROVIDE AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT; AND 
 
 
 (2) MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (I) INFER FROM A USER&#39;S INACTION OR CONTINUED USE OF THE CHATBOT THAT THE USER PROVIDES AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT; 
 
 
 (II) BE PART OF THE CHATBOT&#39;S GENERAL TERMS OF USE; OR 
 
 
 (III) BE OFFERED USING A USER INTERFACE DESIGNED OR MANIPULATED WITH THE SUBSTANTIAL EFFECT OF SUBVERTING OR IMPAIRING THE AUTONOMY, DECISION MAKING, OR CHOICE OF A USER, SUCH AS A PRACTICE CONSIDERED TO BE A DARK PATTERN BY THE FEDERAL TRADE COMMISSION. 
 
 
 (B) IF A PERSON OBTAINS A USER&#39;S CONSENT BY USE OF A MATERIALLY FALSE OR MISLEADING STATEMENT OR REPRESENTATION, THE USER&#39;S CONSENT IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE. 
 
 
 14-5103. 
 
 
 FOR INFORMATION TO BE CONSIDERED DE-IDENTIFIED DATA UNDER THIS SUBTITLE, THE OPERATOR SHALL: 
 
 
 (1) ENSURE AND PUBLICLY COMMIT IN A CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS MANNER THAT AGGREGATED DATA MAY NOT BE USED TO INFER INFORMATION ABOUT A USER OR ESTABLISH A LINK TO AN IDENTIFIABLE USER; AND 
 
 
 (2) CONTRACTUALLY OBLIGATE A THIRD PARTY THAT RECEIVES DATA FROM AN OPERATOR TO ENSURE THAT AGGREGATED DATA MAY NOT BE USED TO INFER INFORMATION ABOUT A USER OR ESTABLISH A LINK TO AN IDENTIFIABLE USER. 
 
 
 14-5104. 
 
 
 (A) AN OPERATOR MAY NOT MAKE AVAILABLE TO A USER IN THE STATE A CHATBOT THAT IS CAPABLE OF: 
 
 
 (1) SUBJECT TO SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION: 
 
 
 (I) USING INPUT DATA AS TRAINING DATA FOR THE CHATBOT, UNLESS THE USER PROVIDES AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT; OR 
 
 
 (II) PROCESSING OR USING PERSONAL DATA OTHER THAN INPUT DATA AS TRAINING DATA FOR THE CHATBOT, UNLESS NECESSARY TO RESPOND TO A USER PROMPT AND THE USER PROVIDES AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT; 
 
 
 (2) PROCESSING A USER&#39;S CHAT LOG TO: 
 
 
 (I) DETERMINE WHETHER TO DISPLAY AN ADVERTISEMENT FOR A GOOD OR A SERVICE TO THE USER; OR 
 
 
 (II) CUSTOMIZE AN ADVERTISEMENT DISPLAYED TO THE USER; 
 
 
 (3) PROFILING A USER OTHER THAN FOR WHAT IS NECESSARY TO RESPOND TO A USER PROMPT; 
 
 
 (4) RETAINING A CHAT LOG FOR LONGER THAN 10 YEARS, UNLESS REQUIRED BY LAW; OR 
 
 
 (5) PROVIDING ADVICE OR A SERVICE THAT AN INDIVIDUAL CANNOT LAWFULLY PROVIDE WITHOUT A LICENSE, INCLUDING FINANCIAL, LEGAL, OR MEDICAL ADVICE, IN VIOLATION OF APPLICABLE STATE LICENSING LAWS. 
 
 
 (B) IF AN OPERATOR OR A DEVELOPER KNOWS OR REASONABLY SHOULD KNOW THAT A USER IS A MINOR WHO IS UNDER THE AGE OF 13 YEARS, UNLESS THE USER&#39;S PARENT OR GUARDIAN PROVIDES WRITTEN AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT TO THE OPERATOR OR THE DEVELOPER, THE OPERATOR AND THE DEVELOPER MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (1) USE INPUT DATA AS TRAINING DATA FOR THE CHATBOT; OR 
 
 
 (2) PROCESS PERSONAL DATA OTHER THAN INPUT DATA. 
 
 
 14-5105. 
 
 
 (A) A DEVELOPER SHALL ESTABLISH AND AN OPERATOR SHALL PROVIDE TO A USER OF THE OPERATOR&#39;S CHATBOT CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS WARNINGS THAT CHATBOTS ARE ARTIFICIALLY GENERATED AND NOT HUMAN USING BOTH: 
 
 
 (1) A STATIC, PERSISTENT WARNING THAT CONTINUOUSLY APPEARS ON THE SCREEN; AND 
 
 
 (2) A DYNAMIC WARNING THAT POPS UP ON THE SCREEN AND REQUIRES THE USER TO RESPOND: 
 
 
 (I) AT THE START OF A USER&#39;S INTERACTION WITH A CHATBOT; 
 
 
 (II) AFTER EVERY HOUR OF A USER&#39;S CONTINUOUS INTERACTION WITH A CHATBOT; AND 
 
 
 (III) WHEN PROMPTED BY A USER IN A MANNER THAT QUESTIONS HOW THE CHATBOT FUNCTIONS OR PROVIDES RESPONSES. 
 
 
 (B) A WARNING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (A) OF THIS SECTION SHALL BE: 
 
 
 (1) AVAILABLE TO A USER IN EACH LANGUAGE IN WHICH THE CHATBOT IS AVAILABLE TO THE USER; 
 
 
 (2) REASONABLY ACCESSIBLE TO A USER WITH A DISABILITY; 
 
 
 (3) PROMINENTLY DISPLAYED WITHIN THE INTERFACE; AND 
 
 
 (4) AT A MINIMUM, BASED ON A MODEL WARNING DEVELOPED BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL UNDER &sect; 14-5111(1) OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 14-5106. 
 
 
 A DEVELOPER SHALL ESTABLISH AND AN OPERATOR SHALL PROVIDE TO A USER OF THE OPERATOR&#39;S CHATBOT A MECHANISM FOR A USER TO INSPECT THE USER&#39;S CHAT LOG THAT THE OPERATOR HAS RETAINED IN A FORMAT THAT IS PORTABLE, DOWNLOADABLE, AND HUMAN-AND MACHINE-READABLE. 
 
 
 14-5107. 
 
 
 (A) A DEVELOPER SHALL ESTABLISH AND AN OPERATOR SHALL PROVIDE: 
 
 
 (1) A COMPREHENSIVE DATA SECURITY PROGRAM THAT CONTAINS ADMINISTRATIVE, TECHNICAL, AND PHYSICAL SAFEGUARDS THAT ARE PROPORTIONATE TO THE VOLUME AND NATURE OF THE PERSONAL DATA AND CHAT LOGS RETAINED BY THE OPERATOR; AND 
 
 
 (2) MONTHLY SAFETY TESTING. 
 
 
 (B) (1) AN OPERATOR SHALL PUBLISH A WRITTEN EXPLANATION OF THE PROGRAM ESTABLISHED AND MAINTAINED UNDER SUBSECTION (A)(1) OF THIS SECTION ON THE OPERATOR&#39;S PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE WEBSITE. 
 
 
 (2) EACH MONTH, AN OPERATOR SHALL PUBLISH THE FINDINGS OF THE SAFETY TESTING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (A)(2) OF THIS SECTION ON THE OPERATOR&#39;S PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE WEBSITE. 
 
 
 14-5108. 
 
 
 (A) AN OPERATOR OR A DEVELOPER MAY NOT: 
 
 
 (1) SELL A CHAT LOG; 
 
 
 (2) DISCRIMINATE OR RETALIATE AGAINST A USER FOR: 
 
 
 (I) REFUSING TO PROVIDE AFFIRMATIVE CONSENT AS REQUIRED BY THIS SUBTITLE; OR 
 
 
 (II) ACCESSING OR REVIEWING A CHAT LOG AS ALLOWED BY THIS SUBTITLE; OR 
 
 
 (3) ADVERTISE THAT A CHATBOT IS CAPABLE OF PROVIDING ADVICE OR A SERVICE THAT AN INDIVIDUAL CANNOT LAWFULLY PROVIDE WITHOUT A LICENSE, INCLUDING A REPRESENTATION THAT USER INPUTS OR CHAT LOGS ARE PROTECTED BY AN INAPPLICABLE FIDUCIARY OR OTHER LEGAL DUTY OF CONFIDENTIALITY. 
 
 
 (B) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, THE DISCLOSURE OF INPUT DATA, INCLUDING PERSONAL DATA, TO A THIRD PARTY RESPONSIBLE FOR PROCESSING THE DATA IS NOT CONSIDERED SELLING A CHAT LOG. 
 
 
 14-5109. 
 
 
 (A) A VIOLATION OF THIS SUBTITLE IS: 
 
 
 (1) AN UNFAIR, ABUSIVE, OR DECEPTIVE TRADE PRACTICE WITHIN THE MEANING OF TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE; AND 
 
 
 (2) SUBJECT TO THE ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTY PROVISIONS CONTAINED IN TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (B) (1) IN ADDITION TO THE REMEDIES CONTAINED IN TITLE 13 OF THIS ARTICLE: 
 
 
 (I) THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY BRING AN ACTION TO SEEK DECLARATORY RELIEF; 
 
 
 (II) AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS AFFECTED BY A VIOLATION OF THIS SUBTITLE MAY BRING AN ACTION TO SEEK AN INJUNCTION, DECLARATORY RELIEF, OR DAMAGES IN ACCORDANCE WITH PARAGRAPH (2) OF THIS SUBSECTION; AND 
 
 
 (III) A CHATBOT SHALL BE CONSIDERED A PRODUCT FOR WHICH: 
 
 
 1. AN OPERATOR AND A DEVELOPER HAVE AN AFFIRMATIVE DUTY TO ENSURE DOES NOT INJURE OR HARM A USER; 
 
 
 2. AN OPERATOR OR A DEVELOPER MAY BE HELD STRICTLY LIABLE FOR CAUSING INJURY OR HARM TO A USER; AND 
 
 
 3. AN INDIVIDUAL MAY BRING AN ACTION FOR A DESIGN DEFECT, A MANUFACTURING DEFECT, OR A MARKETING DEFECT. 
 
 
 (2) IN ADDITION TO COMPENSATORY DAMAGES AWARDED UNDER AN ACTION TO RECOVER FOR INJURY OR LOSS UNDER &sect; 13-408 OF THIS ARTICLE, A COURT MAY ORDER A PERSON WHO KNOWINGLY VIOLATES THIS SUBTITLE TO PAY AN INDIVIDUAL AFFECTED BY A VIOLATION OF THIS SUBTITLE PUNITIVE DAMAGES NOT EXCEEDING THREE TIMES THE AMOUNT OF ANY ACTUAL DAMAGES RESULTING FROM THE INJURY OR LOSS. 
 
 
 (C) IF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL RECOVERS A CIVIL PENALTY THAT IS NOT PAYABLE TO AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS AFFECTED BY A VIOLATION OF THIS SUBTITLE, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL DISTRIBUTE THE PENALTY TO THE BEHAVIORAL HEALTH WORKFORCE INVESTMENT FUND ESTABLISHED UNDER &sect; 10-1502 OF THE HEALTH - GENERAL ARTICLE. 
 
 
 14-5110. 
 
 
 (A) THE DUTIES, OBLIGATIONS, AND REMEDIES UNDER THIS SUBTITLE: 
 
 
 (1) ARE IN ADDITION TO OTHER DUTIES, OBLIGATIONS, AND REMEDIES PROVIDED UNDER ANY FEDERAL, STATE, OR LOCAL LAW OR REGULATION; AND 
 
 
 (2) MAY NOT BE CONSTRUED TO IMPAIR OR DENY ANY OTHER DUTY, OBLIGATION, OR REMEDY PROVIDED UNDER ANY FEDERAL, STATE, OR LOCAL LAW OR REGULATION. 
 
 
 (B) EXCEPT FOR &sect; 14-5109 OF THIS SUBTITLE, THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SUBTITLE APPLY TO ANY UNIT OF STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT THAT DESIGNS, CREATES, OR MAKES AVAILABLE A CHATBOT. 
 
 
 14-5111. 
 
 
 THE DIVISION OF CONSUMER PROTECTION OF THE OFFICE OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL ADOPT REGULATIONS TO CARRY OUT THE PROVISIONS OF THIS SUBTITLE, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 (1) THE STANDARD CONTENT AND FORM OF A WARNING REQUIRED UNDER &sect; 14-5105 OF THIS SUBTITLE, INCLUDING MODEL WARNINGS; 
 
 
 (2) THE SPECIFIC PARAMETERS AND METRICS OF THE SECURITY PROGRAM AND SAFETY TESTING REQUIRED UNDER &sect; 14-5107(A) OF THIS SUBTITLE, INCLUDING THE REPORTING REQUIREMENTS; AND 
 
 
 (3) A PLAN TO DISSEMINATE TO THE PUBLIC THE EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS DEVELOPED BY THE BEHAVIORAL HEALTH ADMINISTRATION UNDER &sect; 10-918 OF THE HEALTH - GENERAL ARTICLE. 
 
 
 14-5112. 
 
 
 THIS SUBTITLE MAY BE CITED AS THE CURBING HARMFUL AI TECHNOLOGY ACT. 
 
 Article - Health - General 
 Part III. Enforcement 
 MISCELLANEOUS . 
 
 10-918. 
 
 
 (A) THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL DEVELOP EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS THAT CONTAIN INFORMATION: 
 
 
 (1) DESCRIBING HOW A PERSON MAY OBTAIN BEHAVIORAL HEALTH CARE, AS DEFINED IN &sect; 10-101 OF THIS TITLE, FROM A LICENSED MEDICAL PROFESSIONAL IN THE STATE; AND 
 
 
 (2) ABOUT FREE OR LOW-COST SERVICES AVAILABLE TO INDIVIDUALS IN THE STATE WHO ARE EXPERIENCING A MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS. 
 
 
 (B) THE EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS DEVELOPED UNDER SUBSECTION (A) OF THIS SECTION SHALL BE: 
 
 
 (1) DISSEMINATED TO THE PUBLIC BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 14-5111(3) OF THE COMMERCIAL LAW ARTICLE; AND 
 
 
 (2) USED FOR ANY OTHER PURPOSE AS DETERMINED BY THE ADMINISTRATION. 
 
 10-1502. 
 (a) There is a Behavioral Health Workforce Investment Fund. 
 (e) The Fund shall consist of: 
 
 (1) CIVIL PENALTIES DISTRIBUTED TO THE FUND UNDER &sect; 14-5109 OF THE COMMERCIAL LAW ARTICLE; 
 
 
 (1) 
 (2) Money appropriated in the State budget to the Fund; 
 
 (2) 
 (3) Interest earnings of the Fund; and 
 
 (3) 
 (4) Any other money from any other source accepted for the benefit of the Fund.
