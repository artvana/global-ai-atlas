 AN ACT PROHIBITING THE UNAUTHORIZED DISSEMINATION OF
 SYNTHETIC INTIMATE IMAGES OF ANOTHER PERSON.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That title 53a of the general statutes be amended to establish the crime
2      of unlawful unauthorized dissemination of a synthetic intimate image
3      of another person as a class A misdemeanor if the image is disseminated
4      to one person and a class D felony if the image is disseminated to more
5      than one person by means of an interactive computer service,
6      information service or telecommunications service.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To prohibit unauthorized dissemination of certain synthetic images.
