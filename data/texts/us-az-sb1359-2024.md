Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona:	1
Section 1. 	Title 	16, 	chapter 	7, 	article 	1, 	Arizona 	Revised	2
Statutes, is amended by adding section 16-1023, to read:	3
16-1023. 	Deep 	fakes; 	candidates; 	political 	parties;	4
exemptions; violation; classification; definitions	5
A. 	WITHIN NINETY DAYS BEFORE AN ELECTION AT WHICH A CANDIDATE FOR	6
ELECTED OFFICE WILL APPEAR ON THE BALLOT, A PERSON WHO ACTS AS A CREATOR	7
SHALL NOT SPONSOR OR CREATE AND DISTRIBUTE A SYNTHETIC MEDIA MESSAGE THAT	8
THE PERSON KNOWS IS A DECEPTIVE AND FRAUDULENT DEEPFAKE OF THAT CANDIDATE	9
OR OF A POLITICAL PARTY THAT IS ON THAT BALLOT UNLESS THE SYNTHETIC MEDIA	10
MESSAGE INCLUDES A CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS DISCLOSURE THAT STATES THAT THE	11
MEDIA INCLUDES CONTENT GENERATED BY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.	12
B. 	IF THE MEDIA CONSISTS OF AUDIO ONLY AND NO VISUAL DISCLOSURE IS	13
POSSIBLE, THE DISCLOSURE SHALL BE READ IN A CLEARLY SPOKEN MANNER AND IN A	14
PITCH THAT CAN BE EASILY HEARD BY THE AVERAGE LISTENER, AT THE BEGINNING	15
OF THE AUDIO, AT THE END OF THE AUDIO AND, IF THE AUDIO IS LONGER THAN TWO	16
MINUTES IN LENGTH, INTERSPERSED WITHIN THE AUDIO AT INTERVALS OF NOT MORE	17
THAN TWO MINUTES EACH.	18
C. 	A 	CANDIDATE 	WHOSE 	APPEARANCE, 	ACTION 	OR 	SPEECH 	IS 	DEPICTED	19
THROUGH 	THE 	USE 	OF 	A 	DECEPTIVE 	AND 	FRAUDULENT 	DEEPFAKE 	IN 	VIOLATION 	OF	20
THIS 	SECTION 	MAY 	SEEK 	INJUNCTIVE 	OR 	OTHER 	EQUITABLE 	RELIEF 	FROM 	THE	21
SPONSOR 	OR 	THE 	CREATOR 	OF 	THE 	MEDIA 	PROHIBITING 	THE 	PUBLICATION 	OF 	THE	22
DECEPTIVE AND FRAUDULENT DEEPFAKE.	23
D. 	THIS SECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO:	24
1. 	A RADIO OR TELEVISION BROADCASTING STATION, INCLUDING A CABLE OR	25
SATELLITE TELEVISION OPERATOR, PROGRAMMER OR PRODUCER:	26
(a) 	THAT 	BROADCASTS 	A 	DECEPTIVE 	AND 	FRAUDULENT 	DEEPFAKE 	THAT 	IS	27
PROHIBITED BY THIS SECTION AND THAT IS PART OF A BONA FIDE NEWSCAST, NEWS	28
INTERVIEW 	OR 	NEWS 	DOCUMENTARY 	OR 	ON-THE-SPOT 	COVERAGE 	OF 	BONA 	FIDE 	NEWS	29
EVENTS, 	IF 	THE 	BROADCAST 	CLEARLY 	ACKNOWLEDGES 	THROUGH 	ITS 	CONTENT 	OR 	A	30
DISCLOSURE 	IN 	A 	MANNER 	THAT 	CAN 	BE 	EASILY 	HEARD 	OR 	READ 	BY 	THE 	AVERAGE	31
LISTENER OR VIEWER THAT THERE ARE QUESTIONS ABOUT THE AUTHENTICITY OF THE	32
MATERIALLY DECEPTIVE AUDIO OR VISUAL MEDIA.	33
(b) 	WHEN 	IT 	IS 	PAID 	TO 	BROADCAST 	A 	DECEPTIVE 	AND 	FRAUDULENT	34
DEEPFAKE AND HAS MADE A GOOD FAITH EFFORT TO ESTABLISH THAT THE DEPICTION	35
IS NOT A DECEPTIVE AND FRAUDULENT DEEPFAKE.	36
2. 	AN INTERNET WEBSITE OR A REGULARLY PUBLISHED NEWSPAPER, MAGAZINE	37
OR 	OTHER 	PERIODICAL 	OF 	GENERAL 	CIRCULATION, 	INCLUDING 	AN 	INTERNET 	OR	38
ELECTRONIC 	PUBLICATION, 	THAT 	ROUTINELY 	CARRIES 	NEWS 	AND 	COMMENTARY 	OF	39
GENERAL INTEREST AND THAT PUBLISHES MATERIALLY DECEPTIVE AUDIO OR VISUAL	40
MEDIA THAT IS PROHIBITED BY THIS SECTION IF THE PUBLICATION CLEARLY STATES	41
THAT 	THE 	MATERIALLY 	DECEPTIVE 	AUDIO 	OR 	VISUAL 	MEDIA 	WAS 	GENERATED 	BY	42
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.	43
3. 	MEDIA THAT CONSTITUTES SATIRE OR PARODY.	44

4. 	AN INTERACTIVE COMPUTER SERVICE AS DEFINED IN 47 UNITED STATES	1

E. 	A PERSON WHO VIOLATES SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION IS GUILTY OF	3
A CLASS 1 MISDEMEANOR, EXCEPT THAT:	4
1. 	A PERSON WHO COMMITS THE VIOLATION WITHIN FIVE YEARS OF ONE OR	5
MORE PRIOR CONVICTIONS UNDER THIS SECTION IS GUILTY OF A CLASS 4 FELONY.	6
2. 	A 	PERSON 	WHO 	COMMITS 	THE 	VIOLATION 	WITH 	THE 	INTENT 	TO 	CAUSE	7
VIOLENCE OR BODILY HARM IS GUILTY OF A CLASS 6 FELONY.	8
F. 	FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:	9
1. 	"CREATOR":	10
(a) 	MEANS 	ANY 	PERSON 	THAT 	USES 	ARTIFICIAL 	INTELLIGENCE 	OR 	OTHER	11
DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY TO GENERATE SYNTHETIC MEDIA.	12
(b) 	DOES 	NOT 	INCLUDE 	THE 	PROVIDER 	OF 	ANY 	TECHNOLOGY 	USED 	IN 	THE	13
CREATION OF SYNTHETIC MEDIA.	14
2. 	"DECEPTIVE 	AND 	FRAUDULENT 	DEEPFAKE" 	MEANS 	SYNTHETIC 	MEDIA 	THAT	15
DEPICTS 	A 	CANDIDATE 	OR 	POLITICAL 	PARTY 	WITH 	THE 	INTENT 	TO 	INJURE 	THE	16
REPUTATION 	OF 	THE 	CANDIDATE 	OR 	POLITICAL 	PARTY 	OR 	OTHERWISE 	DECEIVE 	A	17
VOTER AND THAT EITHER:	18
(a) 	APPEARS 	TO 	A 	REASONABLE 	PERSON 	TO 	DEPICT 	A 	REAL 	INDIVIDUAL	19
SAYING OR DOING SOMETHING THAT DID NOT ACTUALLY OCCUR IN REALITY.	20
(b) 	PROVIDES 	TO 	A 	REASONABLE 	PERSON 	A 	FUNDAMENTALLY 	DIFFERENT	21
UNDERSTANDING 	OR 	IMPRESSION 	OF 	THE 	APPEARANCE, 	ACTION 	OR 	SPEECH 	IN 	AN	22
IMAGE, AUDIO RECORDING OR VIDEO RECORDING THAN A REASONABLE PERSON WOULD	23
HAVE FROM AN UNALTERED, ORIGINAL VERSION OF THE IMAGE, AUDIO RECORDING OR	24
VIDEO RECORDING.	25
3. 	"SYNTHETIC 	MEDIA" 	MEANS 	AN 	IMAGE, 	AUDIO 	RECORDING 	OR 	VIDEO	26
RECORDING OF AN INDIVIDUAL'S APPEARANCE, SPEECH OR CONDUCT THAT HAS BEEN	27
CREATED OR INTENTIONALLY MANIPULATED WITH THE USE OF DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY IN	28
A MANNER TO CREATE A REALISTIC BUT FALSE IMAGE, AUDIO OR VIDEO.	29
Sec. 2. 	Severability	30

circumstance 	is 	held 	invalid, 	the 	invalidity 	does 	not 	affect 	other	32
provisions or applications of the act that can be given effect without the	33
invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this	34
act are severable.	35
