June 03, 2025, Introduced by Reps. Rheingans, Coffia, Price, Byrnes, Morgan, Wegela,
Tsernoglou, Breen, Young, Wilson and MacDonell and referred to Committee on
Communications and Technology.
A bill to amend 1939 PA 280, entitled
"The social welfare act,"
(MCL 400.1 to 400.119b) by adding section 107b.
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT:
1 Sec. 107b. The department or a contracted health plan shall
2 not deny, modify, or delay a claim under the medical assistance
3 program based on a review using artificial intelligence.
