# Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024

**Laws of Malaysia — Act A1727**

*An Act to amend the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 [Act 709].*

- **Date of Royal Assent**: 9 October 2024
- **Date of publication in the Gazette**: 17 October 2024
- **Commencement**: Dates to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette; different dates may be appointed for different provisions.

---

## Section 1 — Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024**.

---

## Section 2 — General amendment

The principal Act is amended by substituting **"data user"** and **"data users"** wherever appearing with **"data controller"** and **"data controllers"**, except in the definition of "register" under section 4 and in section 9.

---

## Section 3 — Amendment of section 4 (Definitions)

**(a)** In the definition of "register": "Register of Data Users, Register of Data User Forums" is replaced with **"Register of Data Controllers, Register of Data Controller Forums"**.

**(b)** New definition inserted:

> **"biometric data"** means any personal data resulting from technical processing relating to the physical, physiological or behavioural characteristics of a person.

**(c)** In the definition of **"sensitive personal data"**: **biometric data** is added as a category of sensitive personal data.

**(d)** New definition inserted:

> **"personal data breach"** means any breach of personal data, loss of personal data, misuse of personal data or unauthorized access of personal data.

**(e)** In the definition of **"requestor"**: scope extended to include **data portability requests** (previously only data access and data correction requests).

**(f)** In the definition of **"data subject"**: **deceased individuals are excluded** from the definition.

---

## Section 4 — Amendment of section 5 (General)

**(a)** New subsection (1A):

> Where processing of personal data is carried out by a **data processor** on behalf of the data controller, the data processor shall comply with the **Security Principle** as specified in section 9.

**(b)** Penalty for breach of section 5 increased from **RM 300,000** or imprisonment not exceeding **2 years** or both, to **RM 1,000,000** or imprisonment not exceeding **3 years** or both. Applies to both data controllers and (now) data processors.

---

## Section 5 — Amendment of section 9 (Security Principle)

**Data processors** are made directly subject to the Security Principle alongside data controllers. Where a data controller engages a data processor, the data processor (not the data controller on behalf of the processor) must ensure the security measures are taken.

---

## Section 6 — New Division 1A of Part II — Accountability of personal data

### Section 12A — Appointment of data protection officer

1. A **data controller** shall appoint one or more **data protection officers** accountable to the data controller for compliance with this Act.

2. Where processing is carried out by a **data processor**, the data processor shall appoint one or more data protection officers accountable to the data processor for compliance with this Act.

3. The data controller shall **notify the Commissioner** of the appointment of a data protection officer in the manner and form determined by the Commissioner.

4. Appointment of a data protection officer does not discharge the data controller or data processor from any duties and functions under this Act.

### Section 12B — Data breach notification

1. Where a data controller has reason to believe that a **personal data breach** has occurred, the data controller shall, **as soon as practicable**, notify the Commissioner in the manner and form determined by the Commissioner.

2. Where the personal data breach causes or is likely to cause **any significant harm** to the data subject, the data controller shall notify the **data subject** of the breach **without unnecessary delay**.

3. A data controller who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding **RM 250,000** or imprisonment not exceeding **2 years**, or both.

---

## Section 9 — New section 43A — Right to data portability

1. A data subject may request the data controller to transmit their personal data to another data controller of their choice **directly**, by giving notice in writing **by way of electronic means**.

2. The request is subject to **technical feasibility and compatibility of the data format**.

3. Upon receiving a portability request, the data controller shall complete transmission within the **prescribed period**.

---

## Section 12 — Amendment of section 129 — Cross-border transfer of personal data

The previous ministerial whitelist mechanism (requiring the Minister to specify by Gazette notice the places to which personal data could be transferred) is **deleted**.

The amended provision allows a **data controller** to transfer personal data to a place outside Malaysia against statutory criteria (the controller-led assessment model). The Minister no longer maintains a whitelist of approved destinations.

---

## Section 13 — Amendment of section 136

**Electronic service of documents** is expressly permitted (service by electronic means is added as an authorised mode).

---

## Summary of substantive changes

| Change | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Terminology | "Data user" → "data controller" throughout |
| Biometric data | Added as new definition; classified as sensitive personal data |
| Personal data breach | Defined for the first time in the Act |
| Data processor security obligation | Processors directly subject to Security Principle (s. 9); penalties up to RM 1M / 3 years |
| Data protection officers | Mandatory for both data controllers and data processors; notify Commissioner |
| Data breach notification | Mandatory: notify Commissioner as soon as practicable; notify data subjects without unnecessary delay where significant harm caused or likely; penalty for failure: up to RM 250,000 / 2 years |
| Data portability | New right for data subjects: direct controller-to-controller transfer by electronic request, subject to technical feasibility |
| Cross-border transfers | Ministerial whitelist replaced with controller-led assessment against statutory criteria |
| Deceased individuals | Excluded from definition of data subject |
| Electronic service | Expressly permitted |
| Penalties | General breach of s. 5 increased from RM 300,000 / 2 years to RM 1,000,000 / 3 years |
