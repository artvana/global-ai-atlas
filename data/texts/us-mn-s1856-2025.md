1.1 A bill for an act​
1.2 relating to health insurance; prohibiting the use of artificial intelligence in the​
1.3 utilization review process; amending Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 62M.02,​
1.4 by adding a subdivision; proposing coding for new law in Minnesota Statutes,​
1.5 chapter 62M.​
1.6 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF MINNESOTA:​
1.7 Section 1. Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 62M.02, is amended by adding a subdivision​
1.8 to read:​
1.9 Subd. 2a. Artificial intelligence. "Artificial intelligence" has the meaning given in​
1.10 United States Code, title 15, section 9401.​
1.11 Sec. 2. [62M.20] PROHIBITION ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.​
1.12 The use of artificial intelligence is prohibited in utilization review. Without limiting the​
1.13 generality of the foregoing, a utilization review organization is prohibited from using artificial​
1.14 intelligence in any part of its review, evaluation, determination, or appeals processes.​
