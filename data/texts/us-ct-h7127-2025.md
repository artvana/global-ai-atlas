 AN ACT CONCERNING STATE AGENCY CONTRACTS FOR THE
 LICENSING OF SOFTWARE APPLICATIONS.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         Section 1. Section 4d-30 of the general statutes is repealed and the
2      following is substituted in lieu thereof (Effective October 1, 2025):

3         As used in this section, [and] sections 4d-31 to 4d-44, inclusive, and
4      section 2 of this act:

5          (1) "Contract" means a contract for state agency information system
6      or telecommunication system facilities, equipment or services, which is
7      awarded pursuant to this chapter or subsection (e) of section 1-205,
8      subsection (c) of section 1-211, subsection (b) of section 1-212, section 4-
9      5, subsection (a) of section 10a-151b, or subsection (b) of section 19a-110.

10       (2) "Contractor" means a business entity or individual who is
11     awarded a contract or an amendment to a contract.

12       (3) "Subcontractor" means a subcontractor of a contractor for work

14      Sec. 2. (NEW) (Effective October 1, 2025) Any contract entered into or
15   amended on or after October 1, 2025, for the licensing of software
16   applications that are designed to be run on generally available desktop
17   or server hardware shall not contain any provision limiting a state
18   agency's ability to install or run such software on the hardware of the
19   state agency's choosing.

      This act shall take effect as follows and shall amend the following
      sections:

      Section 1           October 1, 2025                         4d-30
      Sec. 2              October 1, 2025                         New section

     Statement of Purpose:
     To prohibit state agency contracts for the licensing of software
     applications from limiting a state agency's ability to install the software
     on the state agency's chosen hardware.
