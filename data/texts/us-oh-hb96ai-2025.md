# Ohio House Bill 96 (FY2026-27 State Budget) — AI Policy Requirements for Public Schools

**Ohio 136th General Assembly**
**Enacted:** July 1, 2025 | **Effective:** July 1, 2025

**Citation**
*Ohio — July 1, 2025*

---

## Overview

Ohio HB 96, the state's biennial budget bill for fiscal years 2026–2027, includes embedded provisions requiring Ohio public school districts to adopt formal policies governing the use of artificial intelligence by students and staff. The law directs the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW) to publish a model AI use policy for districts to reference. It also creates the AI Integration in Community Colleges Pilot Grant Program to fund AI adoption in community college instruction. The compliance obligation falls on school districts; no penalties are imposed on students or AI companies directly.

---

## Key Provisions

### School District AI Policy Requirement
- Every Ohio public school district must adopt a **formal written policy governing AI use** by students and staff by **July 1, 2026** (one year after enactment).
- Districts may adopt the model policy published by the Department of Education and Workforce or develop their own compliant policy.
- Policies must address, at minimum:
  - Appropriate and inappropriate uses of AI tools by students in academic work;
  - Staff use of AI in instructional, administrative, and communication functions;
  - Procedures for handling academic integrity concerns related to AI-generated content;
  - Privacy and data protection considerations for student use of AI platforms.

### Model AI Use Policy
- The **Ohio Department of Education and Workforce** is directed to publish a **model AI use policy** for public school districts by a date specified in the Act.
- The model policy is intended to provide districts — particularly smaller and resource-constrained districts — a baseline framework to adopt or adapt.
- The DEW may update the model policy as AI technology and guidance evolve.

### AI Integration in Community Colleges Pilot Grant Program
- Establishes a **grant program** to support community colleges in integrating AI into instructional programs.
- Grants may be used for:
  - AI tools and platforms for student and faculty use;
  - Professional development for faculty in AI-assisted instruction;
  - Development of AI literacy curricula;
  - Infrastructure upgrades to support AI applications.
- Program is administered by the Ohio Department of Higher Education or a designated state agency.

### Compliance Framework
- Compliance obligation rests on **school districts**, not on students or AI technology companies.
- The law does not specify direct penalties for noncompliant districts; enforcement is expected through the Department of Education's existing oversight and accreditation mechanisms.
- Districts that fail to adopt a policy may be subject to state review processes applicable to policy compliance generally.

---

## Scope and Application

- Applies to all Ohio public school districts (K-12).
- Does not directly regulate private schools, charter schools (unless separately covered), or higher education institutions other than through the community college grant program.
- Does not restrict or prohibit specific AI tools or platforms.
- Does not impose obligations on AI developers or vendors.

---

## Legislative Context

HB 96 is Ohio's fiscal years 2026–2027 biennial budget bill, a single omnibus legislative vehicle covering the full state budget plus policy riders. The AI provisions represent Ohio's initial statutory footprint on AI governance in the K-12 education sector, consistent with a national trend of state education agencies developing AI use guidelines for schools. Ohio joins states including California, Texas, and Colorado in using budget legislation to advance AI education policy without standalone AI legislation.
