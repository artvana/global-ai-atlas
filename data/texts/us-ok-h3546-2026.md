 ENGROSSED HOUSE BILL NO. 3546 
 By: Maynard, Hildebrant, Olsen, Pae, Hall, Crosswhite Hader, Clinton, and Stark of the House 
 and 
 Bullard of the Senate 
 
 
 An Act relating to technology; prohibiting the granting of personhood to artificial intelligence systems, environmental elements, nonhuman animals, and inanimate objects; clarifying act does not revoke personhood of entities currently recognized; providing for codification; and providing an effective date. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA: 
 
 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1 of Title 75A, unless there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows: 
 A. Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, artificial intelligence systems, environmental elements, nonhuman animals, and inanimate objects shall not be recognized as or granted the status of personhood under the Constitution or laws of this state. 
 B. Nothing in this section shall be construed to alter or revoke the status of any municipality, corporation, association, partnership, nonprofit organization, or other legal entity duly recognized under the laws of this state prior to November 1, 2026. 
 SECTION 2. This act shall become effective November 1, 2026.
