# Guangdong Province Artificial Intelligence Industry Development Regulations

**Adopted by:** Guangdong Provincial People's Congress Standing Committee  
**Date of Adoption:** November 30, 2023 (at the 7th Meeting of the 14th Guangdong Provincial People's Congress Standing Committee)  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  

---

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1:** These Regulations are formulated in order to accelerate the development of the artificial intelligence industry in Guangdong Province, cultivate new productive forces through AI, promote the high-quality development of the economy, and support Guangdong Province in building an internationally leading AI industry cluster, in accordance with relevant national laws and regulations and in combination with the actual conditions of this Province.

**Article 2:** These Regulations apply to entities and individuals within the administrative area of Guangdong Province that are engaged in AI-related research and development, product and service provision, industry applications, talent development, and international cooperation.

**Article 3:** The Province shall promote the development of the AI industry following the principles of:

(1) Innovation priority — placing innovation at the core of AI development and fostering an ecosystem that encourages breakthrough research, entrepreneurship, and technology commercialization;

(2) Application-driven — deploying AI in the real economy and public services to drive industrial transformation and improve government efficiency;

(3) Safety governance — developing AI responsibly, embedding safety and ethics requirements into AI development and deployment, and preventing and managing AI-related risks;

(4) Open cooperation — promoting international AI cooperation, attracting global AI talent and investment, and supporting Guangdong AI enterprises in expanding internationally.

**Article 4:** The provincial people's government shall provide overall leadership for AI industry development, formulate and implement provincial AI industry development strategies and plans, and establish coordination mechanisms for major AI industry development matters.

The provincial development and reform commission, in conjunction with the provincial department of science and technology, the provincial department of industry and information technology, the provincial department of data, and other relevant departments, shall be responsible for coordinating and promoting AI industry development across the Province. Each relevant department shall fulfill AI industry development and governance responsibilities within their respective scope.

Municipal and county-level people's governments within the Province shall promote AI industry development within their respective administrative areas in accordance with the overall provincial plans and their local conditions.

---

## Chapter II: AI Technology Innovation Ecosystem

**Article 5:** The Province shall establish and continuously improve the AI technology innovation ecosystem, encompassing fundamental research, applied research, technology development, and commercialization. The Province shall implement major AI technology research programs targeting key core technologies in areas including:

(1) Large AI models and foundation model development;

(2) Multimodal AI systems;

(3) Embodied intelligence and human-robot interaction;

(4) AI chip design and next-generation computing hardware;

(5) AI safety, trustworthiness, and interpretability;

(6) Brain-inspired computing and neuromorphic AI.

**Article 6:** The Province shall support the establishment of high-level AI research institutions, including national-level and provincial-level key laboratories, technology innovation centers, and engineering research centers. The Province shall implement policies to attract leading AI research talent from domestic and overseas to work in Guangdong.

**Article 7:** The Province shall promote the construction of AI open innovation platforms, including:

(1) Open computing power platforms and intelligent computing centers to support AI research and development;

(2) Public AI data resource platforms and data exchange platforms;

(3) AI open innovation platforms operated by leading AI enterprises, providing open access to AI tools, frameworks, and pre-trained models;

(4) AI testing and evaluation platforms to support enterprises in assessing AI product safety and performance;

(5) AI standardization and certification platforms.

**Article 8:** The Province shall implement policies to facilitate the transfer and commercialization of AI research results, including:

(1) Supporting enterprises and research institutions in jointly establishing AI research and commercialization platforms;

(2) Simplifying procedures for the licensing and commercialization of AI intellectual property developed with public research funding;

(3) Supporting AI start-ups founded by researchers and graduates in accessing incubation, investment, and market development services;

(4) Establishing AI technology transfer service centers.

---

## Chapter III: Data and Computing Power Infrastructure

**Article 9:** The Province shall implement a coordinated strategy for the development of AI data infrastructure, covering data collection and generation, data quality management, data exchange, and data security. The Province shall develop a provincial AI data resource catalogue and shall promote the orderly opening of government and public data for AI purposes.

**Article 10:** The Province shall establish a multi-level AI data resource platform system, including:

(1) A provincial-level AI public data resource center that aggregates and manages high-value public datasets;

(2) Sector-level AI data platforms in key fields such as manufacturing, healthcare, transportation, and finance;

(3) Enterprise-level AI data management systems.

**Article 11:** The Province shall promote the development of the data element market, including:

(1) Establishing or supporting a provincially authorized data exchange platform for the compliant trading of data products and data services;

(2) Developing data asset valuation standards and data rights frameworks to support the trading of data elements;

(3) Supporting the development of federated learning and privacy-preserving computation technologies for secure data utilization.

**Article 12:** The Province shall prioritize the construction of AI computing power infrastructure to support large-scale AI model training and inference, including:

(1) Provincial intelligent computing centers equipped with AI-optimized processors;

(2) Integration of computing resources across the Pearl River Delta region to form a networked computing power pool;

(3) Edge computing infrastructure to support real-time AI inference applications.

The Province shall implement policies to optimize energy supply and reduce energy costs for AI computing facilities.

**Article 13:** The Province shall promote the domestic research, development, and industrialization of AI chips and core computing components, and shall establish support policies for enterprises in the AI hardware supply chain to scale up manufacturing in Guangdong.

---

## Chapter IV: Key Industry Applications

**Article 14:** The Province shall promote the deep integration of AI with the manufacturing sector, supporting enterprises in implementing:

(1) AI-driven industrial design and simulation tools;

(2) Intelligent quality inspection systems using computer vision and sensor fusion;

(3) Predictive maintenance systems for industrial equipment;

(4) AI-optimized supply chain and logistics management;

(5) Intelligent robot systems for flexible manufacturing.

The Province shall establish AI-enabled intelligent manufacturing demonstration factories and shall promote the replication of successful AI manufacturing application models.

**Article 15:** The Province shall promote AI applications in the medical and health sector, including:

(1) AI-assisted medical imaging analysis and diagnosis;

(2) AI-enabled drug discovery and clinical trials support;

(3) AI applications in genomics and precision medicine;

(4) AI-enhanced hospital management and patient care systems;

(5) AI-enabled epidemic monitoring and public health surveillance systems.

Medical AI applications shall comply with applicable medical device regulations and clinical validation requirements. The Province shall establish coordination mechanisms between health authorities and regulatory authorities to facilitate the market access of compliant medical AI products.

**Article 16:** The Province shall promote AI applications in the financial sector, subject to applicable financial regulatory requirements, including:

(1) AI-enhanced risk management and credit assessment systems;

(2) AI-enabled anti-money laundering and fraud detection systems;

(3) AI-driven intelligent investment advisory and wealth management services;

(4) Regulatory technology applications using AI to support financial compliance.

**Article 17:** The Province shall promote AI applications in transportation and logistics, including:

(1) Autonomous vehicle technology research, testing, and commercial deployment in designated zones;

(2) Intelligent traffic management systems;

(3) Smart port and shipping AI applications;

(4) AI-optimized logistics and cold chain management.

The Province shall establish AI transportation technology test zones and shall formulate policies to facilitate the testing and commercial operation of autonomous vehicles and other AI transportation applications.

**Article 18:** The Province shall promote AI applications in public services and smart government, including:

(1) AI-enhanced government service platforms and intelligent administrative approval systems;

(2) Smart city infrastructure including AI applications in energy management, environmental monitoring, and public safety;

(3) AI applications in education, including personalized learning systems and intelligent teaching assistance;

(4) AI applications in elderly care and social welfare services.

Government AI applications shall be deployed in accordance with applicable data security, personal information protection, and algorithmic governance requirements.

---

## Chapter V: AI Governance

**Article 19:** The Province shall implement AI governance measures consistent with national requirements, promoting the responsible development and deployment of AI. The Province shall:

(1) Establish provincial AI safety and governance standards and guidelines;

(2) Promote the integration of ethics review into AI product and application development;

(3) Support the development of AI safety evaluation and testing capabilities;

(4) Establish an AI incident monitoring and reporting mechanism.

**Article 20:** Enterprises that develop AI applications for high-stakes use cases — including applications that materially affect individuals' employment, access to finance, healthcare decisions, judicial proceedings, or public safety — shall implement the following governance requirements:

(1) Conduct pre-deployment algorithmic impact assessments;

(2) Implement human oversight mechanisms for AI-assisted decisions that significantly affect individuals' rights and interests;

(3) Establish channels for affected individuals to receive explanations of AI-assisted decisions and to contest such decisions;

(4) Conduct regular post-deployment monitoring and evaluation of AI system performance and fairness.

**Article 21:** The Province shall support the development of AI safety assessment and evaluation services, including:

(1) AI system security testing and vulnerability assessment services;

(2) AI fairness and bias evaluation services;

(3) AI robustness and reliability testing;

(4) AI compliance auditing services.

**Article 22:** The Province shall establish an AI governance coordination mechanism to address cross-sectoral AI governance issues and to coordinate the development of provincial AI standards and governance frameworks with national requirements.

**Article 23:** The Province shall promote AI literacy and public education on AI, including AI ethics, AI safety, and the implications of AI for society and the economy. The Province shall encourage enterprises, universities, and civil society organizations to contribute to AI public education efforts.

---

## Chapter VI: Talent and Education

**Article 24:** The Province shall implement a comprehensive AI talent strategy, targeting the attraction, cultivation, and retention of AI talent at all levels, including world-class AI researchers, highly skilled AI engineers, and AI-literate workers in other fields.

**Article 25:** The Province shall support universities and research institutions in Guangdong in establishing high-quality AI research and education programs, and shall implement policies to attract distinguished AI faculty from domestic and overseas. The Province shall support the establishment of provincial AI key disciplines and talent cultivation bases.

**Article 26:** The Province shall promote AI education at all levels, including:

(1) Incorporating AI literacy into primary and secondary school curricula;

(2) Supporting vocational institutions in developing AI application skills training programs;

(3) Supporting enterprises in developing AI training programs for their employees;

(4) Promoting AI upskilling programs for workers in industries undergoing AI-driven transformation.

**Article 27:** The Province shall implement talent attraction policies for leading AI professionals, including streamlined residency registration procedures, housing support, priority access to high-quality educational facilities for the children of AI professionals, and healthcare access.

**Article 28:** The Province shall establish provincial AI talent recognition awards and support leading AI enterprises in implementing competitive compensation and equity incentive programs to retain talent.

---

## Chapter VII: Open Cooperation and Internationalization

**Article 29:** The Province shall actively promote international cooperation in AI, leveraging Guangdong's geographic advantages and international connections to build a globally linked AI ecosystem.

**Article 30:** The Province shall support the establishment of international AI cooperation platforms in Guangdong, including:

(1) International AI research and development centers and joint laboratories;

(2) International AI talent exchange and fellowship programs;

(3) International AI industry cooperation platforms and investment forums.

**Article 31:** The Province shall support Guangdong AI enterprises in internationalizing their operations, including providing support for:

(1) Establishing overseas research and development centers and innovation outposts;

(2) Accessing international markets and complying with international AI regulations and standards;

(3) Building global supply chains and distribution networks.

**Article 32:** The Province shall promote the establishment of bilateral and multilateral AI cooperation mechanisms with other countries and regions, and shall support Guangdong enterprises, universities, and institutions in participating in international AI standards development activities.

---

## Chapter VIII: Support Policies

**Article 33:** The Province shall provide financial support for AI industry development through the following means:

(1) Provincial AI industry development funds to support major AI research projects, platform construction, and application demonstration;

(2) Subsidies for qualified AI enterprises in major research projects, talent recruitment, and international expansion;

(3) Tax preferences for AI enterprises' research and development expenditures in accordance with national and provincial regulations;

(4) Government procurement preferences for domestically developed AI products and services.

**Article 34:** The Province shall optimize land use policies for AI development, including:

(1) Prioritizing the supply of land for AI technology parks and data center construction;

(2) Establishing AI special development zones with streamlined approval procedures;

(3) Supporting the adaptive reuse of industrial buildings for AI industry purposes.

**Article 35:** The Province shall promote AI finance and investment, including:

(1) Government-guided AI industry investment funds;

(2) Supporting AI enterprises in accessing venture capital, angel investment, and other private equity financing;

(3) Supporting qualified AI enterprises in accessing capital markets domestically and internationally.

---

## Chapter IX: Legal Liability and Supervision

**Article 36:** Regulatory authorities shall conduct supervision and inspection of AI industry development activities within the Province in accordance with their respective responsibilities. Enterprises and individuals engaged in AI activities shall cooperate with supervision and inspection activities.

**Article 37:** Where entities violate the provisions of these Regulations, the relevant regulatory authority shall impose penalties in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Where violations constitute crimes, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

**Article 38:** Where regulatory officials abuse their authority, neglect their duties, or engage in corrupt practices in the exercise of AI supervision and administration functions, disciplinary sanctions shall be imposed; where criminal liability is constituted, criminal prosecution shall be pursued in accordance with law.

---

## Chapter X: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 39:** For the purposes of these Regulations, terms such as "artificial intelligence," "AI system," "AI product," and "AI service" have the meanings commonly understood in the field and consistent with applicable national standards and definitions.

**Article 40:** These Regulations take effect on March 1, 2024.
