================================================================================
ABU DHABI LAW NO. 3/2024
ON ESTABLISHING THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY COUNCIL
================================================================================

Issued: 22 January 2024 (corresponding to 10 Rajab 1445 H)
Jurisdiction: Emirate of Abu Dhabi
Published in: Official Gazette, Issue No. 1 of 2024, p. 16

We, Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Ruler of Abu Dhabi,

Pursuant to the perusal of:
- Abu Dhabi Law No. 1/1974 on the Reorganisation of the Government Apparatus in the
  Emirate of Abu Dhabi, and its amendments;
- Abu Dhabi Law No. 1/2017 concerning the Financial System of the Government of
  Abu Dhabi and its amendments;
- Abu Dhabi Law No. 14/2020 on the Establishment of the Advanced Technology Research
  Council (ATRC); and
- Based on what was presented to and approved by the Executive Council,

Have issued the following Law:


Article 1 — Definitions
================================================================================

In application of the provisions of this Law, the following terms and expressions shall
have the meanings assigned thereto unless the context requires otherwise:

Emirate: The Emirate of Abu Dhabi.

Council: The Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council.

Board of Directors: The Board of Directors of the Council.

Chairman of the Board: The Chairman of the Board of Directors.

Secretary-General: The Secretary-General of the Council.

Concerned Entities: Regulatory and supervisory authorities, institutions, companies,
governmental and non-governmental bodies and entities working in the sector of
artificial intelligence, advanced technology, and related investments, research and
industries.

Artificial Intelligence: It includes all systems, tools, and technologies capable of
collecting, analysing, and employing data for the purposes of making predictions, issuing
recommendations, taking a decision, or performing actions with varying levels of
self-control in a manner that mimics human mental capabilities.

Advanced Technology: Advanced tools, methods, systems, techniques, or algorithms
complementary to artificial intelligence that represent the latest innovations in energy
sources, science, engineering, electronics, finance, and other research, commercial, and
consumer uses.


Article 2 — Establishment
================================================================================

Pursuant to the provisions of this Law, a council called the "Artificial Intelligence and
Advanced Technology Council (AIATC)" shall be established. It shall have an independent
legal personality, enjoy full legal capacity to act, and have financial and administrative
independence in all its affairs. It shall report to the Ruler of Abu Dhabi.

The Council's affiliation to any other body may be changed by decision of the Ruler of
Abu Dhabi.


Article 3 — Headquarters
================================================================================

The main headquarters of the Council shall be located in Abu Dhabi City. Branches of
the Office may be established inside or outside the Emirate by a decision of the Minister.


Article 4 — Competences of the Council
================================================================================

The Council shall be the supreme authority responsible for everything related to projects,
investments and research related to artificial intelligence and advanced technology in the
Emirate. To this end, it shall have the following competences:

1. Studying and developing strategies and public policies related to artificial intelligence
   and advanced technology and identifying those that contribute to driving economic
   growth and societal renaissance in the Emirate.

2. Determining the conditions, controls, standards, and regulations that must be observed
   in implementing investment and research operations in the fields of artificial intelligence,
   advanced technology, and related governmental and private projects and industries in
   the Emirate.

3. Setting the priorities and mechanisms for investment, research and development in the
   field of artificial intelligence and advanced technology in the Emirate, in line with the
   directives and policies laid down by the Council.

4. Developing comprehensive investment plans to identify, allocate and effectively use
   infrastructure, human and financial resources to provide a framework for supporting
   and developing the artificial intelligence and advanced technology sector in the Emirate.

5. Proposing legislation and regulations related to artificial intelligence, advanced
   technology, research, industries, and services complementary to and related to them
   and submitting them for approval.

6. Developing the necessary plans and policies to attract leading global and local
   companies and entities in the field of artificial intelligence and advanced technology,
   strengthen global and local partnerships, and stimulate, encourage and support
   investment and research in the artificial intelligence and advanced technology sector
   in the Emirate.

7. Coordinating with all government and private entities in the State to provide the
   necessary climate for establishing and developing projects related to artificial
   intelligence and advanced technology, including investment projects and those related
   to scientific research.

8. Enhancing ways of cooperation and coordination between the government sector, the
   private sector, and the academic sector in areas related to artificial intelligence and
   advanced technology.

9. Launching, implementing and activating investment and research initiatives in the
   artificial intelligence and advanced technology sector and developing the supporting
   digital infrastructure.

10. Empowering industries and research based on artificial intelligence and advanced
    technology by creating and developing a supportive environment and establishing
    research centres to attract leading research capabilities and identify the future skills
    needed for those sectors.

11. Monitoring the performance and usefulness of policies related to artificial intelligence
    and advanced technology and their ability to keep pace with developments on the
    local and global levels.

12. Investing in, financing and establishing companies directly or indirectly in the
    artificial intelligence and advanced technology sector and developing the supporting
    digital infrastructure in this sector in accordance with the terms, conditions and
    controls prescribed by the Council.

13. Setting the terms, conditions and controls related to approving the establishment of
    strategic companies operating in the artificial intelligence and advanced technology
    sector.

14. Any other powers entrusted to it by the Ruler of Abu Dhabi.

The Council shall coordinate with the Supreme Council for Financial and Economic Affairs
regarding the powers mentioned in the first paragraph of this article in accordance with
the controls and provisions prescribed by the Council.


Article 5 — Board of Directors
================================================================================

1. The Council shall be managed by a Board of Directors consisting of at least five
   members, including the Chairman of the Council and the Secretary-General, whose
   appointment shall be issued by a decision from the Ruler of Abu Dhabi.

2. The term of Council membership shall be three years, automatically renewed for
   similar periods.

3. The Board of Directors shall assign the Secretary-General or one or more members of
   the Board of Directors to implement and follow up on some of the tasks assigned to
   the Board.

4. The remuneration of the Chairman and members of the Board of Directors and the
   Secretary-General shall be determined by a decision of the Ruler of Abu Dhabi based
   on the recommendation of the Chairman of the Board.

5. The decisions of the Board of Directors shall be issued by a decision of the Chairman
   of the Board.


Article 6 — Powers of the Board of Directors
================================================================================

The Board of Directors shall be the authority having competence to manage the affairs of
the Council and conduct its affairs, and shall have all the necessary powers in this
respect. To do so, it may:

1. Issue decisions and take the necessary measures to implement its duties and
   competences and direct any of the concerned parties in accordance with the strategies
   and policies approved by the Council.

2. Supervise the implementation of strategies related to investment and research in the
   fields of artificial intelligence and advanced technology.

3. Conclude agreements, memorandums of understanding, contracts, investments and
   loans that enable the Council to exercise its powers with all concerned parties inside
   and outside the State, including for the purposes of investment and cooperation in
   industries and areas of research and development in the field of artificial intelligence
   and advanced technology.

4. Establish companies and entities alone or with other parties inside and outside the
   State to carry out any of its powers.

5. Request all information, data, documents and everything that would support the
   objectives and competences of the Council from the relevant authorities.

6. Contract with experts, technicians and consultants to carry out the work assigned to
   them and determine their remuneration and fees.

7. Form permanent and temporary subcommittees from among its members and others
   and determine their competences, members, tasks and powers.

8. Seek the assistance of knowledgeable and experienced people in supporting the
   aforementioned committees, and determining their allocations or rewards; invite to
   meetings for advice and information from whomever is deemed appropriate.

9. Approve the Council's draft annual budget.

10. Issue financial and administrative regulations that regulate the conduct of the
    Council's work.

11. Any other powers assigned by the Ruler of Abu Dhabi.


Article 7 — Secretary-General
================================================================================

The Secretary-General shall be responsible for managing the daily work and supporting
everything related to the Council. It may in particular:

1. Supervise the implementation and follow-up of the Council's tasks and powers in
   accordance with the provisions of the Law as well as the regulations, decisions and
   recommendations issued and the procedures followed.

2. Collect, study, analyse and prepare all information and everything that would support
   the Council in implementing its tasks and powers and making recommendations
   related to them.

3. Propose legislation, regulatory and executive regulations, systems, policies, decisions
   and circulars related to the Council's powers and submit them to the Board of Directors
   for approval.

4. Propose strategic plans, initiatives, programmes, projects and studies related to the
   Council's competencies and submit them with the necessary recommendations to the
   Board of Directors.

5. Propose fees, tariffs and prices related to the Council's powers and submit them to the
   Board of Directors for approval by the Ruler of Abu Dhabi.

6. Prepare the financial and administrative regulations that regulate the conduct of the
   Council's work, in order to submit them to the Board of Directors for approval.

7. Prepare the draft annual budget for the Council and submit it to the Chairman of the
   Council.

8. Open, manage and operate bank accounts in the name of the Council.

9. Appoint experts, consultants and technicians on a temporary or permanent basis and
   determine their fees.

10. Issue the necessary circulars and documents to inform the concerned authorities of
    the Council's decisions and instructions.

11. Represent the Council before the judiciary and others.

12. Any other powers or tasks assigned by the Chairman of the Council.


Article 8 — Financial Provisions
================================================================================

1. The Finance Department shall provide technical, advisory, administrative, financial and
   operational support and everything that would support the Council and enable it to
   direct and implement its powers.

2. The Board shall have an independent budget. The Chairman shall submit said budget to
   the Ruler of Abu Dhabi for approval.

3. The Council may, by a decision of its Chairman, open its own accounts, manage them,
   and disburse them according to its purposes.


Article 9 — Obligations of Concerned Entities
================================================================================

The concerned authorities shall be committed to the following:

1. Implementing all regulations, strategies, policies and decisions approved by the
   Council.

2. Working and cooperating with the Council, providing it with the necessary information,
   data and documents, and providing support to it to enable it to carry out its
   competences and exercise its powers.


Article 10 — Fiscal Year
================================================================================

The fiscal year of the Council shall begin at the first of January and end on December 31st
each year, with the exception of the first fiscal year which shall begin as of the effective
date of this Law and end on December 31st of the next year.


Article 11 — Repeal
================================================================================

Any provision that is contrary to the provisions of this Law shall be abrogated.


Article 12 — Entry into Force
================================================================================

The present Law shall come into force from the date of its issuance and shall be published
in the Official Gazette.
