# RUSSIAN FEDERATION

# FEDERAL LAW

## On the Foundations of State Regulation of Areas of Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies in the Russian Federation

---

### Article 1. Purpose and Subject of Regulation of this Federal Law

1. This Federal Law regulates relations arising in connection with the development, deployment, use, and other application of artificial intelligence technologies (hereinafter also referred to as the application of artificial intelligence) in the Russian Federation.

2. The purpose of this Federal Law is to create legal conditions for the accelerated development and deployment of artificial intelligence technologies, to ensure the security of the individual, society, and the state, and to ensure the state's technological sovereignty in the use of artificial intelligence technologies in the Russian Federation.

3. This Federal Law applies to natural persons and legal entities engaged in the development, application, and deployment of artificial intelligence technologies in the territory of the Russian Federation.

4. This Federal Law does not apply to relations arising in connection with the application of artificial intelligence technologies for purposes of state defense and security and the protection of law and order, including counter-terrorism protection, for purposes of preventing natural and man-made emergencies, unless otherwise provided by federal laws regulating the said relations or by acts of the President of the Russian Federation.

---

### Article 2. Legal Regulation of Relations Connected with the Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

1. Relations connected with the application of artificial intelligence technologies are regulated by this Federal Law, decrees of the President of the Russian Federation, and the regulatory legal acts of the Government of the Russian Federation and state bodies adopted in accordance therewith.

2. Specific features of regulation of relations connected with the application of artificial intelligence technologies may be established by federal laws taking into account the provisions of this Federal Law.

3. On the basis of, and in execution of, this Federal Law and other federal laws, international treaties, and decrees of the President of the Russian Federation, the Government of the Russian Federation, state bodies of the constituent entities of the Russian Federation, and other state bodies may, within the limits of their authority, adopt regulatory legal acts on individual matters concerning the application of artificial intelligence technologies.

4. Regulatory legal acts of federal executive authorities adopted on the basis of, and in execution of, this Federal Law and other federal laws specified in part 2 of this Article are subject to mandatory coordination with the federal executive authority performing the functions of developing and implementing state policy and regulatory legal regulation in the field of information technology (hereinafter — the authorized body in the field of artificial intelligence).

5. Regulatory legal acts of federal executive authorities and constituent entities of the Russian Federation adopted on the basis of, and in execution of, this Federal Law and other federal laws specified in part 2 of this Article are subject to mandatory coordination with the federal executive authority authorized in the field of countering technical intelligence and technical protection of information, and with the federal executive authority authorized in the field of security, in the event that the said acts contain matters relating to the scope of activity of such bodies.

---

### Article 3. Basic Concepts Used in this Federal Law

For the purposes of this Federal Law, the following basic concepts are used:

1) **artificial intelligence** — a complex of technological solutions that makes it possible to imitate human cognitive functions (including self-learning and the search for solutions without a predefined algorithm) and, in performing specific tasks, to obtain results comparable to or exceeding the results of human intellectual activity. The complex of technological solutions includes information and communications infrastructure, software (including software using machine learning methods), and processes and services for data processing and the search for solutions;

2) **artificial intelligence system** — an information system or a set of information systems and technical means that uses one or more artificial intelligence technologies;

3) **artificial intelligence service** — a solution using artificial intelligence technologies for electronic computers (a component thereof), which provides users, through a user interface, with access to the capabilities of artificial intelligence technologies for performing applied tasks;

4) **artificial intelligence technologies** — technologies based on the use of artificial intelligence, including computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition and synthesis, intelligent decision-making support, and prospective methods of artificial intelligence;

5) **datasets** — a composition of data (with a machine-readable description) that is structured or grouped according to particular characteristics and is necessary for the development and advancement of artificial intelligence models;

6) **artificial intelligence model** — a type of program for electronic computers (a component thereof), or a set of programs for electronic computers, intended to perform intellectual tasks at a level comparable to or exceeding the results of human intellectual activity, using algorithms and being trained or previously trained on datasets in order to derive patterns, provide information, make decisions, or predict results based on objectives set by humans;

7) **training of an artificial intelligence model** — the process of adjusting the numerical values (algorithms) of an artificial intelligence model that govern the influence of each query to such a model and its response to the query, using datasets, in order to form or improve the ability of the artificial intelligence model to achieve objectives set by humans;

8) **developer of an artificial intelligence model** — a natural person, legal entity, or individual entrepreneur engaged in the research, design, development, training, testing, or modification of an artificial intelligence model;

9) **operator of an artificial intelligence system** — a citizen or legal entity engaged in the operation of an artificial intelligence system, including the processing of information contained in its databases;

10) **owner of an artificial intelligence service** — a person holding the corresponding rights to an artificial intelligence service, including exclusive or other rights to such a system, unless otherwise established by contract or law;

11) **user of an artificial intelligence service** — a person using an artificial intelligence service in accordance with this Federal Law;

12) **large foundation models** — artificial intelligence models that serve as a basis for the creation and adaptation of various types of software, that have been trained to recognize particular types of patterns, that are applied to perform a large number of different tasks, and that contain parameters whose minimum number is established by the authorized body in the field of artificial intelligence;

13) **trusted artificial intelligence models** — artificial intelligence models that meet the requirements established by this Federal Law and that are included in the register of trusted artificial intelligence models;

14) **exploitation of human vulnerabilities** — the use of characteristics of a natural person or group of persons (including age-related, psychological, cognitive, socio-economic, and physical characteristics, as well as temporary states) for the purposeful influencing of behavior, decision-making, or unauthorized access to information that causes or is capable of causing harm.

### Article 4. General Principles for the Regulation of Relations in the Sphere of Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

The legal regulation of relations in the sphere of artificial intelligence is based on the following principles:

1) **supremacy of the rights, freedoms, and lawful interests of human beings and citizens** — ensuring strict observance of the rights and freedoms of the individual, including the right to privacy, the protection of personal data and property, and the prohibition of discrimination;

2) **respect for human autonomy and freedom of will** — regulatory legal regulation in the field of artificial intelligence must not diminish the right of choice and the intellectual capacities of the human being, which constitute an independent value and a system-forming factor of society;

3) **risk-oriented approach to the regulation of the application of artificial intelligence** — the establishment of requirements for the application of artificial intelligence taking into account the probability of risks of causing harm (damage) and the scale of harm caused to the life and health of natural persons, the business reputation and property of natural persons and legal entities, the security and technological independence of the state, the environment, and other values protected by law in connection with the application of artificial intelligence;

4) **provision of favorable conditions for the development of artificial intelligence technologies** — the creation of conditions for the accelerated development of artificial intelligence in the Russian Federation;

5) **technological independence** — ensuring the independence of the Russian Federation in the field of the development of artificial intelligence technologies, including through the development of sovereign and national artificial intelligence models;

6) **consideration and respect for traditional Russian spiritual and moral values** — ensuring the development, deployment, and application of artificial intelligence technologies on the basis of such values as life, dignity, human rights and freedoms, patriotism, civic-mindedness, service to the Fatherland and responsibility for its fate, high moral ideals, the strong family, creative labor, the priority of the spiritual over the material, humanism, mercy, justice, collectivism, mutual assistance and mutual respect, historical memory and continuity of generations, and the unity of the peoples of Russia;

7) **security** — preventing threats to the constitutional order, defense and state security, the technological independence of the state, the life and health of natural persons, the business reputation and property of natural persons and legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, and the environment, as well as ensuring information security and the stability of the functioning of facilities of informatization that use artificial intelligence technologies.

---

### Article 5. Specific Features of the Regulation of Relations in the Sphere of Artificial Intelligence

The legal regulation of relations arising in connection with the development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence in the territory of the Russian Federation is carried out taking into account the need to assess, among other things, the following circumstances of the application of artificial intelligence technologies:

1) the purpose of the artificial intelligence technologies;

2) the probability of risks of causing harm (damage) and the scale of damage caused in connection with the application of artificial intelligence technologies;

3) the degree of autonomy of the artificial intelligence systems implementing artificial intelligence technologies;

4) the degree of influence of artificial intelligence systems implementing artificial intelligence technologies on the taking of legally significant actions;

5) the category of information processed (publicly accessible information, restricted-access information, or other information).

---

### Article 6. Subjects of Relations in the Sphere of Artificial Intelligence

The subjects of relations in the sphere of artificial intelligence are:

1) the developer of an artificial intelligence model;

2) the operator of an artificial intelligence system;

3) the owner of an artificial intelligence service;

4) the user of an artificial intelligence service;

5) state authorities, within the limits of their powers.

---

### Article 7. Ensuring the Development of Artificial Intelligence Technologies in the Russian Federation

1. For the purposes of ensuring technological independence and preserving traditional Russian spiritual and moral values, the Russian Federation provides support for the deployment of sovereign large foundation artificial intelligence models and national large foundation artificial intelligence models.

2. For the purposes of this Federal Law, sovereign and national artificial intelligence models are understood to mean artificial intelligence models meeting the following requirements:

a) all stages of development and training of the artificial intelligence models are carried out in the territory of the Russian Federation;

b) all stages of development, training, and operation of the artificial intelligence models are carried out by citizens of the Russian Federation and Russian legal entities;

c) the training of the artificial intelligence models uses datasets whose formation is carried out in the territory of the Russian Federation by citizens of the Russian Federation and Russian legal entities.

3. For the purposes of this Article, a Russian legal entity is a legal entity which, unless otherwise provided by an international treaty of the Russian Federation, is under the control of the Russian Federation, and/or a constituent entity of the Russian Federation, and/or a municipal entity, and/or a citizen of the Russian Federation who does not hold citizenship of another state, and/or persons jointly or separately controlled by them. For these purposes, control is understood to mean the ability to determine decisions taken by the legal entity by virtue of the right to dispose, directly or indirectly, of more than fifty percent of the total number of votes attributable to voting shares (interests) constituting the charter capital of the said legal entity.

4. Additional requirements for sovereign and national large foundation models, the procedure and conditions for confirming compliance with the said requirements, and the cases of mandatory application of sovereign and national large foundation models, may be established by the Government of the Russian Federation.

6. The Russian Federation provides favorable conditions for the development, deployment, and application of sovereign and national large foundation models.

---

### Article 8. Ensuring Trust and Security in the Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

1. Only artificial intelligence models included in the register of trusted artificial intelligence models (hereinafter — trusted artificial intelligence models) are permitted for use in state information systems and at significant facilities of the critical information infrastructure of the Russian Federation belonging to state bodies, state institutions, and enterprises. The procedure for maintaining the register of trusted artificial intelligence models and for including artificial intelligence models therein is established by the Government of the Russian Federation.

2. Trusted artificial intelligence models include artificial intelligence models, including sovereign and national large foundation models, that meet the following requirements:

a) the existence of confirmation that the artificial intelligence model meets the security requirements established by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of countering technical intelligence and technical protection of information, and by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of security (hereinafter — security requirements);

b) ensuring that data processing is carried out exclusively in the territory of the Russian Federation when the artificial intelligence model is applied;

c) the existence of confirmation that the artificial intelligence model meets the quality requirements established by federal executive authorities, state corporations, and the Bank of Russia in the corresponding spheres (sectors) of activity (hereinafter — quality requirements).

3. Confirmation that an artificial intelligence model meets the security requirements is carried out by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of countering technical intelligence and technical protection of information, and by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of security, in the manner established by them.

4. Confirmation that an artificial intelligence model meets the quality requirements is carried out in the manner established by the Government of the Russian Federation.

5. In the interaction of artificial intelligence systems and artificial intelligence services with state information systems and facilities of the critical information infrastructure of the Russian Federation, the protection of information is ensured in accordance with the requirements established by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of countering technical intelligence and technical protection of information, and by the federal executive authority authorized in the field of security.

6. Cases of mandatory use of trusted artificial intelligence models at particular facilities of the critical information infrastructure of the Russian Federation are established by the Government of the Russian Federation upon the proposal of sectoral federal executive authorities.

---

### Article 9. Rights of Citizens in the Use of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

1. A person engaged in the sale of goods or the provision of services using artificial intelligence technologies without human participation in the decision to sell goods or to provide services is obliged to inform purchasers and consumers of the services of the application of artificial intelligence technologies.

2. A person using artificial intelligence technologies for the purpose of taking autonomous (without human participation) decisions affecting the rights, obligations, freedoms, and lawful interests of a citizen is obliged to notify the citizen in respect of whom the said autonomous decision is taken.

3. In cases established by the Government of the Russian Federation, persons and organizations providing services are obliged to provide a citizen who has refused the use of artificial intelligence technologies in his or her case with the possibility of receiving services in a form and manner that does not envisage the autonomous use of artificial intelligence technologies.

4. A citizen must be afforded the possibility of pre-trial appeal of decisions, as well as actions (or inaction), of state authorities, regional authorities of constituent entities of the Russian Federation, and organizations with state participation, or their officials, taken or carried out using artificial intelligence technologies.

5. A citizen has the right to compensation for harm caused by the unlawful use of artificial intelligence technologies, in the manner established by the civil legislation of the Russian Federation.

---

### Article 10. Obligations of the Developer of an Artificial Intelligence Model, the Operator of an Artificial Intelligence System, the Owner of an Artificial Intelligence Service, and the User of an Artificial Intelligence Service

1. The developer of an artificial intelligence model is obliged to ensure the security of the model created, including:

a) eliminating functional features capable of leading to discrimination on the basis of behavior or personal characteristics;

b) informing the developer of the artificial intelligence system of the impossibility of using it for prohibited purposes;

c) documenting the architecture, logic of functioning, and limitations of the artificial intelligence models applied, in the volume necessary for verifying their compliance with the regulatory legal regulation established in accordance with this Federal Law;

d) carrying out modeling of potential risks associated with the functioning of the artificial intelligence technologies being developed, taking into account their intended application;

e) determining the procedure for the maintenance and monitoring of operational parameters of facilities using artificial intelligence.

2. The operator of an artificial intelligence system is obliged to:

a) include in the documentation a manual on safe operation containing an indication of the inadmissibility of using the system for the manipulation of behavior and the exploitation of human vulnerabilities;

b) carry out testing of the system to identify potential opportunities for its use for purposes contrary to the legislation of the Russian Federation;

c) provide users with information on its functional purpose and limitations;

d) ensure technical maintenance and monitoring of operational parameters of facilities using artificial intelligence in accordance with the requirements established by the owner and developer;

e) immediately suspend the operation of facilities using artificial intelligence upon the identification of a threat of causing harm to the life and health of citizens, the security of the state, the property of natural persons or legal entities, or the environment;

f) ensure the recording of incidents associated with the functioning of facilities using artificial intelligence;

g) appoint persons responsible for ensuring the safe functioning of facilities using artificial intelligence.

3. The owner of an artificial intelligence service is obliged to:

a) determine the rules of access to the artificial intelligence service and to provide therein for a direct prohibition on the use of such service for purposes contrary to the law;

b) take measures to prevent the use of the service for unlawful purposes;

c) inform users of the artificial intelligence service that they are interacting with an artificial intelligence system, except in cases where such circumstance is obvious to the user based on the purpose of the service, the nature of its functions, or the conditions of use;

d) where the service is accessed within twenty-four hours by more than five hundred thousand users of the "Internet" network located in the territory of the Russian Federation, fulfill the obligations provided for by Article 10.1 of Federal Law No. 149-FZ of 27 July 2006 "On Information, Information Technologies, and the Protection of Information";

e) determine the procedure for the maintenance and monitoring of operational parameters of facilities using artificial intelligence;

f) implement mechanisms limiting the possibility of creating, using the artificial intelligence service, information material that contradicts the legislation of the Russian Federation.

4. The user of an artificial intelligence service and model is obliged to:

a) observe the established rules of access to the artificial intelligence service;

b) use artificial intelligence services and artificial intelligence models for purposes that do not contradict the legislation of the Russian Federation;

c) not commit actions aimed at circumventing the built-in security and control mechanisms in violation of the established operational parameters of facilities using artificial intelligence.

### Article 11. Liability of Subjects of Relations in the Sphere of Artificial Intelligence in Accordance with the Legislation of the Russian Federation

1. Subjects of relations in the sphere of artificial intelligence bear liability in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation for a result obtained using artificial intelligence that violates the legislation of the Russian Federation, in proportion to the degree of fault of each.

2. The developer of an artificial intelligence model, the operator of an artificial intelligence system, and the owner of an artificial intelligence service bear liability in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation for a result obtained using artificial intelligence that violates the legislation of the Russian Federation, on the condition that the said persons knew or should have known of the possibility of obtaining such a result using the model, system, or service of artificial intelligence of which they are the developer, operator, or owner, unless the contrary is proven as a result of investigative actions.

3. The developer of an artificial intelligence model, the operator of an artificial intelligence system, and the owner of an artificial intelligence service are released from the liability provided for in part 2 of this Article in the event that they have taken exhaustive measures to prevent the obtaining of such a result and have observed the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation in the development of the model, the operation of the system, and the provision of access to the artificial intelligence service.

4. The user of an artificial intelligence service bears liability in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation for the use of a result obtained by means of artificial intelligence that violates the legislation of the Russian Federation, where such result is the consequence of the user's intentional actions or non-observance of the conditions of use of the artificial intelligence service.

5. The user and/or operator is released from liability in cases where harm has been caused as a consequence of:

a) force majeure;

b) the intent of the injured party;

c) defects of the facility using artificial intelligence which could not have been identified by the user/operator with the exercise of all reasonable diligence provided for by the conditions of operation and the technical documentation;

d) unlawful actions of third parties which the user and/or operator could not have foreseen and prevented, having taken all measures within their power corresponding to the nature of the facility and the conditions of its operation.

6. The operator of an artificial intelligence system who has compensated harm caused as a result of the operation of a facility using artificial intelligence has the right to bring a recourse claim against the developer of that facility, if the operator proves that the harm arose exclusively as a consequence of defects of the facility that existed at the time of its transfer to the operator and that could not have been identified with the exercise by the operator of all reasonable diligence provided for by the conditions of operation and the technical documentation.

7. Persons guilty of violating the legislation of the Russian Federation in the sphere of the application of artificial intelligence technologies bear administrative and criminal liability in the manner established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

### Article 12. Identification of Synthesized Information Material

1. The owner of an artificial intelligence service ensures the placement of an information warning to the user concerning the application of artificial intelligence technologies for the creation of audio and/or visual information material or other content (hereinafter — synthesized, generated information material).

2. The information warning specified in part 1 of this Article must be placed within the synthesized, generated information material in a format perceptible to humans and in a machine-readable format, and must be provided to the user by the means by which the synthesized, generated information material is provided.

3. The requirements for the content and the procedure for placement of the information warning specified in parts 1 and 2 of this Article, as well as the procedure for monitoring compliance with the said requirements by the federal executive authority authorized to carry out monitoring in the sphere of artificial intelligence, are established by the Government of the Russian Federation.

4. The persons specified in part 1 of Article 10.6 of the Federal Law "On Information, Information Technologies, and the Protection of Information," in the event that their daily audience in the territory of the Russian Federation exceeds 100 thousand users, ensure verification of the presence of the information warning within the synthesized information material distributed, as specified in part 1 of this Article.

Upon detection of the absence of the information warning within the synthesized, generated information material distributed, the said organizations shall place the corresponding information warning or shall ensure the deletion of the synthesized information material.

The persons specified in this part shall notify users, within the framework of the user agreement, of the probabilistic nature of the determination of synthesized, generated information material.

5. The agreement (contract) between the user and the persons specified in parts 1 and 4 of this Article may provide for the absence of an obligation to place an information warning within the synthesized, generated information material in a format perceptible to humans.

The user's refusal to receive an information warning within the synthesized, generated information material in a format perceptible to humans must be specific, informed, conscious, and unambiguous.

6. Users of artificial intelligence services are obliged to observe the rights and interests of third parties when using the synthesized, generated information material provided for in part 1 of this Article.

7. Persons engaged in the processing of synthesized, generated information material for the purpose of removing the information warning therefrom bear liability in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation on administrative offenses.

### Article 13. Specific Features of the Disposition of Rights to Intellectual Property Items

1. Items of intellectual property created through the application of artificial intelligence services are protected in the manner provided for by the Civil Code of the Russian Federation. Exclusive rights to the said items are likewise determined in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation.

In so doing, only original creations meeting the criteria of protectability established by civil legislation are recognized as objects of intellectual activity, regardless of whether they were created by a human being or by an automated system.

2. Owners of artificial intelligence services are obliged to:

a) before commencement of use, notify users of the rules and limitations of the use of the results of intellectual activity obtained by means of artificial intelligence services. The notification must contain a reference to the license agreement containing a detailed statement of the limitations relating to intellectual property rights;

b) provide the user with access to, the use of, and the export of the results of intellectual activity created by means of the service in strict compliance with the conditions of the contract concluded with the user. The contract must include information on the lawfulness of the origin of the material used for training the system and warnings concerning the risks of repeated reproduction of intellectual property protected by law;

c) when concluding contracts with developers of artificial intelligence models, agree on the procedure for the transfer of rights to results of intellectual activity created using the corresponding models. These conditions must comply with the requirements of civil legislation, including respect for the rights of the authors of the original works underlying the training of the models;

d) include in the user agreement and the contract with the user of the artificial intelligence service a condition concerning the ownership of the rights to the synthesized, generated information material.

3. Users of artificial intelligence services are prohibited from:

a) using results of intellectual activity available through artificial intelligence services in ways that violate the norms of current legislation, in particular the norms regulating the protection of copyright and related rights;

b) applying the results of intellectual activity obtained in a manner contrary to the conditions of the contract with the owner of the artificial intelligence service. Liability for the violation of the rights of third parties is borne directly by the user.

4. Developers of artificial intelligence models are obliged to:

a) provide owners of artificial intelligence services with free access to, the possibility of using, and the export of the results of intellectual activity produced by means of the models they have developed. The results provided must be accompanied by an indication of the possible liability for the unlawful use of materials forming part of the database for the training of the model;

b) when developing artificial intelligence models, guarantee the absence of violations of the intellectual property rights of third parties. The use of materials of others is permitted only subject to compliance with the requirements of the law and the contract with the rights holders.

5. The extraction of information from items protected by copyright or patent rights — including, within the framework of computer processing, the extraction, comparison, classification, and analysis of patterns, trends, and correlations contained in such items — for the purpose of producing a dataset and/or training artificial intelligence does not constitute a violation of the copyright or patent rights of their rights holders, on the condition that the producer of the dataset or the developer obtained a lawful copy of such work, or that this item was made available to the public and/or accessible for analysis.

### Article 14. Monitoring, Analysis, and Operational Response to Risks and Threats in the Sphere of Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

The authorized federal executive authorities, within the limits of their competence, carry out continuous monitoring and analysis of the consequences of the application of artificial intelligence technologies on the basis of data received from operators of artificial intelligence systems.

The monitoring of the application of artificial intelligence technologies includes:

a) the collection and systematization of information on incidents, threats, and risks of information security;

b) the assessment of the scale and nature of the negative consequences of the said incidents;

c) the forecasting of the emergence of threats and risks.

Upon the identification of a threat or risk capable of causing substantial harm to the rights and lawful interests of the individual, society, or the state, the authorized federal executive authority has the right to:

a) initiate the conduct of an unscheduled inspection of the operator of a system or facility using artificial intelligence;

b) issue mandatory instructions for the elimination of identified violations.

### Article 15. Goals, Principles, and Tasks of International Cooperation in the Sphere of Application of Artificial Intelligence Technologies

1. International cooperation of the Russian Federation in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies is carried out in accordance with international treaties and the legislation of the Russian Federation.

2. The goal of international cooperation in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies is to strengthen the position of the Russian Federation in international bodies and organizations forming policy and standards in the field of artificial intelligence, and in other international and regional organizations and associations.

3. The principal underlying principle of international cooperation in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies is the provision of equal rights and opportunities of nations in the field of the development and use of artificial intelligence technologies.

4. The tasks of international cooperation in the sphere of artificial intelligence are:

a) the formation of a "single space" of trusted artificial intelligence technologies, with the leading role of the Russian Federation in international and regional cooperation;

b) the promotion of policies and standards ensuring access of developers of artificial intelligence technologies and consumers of such technologies to markets, technologies, and resources, and contributing to the development of equal trade-economic and scientific-technical cooperation with foreign partners;

c) the promotion abroad of domestic artificial intelligence technologies, including trusted technologies;

d) the use of the technological foundations of the Russian Federation in the field of artificial intelligence for reducing the gap in the level of development of digital technologies between various countries and increasing the technological potential of partner states.

---

### Article 16. Directions and Forms of International Cooperation

1. International cooperation in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies is implemented by means of joint research, the exchange of data and educational resources, participation in standardization, and the creation of joint infrastructure and innovation products.

2. The principal directions of strengthening international cooperation in the field of the use of artificial intelligence technologies are:

a) preserving and strengthening the cooperation of Russian scientists with leading foreign scientific research centers and organizations on issues of prospective fundamental research in the field of artificial intelligence;

b) expanding international cooperation in the sphere of education on issues of the development of artificial intelligence technologies, including the development, jointly with foreign partners, of higher-education programs on issues of the development of artificial intelligence technologies and of multilingual educational online platforms for general and professional education, programs for advanced training, and programs for professional retraining, including for state civil servants in partner states, and the training in the Russian Federation of foreign specialists in the field of artificial intelligence;

c) strengthening the human-resources potential of federal executive authorities and educational, scientific, and other organizations; attracting talented foreign specialists and prospective students to work and study in Russia and supporting their professional development, including the adoption of measures of financial and visa support on a long-term basis, for the purposes of ensuring the development of international scientific-technical cooperation and the development of organizations engaged in the development and use of artificial intelligence technologies;

d) promoting Russian approaches to the measurement of the level of development of artificial intelligence technologies (the use of indicators and indices based on verified and scientifically grounded methodologies for assessing such technologies) and harmonizing Russian and international methodologies;

e) facilitating the export of Russian artificial intelligence technologies and their legal protection, and the development of foreign-economic cooperation in this sphere;

f) promoting the advanced experience of the Russian Federation in the field of regulatory legal regulation and the ethics of the use of artificial intelligence technologies on the platforms of intergovernmental and international sectoral organizations, and participating in international exchanges of expert-analytical information in volumes corresponding to the national interests of the Russian Federation;

g) analyzing, jointly with international and regional organizations and associations, solutions in the field of artificial intelligence in the key directions of the use of such solutions.

---

### Article 17. Protection of Intellectual Property and Control over Cross-Border Data Exchange in International Cooperation

1. Cross-border artificial intelligence technologies (objects of legal relations connected with the functioning of artificial intelligence technologies and having a cross-border character) include technologies whose creation (development), use, transfer, conservation, or destruction is connected with subjects and/or the territory of two or more states.

2. The functioning of cross-border artificial intelligence technologies may be prohibited or restricted in cases established by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

---

### Article 18. Ensuring the Confidentiality of Information and the Protection of Critical Technologies in Participation in International Projects

1. Issues connected with ensuring the confidentiality of information and the protection of critical artificial intelligence technologies in the implementation of international projects are regulated by regulatory legal acts regulating relations in the sphere of the protection of information and restricted-access critical technologies.

### Article 19. Powers of the President of the Russian Federation, the Government of the Russian Federation, Federal Executive Authorities, and Constituent Entities of the Russian Federation in the Sphere of Artificial Intelligence

1. In accordance with this Federal Law and other federal laws, the President of the Russian Federation:

a) approves the National Strategy for the Development of Artificial Intelligence;

b) determines the specific features of the application of artificial intelligence technologies in the field of defense, ensuring national security, and ensuring public safety and law and order.

2. In accordance with this Federal Law and other federal laws, the Government of the Russian Federation:

a) ensures the implementation of a unified state policy in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies, with the exception of issues in the field of defense, ensuring national security, and ensuring public safety and law and order;

b) establishes the list of measures of state support for the deployment of artificial intelligence technologies;

c) approves programs and plans of measures for the development of artificial intelligence technologies, and determines the information system and the operator of the information system for their monitoring;

d) determines the bodies authorized to implement particular functions and tasks in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies;

e) establishes coordinating and consultative bodies for the implementation of particular powers in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence;

f) determines the authorized body (expert organization) for the formation of criteria of compliance of artificial intelligence models, systems, and services with traditional Russian spiritual and moral values, and determines the procedure for its activity;

g) exercises other powers in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies in accordance with this Federal Law and other federal laws.

3. The authorized body in the sphere of artificial intelligence:

a) develops and submits for the approval of the Government of the Russian Federation state programs and plans of measures for the development of artificial intelligence technologies;

b) in accordance with this Federal Law, other federal laws, and the acts of the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation adopted thereunder, ensures the deployment of artificial intelligence technologies in state and municipal administration;

c) provides methodological support to state bodies and bodies of local self-government in the application of artificial intelligence systems and artificial intelligence services for the purpose of increasing the effectiveness of state and municipal administration.

4. Federal executive authorities, within the limits of their competence, exercise the following powers:

a) develop sectoral programs and plans for the deployment of artificial intelligence technologies, taking into account the requirements of this Federal Law, other federal laws, and the acts of the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation adopted thereunder;

b) coordinate the application of trusted artificial intelligence models in the corresponding spheres (sectors) of activity, taking into account the requirements for ensuring trust in the use of artificial intelligence established by Article 8 of this Federal Law;

c) in cases provided for by this Federal Law, other federal laws, and the acts of the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation adopted thereunder, determine the procedure for the provision of measures of support for the development of artificial intelligence technologies;

d) form a mechanism for the assessment of risks of violation of ethical norms in the application of artificial intelligence technologies;

e) exercise other powers connected with issues of the application of artificial intelligence technologies in cases provided for by this Federal Law, other federal laws, and the acts of the President of the Russian Federation and the Government of the Russian Federation adopted thereunder.

5. The constituent entities of the Russian Federation, in accordance with this Federal Law, exercise the following powers in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies:

a) carry out interregional cooperation on issues of the application of artificial intelligence technologies;

b) provide consultative and other methodological support on issues of the use of artificial intelligence technologies;

c) develop programs and plans for the deployment of artificial intelligence technologies in interregional cooperation with constituent entities of the Russian Federation, taking into account the requirements of this Federal Law;

d) determine the coordinating and executive body of the constituent entity of the Russian Federation authorized in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies;

e) issue regulatory legal acts of the constituent entity of the Russian Federation in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies;

f) ensure the implementation of measures of state support in the sphere of application of artificial intelligence technologies.

---

### Article 20. Specific Features of the Creation and Operation of Computing Infrastructure for Artificial Intelligence

1. For the purposes of increasing the accessibility of computing infrastructure for the development of artificial intelligence technologies, the Government of the Russian Federation has the right to approve a list of data centers (supercomputers) whose creation and operation are carried out in accordance with the special regulation provided for in part 3 of this Article.

2. The initiator of the inclusion of data centers in the list provided for in part 1 of this Article may be Russian legal entities and citizens of the Russian Federation meeting the requirements established by the Government of the Russian Federation.

3. In respect of the creation and operation of data centers (supercomputers) included in the list of data centers provided for in part 1 of this Article, the Government of the Russian Federation establishes special regulation, including specific features of obtaining authorization documentation, of conformity assessment results, of the provision of measures of support, and of other conditions.

---

### Article 21. Entry into Force of the Federal Law

This Federal Law shall enter into force on 1 September 2027.
