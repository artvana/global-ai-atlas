 HOUSE BILL 9 
 
 (PRE-FILED) 
 By: Delegates Lopez and Kerr 
 
 Requested: October 17, 2025 
 Introduced and read first time: January 14, 2026 
 Assigned to: Government, Labor, and Elections 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 3-1-1 Systems - Expansion Program and Oversight Board - Establishment 
 
 FOR the purpose of establishing the Maryland 3-1-1 Oversight Board in the Maryland Information Network; establishing the 3-1-1 Program to use certain artificial intelligence to answer certain questions and route certain calls in certain counties; requiring the expansion of the 3-1-1 Program to all of the counties in Maryland on or before a certain date; and generally relating to 3-1-1 systems. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - Public Safety 
 Section 1-3A-01 through 1-3A-05 to be under the new subtitle &quot;Subtitle 3A. 3-1-1 Nonemergency Community Information and Referral Systems&quot; 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2022 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 BY repealing and reenacting, without amendments, 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 Section 3.5-801(a) and (c) 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 
 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Public Safety SUBTITLE 3A. 
 
 
 3-1-1 NONEMERGENCY COMMUNITY INFORMATION AND REFERRAL SYSTEMS. 
 
 
 1-3A-01. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SUBTITLE THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (B) &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE&quot; HAS THE MEANING STATED IN &sect; 3.5-801 OF THE STATE FINANCE AND PROCUREMENT ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (C) &quot;BOARD&quot; MEANS THE MARYLAND 3-1-1 OVERSIGHT BOARD ESTABLISHED UNDER &sect; 1-3A-02 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (D) &quot;CHATBOT&quot; MEANS A COMPUTER PROGRAM AVAILABLE THROUGH A PHONE NUMBER, A WEBSITE, OR AN APPLICATION THAT SIMULATES HUMAN CONVERSATION THROUGH TEXT OR VOICE, USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO RESPOND TO TYPED QUESTIONS USING INFORMATION FROM CURATED, AGENCY-APPROVED SOURCES SUCH AS GOVERNMENT WEBSITES, SERVICE CATALOGS, ORDINANCES, FORMS, AND GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM LAYERS. 
 
 
 (E) &quot;PROGRAM&quot; MEANS THE 3-1-1 PROGRAM ESTABLISHED UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04 OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (F) &quot;3-1-1 SYSTEM&quot; MEANS A TELEPHONE SERVICE OR OTHER TECHNOLOGY USED TO ACCESS A LOCAL GOVERNMENT SERVICE LINE, DESIGNED FOR REPORTING AND RESOLVING COMMUNITY INFRASTRUCTURE AND SERVICE ISSUES ON A NONEMERGENCY BASIS. 
 
 
 (G) &quot;VOICEBOT&quot; MEANS A COMPUTER PROGRAM AVAILABLE THROUGH A PHONE NUMBER THAT SIMULATES HUMAN CONVERSATION USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY THAT: 
 
 
 (1) RESPONDS TO VOCAL QUESTIONS OVER THE PHONE USING INFORMATION FROM CURATED, AGENCY-APPROVED SOURCES SUCH AS GOVERNMENT WEBSITES, SERVICE CATALOGS, ORDINANCES, FORMS, AND GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM LAYERS; AND 
 
 
 (2) ROUTES CALLS TO APPROPRIATE DESTINATIONS. 
 
 
 1-3A-02. 
 
 
 (A) THERE IS A MARYLAND 3-1-1 OVERSIGHT BOARD IN THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK. 
 
 
 (B) THE BOARD CONSISTS OF: 
 
 
 (1) TWO MEMBERS OF THE SENATE OF MARYLAND, APPOINTED BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE; 
 
 
 (2) TWO MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE OF DELEGATES, APPOINTED BY THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE; 
 
 
 (3) THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS APPOINTED BY THE GOVERNOR: 
 
 
 (I) A PERSON WITH EXPERTISE IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; 
 
 
 (II) A PERSON WITH EXPERTISE IN GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEMS; AND 
 
 
 (III) A MEMBER OF THE GENERAL PUBLIC TO SERVE AS THE CHAIR OF THE BOARD; AND 
 
 
 (4) ONE REPRESENTATIVE FROM EACH OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 (I) THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK; 
 
 
 (II) THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY; 
 
 
 (III) THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT; 
 
 
 (IV) THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF AGING; 
 
 
 (V) THE MARYLAND DEPARTMENT OF DISABILITIES; 
 
 
 (VI) THE MARYLAND 9-1-1 BOARD; 
 
 
 (VII) THE MARYLAND ASSOCIATION OF COUNTIES; 
 
 
 (VIII) THE MARYLAND MUNICIPAL LEAGUE; 
 
 
 (IX) A COUNTY IN THE STATE THAT IS NOT PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04 OF THIS SUBTITLE AND DOES NOT HAVE AN EXISTING 3-1-1 SYSTEM; 
 
 
 (X) A COUNTY IN THE STATE THAT IS NOT PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04 OF THIS SUBTITLE AND HAS AN EXISTING 3-1-1 SYSTEM; 
 
 
 (XI) A COUNTY IN THE STATE THAT IS PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04 OF THIS SUBTITLE AND DOES NOT HAVE AN EXISTING 3-1-1 SYSTEM; AND 
 
 
 (XII) A COUNTY IN THE STATE THAT IS PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04 OF THIS SUBTITLE AND HAS AN EXISTING 3-1-1 SYSTEM. 
 
 
 (C) THE TERMS OF THE MEMBERS DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION (B)(1), (2), AND (4)(IX) THROUGH (XII) OF THIS SECTION SHALL TERMINATE AFTER THE BOARD SUBMITS THE REPORT REQUIRED UNDER &sect; 1-3A-05(B) OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 (D) THE BOARD SHALL MEET AT LEAST FOUR TIMES PER CALENDAR YEAR. 
 
 
 (E) FOR PURPOSES OF THE OPEN MEETINGS ACT, A PROJECT SITE VISIT OR EDUCATIONAL FIELD TOUR MAY NOT BE CONSIDERED A MEETING OF THE BOARD IF NO ORGANIZATIONAL BUSINESS IS CONDUCTED. 
 
 
 (F) A MEMBER OF THE BOARD: 
 
 
 (1) MAY NOT RECEIVE COMPENSATION AS A MEMBER OF THE BOARD; BUT 
 
 
 (2) IS ENTITLED TO REIMBURSEMENT FOR EXPENSES UNDER THE STANDARD STATE TRAVEL REGULATIONS, AS PROVIDED IN THE STATE BUDGET. 
 
 
 (G) THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK SHALL PROVIDE STAFF TO THE BOARD. 
 
 
 1-3A-03. 
 
 
 (A) THE BOARD SHALL: 
 
 
 (1) DESIGNATE COUNTIES TO PARTICIPATE IN THE PROGRAM; 
 
 
 (2) ESTABLISH EVALUATION CRITERIA FOR THE PROGRAM; 
 
 
 (3) REVIEW VENDOR APPLICATIONS SUBMITTED TO THE BOARD BY THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK UNDER &sect; 1-3A-04(D) OF THIS SUBTITLE AND SELECT A VENDOR; 
 
 
 (4) ENSURE 3-1-1 SYSTEMS ALIGN WITH BEST PRACTICES FOR TECHNOLOGY, ACCESSIBILITY, AND EQUITY; 
 
 
 (5) APPROVE AND COORDINATE STATEWIDE MARKETING STRATEGIES FOR THE PROGRAM UNDER THIS SUBTITLE; 
 
 
 (6) IMPLEMENT THE PROGRAM; AND 
 
 
 (7) DEVELOP A SET OF UNIFIED STATEWIDE DATA STANDARDS FOR 3-1-1 SYSTEMS. 
 
 
 (B) MARKETING STRATEGIES APPROVED UNDER THIS SECTION SHALL BE: 
 
 
 (1) ACCESSIBLE; 
 
 
 (2) MULTILINGUAL; AND 
 
 
 (3) CULTURALLY COMPETENT. 
 
 
 1-3A-04. 
 
 
 (A) THE BOARD SHALL ESTABLISH A 3-1-1 PROGRAM. 
 
 
 (B) THE PURPOSE OF THE PROGRAM IS TO EVALUATE AND EXPAND THE USE OF 3-1-1 SYSTEMS IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 (C) THE BOARD SHALL DESIGNATE TWO COUNTIES THAT HAVE 3-1-1 SYSTEMS AND TWO COUNTIES THAT DO NOT HAVE 3-1-1 SYSTEMS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE PROGRAM. 
 
 
 (D) THE BOARD SHALL REQUIRE THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK TO: 
 
 
 (1) SOLICIT REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS FROM VENDORS FOR TECHNOLOGY PLATFORMS AND OTHER SERVICES NECESSARY TO RUN 3-1-1 SYSTEMS FOR THE PROGRAM; AND 
 
 
 (2) SUBMIT A LIST OF POSSIBLE VENDORS TO THE BOARD. 
 
 
 (E) (1) ON OR BEFORE JUNE 30, 2027, THE BOARD SHALL ESTABLISH A CHATBOT IN EACH COUNTY DESIGNATED BY THE BOARD UNDER SUBSECTION (C) OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (2) EACH CHATBOT SHALL INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT; 
 
 
 (II) INTEGRATION WITH GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM DATA FOR ACCURATE ROUTING; AND 
 
 
 (III) CLEAR ESCALATION PROTOCOLS TO ROUTE COMPLEX REQUESTS TO LIVE AGENTS. 
 
 
 (3) (I) EACH COUNTY PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM SHALL DESIGNATE THE SOURCES A CHATBOT MAY USE IN RESPONDING TO QUESTIONS. 
 
 
 (II) 1. THE VENDOR OF THE 3-1-1 SYSTEM CHOSEN TO HOST A CHATBOT SHALL ANALYZE THE PERFORMANCE OF THE CHATBOT BASED ON USER FEEDBACK REGARDING ACCURACY OF CHATBOT RESPONSES. 
 
 
 2. A. THE VENDOR SHALL INVESTIGATE AN ANSWER THAT A USER DESIGNATES AS INACCURATE. 
 
 
 B. IF THE SOURCE IS DETERMINED TO BE INACCURATE, THE AGENCY OPERATING THE SOURCE WEBPAGE SHALL UPDATE IT TO BECOME ACCURATE. 
 
 
 (F) (1) ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1, 2028, THE BOARD SHALL ESTABLISH A VOICEBOT IN EACH PARTICIPATING COUNTY. 
 
 
 (2) EACH VOICEBOT SHALL INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT; 
 
 
 (II) THE ABILITY TO TRANSFER CALLS TO 9-1-1, 2-1-1, AND 9-8-8, AS NEEDED; 
 
 
 (III) INTEGRATION WITH GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM DATA FOR ACCURATE ROUTING; AND 
 
 
 (IV) CLEAR ESCALATION PROTOCOLS TO ROUTE COMPLEX REQUESTS TO LIVE AGENTS. 
 
 
 (3) THE VENDOR OF THE 3-1-1 SYSTEM CHOSEN TO HOST A VOICEBOT SHALL ANALYZE THE PERFORMANCE OF THE VOICEBOT IN THE SAME WAY AS THE CHATBOT UNDER SUBSECTION (E)(3)(II) OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (G) ON OR BEFORE JULY 1, 2028, THE BOARD SHALL IMPLEMENT THE PROGRAM IN EACH COUNTY IN THE STATE ACCORDING TO THE IMPLEMENTATION PLAN SUBMITTED UNDER &sect; 1-3A-05(B) OF THIS SUBTITLE. 
 
 
 1-3A-05. 
 
 
 (A) (1) ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1, 2027, THE BOARD, IN CONSULTATION WITH THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK, SHALL SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR AND, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY ON THE PROGRESS OF THE PROGRAM. 
 
 
 (2) THE REPORT REQUIRED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION SHALL INCLUDE: 
 
 
 (I) CALL DEFLECTION TRACKING BASED ON THE REDUCTION IN 9-1-1 CALLS FOR THAT TIME PERIOD; 
 
 
 (II) USER SATISFACTION; 
 
 
 (III) ACCESSIBILITY OF THE 3-1-1 SYSTEM; 
 
 
 (IV) COSTS SAVED BY A PARTICIPATING COUNTY, IF ANY; 
 
 
 (V) AN EVALUATION OF THE INTEGRATION OF THE CHATBOTS AND VOICEBOTS INTO EACH COUNTY; AND 
 
 
 (VI) RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT. 
 
 
 (B) ON OR BEFORE JULY 1, 2028, THE BOARD, IN CONSULTATION WITH THE MARYLAND INFORMATION NETWORK, SHALL SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR AND, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY THAT: 
 
 
 (1) EVALUATES THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PROGRAM INCLUDING: 
 
 
 (I) COST EFFECTIVENESS OF THE CHATBOT AND VOICEBOT; 
 
 
 (II) EQUITY IN SERVICE ACCESS; 
 
 
 (III) CHALLENGES OF INTEGRATING THE CHATBOT AND VOICEBOT INTO COUNTY SYSTEMS; 
 
 
 (IV) USER SATISFACTION; AND 
 
 
 (V) CALL VOLUME DATA FOR 9-1-1 IN THE PROGRAM COUNTIES; AND 
 
 
 (2) CREATES A COMPREHENSIVE PLAN TO EXPAND THE PROGRAM TO EACH COUNTY IN THE STATE. 
 
 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 
 3.5-801. 
 (a) In this subtitle the following words have the meanings indicated. 
 (c) &quot;Artificial intelligence&quot; means a machine-based system that: 
 (1) can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments; 
 (2) uses machine and human-based inputs to perceive real and virtual environments and abstracts those perceptions into models through analysis in an automated manner; and 
 (3) uses model inference to formulate options for information or action. 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
