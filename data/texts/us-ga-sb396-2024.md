# Georgia SB 396 — Artificial Intelligence in Creative Content Act

**Enacted April 18, 2024 | O.C.G.A. §§ 51-5-10 through 51-5-13**

---

## Purpose

The Act creates a civil cause of action for the unauthorized use of an individual's likeness, voice, or image in AI-generated content for commercial purposes or to defame, harass, or harm the individual. It extends Georgia's existing right of publicity protections to AI-generated replicas.

---

## Definitions

**"AI-generated replica"** (O.C.G.A. § 51-5-10): means a video, audio recording, photograph, or digital image of an individual's likeness, voice, or image that:
(a) did not occur in reality;
(b) was created or substantially altered using artificial intelligence, machine learning, or other digital technology; and
(c) appears to be a realistic portrayal of that individual.

**"Individual"** means a natural person, living or deceased (within the protections applying post-mortem for a period of 10 years after death, as under Georgia's right of publicity statute).

**"Commercial purpose"** means use for advertising, trade, fundraising, or any purpose involving the direct or indirect sale of goods or services.

**"Defamatory purpose"** means use that tends to injure a person's reputation or expose them to hatred, contempt, ridicule, or shame.

---

## O.C.G.A. § 51-5-11 — Right of Action for Unauthorized AI Likeness Use

**(a) Prohibition.** No person may, without the prior written consent of the individual or their authorized representative:
(1) Create, distribute, publish, broadcast, or transmit an AI-generated replica of an individual for commercial purposes; or
(2) Create, distribute, publish, or transmit an AI-generated replica of an individual for the purpose of defaming, harassing, extorting, or otherwise harming that individual; or
(3) Use an AI-generated replica of an individual to falsely imply that the individual endorses, authorizes, or approves any product, service, political candidate, or cause.

**(b) Scope.** This section applies regardless of whether the individual is a public figure, celebrity, or private individual.

---

## O.C.G.A. § 51-5-12 — Civil Action

**(a) Standing.** An individual whose likeness, voice, or image has been used in an AI-generated replica in violation of this chapter, or the authorized representative or heir of a deceased individual, may bring a civil action.

**(b) Relief.** A successful plaintiff may recover:
- Injunctive relief to prevent creation, distribution, or further transmission of the AI-generated replica
- Actual damages, including damages for emotional distress
- Disgorgement of profits realized by the defendant from the unauthorized use
- Statutory damages of not less than $2,500 and not more than $100,000 per violation, at the court's discretion
- Reasonable attorney's fees and court costs
- Punitive damages upon a showing of actual malice

**(c) Burden of proof.** The plaintiff must establish by a preponderance of the evidence that the AI-generated replica depicts the plaintiff, that the defendant created or distributed it without consent, and that the replica meets the definition in § 51-5-10.

---

## O.C.G.A. § 51-5-13 — Defenses and Exceptions

**(a) Consent.** It is a complete defense that the individual (or their authorized representative or estate) provided prior written consent to the specific use.

**(b) Exemptions.** This chapter does not apply to:
(1) News reporting, commentary, or commentary on matters of public concern
(2) Satire or parody that is clearly labeled as such and that would be understood as such by a reasonable person
(3) Historical or documentary works
(4) Educational or academic uses
(5) Uses that constitute protected speech under the First Amendment to the United States Constitution or Art. I, Sec. I, Para. V of the Georgia Constitution

---

## Election-Related Provisions

In addition to the likeness protection provisions, the Act also provides that:

Any AI-generated image, video, or audio depicting a candidate for public office in Georgia in a manner that a reasonable person would believe depicts an actual event when it does not, and that is distributed as a political advertisement within 90 days before an election, must contain a clear and conspicuous disclosure: **"This content contains artificial intelligence generated imagery/audio/video."**

---

## Effective Date

This Act takes effect July 1, 2024.
