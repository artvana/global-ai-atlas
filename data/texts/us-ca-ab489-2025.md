# California Assembly Bill 489 — Healthcare AI Deception Prevention Act

**Assembly Bill No. 489**

*California — October 11, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

AB 489 prohibits the use of artificial intelligence systems in ways that deceive patients into believing they are receiving care from a licensed healthcare professional, and requires clear AI disclosure in patient communications by healthcare facilities.

### Prohibition on Healthcare Licensing Term Impersonation

The bill prohibits AI systems from using terms associated with licensed healthcare professions — including "doctor," "physician," "nurse," "therapist," and "counselor" — in ways that imply users are receiving licensed professional care from a qualified human practitioner. This prohibition targets AI-powered chatbots, virtual assistants, and automated patient communication systems that may mislead patients about the nature of the interaction.

### Disclosure Requirements for Generative AI in Patient Communications

Healthcare facilities that use generative AI in patient-facing communications must include a clear, conspicuous disclaimer stating that the communication is AI-generated and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The disclaimer must be present in the communication itself, not merely in terms of service or privacy notices.

### Enforcement

The Attorney General may bring civil enforcement actions. The bill also creates a private right of action for patients harmed by violations, permitting individuals to sue for actual damages and injunctive relief. Enforcement by city attorneys and county counsel is also permitted.

### Effective Date

The act takes effect January 1, 2026.
