# Cloud and AI Development Act (CADA)

**COM(2026) 502 final — Brussels, 3.6.2026**
*Proposal for a Regulation of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing a framework of measures for strengthening Europe's cloud and AI ecosystem*

---

## Overview

The European Commission adopted this proposal on 3 June 2026. It addresses two vulnerabilities the Commission identifies in the EU's digital landscape: a structural deficit in data centre capacity, and dependence on a small number of non-EU cloud computing service providers. EU-based providers' share of the European cloud market fell from approximately 29% in 2017 to around 15% by 2022.

CADA is part of the **AI Continent Action Plan**, whose principal objectives are to expand the EU's AI computing capacity, improve access to high-quality data, reinforce the EU's AI skills base, and facilitate implementation of the EU AI Act. It is designed to complement the **Apply AI Strategy**, and sits alongside the **Chips Act 2.0** and the **EU Open Source Strategy** as part of what the Commission describes as building "a more competitive, secure and resilient European digital economy."

Unlike the AI Act, CADA does not regulate the conduct of AI systems. It operates at the infrastructure and procurement layer — where and on whose infrastructure AI is built, hosted and bought in Europe.

## Three focus areas

**1. Research and innovation.** Member states and organisations are to support the deployment of next-generation, sustainable cloud and AI technologies.

**2. Data centre capacity.** The Regulation would accelerate the conditions for deploying data centres across the EU, with particular emphasis on facilities supporting public sector functions. It targets barriers including lengthy permitting processes, site constraints and energy access, and emphasises energy-efficient capacity.

**3. Cloud and AI sovereignty.** A single EU-wide assessment framework is introduced to evaluate cloud and AI sovereignty, coupled with a public sector adoption mechanism for assessed offerings.

## Status

Adopted by the Commission on 3 June 2026 and transmitted to the European Parliament and the Council under the ordinary legislative procedure. The file has been assigned in the Parliament's Legislative Train Schedule under "A new plan for Europe's sustainable prosperity and competitiveness", and an opinion of the European Economic and Social Committee is in preparation. As of 27 July 2026 there is no committee report and no Council general approach.

Some commentary dates the proposal to 2 July 2026; that is the date on which the Commission opened the stakeholder feedback period under the AI Continent Action Plan consultation, which runs to 27 August 2026. The proposal itself is dated 3 June 2026.
