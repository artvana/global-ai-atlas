CODE OF ETHICS IN THE SPHERE OF
DEVELOPMENT AND APPLICATION OF
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
IN THE FINANCIAL MARKET

                                                                      2
Code of Ethics in the Sphere of Development and Application of
Artificial Intelligence in the Financial Market




1.	OBJECTIVES AND PRINCIPLES OF THE DEVELOPMENT
    AND APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL
    INTELLIGENCE IN THE FINANCIAL
    MARKET

1.1.   The objectives of this Code are:

1)     to increase the trust of natural persons and legal entities (hereinafter –
       clients) in the application of artificial intelligence by credit
       institutions, foreign banks operating
       on the territory of the Russian Federation through their
       branches, non-credit financial institutions, persons
       providing professional services in the financial market,
       entities of the national payment system (hereinafter – organizations)
       in the provision of services to clients;

2)     to facilitate the development of artificial intelligence in the financial
       market and the application by organizations of trusted artificial
       intelligence technologies;

3)     to minimize risks associated with the development and application
       of artificial intelligence in the financial market (hereinafter – risks
       of artificial intelligence).

1.2.   In order to achieve the stated objectives, when developing and applying
       artificial intelligence, organizations are recommended to comply with
       the following principles:

1)     human-centricity;

2)     fairness;

3)     transparency;

4)     safety, reliability, and effectiveness;

5)     responsible risk management.

1.3.   Organizations are recommended to exchange experience, as well as
       to participate in the development of joint documents and analytical
       materials that contribute to achieving the objectives of this Code.

1.4.   In the event that organizations engage, in connection with the development
       and application of artificial intelligence, persons in respect of whom the Bank
       of Russia does not exercise control (supervision), organizations are recommended
       to ensure compliance by such persons with this Code.

2.	PRINCIPLE OF HUMAN-CENTRICITY
2.1.   Organizations are recommended to take the following measures:

1)     improving the quality of the provision of services;

2)     providing clients with the opportunity to refuse
       interaction involving the application of artificial intelligence;

3)     ensuring the possibility of revising a decision made
       with the application of artificial intelligence;

4)     raising client awareness regarding the making of decisions
       with the application of artificial intelligence;

5)     enhancing financial accessibility.

2.2.   As part of improving the quality of the provision of services, organizations
       are recommended to assess the satisfaction of clients
       to whom services have been provided with the application of artificial intelligence,
       as well as to monitor the quality of the provision of such services.

2.3.   As part of providing clients with the opportunity to refuse
       interaction involving the application of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to provide clients with the opportunity
       to interact with an employee of the organization.

2.4.   As part of ensuring the possibility of revising a decision
       made with the application of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended, on the basis of a client's request,
       to arrange for such a decision to be reviewed by an employee
       of the organization.

2.5.   As part of raising client awareness regarding the making
       of decisions with the application of artificial intelligence, organizations
       are recommended to explain the actions that the client must
       perform in order for artificial intelligence to make a decision
       on the basis of which a conclusion is drawn regarding the possibility of providing
       services. The organization has the right not to provide such
       explanations if there are reasonable grounds to believe that
       such notification may reduce the effectiveness of the application
       of artificial intelligence.

2.6.   As part of enhancing financial accessibility, organizations
       are recommended, when developing and applying artificial
       intelligence, to take into account factors of client vulnerability (age,
       education, limited capabilities, and others) and the possible
       influence of such factors on the provision of services to such clients.

3.	PRINCIPLE OF FAIRNESS
3.1.   Organizations are recommended to take the following measures:

1)     justification of the use of personal data of clients;

2)     inadmissibility of the use of discriminatory factors;

3)     ensuring the non-discriminatory character of data sets.

3.2.   As part of the justification of the use of personal data
       of clients, organizations are recommended, when developing
       artificial intelligence, to use the personal data of clients
       only in cases where their use is required
       for a substantial increase in the effectiveness of the application
       of artificial intelligence in the provision of services.

3.3.   As part of the inadmissibility of the use of discriminatory
       factors, organizations are recommended, when making decisions
       with the application of artificial intelligence, not to take into account, in particular,
       the following factors:

1)     national, linguistic, and racial affiliation;

2)     affiliation with political parties or public associations;

3)     religious denomination and attitude toward religion.

3.4.   As part of ensuring the non-discriminatory character of data
       sets, organizations are recommended, when developing artificial
       intelligence, to verify the completeness, currency, and reliability
       of data on social groups of clients in the provision of services to whom
       the organization has the right to apply artificial intelligence.



4.	PRINCIPLE OF TRANSPARENCY

4.1.   Organizations are recommended to take the following measures:

1)     informing about the application of artificial intelligence;

2)     disclosure of information about artificial intelligence;

3)     labeling of information created with the application of large
       generative models.

4.2.   As part of informing about the application of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to inform clients about the application
       of artificial intelligence in the provision of services to them, where the application
       of artificial intelligence is not evident from the circumstances.

4.3.   As part of the disclosure of information about artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to provide clients with complete
       and reliable information about the risks of artificial intelligence
       and the conditions of the organization's application of artificial intelligence.

4.4.   As part of the labeling of information created with the application
       of large generative models, organizations are recommended
       to label data, including images, audio
       and video materials, created with the application of large generative
       models, except in the following cases:

1)     large generative models are applied solely for
       the editing of data which does not entail substantial
       alteration of the content of such data;

2)     the application of large generative models is evident from the circumstances;

3)     there is no risk of harm being caused to clients.



5.	PRINCIPLE OF SAFETY,
    RELIABILITY, AND EFFECTIVENESS
5.1.   Organizations are recommended to take the following measures:

1)     verification of the quality of data sets;

2)     verification of the quality of artificial intelligence;

3)     monitoring of the quality of artificial intelligence;

4)     ensuring information security;

5)     ensuring the confidentiality of information;

6)     ensuring continuity of operations.

5.2.   As part of the verification of the quality of data sets for the development
       of artificial intelligence, organizations are recommended
       to verify the data sets used, including for
       their reliability, accuracy, and completeness, to collect and store information
       about the sources of the data sets and the labeling of the data, to correct
       inaccuracies in the data sets, and to keep them up to date.

5.3.   As part of the verification of the quality of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to establish quality indicators
       for artificial intelligence and, during the development
       of artificial intelligence, to verify the conformity of the artificial intelligence
       with the quality indicators established by the organization, including
       through validation and voluntary certification.

5.4.   As part of the monitoring of the quality of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to verify on a regular basis
       the artificial intelligence in use for compliance with
       the quality indicators established by the organization.

5.5.   As part of ensuring information security, organizations
       are recommended to take into account the risks of artificial intelligence
       associated with breaches of information security
       and operational reliability, and to assess the adequacy
       of the measures available and planned for implementation in counteracting
       information security threats at all stages of the development
       and application of artificial intelligence, including with respect to
       data sets.

5.6.   As part of ensuring the confidentiality of information,
       organizations are recommended to develop a system of technological
       and organizational measures to ensure the security of restricted-access data
       used in the development and application of
       artificial intelligence, including measures related
       to the depersonalization of such data and counteracting their unauthorized
       dissemination in the application of large generative models
       by employees of the organization and its clients.

5.7.   As part of ensuring continuity of operations, organizations
       are recommended to ensure the performance of significant functions
       carried out with the application of artificial intelligence
       by employees of the organization in the event of a temporary inability
       to apply artificial intelligence.



6.	PRINCIPLE OF RESPONSIBLE
    RISK MANAGEMENT
6.1.   Organizations are recommended to organize and carry out
       risk management in the development and application of artificial
       intelligence as part of the overall risk management system
       in the organization.

6.2.   As part of organizing risk management in the development
       and application of artificial intelligence, organizations are recommended,
       taking into account the particular features of the type and scale of their activities,
       organizational structure, existing risk management
       system, and the nature of services provided by the organization, to develop
       and approve rules for managing the risks of artificial intelligence,
       constituting an integral part of the documents on risk management applied
       in the organization, or in the form of a stand-alone document, and also
       to designate a person or division responsible for compliance with
       the said rules and their conformity with this Code.

6.3.   Organizations are recommended, in the management of the risks
       of artificial intelligence, to ensure the following processes:

1)   accounting of the artificial intelligence models in use;

2)   identification of the risks of artificial intelligence;

3)   assessment and assignment of the risk level of artificial intelligence;

4)   monitoring and control of the risks of artificial intelligence;

5)   minimization of the identified risks of artificial intelligence;

6)   response to materialized risks of artificial intelligence
     (hereinafter – risk events);

7)   maintenance of a database of risk events.

6.4.   As part of accounting of the artificial intelligence models in use,
       organizations are recommended to maintain centralized accounting of artificial
       intelligence models and instances of their application, taking into account
       the assigned risk level of artificial intelligence.

6.5.   As part of the identification of the risks of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to document information
       on the procedure for the development of artificial intelligence, including a description
       of the process of artificial intelligence development, the data sets used
       in development, information on the conditions of the application
       of artificial intelligence, and the persons responsible for the application
       of artificial intelligence in the organization, as well as to analyze
       the said information for the presence of risks of artificial
       intelligence.

6.6.   As part of the assessment and assignment of the risk level of artificial
       intelligence, organizations are recommended to formulate a system
       of risk factors and risk levels, and to assign risk levels
       to the artificial intelligence being developed and applied,
       taking into account the established risk factors.

6.7.   When assigning the risk level of artificial intelligence,
       it is recommended to take into account the following risk factors:

1)   the sphere of application of artificial intelligence (for example,
     application of artificial intelligence in the provision of services
     to clients, in risk management systems, in the management
     of the property of the organization, in critically important processes
     of the organization, in processes for which requirements
     of operational reliability are established);

2)   the use, in the development of artificial intelligence, of restricted-access
     data;

3)   the size of losses or damage to business reputation that may be
     caused to the organization in the event of materialization of risk;

4)   the number of clients in the provision of services to whom
     artificial intelligence is applied;

5)   the explainability of decisions made by artificial intelligence;

6)   the application of artificial intelligence developed by a third party;

7)   the use of data sets obtained from third parties, or
     of data sets in the public domain on the information and
     telecommunications network "Internet";

8)   the existence of risk events related to the application of artificial
     intelligence.

6.8.   As part of monitoring and control of the risks of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to verify on a regular basis the processes
       associated with the development and application of artificial intelligence
       for the presence of risks, as well as to carry out preliminary,
       current, and subsequent control of risks taking into account the specifics
       of the particular case of application of artificial intelligence.

6.9.   As part of the minimization of the identified risks of artificial intelligence,
       organizations are recommended to determine measures aimed
       at reducing the risk level of artificial intelligence, and the persons
       responsible for the implementation of the said measures, as well as to ensure
       that employees of the organization exercise control over the decisions of artificial
       intelligence to which a high risk level has been assigned.

6.10.  As part of responding to materialized risks of artificial
       intelligence, organizations are recommended to determine the types of risk events
       and to take measures aimed at minimizing the consequences
       of risk events, and also to provide their employees and clients with
       the opportunity to submit complaints and reports concerning risk events.

6.11.  As part of maintaining a database of risk events, organizations are recommended
       to identify risk events and to carry out their registration with a description
       of the risk event, the circumstances giving rise to the occurrence of the risk event,
       as well as the inclusion of information on measures aimed
       at minimizing the consequences of the risk event, and the status of the implementation
       of such measures.
