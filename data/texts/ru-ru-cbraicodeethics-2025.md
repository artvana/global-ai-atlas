Bank of Russia (Central Bank of the Russian Federation) — Code of Ethics for Working with Artificial Intelligence for Financial Organizations
RUSSIA | 2025

Source: https://en.iz.ru/en/1918214/2025-07-09/central-bank-russian-federation-has-developed-code-ethics-artificial-intelligence
Original source: Central Bank of the Russian Federation (Bank of Russia) — cbr.ru

The Central Bank of the Russian Federation (Bank of Russia) published a voluntary Code of Ethics for Working with Artificial Intelligence for Financial Organizations on 9 July 2025. The code provides sector-specific guidance for banks, insurance companies, and other financial organizations on the ethical, responsible, and transparent use of AI systems.

Key Provisions (based on publicly reported information):

1. Voluntary Nature
The Code of Ethics is a non-binding document establishing supervisory expectations and best practice guidance for Russian financial sector participants. Adoption is voluntary, though the Central Bank indicated it would take code adherence into account in supervisory assessments.

2. Core Ethical Principles
The code articulates key principles for AI use in financial services:
   - Human accountability: Humans must remain accountable for AI-driven decisions; responsibility cannot be fully delegated to AI systems
   - Non-discrimination: AI systems must not produce discriminatory or biased outcomes in customer treatment
   - Transparency: Financial organizations must be transparent about the use of AI systems in their operations
   - Fairness: AI systems must treat customers fairly and equitably
   - Safety and reliability: AI systems must be safe, reliable, and robust

3. Governance Requirements
   - Financial organizations must establish internal governance frameworks for AI
   - Board-level oversight of AI strategy and risk management
   - Designation of responsible officers for AI governance
   - Documentation and audit requirements for AI systems

4. Consumer Protection
   - Customers should be informed when AI is used in decisions affecting them
   - Mechanisms for customers to challenge AI-driven decisions must be available
   - AI must not be used to exploit customer vulnerabilities

5. Model Risk Management
   - AI models must undergo validation and testing before deployment
   - Ongoing monitoring for model drift, bias, and performance degradation
   - Incident response procedures for AI system failures

6. Third-Party AI
   - Financial organizations remain responsible for third-party AI systems they use
   - Due diligence requirements for AI vendor selection

7. Context
The code was developed as part of the Central Bank's broader AI regulatory agenda, which also includes:
- Participation in the development of federal AI legislation (Draft Law on Fundamentals of State Regulation of AI Technologies, 2026)
- Alignment with the National AI Code of Ethics (2021) developed by the Analytical Center under the Government of the Russian Federation
