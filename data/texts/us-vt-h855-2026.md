 Subject: Court procedure; defenses; actions based on harm caused by artificial intelligence 
 Statement of purpose of bill as introduced: This bill proposes, for purposes of a civil action based on harm caused to a plaintiff by artificial intelligence that was developed or used by a defendant, to prohibit the defendant from raising as a defense that the artificial intelligence autonomously caused the harm. 
 
 
 An act relating to defenses in civil actions based on harm caused by artificial intelligence 
 
 
 
 It is hereby enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Vermont: 
 
 Sec. 1. 12 V.S.A. Section 1036a is added to read: 
 
 Section 1036a. DEFENSE PROHIBITED; HARM CAUSED BY AUTONOMOUS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE 
 
 
 (a) In an action against a defendant who developed, modified, or used artificial intelligence that is alleged to have caused harm to the plaintiff, it shall not be a defense, and the defendant shall not assert, that the artificial intelligence autonomously caused the harm to the plaintiff. 
 
 
 (b) This section shall not limit or preclude a defendant from presenting: 
 
 
 (1) any other affirmative defenses allowed by law; or 
 
 
 (2) any other relevant evidence, including evidence relevant to causation, foreseeability, or contributory or comparative negligence. 
 
 
 (c) As used in this section, &quot;artificial intelligence&quot; means an engineered or machine-based system that varies in its level of autonomy and that can, for explicit or implicit objectives, infer from the input it receives how to generate outputs that can influence physical or virtual environments. 
 
 
 Sec. 2. EFFECTIVE DATE 
 
 This act shall take effect on passage.
