 SENATE BILL NO. 283 
 
 IN THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF ALASKA 
 THIRTY-FOURTH LEGISLATURE - SECOND SESSION 
 
 
 
 A BILL 
 
 
 FOR AN ACT ENTITLED 
 
 
 &quot;An Act relating to staffing standards for registered nurses; and providing for an effective date.&quot; 
 
 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF ALASKA: 
 
 
 
 * Section 1. AS 18.20 is amended by adding new sections to read: 
 
 Article 2A. Hospital Registered Nurse Staffing. 
 
 
 Sec. 18.20.265. Registered nurse minimum staffing standards for hospitals. (a) A hospital shall comply with the registered nurse minimum staffing standards established in this section. 
 (b) The maximum number of patients each registered nurse may be assigned during a shift is as follows: 
 (1) in an emergency department, four patients or, if an assigned patient will be admitted as an in-patient in another unit, the lowest maximum number of patients permitted in that unit; a registered nurse may not be assigned more than one trauma or critical care patient; 
 (2) in an intensive care unit, 
 (A) two patients; or 
 (B) one patient, if the patient is assessed as a high acuity patient; 
 (3) in a labor and delivery unit, 
 (A) two patients; or 
 (B) one patient, if the patient is in active labor or is in any stage of labor and experiencing complications; 
 (4) in a postpartum or antepartum unit or well-baby nursery, six patients; for the purpose of this paragraph, each mother and each baby is a separate patient; 
 (5) in an operating room, one patient; 
 (6) in an oncology unit, four patients; 
 (7) in a post-anesthesia care unit, two patients; 
 (8) in a progressive care or step-down unit, four patients; 
 (9) in an intermediate specialty care unit, three patients; 
 (10) in a medical-surgical unit, four patients; 
 (11) in a telemetry unit, four patients; 
 (12) in a psychiatric unit, 
 (A) four patients; or 
 (B) fewer than four patients, as determined by the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing standards, if an assigned patient 
 (i) requires one-on-one observation; 
 (ii) is in an acute-manic phase; 
 (iii) is volatile; 
 (iv) is physically compromised; or 
 (v) is assessed as a high acuity patient; 
 (13) in a pediatrics unit, four patients; and 
 (14) in an in-patient hemodialysis unit, one patient. 
 (c) The registered nurse staffing standards established in this section are based on the type of care provided in a patient care unit, regardless of the specific name or reference used by the hospital for the unit. 
 (d) A hospital may not average the number of patients or the total number of registered nurses assigned to patients in a patient care unit during a single shift or over any period to meet the registered nurse staffing standards established in this section. 
 (e) A hospital may not include a registered nurse in charge of coordinating patient care responsibilities among nurses, a coordinator of patient care flow, or a persona generated by artificial intelligence or other nonhuman persona in meeting the registered nurse staffing standards. Registered nurses who are working remotely or virtually and are not physically present in the hospital department may not be included to meet the registered nurse staffing standards. 
 (f) Before a registered nurse may be assigned to a patient care unit or clinical area, the registered nurse must have received an orientation in the clinical area sufficient to demonstrate current competence in providing care in the area. 
 (g) Nothing in this section precludes a hospital from assigning fewer patients to a registered nurse than the maximum determined in the staffing standards established in this section. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.270. Registered nurse staffing plans for hospitals. (a) A hospital shall have a registered nurse staffing plan. The registered nurse staffing plan must include minimum staffing levels for each unit in the hospital, including 
 (1) the number of registered nurses employed in each care unit in the hospital required to meet the registered nurse minimum staffing standards established in AS 18.20.265; 
 (2) the minimum registered nurse skill mix for each patient care unit in the hospital, including in-patient, critical care, and emergency units; and 
 (3) the number of supportive direct patient care personnel required to be assigned to each patient care unit and the classification of each supportive direct patient care personnel member assigned to a patient care unit. 
 (b) A hospital shall include the following in the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan: 
 (1) a patient acuity classification system for determining the nursing care needs of an individual patient; 
 (2) a written certification that the registered nurse staffing plan is sufficient to provide adequate, appropriate, and safe delivery of health care services to patients during the year; 
 (3) the hospital&rsquo;s employment practices concerning the use of temporary and travel nurses; and 
 (4) the hospital&rsquo;s process for internal review of the registered nurse staffing plan. 
 (c) A hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan must be approved by a majority vote of the members of the hospital&rsquo;s staffing committee. 
 (d) A hospital shall post the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan in a location in each unit of the hospital that is visible, conspicuous, and accessible to staff, patients, and the public. 
 (e) The department shall post each hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan on the department&rsquo;s Internet website. 
 (f) A hospital may not reduce the level of supportive direct patient care personnel established in a hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan or a collective bargaining agreement. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.275. Variances. (a) A hospital may deviate from the registered nurse minimum staffing standards established in AS 18.20.265 or the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan required by AS 18.20.270 in the following circumstances: 
 (1) an unforeseen emergency situation that could jeopardize patient safety; in this paragraph, &quot;unforeseen emergency situation&quot; has the meaning given in AS 18.20.400(c)(3)(B); 
 (2) a scheduling problem caused by unforeseen weather conditions that prevent a second nurse from arriving at the hospital to relieve the nurse on duty; in this paragraph, &quot;unforeseen weather conditions&quot; has the meaning given in AS 18.20.400(c)(3)(C); 
 (3) the hospital is located in a rural community and declares a temporary nurse staffing emergency under AS 18.20.410. 
 (b) Within 30 days after a hospital deviates from a written registered nurse staffing plan under circumstances outlined in (a)(1) or (2) of this section, the hospital shall report the deviation to the staffing committee along with an assessment of the staffing needs arising from the circumstances. A deviation from the written registered nurse staffing plan may continue for longer than 90 days only upon a majority vote by the staffing committee approving the deviation. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.280. Staffing committees for hospitals. (a) A hospital shall maintain a staffing committee to prepare the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan. 
 (b) At least 51 percent of the staffing committee members must be registered nurses who are employed by the hospital in a nonsupervisory capacity and whose primary responsibility is to provide direct patient care. For a hospital in which registered nurses are part of a collective bargaining unit, the representative of the collective bargaining unit shall appoint the registered nurse members of the staffing committee. If the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurses are not part of a collective bargaining unit, the registered nurse members of the staffing committee shall be selected by peers of the registered nurses. 
 (c) At least one member of the staffing committee must be a supportive direct patient care personnel member employed by the hospital in a nonsupervisory and nonmanagerial capacity. For a hospital in which supportive direct patient care personnel are part of a collective bargaining unit, the representative of the collective bargaining unit shall appoint the supportive direct patient care personnel of the staffing committee. If the hospital&rsquo;s supportive direct patient care personnel are not part of a collective bargaining unit, the supportive direct patient care personnel members shall be selected by peers of the supportive direct patient care personnel. 
 (d) The remainder of the members of the staffing committee shall be appointed by the hospital administration. The staffing committee must include the hospital&rsquo;s chief financial officer, chief nursing officer, and patient care unit directors or managers, or designees of the officers, directors, or managers. 
 (e) A hospital shall release each member of the staffing committee from the member&rsquo;s assignment and ensure adequate staffing to cover the member&rsquo;s assignment so that the member can participate in staffing committee meetings. The hospital shall pay each member the member&rsquo;s regular rate of pay, including any differentials, for time spent at committee meetings. Time spent at committee meetings constitute hours worked for the purpose of payment of overtime wages. 
 (f) If less than all the committee members are present, the committee may take action if least 51 percent of the members present are registered nurses and supportive direct patient care personnel appointed under (b) and (c) of this section. 
 (g) A hospital may not retaliate, discriminate, or take any other adverse action against an employee or any aspect of an employee&rsquo;s employment, including discharge, demotion, reduction in compensation, or changes to terms, conditions, or privileges of employment, because of the employee&rsquo;s participation in the staffing committee or because the employee files a report or complaint or otherwise raises concerns regarding unsafe staffing. 
 (h) A registered nurse, collective bargaining representative, if applicable, patient, or other person may file a report or complaint with the staffing committee about an assignment in a patient care unit that deviates from the hospital&rsquo;s staffing plan. A hospital shall include any report or complaint filed with the staffing committee in the hospital&rsquo;s quarterly report to the department required by AS 18.20.285. 
 (i) The department shall develop a process for issuing a corrective plan of action to a hospital that fails to meet the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan required by AS 18.20.270 or that fails to meet the registered nurse minimum staffing standards established in AS 18.20.265. If the department determines, through regular reporting or review of complaints, that a hospital has been out of compliance with the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan or staffing standards, the department shall issue a corrective plan of action within 30 days after the department presents its findings to the hospital. Failure to comply with a corrective plan of action may result in a civil penalty in accordance with AS 18.20.290. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.285. Tracking, reporting, and compliance . (a) By October 1 of each year, a hospital shall provide to the department, through a process determined by the department, the hospital&rsquo;s registered nurse staffing plan for the following calendar year. A hospital shall notify the department each time the registered nurse staffing plan is updated. 
 (b) Not later than the last business day of the month following each quarter, a hospital shall submit a quarterly report to the department. The quarterly report must include 
 (1) each date the hospital failed to meet the requirements of the registered nurse staffing plan established in AS 18.20.270, the unit in which the failure occurred, the classification of each patient care provider not adequately staffed, and the staffing levels for that classification; and 
 (2) any report or complaint filed with the staffing committee. 
 (c) If a hospital has not submitted a registered nurse staffing plan to the department, the quarterly report must include 
 (1) each date the hospital failed to meet the registered nurse minimum staffing standards established in AS 18.20.265, the unit in which the failure occurred, the classification of each patient care provider not adequately staffed, and the staffing levels for that classification; and 
 (2) any report or complaint filed with the staffing committee. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.290. Civil penalties. (a) In accordance with regulations adopted by the department under AS 44.62.010 - 44.62.305, the department may assess and collect a civil penalty of 
 (1) $10,000 for each day a hospital with 100 beds or more, or $2,500 for each day a hospital with fewer than 100 beds, is found to be in violation of AS 18.20.285(a); 
 (2) $5,000 for each business day a hospital with 100 beds or more, or $1,000 for each business day a hospital with fewer than 100 beds, is found to be in violation of AS 18.20.285(b) or (c); 
 (3) $5,000 for each violation for which a hospital with 100 beds or more, or $1,000 for each violation for which a hospital with fewer than 100 beds, is found to have failed to follow a corrective plan issued under AS 18.20.280(i). 
 (b) The department may use any remedy available under law to collect an unpaid civil penalty for a violation of AS 18.20.265 - 18.20.295. 
 
 Sec. 18.20.295. Definitions. In AS 18.20.265 - 18.20.295, 
 (1) &quot;acuity&quot; means the level of a patient&rsquo;s need for nursing care, as determined by a nursing assessment; 
 (2) &quot;department&quot; means the Department of Health; 
 (3) &quot;direct care&quot; means care that is delivered directly to patients, not telephonically or virtually; 
 (4) &quot;hospital&quot; has the meaning given in AS 18.20.130; 
 (5) &quot;intensive care unit&quot; includes a critical care unit, special care unit, coronary care unit, pediatric intensive care unit, neonatal intensive care unit, neurological critical care unit, burn unit, or any unit within a hospital that provides specialized care to a patient with a life-threatening illness or injury that requires continuous monitoring and treatment 24 hours a day; 
 (6) &quot;registered nurse&quot; has the meaning given to &quot;licensed registered nurse&quot; in AS 08.68.850; 
 (7) &quot;skill mix&quot; means the experience levels and number of nursing and ancillary health personnel; 
 (8) &quot;staffing committee&quot; means the staffing committees for hospitals established in AS 18.20.280; 
 (9) &quot;supportive direct patient care personnel&quot; includes nurse aides, orderlies, assistants, technicians, mental health professionals or associates, or other persons who provide direct care to patients. 
 
 * Sec. 2. The uncodified law of the State of Alaska is amended by adding a new section to read: 
 TRANSITION. A hospital subject to AS 18.20.285, enacted by sec. 1 of this Act, shall implement its initial registered nurse staffing plan on or before January 1, 2027. 
 
 * Sec. 3. This Act takes effect immediately under AS 01.10.070(c).
