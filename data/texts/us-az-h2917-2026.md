 &quot;Section 1. Title 28, chapter 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding article 23, to read: 
 
 
 ARTICLE 23. GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK 
 
 
 28-1241. Definitions 
 
 
 
 IN THIS ARTICLE, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES: 
 
 
 1. &quot;AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER&quot; MEANS A TECHNOLOGY THAT PROVIDES STATIONARY OR MOBILE DETECTION OF LICENSE PLATES AND THAT CAPTURES DATA ASSOCIATED WITH LICENSE PLATES FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT PURPOSES. 
 
 
 2. &quot;GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK&quot; MEANS A SYSTEM OF MONITORING THROUGH TECHNOLOGICAL DEVICES THAT ARE STATIONARY, MOBILE OR SATTELLITE OR THROUGH ANY OTHER TECHNOLOGICAL MEANS THAT ARE OWNED OR OPERATED BY THIS STATE OR A STATE AGENCY, CITY, TOWN, COUNTY OR POLITICAL SUBDIVISION OF THIS STATE, INCLUDING AN AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER. 
 
 
 28-1242. Government mass surveillance network; voter approval requirement; notice of intent and hearing 
 
 
 
 A. ANY LOCAL GOVERNMENT OR STATE AGENCY IN THIS STATE MAY NOT OPERATE OR ESTABLISH A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK WITHOUT NOTIFICATION AND APPROVAL BY A MAJORITY OF THE VOTERS. A VOTE TO APPROVE OR DENY DEPLOYMENT OF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY PERTAIN ONLY TO THE AREA THAT THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT HAS EXCLUSIVE CONTROL AS PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 28-1251. 
 
 
 B. ANY LOCAL GOVERNMENT OR STATE AGENCY THAT PROPOSES TO DEPLOY, CONTRACT FOR OR PROVIDE FUNDING FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK TECHNOLOGY SHALL PUBLISH A PUBLIC NOTICE DECLARING ITS INTENT TO ENGAGE IN GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE OF THE POPULATION AT LEAST SIXTY DAYS BEFORE ANY VOTE REQUIRED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION. THE PUBLIC NOTICE MUST BE POSTED ON THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT&#39;S OR STATE AGENCY&#39;S OFFICIAL WEBSITE, PHYSICALLY POSTED AT THE BUILDING AT WHICH THE PUBLIC HEARING WILL OCCUR AND MAILED OR ELECTRONICALLY DELIVERED TO EACH REGISTERED VOTER WITHIN THE PROPOSED GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SURVEILLANCE COVERAGE AREA. THE NOTICE REQUIRED BY THIS SUBSECTION MUST INCLUDE ALL OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 1. THE TYPE AND NUMBER OF GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORKS PROPOSED TO BE DEPLOYED. 
 
 
 2. THE VENDOR&#39;S NAME AND THE TERMS OF SERVICE FOR THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK. 
 
 
 3. THE PROJECTED ANNUAL COSTS OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK. 
 
 
 4. HOW THE DATA COLLECTED FROM THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK WILL BE HANDLED, RETAINED AND DESTROYED. 
 
 
 5. THE DATE, TIME AND LOCATION OF THE PUBLIC HEARINGS REQUIRED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 C. AT LEAST TWO PUBLIC HEARINGS SHALL BE HELD, SEPARATED BY AT LEAST TWENTY CALENDAR DAYS, WITH THE SECOND HEARING SCHEDULED AT A DIFFERENT TIME OF DAY THAN THE FIRST HEARING. VERBAL AND WRITTEN PUBLIC TESTIMONY SHALL BE RECEIVED AT EACH HEARING REGARDING THE DEPLOYMENT OF ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK. A LIMIT MAY NOT BE PLACED ON THE NUMBER OF PARTICIPANTS WHO OFFER VERBAL OR WRITTEN PUBLIC TESTIMONY AT EACH HEARING. 
 
 
 D. IF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IS PROPOSED BY A CITY OR TOWN, ONLY AFTER COMPLETING THE PUBLIC HEARINGS REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION, THE GOVERNING BODY MAY AUTHORIZE A BALLOT MEASURE AUTHORIZING THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DEPLOYMENT ON SIXTY-FIVE PERCENT AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF THE FULL MEMBERSHIP OF THE GOVERNING BODY. THE VOTE SHALL BE RECORDED BY NAME OF EACH OFFICIAL AND SHALL BE MADE PART OF THE PUBLIC RECORD. AFTER THE GOVERNING BODY AUTHORIZES A BALLOT MEASURE AUTHORIZING THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DEPLOYMENT THE VOTERS MUST APPROVE THE QUESTION AT THE NEXT GENERAL ELECTION WITH AT LEAST A SIXTY PERCENT AFFIRMATIVE VOTE. 
 
 
 E. IF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IS PROPOSED BY A COUNTY, ONLY AFTER COMPLETING THE PUBLIC HEARINGS REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION, THE COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MAY AUTHORIZE A BALLOT MEASURE AUTHORIZING THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DEPLOYMENT ON SIXTY-FIVE PERCENT AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF THE FULL MEMBERSHIP OF THE COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS. THE VOTE SHALL BE RECORDED BY NAME OF EACH OFFICIAL AND SHALL BE MADE PART OF THE PUBLIC RECORD. IF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IS PROPOSED TO BE DEPLOYED BY A COUNTY, THE AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF AT LEAST SIXTY PERCENT OF THE VOTERS AT A REGULAR GENERAL ELECTION SHALL AUTHORIZE THE DEPLOYMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK. THE BALLOT MEASURE REQUIRED BY THIS SUBSECTION MUST CLEARLY DESCRIBE THE SCOPE, TOTAL COST, DATA HANDLING PRACTICES AND DURATION OF THE DEPLOYMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN PLAIN LANGUAGE. 
 
 
 F. IF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IS PROPOSED BY THIS STATE OR A STATE AGENCY, ALL OF THE FOLLOWING MUST OCCUR BEFORE DEPLOYMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK: 
 
 
 1. AT LEAST TWO PUBLIC HEARINGS MUST BE HELD IN EACH COUNTY IN THIS STATE, WITH ONE PUBLIC HEARING HELD AT THE MOST POPULOUS CITY OR TOWN WITHIN THE COUNTY AND ANOTHER PUBLIC HEARING HELD AT THE SECOND MOST POPULOUS CITY OR TOWN WITHIN THE COUNTY. 
 
 
 2. THE PUBLIC HEARINGS REQUIRED BY PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION MUST FOLLOW THE PROCEDURES OF SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 3. UPON COMPLETION OF THE HEARINGS REQUIRED BY PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION THE LEGISLATURE MAY REFER THE QUESTION OF DEPLOYMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK BY THIS STATE OR A STATE AGENCY FOR APPROVAL BY THE VOTERS AT THE NEXT GENERAL ELECTION WITH AT LEAST A SIXTY PERCENT AFFIRMATIVE VOTE. 
 
 
 G. THE AUTHORITY TO DEPLOY AND ADMINISTER GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORKS AFTER THE AFFIRMATIVE VOTE OF THE GOVERNING BODY OR A MAJORITY OF VOTERS REQUIRED BY THIS SECTION SHALL BE VESTED IN THE CHIEF OF POLICE, COUNTY SHERIFF OR DIRECTOR OF THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY OR EQUIVALENT AGENCY HEAD. 
 
 
 H. ANY PUBLIC ENTITY THAT IS NOT EXPLICITLY AUTHORIZED BY THIS SECTION TO AUTHORIZE THE DEPLOYMENT OF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IS NOT ALLOWED TO AUTHORIZE THE DEPLOYMENT OF A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK FOR ANY PURPOSE. 
 
 
 28-1243. Government mass surveillance network; requirements; captured data 
 
 
 
 A. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK AUTHORIZED PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-1242 SHALL BE INSTALLED AND CONFIGURED FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF RECORDING AND SCANNING LICENSE PLATES. THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY NOT BE CAPABLE OF PHOTOGRAPHING, RECORDING, PRODUCING OR ANALYZING IMAGES OF THE OCCUPANTS OR CONTENTS OF ANY VEHICLE. 
 
 
 B. ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DEPLOYED IN THIS STATE MAY NOT ALLOW RETROACTIVE SEARCHES OF ANY ARCHIVED LICENSE PLATE DATA. 
 
 
 C. ANY LICENSE PLATE DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SHALL BE COMPARED TO THE ACTIVE WARRANT AND ALERT LIST WITHIN THREE MINUTES AFTER THE INITIAL CAPTURE OF THE LICENSE PLATE DATA. IF NO MATCH TO THE ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST IS FOUND, ANY LICENSE PLATE DATA CAPTURED BY THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SHALL BE AUTOMATICALLY AND IRREVERSIBLY DESTROYED IN A MANNER THAT RENDERS THE DATA UNRECOVERABLE. 
 
 
 D. LICENSE PLATE DATA MAY NOT BE TRANSMITTED, COPIED, UPLOADED OR TRANSFERRED TO ANY EXTERNAL SERVER, CLOUD SYSTEM, DATA WAREHOUSE OR THIRD-PARTY SYSTEM WITHIN FOUR MINUTES AFTER INITIAL CAPTURE OF THE LICENSE PLATE DATA, EXCEPT FOR THE EXTENT NECESSARY TO PROCESS THE COMPARISON BETWEEN THE CAPTURED LICENSE PLATE DATA AND THE ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST. 
 
 
 E. IF LICENSE PLATE DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK PRODUCES A CONFIRMED MATCH WITH THE ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST, DATA ON THE SPECIFIC LICENSE PLATE SCAN MAY BE RETAINED ONLY UNTIL FINAL COURT DISPOSITION OF THE ASSOCIATED CASE, AFTER WHICH THE LICENSE PLATE DATA SHALL BE DESTROYED WITHIN THIRTY DAYS. LICENSE PLATE DATA RETAINED PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION SHALL BE STORED BY THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY THAT COLLECTED THE LICENSE PLATE DATA AND MAY NOT BE TRANSMITTED TO ANY THIRD PARTY, EXCEPT AS AUTHORIZED BY A COURT ORDER. 
 
 
 F. BEFORE DEPLOYING ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK, THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY SHALL REGISTER THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY ON FORMS APPROVED BY THE DIRECTOR OF THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY. THE HEAD OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY SHALL CERTIFY IN WRITING THAT: 
 
 
 1. THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK TO BE DEPLOYED MEETS ALL REQUIREMENTS OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 2. THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY HAS POLICIES THAT CONFORM WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 3. EACH LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO WILL OPERATE THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK HAS SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETED THE TRAINING REQUIRED BY SECTION 28-1248. 
 
 
 28-1244. Government mass surveillance network; prohibited uses 
 
 
 
 A. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK THAT IS DEPLOYED IN THIS STATE MAY NOT INCORPORATE, ENABLE OR BE CONNECTED TO FACIAL RECOGNITION TECHNOLOGY, VEHICLE OCCUPANT COUNTING, BUMPER STICKER OR DECAL READING, VEHICLE COLOR DETECTION AND MAKE AND MODEL PROFILING BEYOND WHAT IS NECESSARY FOR LICENSE PLATE IDENTIFICATION OR ANY OTHER SURVEILLANCE BEYOND LICENSE PLATE READING. 
 
 
 B. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY NOT CAPTURE OR STORE GLOBAL POSITIONING SYSTEM COORDINATES, SPEED DATA OR DIRECTIONAL TRAFFIC INFORMATION OF ANY VEHICLE BEYOND THE FIXED LOCATION OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK. 
 
 
 C. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY NOT BE DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY USED FOR THE DETECTION, ENFORCEMENT OR PROSECUTION OF TRAFFIC VIOLATIONS, INCLUDING SPEEDING, RED LIGHT VIOLATIONS, EXPIRED REGISTRATION, LAPSED INSURANCE OR PARKING OFFENSES. GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA, INCLUDING ANY POSITIVE MATCH WITH ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LISTS OR ANY SPECIFIED AUTHORIZED PURPOSES PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-1247, MAY NOT BE USED AS A BASIS FOR ISSUING ANY TRAFFIC VIOLATION OR RELATED CIVIL PENALTY. 
 
 
 D. ANY PERSON, AGENCY OR ENTITY MAY NOT USE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA, INCLUDING ANY POSITIVE MATCH WITH ACTIVE WARRANTS OR ALERT LISTS, SCAN LOGS, TIME STAMPS OR LOCATION DATA OR ANY COMBINATION THEREOF TO CONSTRUCT, INFER, ANALYZE OR COMPILE A PATTERN OF LIFE, BEHAVIORAL PROFILE, TRAVEL HISTORY, ROUTINE ANALYSIS OR MOVEMENT PATTERN OF ANY PERSON. THIS PROHIBITION INCLUDES BOTH MANUAL AND ANY AUTOMATED OR ALGORITHMIC PROCESSING, INCLUDING MACHINE LEARNING, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PATTERN RECOGNITION OR ANY RELATED TECHNOLOGICAL CAPABILITIES. 
 
 
 E. ANY DECLARATION OF EMERGENCY FROM ANY OFFICIAL, INCLUDING THE GOVERNOR OF THIS STATE, A LOCAL GOVERNING BODY, THE HEAD OF A LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY OR ANY OTHER OFFICIAL, MAY NOT SUSPEND, MODIFY, WAIVE OR OVERRIDE ANY PROVISION OF THIS ARTICLE. ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA COLLECTED DURING ANY DECLARED EMERGENCY IS SUBJECT TO ALL THE REQUIREMENTS AND LIMITATIONS OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 F. ANY DATA OR METADATA OBTAINED FROM GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORKS, INCLUDING ANY DE-IDENTIFIED, ANONYMIZED, AGGREGATED OR REDACTED DATA, MAY NOT BE USED FOR MASS SURVEILLANCE, CONTINUOUS MONITORING, POPULATION-LEVEL MOVEMENT ANALYSIS, TRAFFIC FLOW STUDIES OR OTHER PURPOSE THAT INVOLVES TRACKING THE MOVEMENTS OF PERSONS WHO ARE NOT THE SPECIFIC SUBJECT OF AN ACTIVE AUTHORIZED INVESTIGATION. 
 
 
 G. GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA MAY NOT BE COMBINED WITH DATA FROM ANY OTHER SURVEILLANCE TECHNOLOGY, DATABASE OR DATA SOURCE TO ENABLE BROADER TRACKING OR IDENTIFICATION CAPABILITIES. 
 
 
 H. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY NOT BE DEPLOYED, POSITIONED OR USED IN THIS STATE FOR THE PURPOSE OF MONITORING, SURVEILLING OR IDENTIFYING VEHICLES OR PERSONS ENGAGED IN OR TRAVELLING TO OR FROM CONSTITUTIONALLY PROTECTED ACTIVITIES, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 1. POLITICAL PROTESTS, MARCHES, DEMONSTRATIONS OR RALLIES. 
 
 
 2. ATTENDANCE AT RELIGIOUS SERVICES OR INSTITUTIONS. 
 
 
 3. VISITS TO LEGAL COUNSEL. 
 
 
 4. VISITS TO NEWS MEDIA ORGANIZATIONS. 
 
 
 5. VISITS TO ANY LOCATION WHERE THE PRIMARY PURPOSE OF THE VISIT IS THE EXERCISE OF RIGHTS PROTECTED BY THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES, THE BILL OF RIGHTS OR THE ARIZONA CONSTITUTION. 
 
 
 I. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK MAY NOT BE DEPLOYED WITHIN FIVE HUNDRED FEET OF ANY: 
 
 
 1. POLLING PLACE DURING ANY ELECTION PERIOD. 
 
 
 2. LOCATION IN WHICH AN AUTHORIZED PROTEST, MARCH OR DEMONSTRATION IS OCCURRING. 
 
 
 3. PHYSICAL ADDRESS OF ANY NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION THAT IS EXEMPT FROM TAXATION UNDER SECTION 501(c)(3) OR 501(c)(4) AND THAT IS REGISTERED WITH THIS STATE, EXCLUDING ANY POST OFFICE ADDRESS. 
 
 
 4. A MEDICAL FACILITY. 
 
 
 28-1245. Vendor requirements; intellectual property restrictions; data destruction; audit 
 
 
 
 A. NO VENDOR THAT PROVIDES GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK HARDWARE, SOFTWARE, DATA PROCESSING OR RELATED SERVICES SHALL ACQUIRE, CLAIM, ASSERT OR RETAIN ANY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHT, TRADE SECRET CLAIM, COPYRIGHT, PROPRIETARY INTEREST, LICENSE, TITLE OR OTHER INTEREST IN OR TO ANY LICENSE PLATE DATA, IMAGES, METADATA, SCAN RECORDS, HIT RECORDS, ALERT DATA OR OTHER DATA CAPTURED BY OR DERIVED FROM ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK OPERATED UNDER A VALID CONTRACT WITH A GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY IN THIS STATE. 
 
 
 B. ANY PROVISION IN A VENDOR&#39;S TERMS OF SERVICE, END USER LICENSE AGREEMENT, SERVICES AGREEMENT, ORDER FORM OR ANY OTHER CONTRACT OR INSTRUMENT THAT PURPORTS TO GRANT THE VENDOR ANY OF THE FOLLOWING IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE AS AGAINST THE PUBLIC POLICY OF THIS STATE: 
 
 
 1. A PERPETUAL, IRREVOCABLE, ROYALTY-FREE, WORLDWIDE OR OTHERWISE SURVIVING LICENSE TO USE, REPRODUCE, MODIFY, DISTRIBUTE OR OTHERWISE EXPLOIT DATA CAPTURED BY OR DERIVED FROM A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE FOR ANY PURPOSE, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO THE DEVELOPMENT, TRAINING, TESTING, IMPROVEMENT OR ENHANCEMENT OF ANY PRODUCT, ALGORITHM, SERVICE, MODEL OR SYSTEM. 
 
 
 2. OWNERSHIP OF OR ANY INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INTEREST IN DERIVATIVE WORKS, INTERMEDIATE OR FINAL OUTPUTS, ANALYSES, REPORTS, MODELS, ALGORITHMS, MACHINE LEARNING WEIGHTS, FEATURE VECTORS OR OTHER RESULTS GENERATED FROM OR THROUGH THE PROCESSING OF DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE. 
 
 
 3. THE RIGHT TO RETAIN, COPY, AGGREGATE, ANALYZE, SELL, LICENSE OR OTHERWISE USE ANY DATA CAPTURED BY OR DERIVED FROM A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE AFTER THE TERMINATION OR EXPIRATION OF THE CONTRACT UNDER WHICH THE DATA WAS COLLECTED, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE CONTRACT CONTAINS PROVISIONS THAT PURPORT TO SURVIVE TERMINATION. 
 
 
 4. THE RIGHT TO REQUIRE ANY GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY TO WAIVE ANY PROVISION OF THIS ARTICLE AS A CONDITION OF SERVICE. 
 
 
 C. A VENDOR SHALL NOT RETAIN ANY COPY, BACKUP, DERIVATIVE OR REPRODUCTION OF DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE BEYOND THE RETENTION PERIODS AUTHORIZED BY SECTION 28-1243. FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBSECTION, DATA THAT MUST BE DESTROYED INCLUDES ALL OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 1. THE ORIGINAL DATA AND ALL COPIES, BACKUPS AND REPRODUCTIONS IN ANY FORMAT ON ANY SERVER, STORAGE MEDIUM OR SYSTEM ANYWHERE IN THE WORLD. 
 
 
 2. ALL DE-IDENTIFIED, ANONYMIZED, REDACTED OR AGGREGATED VERSIONS OF THE DATA. 
 
 
 3. ALL MACHINE LEARNING TRAINING SETS, MODEL WEIGHTS, FEATURE VECTORS AND NEURAL NETWORK PARAMETERS GENERATED IN WHOLE OR IN PART FROM THE DATA. 
 
 
 4. ALL DERIVATIVE WORKS, ANALYTICAL PRODUCTS, REPORTS, PROFILES AND OUTPUTS GENERATED FROM THE DATA. 
 
 
 5. ALL INDEXES, HASH TABLES, LOOKUP TABLES AND DATABASE ENTRIES REFERENCING THE DATA. 
 
 
 A VENDOR SHALL NOT CIRCUMVENT THE DATA DESTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS OF THIS ARTICLE BY CLAIMING THAT DATA HAS BEEN DE-IDENTIFIED, ANONYMIZED, REDACTED, AGGREGATED OR OTHERWISE TRANSFORMED IF THE DATA WAS ORIGINALLY CAPTURED BY OR DERIVED FROM A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE. 
 
 
 D. WITHIN THIRTY DAYS OF THE TERMINATION OR EXPIRATION OF ANY CONTRACT FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SERVICES, THE VENDOR SHALL PERMANENTLY DESTROY ALL DATA DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION AND SHALL CERTIFY SUCH DESTRUCTION IN WRITING UNDER PENALTY OF PERJURY. THE CERTIFICATION SHALL BE SIGNED BY AN OFFICER OF THE VENDOR WITH AUTHORITY TO BIND THE COMPANY AND SHALL SPECIFICALLY ATTEST THAT NO COPY, DERIVATIVE, TRAINING SET, MODEL WEIGHT OR OTHER DATA PRODUCT GENERATED FROM DATA CAPTURED IN THIS STATE HAS BEEN RETAINED IN ANY FORM ON ANY SYSTEM. 
 
 
 E. A VENDOR SHALL NOT MODIFY, AMEND OR UPDATE THE TERMS OF SERVICE, END USER LICENSE AGREEMENT, SERVICES AGREEMENT OR ANY OTHER CONTRACT GOVERNING DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE WITHOUT THE PRIOR WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE CONTRACTING GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY. ANY PROVISION THAT PERMITS A VENDOR TO UNILATERALLY MODIFY CONTRACT TERMS BY POSTING UPDATED TERMS ON A WEBSITE, SENDING A NOTIFICATION OR THROUGH ANY OTHER MECHANISM THAT DOES NOT REQUIRE THE AFFIRMATIVE WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE CONTRACTING GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE WITH RESPECT TO DATA CAPTURED IN THIS STATE. 
 
 
 F. ANY CONTRACT FOR THE PROVISION OF GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SERVICES TO A GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY IN THIS STATE SHALL BE GOVERNED BY THE LAWS OF THIS STATE. ANY PROVISION IN A VENDOR&#39;S CONTRACT THAT REQUIRES MANDATORY ARBITRATION, SELECTS THE LAWS OF ANOTHER STATE TO GOVERN DISPUTES OR DESIGNATES A FORUM OUTSIDE OF THIS STATE FOR THE RESOLUTION OF DISPUTES ARISING FROM THE COLLECTION, RETENTION, USE, SHARING OR DESTRUCTION OF DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE. 
 
 
 G. A VENDOR SHALL NOT AGGREGATE DATA CAPTURED BY A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK IN THIS STATE WITH DATA COLLECTED IN ANY OTHER STATE, JURISDICTION OR SOURCE. A VENDOR SHALL NOT MAKE DATA CAPTURED IN THIS STATE ACCESSIBLE THROUGH ANY NATIONAL, REGIONAL OR MULTI-JURISDICTIONAL DATABASE, LOOKUP SYSTEM, DATA-SHARING NETWORK OR PLATFORM, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE VENDOR CHARACTERIZES SUCH ACCESS AS A FEATURE OF ITS SERVICE. 
 
 
 H. EACH VENDOR ASSOCIATED WITH A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SHALL SUBMIT TO AN ANNUAL COMPLIANCE AUDIT BY THE CONTRACTING GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY OR AN INDEPENDENT AUDITOR SELECTED BY THE GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY AT THE VENDOR&#39;S EXPENSE. THE AUDIT SHALL INCLUDE EXAMINATION OF THE VENDOR&#39;S SERVERS, DATA STORAGE SYSTEMS, BACKUP SYSTEMS, MACHINE LEARNING TRAINING PIPELINES AND ALL SYSTEMS ON WHICH DATA CAPTURED IN THIS STATE HAS BEEN PROCESSED, STORED OR TRANSMITTED. 
 
 
 I. EACH VENDOR ASSOCIATED WITH A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SHALL PROVIDE COMPLETE TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION OF ALL DATA ARCHITECTURE, DATA FLOWS, THIRD-PARTY INTEGRATIONS AND MACHINE LEARNING TRAINING PROCESSES, WHICH SHALL DEMONSTRATE THE SYSTEM IS CAPABLE OF AND CONFIGURED FOR COMPLIANCE WITH THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 J. A VENDOR THAT VIOLATES ANY PROVISION OF THIS SECTION IS SUBJECT TO THE CRIMINAL PENALTIES AND PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 28-1250 AND SHALL BE PERMANENTLY BARRED FROM CONTRACTING WITH ANY GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY IN THIS STATE FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SERVICES. 
 
 
 K. THE PROVISIONS OF THIS SECTION ARE NOT WAIVABLE BY CONTRACT. ANY CONTRACTUAL PROVISION THAT PURPORTS TO WAIVE, LIMIT, DISCLAIM OR OTHERWISE CIRCUMVENT THE PROTECTIONS OF THIS SECTION IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE AS AGAINST THE PUBLIC POLICY OF THIS STATE. A VENDOR SHALL NOT CONDITION THE PROVISION OF GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SERVICES ON THE ACCEPTANCE OF TERMS THAT CONFLICT WITH THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 28-1246. Third party data access; restrictions 
 
 
 
 A. GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA, INCLUDING POSITIVE OR NEGATIVE MATCHES WITH ANY ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST, METADATA, SCAN LOGS, IMAGES OR DERIVATIVE INFORMATION, MAY NOT BE SHARED WITH, SOLD TO, TRANSFERRED TO, ACCESSED BY OR MADE AVAILABLE TO ANY THIRD PARTY, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 1. A FEDERAL AGENCY. 
 
 
 2. ANOTHER AGENCY OF THIS STATE, INCLUDING ANY TASK FORCE OR FOR ANY INTERGOVERNMENTAL COMPACT OR MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING. 
 
 
 3. AN OUT-OF-STATE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY OR DEPARTMENT. 
 
 
 4. A PRIVATE ENTITY. 
 
 
 5. A DATA BROKER. 
 
 
 6. A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK VENDOR&#39;S NETWORK, SHARED DATABASE OR NATIONAL LOOKUP SYSTEM. 
 
 
 B. ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA OBTAINED, RETAINED, ACCESSED, SHARED OR USED IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE IS INADMISSIBLE IN ANY CRIMINAL, CIVIL OR ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEEDING IN THIS STATE. VIOLATIONS OF THIS ARTICLE RENDER DATA INADMISSIBLE REGARDLESS OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER&#39;S SUBJECTIVE BELIEF REGARDING COMPLIANCE. FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBSECTION, EVIDENCE IS DERIVED FROM GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA OBTAINED IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE IF THE INVESTIGATION THAT PRODUCED THE EVIDENCE WAS INITIATED, DIRECTED OR MATERIALLY ADVANCED BY INFORMATION OBTAINED FROM SUCH DATA, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA ITSELF IS INTRODUCED AS EVIDENCE OR WHETHER AN INDEPENDENT SOURCE FOR THE EVIDENCE IS SUBSEQUENTLY IDENTIFIED. NO LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY OR OFFICER MAY USE DATA OBTAINED IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE TO DEVELOP A PARALLEL LINE OF INVESTIGATION OR TO IDENTIFY WITNESSES, LEADS, LOCATIONS OR OTHER EVIDENCE THAT IS THEN PRESENTED AS INDEPENDENTLY OBTAINED. 
 
 
 28-1247. Government mass surveillance network; warrant requirements; specific authorized purposes 
 
 
 
 A. A LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY MAY NOT QUERY, ACCESS OR REQUEST GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA WITHOUT A WARRANT THAT IS SUPPORTED BY PROBABLE CAUSE AND THAT IS ISSUED BY A JUDGE OF A COURT OF COMPETENT JURISDICTION THAT SPECIALLY AUTHORIZES THE QUERY, ACCESS OR REQUEST. BLANKET WARRANTS, INCLUDING MONITORING OF ALL LICENSE PLATES AT A LOCATION TO DETERMINE WHICH PERSONS WERE PRESENT OR MONITORING OF A LICENSE PLATE ACROSS MULTIPLE JURISDICTIONS WITHOUT SEPARATE AUTHORIZATION FOR EACH JURISDICTION, GENERAL WARRANTS, WARRANTS AUTHORIZING QUERIES ACROSS ALL JURISDICTIONS OR ALL PLATES OR WARRANTS AUTHORIZING MONITORING OF LICENSE PLATES IN ASSOCIATION WITH ANY CONSTITUTIONALLY PROTECTED ACTIVITY, ARE PROHIBITED. A WARRANT REQUIRED BY THIS SUBSECTION FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA MUST SPECIFY ALL OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 1. THE LICENSE PLATE NUMBER TO BE QUERIED. 
 
 
 2. THE SPECIFIC AUTHORIZED PURPOSE PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION B OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 3. THE SPECIFIC LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY OR AGENCIES TO BE QUERIED. 
 
 
 4. THE TIME PERIOD FOR WHICH ANY RETAINED MATCHES WITH ACTIVE WARRANTS OR ALERT LISTS ARE SOUGHT. 
 
 
 B. GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA MAY BE USED EXCLUSIVELY FOR THE FOLLOWING SPECIFIC AUTHORIZED PURPOSES: 
 
 
 1. IDENTIFICATION OF STOLEN VEHICLES. 
 
 
 2. IDENTIFICATION OF VEHICLES ASSOCIATED WITH PERSONS WHO ARE WANTED, MISSING OR IN DANGER, INCLUDING AMBER ALERTS, BLUE ALERTS PURSUANT TO SECTION 41-1726, SEEK AND FIND ALERTS PURSUANT TO SECTION 41-1728 AND TURQUOISE ALERTS PURSUANT TO SECTION 41-1728.01. 
 
 
 3. IDENTIFICATION OF VEHICLES REGISTERED TO A PERSON AGAINST WHOM THERE IS AN OUTSTANDING ARREST WARRANT. 
 
 
 4. IDENTIFICATION OF VEHICLES INVOLVED IN OR CONNECTED TO A CLASS 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 OR 6 FELONY. 
 
 
 5. IDENTIFICATION OF VEHICLES SUBJECT TO AN ACTIVE LAW ENFORCEMENT ALERT OR BULLETIN RELATED TO ANY HOMICIDE, KIDNAPPING, SEXUAL ASSAULT OR ACT OF TERRORISM. 
 
 
 28-1248. Law enforcement officer training program; requirements 
 
 
 
 A. ONLY A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO HAS COMPLETED A DOCUMENTED TRAINING PROGRAM CONDUCTED BY AN AUTHORIZED NONPROFIT FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK OPERATION, LEGAL REQUIREMENTS AND PRIVACY PROTECTIONS AND THE PROVISIONS OF THIS ARTICLE SHALL BE AUTHORIZED TO OPERATE AND HANDLE A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK AND ASSOCIATED DATA. 
 
 
 B. ONLY A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER MAY REQUEST OR OBTAIN A WARRANT FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA PURSUANT TO THIS ARTICLE. THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER SHALL ATTEST UNDER OATH THAT THE REQUEST MEETS ONE OF THE SPECIFIC AUTHORIZED PURPOSES PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-1247. 
 
 
 C. ANY ACCESS TO GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA, INCLUDING REAL-TIME ALERTS AND STORED POSITIVE MATCH RECORDS, SHALL BE LOGGED WITH THE IDENTITY OF THE ACCESSING LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER, THE DATE AND TIME OF ACCESS, THE LICENSE PLATE NUMBER QUERIED AND THE PURPOSE OF THE ACCESS. AUDIT LOGS OF ACCESS SHALL BE RETAINED FOR FIVE YEARS AND SHALL BE MADE AVAILABLE TO THE COURT ON REQUEST. 
 
 
 D. FOR THE PURPOSE OF THIS SECTION, &quot;AUTHORIZED NONPROFIT&quot; MEANS A NATIONAL NONPROFIT PUBLIC INTEREST LAW FIRM FOUNDED AFTER 1990 WITH A MISSION TO END WIDESPREAD ABUSES OF GOVERNMENT POWER AND SECURE THE CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS OF THE AMERICAN PEOPLE. 
 
 
 28-1249. Visual verification of license plates; traffic stops 
 
 
 
 A. BEFORE A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER MAY INITIATE A TRAFFIC STOP, VEHICLE STOP OR APPREHENSION BASED ON ANY INFORMATION OBTAINED FROM A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK, THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER SHALL VISUALLY VERIFY THE LICENSE PLATE NUMBER OF THE VEHICLE AND CONFIRM THAT THE LICENSE PLATE MATCHES THE LICENSE PLATE IDENTIFIED IN THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK ALERT. 
 
 
 B. THE VISUAL VERIFICATION OF THE LICENSE PLATE MATCH SHALL BE COMMUNICATED TO DISPATCH BY RADIO AND LOGGED BEFORE THE STOP IS INITIATED. 
 
 
 C. A STOP, SEIZURE OR DETENTION OF ANY PERSON OR VEHICLE MAY NOT BE BASED SOLELY ON AN UNVERIFIED GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK ALERT. 
 
 
 28-1250. Personal data access; data deletion; private right of action 
 
 
 
 A. A PERSON MAY SUBMIT A PUBLIC RECORDS REQUEST TO ANY LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY THAT OPERATES A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK FOR ANY DATA ASSOCIATED WITH THE PERSON&#39;S LAWFULLY REGISTERED VEHICLE. 
 
 
 B. THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY SHALL RESPOND TO A REQUEST MADE PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION WITHIN TEN BUSINESS DAYS. 
 
 
 C. IF RECORDS ARE FOUND IN THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA, THE RESPONSE FROM THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY MUST INCLUDE THE DATE, TIME AND GENERAL LOCATION OF EACH SCAN OF THE PERSON&#39;S LICENSE PLATE, WHETHER THE SCAN RESULTED IN A POSITIVE MATCH WITH ANY ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST AND THE CASE NUMBER ASSOCIATED WITH ANY RETAINED LICENSE PLATE DATA. 
 
 
 D. A PERSON MAY REQUEST THE DELETION OF ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA ASSOCIATED WITH THE PERSON&#39;S LAWFULLY REGISTERED VEHICLE THAT IS NOT ASSOCIATED WITH AN ACTIVE CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION OR AN ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST MATCH. THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY SHALL COMPLY WITH THE DELETION REQUEST WITHIN TEN BUSINESS DAYS AND SHALL CONFIRM THE DELETION IN WRITING. AN AGENCY MAY WITHHOLD DATA PURSUANT TO AN ACTIVE INVESTIGATION FOR NOT MORE THAN ONE YEAR FROM THE DATE OF THE REQUEST. IF THE INVESTIGATION REMAINS ACTIVE AFTER ONE YEAR, THE AGENCY SHALL PROVIDE THE REQUESTING PERSON WITH ALL DATA RESPONSIVE TO THE REQUEST, REDACTED AS NECESSARY TO PROTECT THE INTEGRITY OF THE INVESTIGATION, WITHIN THIRTY DAYS OF THE EXPIRATION OF THE ONE-YEAR PERIOD. 
 
 
 E. ANY PERSON WHOSE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA IS COLLECTED, RETAINED, SHARED, ACCESSED OR USED IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE SHALL HAVE A PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION IN THE SUPERIOR COURT. A PRIVATE RIGHT OF ACTION IS NOT SUBJECT TO ANY REQUIREMENT OF EXHAUSTION OF ADMINISTRATIVE REMEDIES. GOVERNMENTAL ENTITIES AND VENDORS MAY NOT ASSERT QUALIFIED IMMUNITY, SOVEREIGN IMMUNITY OR ANY CONTRACTUAL LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AS A DEFENSE TO CLAIMS BROUGHT PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION. A PREVAILING PLAINTIFF SHALL BE ENTITLED TO: 
 
 
 1. STATUTORY DAMAGES OF AT LEAST $2,500 PER NEGLIGENT VIOLATION AND $10,000 PER WILFUL OR RECKLESS VIOLATION. 
 
 
 2. ACTUAL DAMAGES. 
 
 
 3. REASONABLE ATTORNEY FEES AND COSTS. 
 
 
 4. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF. 
 
 
 28-1251. Jurisdictional circumvention; prohibition 
 
 
 
 A. IF THE QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF A CITY, TOWN, COUNTY OR STATE AGENCY HAS REJECTED A PROPOSAL TO AUTHORIZE A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-1242, NO OTHER GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY MAY DEPLOY, OPERATE OR CONTRACT FOR ANY GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK WITHIN THE BOUNDARIES OF THE CITY, TOWN, COUNTY OR OTHER JURISDICTION. THIS PROHIBITION INCLUDES: 
 
 
 1. A COUNTY AGENCY OPERATING WITHIN A CITY&#39;S OR TOWN&#39;S LIMITS. 
 
 
 2. A STATE AGENCY OPERATING WITHIN A CITY&#39;S, TOWN&#39;S OR COUNTY&#39;S LIMITS. 
 
 
 3. A FEDERAL AGENCY OPERATING WITH THE ASSISTANCE, COOPERATION OR DATA-SHARING OF STATE AGENCIES OR LOCAL GOVERNMENTS. 
 
 
 4. TASK FORCES, JOINT OPERATIONS OR MULTI-JURISDICTIONAL PARTNERSHIPS. 
 
 
 B. A LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY IN THIS STATE MAY NOT PROVIDE OR ACCEPT GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA TO OR FROM ANY FEDERAL AGENCY FOR THE PURPOSE OF CIRCUMVENTING THE PROVISIONS OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 C. A LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY IN THIS STATE MAY NOT PARTICIPATE IN ANY FEDERAL GRANT PROGRAM, TASK FORCE OR COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT THAT REQUIRES OR INCENTIVIZES THE COLLECTION, RETENTION OR SHARING OF GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE. 
 
 
 28-1252. Annual report 
 
 
 
 ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 31 OF EACH YEAR, EACH LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY THAT OPERATES A GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK SHALL PUBLISH AN ANNUAL TRANSPARENCY REPORT. THE ANNUAL TRANSPARENCY REPORT SHALL BE POSTED ON THE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY&#39;S PUBLIC WEBSITE AND SHALL BE SUBMITTED TO THE GOVERNOR, THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE AND THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SHALL PROVIDE A COPY OF THIS REPORT TO THE SECRETARY OF STATE. THE ANNUAL TRANSPARENCY REPORT MUST CONTAIN: 
 
 
 1. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF LICENSE PLATE SCANS CONDUCTED IN THE PREVIOUS YEAR. 
 
 
 2. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF POSITIVE MATCHES WITH AN ACTIVE WARRANT OR ALERT LIST. 
 
 
 3. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF POSITIVE MATCHES THAT RESULTED IN AN ARREST, A CITATION OR PROTECTIVE CUSTODY. 
 
 
 4. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF FALSE POSITIVE MATCHES OR ERRONEOUS ALERTS. 
 
 
 5. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF WARRANTS OBTAINED FROM GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA. 
 
 
 6. THE TOTAL NUMBER AND THE IDENTITY OF EACH LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO IS CERTIFIED TO OPERATE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORKS. 
 
 
 7. THE TOTAL COST OF THE GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK, INCLUDING ALL VENDOR FEES. 
 
 
 8. CONFIRMATION THAT THE ANNUAL COMPLIANCE AUDIT REPORT FOR VENDORS REQUIRED PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-1245 WAS COMPLETED AND A SUMMARY OF FINDINGS OF THE REPORT. 
 
 
 9. THE NUMBER OF PUBLIC RECORDS REQUESTS RECEIVED FOR GOVERNMENT MASS SURVEILLANCE NETWORK DATA AND A DESCRIPTION OF EACH REQUEST.&quot;
