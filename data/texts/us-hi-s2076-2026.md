 THIRTY-THIRD LEGISLATURE, 2026 
 S.D. 1 
 STATE OF HAWAII 
 
 
 
 A BILL FOR AN ACT 
 
 RELATING TO PUBLICITY RIGHTS. 
 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF HAWAII: 
 
 
 SECTION 1. Section 482P-1, Hawaii Revised Statutes, is amended as follows: 
 1. By adding three new definitions to be appropriately inserted and to read: 
 
 &quot;Digital Replica&quot; means a computer-generated, highly realistic electronic representation that is readily identifiable as the voice or visual likeness of an individual that is embodied in a sound recording, image, audiovisual work, or transmission in which the actual individual either did not actually perform or appear, or the actual individual did perform or appear, but the fundamental character of the performance or appearance has been materially altered. 
 
 
 &quot;Digital replica&quot; does not include the electronic reproduction, use of a sample of one sound recording or audiovisual work into another, remixing, mastering, or digital remastering of a sound recording or audiovisual work authorized by the copyright holder. 
 
 
 &quot;Voice&quot; means a sound in a medium that is readily identifiable as and attributable to a particular individual, regardless of whether the sound contains the voice or a simulation or digital replica of the voice of the individual. &quot; 
 2. By amending the definition of &quot;likeness&quot; to read: 
 &quot;&quot;Likeness&quot; means an image, photograph, painting, sketching, model, diagram, digital replica, or other recognizable representation of an individual&#39;s face or body, and includes, in the case of a personality, a characteristic.&quot; 
 SECTION 2. This Act does not affect rights and duties that matured, penalties that were incurred, and proceedings that were begun before its effective date. 
 SECTION 3. New statutory material is underscored. 
 
 SECTION 4. This Act shall take effect on January 1, 2077. 
 Digital Replica; Publicity Rights; Likeness 
 
 Description: 
 
 Protects a person&#39;s right to publicity from digital replicas by including &quot;digital replica&quot; in the definition of &quot;likeness&quot; that is protected under state law that governs publicity rights. Effective 1/1/2077. (SD1)
