 AN ACT to amend Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 1, relative to certain conditions of personhood. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF TENNESSEE: 
 
 SECTION 1. Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 1-3-105(a), is amended by deleting subdivision (19) and substituting instead: 
 (19) &quot;Person&quot;: 
 (A) Includes a corporation, firm, company, or association; and 
 (B) Does not include artificial intelligence, a computer algorithm, a software program, computer hardware, or any type of machine; 
 SECTION 2. Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 1-3-105(a), is amended by adding the following new, appropriately designated subdivisions: ( ) &quot;Human being&quot; means an individual living member of the species, homo sapiens, including the unborn human being during the entire embryonic and fetal stages from fertilization to full gestation; ( ) &quot;Life&quot;: 
 (A) Means the condition that distinguishes animals from inorganic matter, including the capacity for growth, reproduction, functional activity, and continual change preceding death; and 
 (B) Does not include artificial intelligence, a computer algorithm, a software program, computer hardware, or any type of machine; ( ) &quot;Natural person&quot;: 
 (A) Means an individual living member of the species, homo sapiens; and 
 (B) Does not include artificial intelligence, a computer algorithm, a software program, computer hardware, or any type of machine; 
 SECTION 3. This act takes effect upon becoming a law, the public welfare requiring it.
