# Loi n° 2013-450 du 19 juin 2013 relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel — Côte d'Ivoire
**Loi n° 2013-450**
*Côte d'Ivoire — June 19, 2013*

---

## Overview

Côte d'Ivoire's Law 2013-450 of June 19, 2013, on the protection of personal data is the country's comprehensive data protection framework. The law designates the Autorité de Régulation des Télécommunications / TIC de Côte d'Ivoire (ARTCI) — the telecommunications and ICT regulatory authority — as the supervisory body for personal data protection in addition to its existing telecom regulatory mandate. The law applies to all processing of personal data carried out in Ivory Coast, covering both automated and manual processing. It follows the French civil law tradition and draws on the ECOWAS data protection framework, reflecting regional harmonization efforts in West Africa.

---

## Key Provisions

### ARTCI as Supervisory Authority
- ARTCI exercises data protection supervisory functions alongside its telecommunications regulatory role
- Receives declarations and prior authorization requests for data processing activities
- Investigates complaints from data subjects and ex officio investigations
- Issues opinions and recommendations on data protection matters
- May impose administrative sanctions and refer matters for criminal prosecution

### Core Data Protection Principles
- Proportionality: data must be adequate, relevant, and not excessive
- Accuracy and currency: data must be accurate and kept up to date
- Purpose limitation: processing limited to specified, legitimate purposes
- Lawfulness: processing must have a legal basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest)
- Security: appropriate technical and organizational measures required
- Accountability: data controllers responsible for compliance

### Lawful Processing Bases
- Unambiguous consent of the data subject
- Performance of a contract to which the data subject is party
- Compliance with a legal obligation applicable to the controller
- Protection of vital interests of the data subject
- Legitimate interests of the controller, where not overridden by the data subject's interests

### Special Category Data
- Categories requiring explicit consent or specific legal authority:
  - Health and medical data
  - Biometric data
  - Political opinions
  - Religious or philosophical beliefs
  - Racial or ethnic origin
  - Criminal record and judicial measures

### Data Subject Rights
- Right to information at the time of collection
- Right of access to personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Right to opposition to processing for legitimate and compelling reasons, including for direct marketing purposes

### Mandatory Declaration
- Data controllers must declare processing activities to ARTCI before commencing
- Sensitive data processing and certain high-risk activities require prior ARTCI authorization
- Failure to declare is a criminal offense

### Cross-Border Data Transfers
- Transfers outside Côte d'Ivoire prohibited unless recipient country provides adequate protection or ARTCI approves transfer through appropriate safeguards

### Security Requirements
- Controllers must implement technical and organizational measures to ensure data security
- Obligation of confidentiality for persons processing personal data

## Enforcement & Penalties

- ARTCI may issue formal warnings, compliance orders, and temporary suspension of processing
- Criminal penalties: fines up to XOF 10,000,000 (approximately $17,000 USD) and/or up to 5 years imprisonment for serious violations including unlawful processing and failure to declare
- Aggravated penalties may apply for processing involving sensitive categories
- Civil liability: data subjects may seek damages through Ivorian courts
