# Turkey — Kanun Teklifi Esas No. 2/3358 — Bill on Amendments to Certain Laws Regarding Artificial Intelligence

**Kanun Teklifi Esas No. 2/3358**
*Turkey — Submitted to Grand National Assembly 7 November 2025; pending as of May 2026*

---

## Overview

The Bill on Amendments to Certain Laws Regarding Artificial Intelligence (Yapay Zeka Alanında Bazı Kanunlarda Değişiklik Yapılması Hakkında Kanun Teklifi, Esas No. 2/3358) was submitted to the Turkish Grand National Assembly (Türkiye Büyük Millet Meclisi) on 7 November 2025. The bill is an omnibus instrument amending multiple existing Turkish laws to address AI-related challenges, particularly around AI-generated content, deepfakes, and digital platform liability.

The bill does not establish a comprehensive AI regulatory framework modeled on the EU AI Act; instead, it targets specific harms identified by Turkish regulators — particularly deepfake content, AI-generated disinformation, and unauthorized use of personal likenesses — through amendments to existing legislation governing internet content, telecommunications, criminal law, and data protection.

As of May 2026, the bill was in committee review in the Turkish Grand National Assembly. Passage timeline remains uncertain.

## Key Provisions

### Statutory Definition of AI Systems

The bill introduces a statutory definition of "artificial intelligence system" into Turkish law, amending the Law on the Organization and Broadcasting Services of Radio and Television (Law No. 6112) and the Law on the Regulation of Electronic Commerce (Law No. 6563). The definition tracks the OECD AI definition (systems that process inputs to generate outputs such as predictions, recommendations, or decisions) and provides a common baseline for AI-specific obligations across multiple amended laws.

### Deepfake Labelling Requirements

The bill amends the Internet Law (İnternet Ortamında Yapılan Yayınların Düzenlenmesi ve Bu Yayınlar Yoluyla İşlenen Suçlarla Mücadele Edilmesi Hakkında Kanun — Law No. 5651) to introduce mandatory disclosure obligations for AI-generated content distributed online:

- Content generated by AI systems that depicts real persons (deepfake video, audio, or images) must be labelled as "yapay zeka ile üretilmiştir" (produced with artificial intelligence) or equivalent disclosure
- The labelling requirement applies to content distributors and platform operators, not only original creators
- Online platforms hosting unlabelled AI-generated content of real persons may face administrative liability
- The Information Technologies and Communication Authority (Bilgi Teknolojileri ve İletişim Kurumu — BTK) is authorized to issue technical standards for labelling implementation

### 6-Hour Takedown Obligation

The bill introduces a 6-hour emergency takedown obligation for AI-generated content that violates personal rights, amending Law No. 5651. This is notably shorter than comparable obligations under EU law:

- Platform operators must remove or disable access to AI-generated content that infringes personal rights (honor, reputation, privacy, likeness) within 6 hours of receiving a valid notification
- BTK may issue emergency orders requiring immediate restriction of access without prior judicial authorization where content causes "clear and present harm"
- Failure to comply within the 6-hour window triggers escalating administrative fines and potential platform blocking orders

### BTK Emergency Blocking Powers

The bill grants the BTK expanded emergency powers to block access to AI-generated content causing harm, without requiring a court order in urgent situations. This expands BTK's existing blocking authority under Law No. 5651, which already permits administrative blocking of content in enumerated categories. The new authority covers:

- AI-generated content used to spread election disinformation
- AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery
- AI-generated content impersonating public officials or candidates in a manner likely to mislead voters

Court review is available post-blocking, but platform access restrictions are immediately effective upon BTK order.

### Joint Liability for AI Developers and Content Providers

The bill amends the Electronic Commerce Law (Law No. 6563) and introduces provisions into the Law on Protection of Personal Data (KVKK — Law No. 6698) establishing joint liability for:

- AI system developers whose systems are used to generate harmful or infringing content
- Online platform operators that host or distribute such content
- Liability attaches where the developer was aware or should have been aware that the system could be used to generate harmful content and failed to implement reasonable safeguards

This joint liability model departs from the traditional notice-and-takedown approach and imposes affirmative design obligations on AI developers.

### Amendments to the Turkish Penal Code

The bill amends Articles 5237 of the Turkish Penal Code to introduce specific criminal offences for:

- Deliberate creation and distribution of AI-generated deepfake content to harm another person's reputation or cause psychological harm (up to 3 years imprisonment)
- Non-consensual creation and distribution of AI-generated intimate imagery (enhanced penalties under the bill)

### Cybersecurity Law Amendments

The bill amends Turkey's Cybersecurity Law to require operators of critical AI systems (government services, financial infrastructure, healthcare) to conduct periodic security assessments and report AI-related cybersecurity incidents to the national cybersecurity authority.
