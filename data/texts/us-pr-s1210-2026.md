                                          ACT

To amend subsection (f) of Article 6 of Act 75-2019, as amended, known
      as the "Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Services Act," for the purpose of
      incorporating the development and implementation of a technological application based
      on artificial intelligence (AI), for the efficient, transparent, and
      automated monitoring of the public auction processes in the Government of Puerto Rico; and
      for other related purposes.


                            STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
      Act 75-2019, as amended, created the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology
Service (hereinafter "PRITS") with the mandate to develop, coordinate, and promote
technological innovation and digital transformation in public administration. This
function is of vital importance to modernize governmental management and promote
a more efficient, transparent, and accessible administration for the citizenry.
      In view of the global technological revolution and the rise of artificial intelligence (AI),
the adoption of technological tools that allow for automated monitoring,
in real time, and with advanced analytical capacity of public processes is imposed.
As is known, AI, among other things, can detect atypical patterns,
validate documents, and ensure the correct execution of the stages of public
auctions.


                                           1
This is aligned with the public policies of the Government of Puerto Rico, which seek to
promote technological modernization to improve management and accountability.
       The current Administration has as a priority to include AI elements for the purpose of
achieving a digital integration of the services offered by different agencies in order to
offer a more fluid experience for citizens. That is why the application of AI for public
auctions should be founded on principles already established in Act 75-2019, and
should recognize technological innovation, the integration of systems, and digital
transformation as strategic axes for economic development and effective governance
in Puerto Rico.
       In addition, this tool will contribute to consolidating the digital unification of the
Government, facilitating interoperability between agencies and improving citizen
access to truthful and timely information, in adherence to the Transparency and Access
to Public Information Act. Likewise, the inter-agency coordination with the General
Services Administration will ensure the administrative and technical interoperability necessary
for the successful implementation of the system.
       The purpose of this amendment is to incorporate the development and implementation of
a technological application based on artificial intelligence (AI), for the efficient,
transparent, and automated monitoring of public auction processes in Puerto Rico,
thus promoting the integrity, efficiency, and oversight in such processes in accordance with
the technological public policies already established in Act 75-2019.
       For all the foregoing, this Legislative Assembly understands it indispensable to develop
and operate an innovative system based on artificial intelligence for the monitoring of
public auction processes, that strengthens transparency, efficiency, and confidence
in the public administration in Puerto Rico.
