 SENATE BILL 375 
 
 By: Senator Watson 
 
 Introduced and read first time: January 27, 2026 
 Assigned to: Education, Energy, and the Environment 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 County Boards of Education - Artificial Intelligence Training Program - Requirement 
 
 FOR the purpose of requiring the State Department of Education to develop or approve an artificial intelligence training program for members of county boards of education that meets certain requirements; requiring each member of a county board to complete a certain artificial intelligence training program each year; and generally relating to an artificial intelligence training program for members of county boards of education. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - Education 
 Section 4-148 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2025 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 BY repealing and reenacting, without amendments, 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 Section 3.5-801(a) and (c) 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2025 Supplement) 
 
 
 Preamble 
 WHEREAS, Rapid advances in artificial intelligence technology continue to transform education; and 
 WHEREAS, Recent federal actions, including Executive Order 14277, Advancing Artificial Intelligence Education for American Youth, issued by President Donald J. Trump on April 23, 2025, establish a national priority to responsibly implement artificial intelligence technology in educational settings; and 
 WHEREAS, County boards of education are tasked with making decisions to solve complex problems regarding the use of artificial intelligence technology in the State&#39;s public schools, including student privacy concerns; and 
 WHEREAS, Members of county boards of education need foundational and continuing training to successfully implement the safe, equitable, and strategic use of artificial intelligence technology in the State&#39;s public schools; now, therefore, 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Education 
 
 
 4-148. 
 
 
 (A) IN THIS SECTION,&quot; ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE&quot; HAS THE MEANING STATED IN &sect; 3.5-801 OF THE STATE FINANCE AND PROCUREMENT ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (B) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL DEVELOP OR APPROVE AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING PROGRAM FOR MEMBERS OF COUNTY BOARDS THAT INCLUDES: 
 
 
 (1) A FOUNDATIONAL TRAINING; AND 
 
 
 (2) CONTINUING ANNUAL TRAINING. 
 
 
 (C) (1) THE FOUNDATIONAL TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION SHALL CONTAIN THE FOLLOWING CURRICULUM COMPONENTS: 
 
 
 (I) FUNDAMENTALS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, INCLUDING THE CAPABILITIES, LIMITATIONS, AND RISKS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; 
 
 
 (II) ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY USE POLICY DEVELOPMENT, INCLUDING SAFETY AND ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS BY AGE AND GRADE LEVEL; 
 
 
 (III) STUDENT PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION LAWS; AND 
 
 
 (IV) ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY PROCUREMENT GUIDELINES AND OVERSIGHT. 
 
 
 (2) THE CONTINUING ANNUAL TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION SHALL CONTAIN THE FOLLOWING CURRICULUM COMPONENTS: 
 
 
 (I) A SUMMARY OF THE TOPICS COVERED IN THE FOUNDATIONAL TRAINING; AND 
 
 
 (II) DEVELOPMENTS IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY, EVOLVING BEST PRACTICES FOR USE IN EDUCATIONAL SETTINGS, AND NEW GUIDANCE. 
 
 
 (D) THE TRAINING PROGRAM DEVELOPED OR APPROVED BY THE DEPARTMENT UNDER THIS SECTION MAY BE OFFERED: 
 
 
 (1) IN PERSON, ONLINE, OR IN A HYBRID FORMAT; AND 
 
 
 (2) BY AN ORGANIZATION RESPONSIBLE FOR THE PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND EDUCATION OF MEMBERS OF COUNTY BOARDS. 
 
 
 (E) (1) (I) WITHIN 1 YEAR AFTER THE FIRST DAY OF A COUNTY BOARD MEMBER&#39;S TERM OF OFFICE, THE MEMBER SHALL COMPLETE THE FOUNDATIONAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (II) A MEMBER OF A COUNTY BOARD IN OFFICE ON JULY 1, 2026, SHALL COMPLETE THE FOUNDATIONAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION ON OR BEFORE JUNE 30, 2027. 
 
 
 (2) ON OR BEFORE JUNE 30 OF EACH YEAR OF A COUNTY BOARD MEMBER&#39;S TERM AFTER COMPLETION OF THE FOUNDATIONAL TRAINING, THE MEMBER SHALL COMPLETE THE CONTINUING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 (F) (1) ON COMPLETION OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER THIS SECTION, A MEMBER OF A COUNTY BOARD SHALL SUBMIT A COPY OF THE MEMBER&#39;S CERTIFICATE OF COMPLETION TO: 
 
 
 (I) THE PRESIDENT OF THE COUNTY BOARD ON WHICH THE MEMBER SERVES; AND 
 
 
 (II) THE DEPARTMENT. 
 
 
 (2) A COUNTY BOARD OR THE DEPARTMENT MAY ESTABLISH PROCESSES TO AUTOMATICALLY RECORD A COUNTY BOARD MEMBER&#39;S COMPLETION OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION IN PLACE OF THE SUBMISSION REQUIREMENT UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION. 
 
 
 (3) THE DEPARTMENT SHALL PUBLISH A REPORT ON ITS WEBSITE LISTING ANY MEMBER OF A COUNTY BOARD WHO DID NOT COMPLETE THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION DURING THE PRIOR YEAR. 
 
 
 (G) ON OR BEFORE OCTOBER 1, 2028, AND EACH OCTOBER 1 EVERY 2 YEARS THEREAFTER, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR AND, IN ACCORDANCE WITH &sect; 2-1257 OF THE STATE GOVERNMENT ARTICLE, THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY ON THE EFFECTIVENESS OF THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TRAINING PROGRAM FOR MEMBERS OF COUNTY BOARDS REQUIRED UNDER THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 Article - State Finance and Procurement 
 
 3.5-801. 
 (a) In this subtitle the following words have the meanings indicated. 
 (c) &quot;Artificial intelligence&quot; means a machine-based system that: 
 (1) can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments; 
 (2) uses machine and human-based inputs to perceive real and virtual environments and abstracts those perceptions into models through analysis in an automated manner; and 
 (3) uses model inference to formulate options for information or action. 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
