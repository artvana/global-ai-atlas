# Federal Law No. 169-FZ on Amendments to Federal Law on Experimental Legal Regimes in the Field of Digital Innovations

**Федеральный закон № 169-ФЗ**
*Russia — Signed and in force 4 January 2025*

---

## Overview

Federal Law No. 169-FZ, signed by the President of the Russian Federation on 4 January 2025, amends Federal Law No. 258-FZ of 31 July 2020 "On Experimental Legal Regimes in the Field of Digital Innovations in the Russian Federation." This Law extends and updates Russia's experimental legal regime (ELR) framework — sometimes referred to as the "regulatory sandbox" system — for AI development and testing.

The amendments build on the foundational ELR framework established in 2020, which created the original Moscow digital sandbox for AI technologies (supplemented by Federal Law No. 123-FZ of 2020 specifically addressing Moscow's AI sandbox). Law No. 169-FZ expands the ELR mechanism nationally, adds new requirements including mandatory liability insurance for participants, and extends the framework to cover generative AI systems that had not been addressed in the original 2020 legislation.

Russia's ELR framework reflects the government's approach of promoting AI innovation through controlled derogation from otherwise applicable regulations, while the broader Russian AI regulatory environment is shaped by state sovereignty concerns and the National AI Development Strategy (2019–2030).

## Key Provisions

### Extension and Update of Experimental Legal Regime Framework

The original Federal Law No. 258-FZ authorized the Russian government to establish experimental legal regimes allowing AI developers to operate under modified (relaxed) regulatory conditions in defined geographic and sectoral perimeters. Law No. 169-FZ:

- Extends the statutory authority for ELRs for an additional five-year period
- Updates the procedural requirements for applying for, obtaining, and operating under an ELR
- Expands the range of regulatory norms that can be modified within an ELR, including certain data localization requirements under Federal Law No. 242-FZ and AI-related provisions under sectoral legislation

### Mandatory Civil Liability Insurance

A significant innovation in Law No. 169-FZ is the introduction of a mandatory civil liability insurance requirement for AI developers and operators participating in regulatory sandboxes. Participants must:

- Obtain and maintain civil liability insurance covering harm caused to third parties by AI systems under testing during the ELR period
- Demonstrate financial capacity to compensate affected parties where insurance does not cover the full extent of harm
- Provide insurance documentation to the supervisory authority (Ministry of Digital Development) as a condition of ELR authorization

This provision addresses a gap in the original 2020 framework, which provided liability waivers without requiring participants to carry insurance against third-party claims arising from AI testing.

### Expansion to Generative AI Systems

The 2020 ELR framework was primarily designed for AI systems performing defined decision tasks (medical diagnosis assistance, transport, financial services). Law No. 169-FZ explicitly extends ELR eligibility to generative AI systems, including large language models and image generation systems. Generative AI ELR participants are subject to additional requirements:

- Content moderation and filtering obligations during the testing period
- Disclosure requirements to users that they are interacting with a system under experimental legal regime conditions
- Incident reporting obligations for content-related harms caused during testing

### Governance Procedures for Sandbox Applications

The Law updates the procedural framework for ELR applications:
- The Ministry of Digital Development, Communications and Mass Media remains the primary coordinating authority
- The Bank of Russia retains authority over ELRs involving AI in the financial sector
- Sector-specific ministries (Health, Transport) retain authority over ELRs in their domains
- Application review periods are standardized
- An inter-agency ELR coordination council is established to manage cross-sector applications

### Relationship to National AI Strategy

Law No. 169-FZ is framed as implementing Russia's National AI Development Strategy (2019–2030) and its 2024 update, which identifies the ELR framework as a key tool for accelerating AI innovation while maintaining state oversight. The Law explicitly references the goals of the Strategy and positions ELR expansion as contributing to Russia's target of achieving global leadership in AI by 2030.
