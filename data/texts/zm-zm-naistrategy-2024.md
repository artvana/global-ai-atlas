Zambia National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2024–2026
Ministry of Technology and Science, Republic of Zambia
ZAMBIA | 2024

Source: https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Zambia-Ai-Strategy-Book-option1-Web.pdf

Non-binding national AI strategy for the period 2024–2026, launched by Zambia's Ministry of Technology and Science (MOTS) on 24 October 2024 (Zambia's Independence Day). The strategy was developed with support from the Tony Blair Institute for Global Change and represents Zambia's first comprehensive AI policy framework.

Key Elements (based on publicly reported information):

1. Vision and Objectives
   The strategy envisions Zambia as a leading AI-enabled nation in Africa, using artificial intelligence to drive economic transformation, improve public services, and enhance citizen welfare. It sets out to position Zambia as a hub for AI development and deployment in the region.

2. Six Strategic Pillars
   The strategy is organized around six implementation pillars:

   Pillar 1 — Policy and Regulation
   - Developing an AI governance and regulatory framework
   - Establishing clear legal and ethical guidelines for AI use
   - Reviewing existing legislation for AI compatibility

   Pillar 2 — Human Capital Development
   - Expanding AI education and skills training at all levels
   - Partnerships with universities and TVET institutions
   - Building a pipeline of AI practitioners and researchers

   Pillar 3 — Infrastructure and Data
   - Improving digital infrastructure to support AI workloads
   - Developing national data governance frameworks
   - Creating data repositories and open data initiatives

   Pillar 4 — Research and Innovation
   - Supporting AI research at Zambian universities
   - Establishing innovation hubs and incubators
   - Promoting collaboration between academia, government, and industry

   Pillar 5 — Industry and Sectoral Development
   - Applying AI to priority sectors: agriculture, healthcare, mining, education, and financial services
   - Supporting local AI startups and enterprises
   - Attracting AI investment to Zambia

   Pillar 6 — International Collaboration
   - Engaging with regional AI initiatives (African Union, SADC)
   - Bilateral partnerships for AI capacity building
   - Participation in global AI governance forums

3. Implementation and Governance
   Implementation is overseen by the Ministry of Technology and Science, with cross-ministerial coordination. A National AI Coordination Committee provides strategic oversight.

4. Funding
   The strategy outlines funding requirements from government budget allocations, development partners, and private sector investment.

5. Monitoring and Evaluation
   Key performance indicators track progress across the six pillars with annual reviews.
