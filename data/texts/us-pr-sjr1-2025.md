GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO
SENATE OF PUERTO RICO
S. J. R. 1
   January 2, 2025
Introduced by Mr. Rivera Schatz
Referred to the Committee on Science, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence
JOINT RESOLUTION
To order the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), to conduct a registry of all the companies or businesses that develop languages or codes of Artificial Intelligence in Puerto Rico study on the rules that should be established regarding artificial intelligence in Puerto Rico that protect the interests of our citizens and the economic competitiveness of Puerto Rico, in coordination with Congress and the other states of our country; and for other related purposes.
STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
According to the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020, Artificial Intelligence is a computerized system capable of making predictions, recommendations, or making decisions that influence real or virtual environments, in accordance with a set of objectives defined by human beings. Artificial intelligence systems use information from computers and human beings to: perceive real and virtual environments; convert those perceptions into models through automated analysis; and employ inference models to formulate information or conclusions.
Artificial intelligence is a technological instrument that has come into our lives to stay. For this reason, both the Congress of the United States and various states have introduced legislation in that direction. Among the measures that stand out are those aimed at regulating the information that is shared about citizens, as well as preventing the discrimination that this type of technology can generate. As has been verified, there are discriminatory algorithms that, on occasions, deny the same opportunities to our citizens.
In accordance with the foregoing, this legislation is born with the objective that the state has knowledge of the companies that handle this type of technology in its territory. To this end, this Legislative Assembly considers it pertinent to create a registry of companies that develop languages or codes of artificial intelligence, in order to safeguard the security and rights of our citizens. In order to be able to determine how artificial intelligence should be regulated in the future in coordination with Congress and the other states, it is necessary to order the agency with expertise in this matter, the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), to conduct a study that determines what the specific rules should be that must be observed so that the interests of our citizens are duly protected as artificial intelligence develops. Said rules should ensure that the competitiveness of the Puerto Rican economy is equally protected, in such a way that the economic activity that artificial intelligence can generate in our jurisdiction does not decide to abandon us to establish itself in other states of our country or, worse still, other countries.
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:
Article 1.- Order
The Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) is hereby ordered to conduct a registry of developers of languages and codes of artificial intelligence.
PRITS shall establish a digital form in coordination with all the agencies and instrumentalities of the Government of Puerto Rico that develop languages or codes of Artificial Intelligence in Puerto Rico classified into categories: Health, medicine, finance, insurance, gambling, betting, education, learning, transportation, mobility, cybersecurity, surveillance, entertainment, digital content, energy, environment, work automation, customer services, back office support, government, and public administration. The Department of State, as well as the various instrumentalities of the government of Puerto Rico shall assist PRITS in the registry of said companies. study that:
(1)    proposes state governance policies that develop and update state policies and guidelines on the use, selection, development, and implementation of artificial intelligence systems in a manner consistent with state and federal legislation, and
(2)    develops a risk management policy before implementing an artificial intelligence system that is reasonable taking into consideration the guidelines and standards established in the most recent version at that time of the Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework, the Standard ISO/IEC 42001L or other nationally or internationally recognized risk management system for artificial intelligence systems.
(3) in collaboration with institutions of higher education and the technological sector, designs and publishes a framework of best practices for the ethical use of Artificial Intelligence in Puerto Rico, based on the following principles:
a.  Technological Literacy: Support citizens, public employees, and community stakeholders so that they acquire literacy in Artificial Intelligence.
b.  Access and Practical Application: Provide AI tools and resources to the community so that they can apply them in an appropriate, responsible, and productive manner in their daily and professional lives.
c.  Education and Rigor: Design and promote educational tools that inform and address the ethical aspects of the social integration of AI, maintaining the rigor expected in academic, administrative, and citizen preparation.
d.  Protection of Intellectual Property: Adopt and promote intellectual property policies that respond to regulation, law, and ethical aspects in the use and development of AI.
e.  Multisectoral Collaboration: Actively collaborate with all parties in the AI ecosystem (government, academia, and private sector) to share best practices in its use.
d.  Equity and Mitigation of Biases: Actively contribute to the identification and elimination of biases in AI systems to avoid algorithmic discrimination and ensure equity.
Article 2.– Content of the Form
The form established in the previous Article shall:
 contain a message where the purpose of the registry is explained;
the name of the business developing languages or codes of artificial intelligence;
 (c) the first and last name of a contact person at the company;
 (d) the postal and physical address, email, and telephone number of the contact person;
 (e) a checkbox where the company that develops languages or codes of artificial intelligence can check that it accepts that PRITS store the information of the company in the registry.
The information obtained under this section may not be sold or used for commercial purposes and shall be of public use. PRITS shall maintain an online database with search capability for companies that develop languages or codes of artificial intelligence.
Nothing provided in this section shall be interpreted as:
(1) requiring the disclosure of any commercial secret; or
(2) annulling any protection of the work product of the companies that form part of the registry.
Article 3 Article 2.– Report
Beginning on July 1, 2025 2026, PRITS shall submit an annual semiannual report to the Legislative Assembly that shall include, but not be limited to the following:
the number of companies or businesses that develop languages and codes of artificial intelligence registered up to that moment efforts made to advance the study;
the number of registries received by each agency or instrumentality of the government preliminary conclusions reached;
expenses incurred if any by the department during the semester subject of the report and projected expenses for the next semester;
copy of all documentation used as a source for obtaining information summary of current state legislation and federal legislation approved on the subject; and
any other documentation related to the preparation of the registry report.
Article 4 Article 3.- Effectiveness
This Joint Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
