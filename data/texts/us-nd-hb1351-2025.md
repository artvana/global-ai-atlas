# North Dakota House Bill 1351 — Nonconsensual Distribution of Intimate Deepfakes Prohibition

**North Dakota 69th Legislative Assembly, 2025 Regular Session**
**Enacted:** April 11, 2025 | **Effective:** August 1, 2025

**Citation**
*North Dakota — April 11, 2025*

---

## Overview

North Dakota HB 1351 creates both a criminal offense and a civil cause of action for the creation or distribution of nonconsensual AI-generated or digitally altered sexually explicit images of identifiable persons. The law expressly covers deepfake technology, generative AI, and other digital manipulation tools used to produce intimate images without the depicted person's consent. Criminal penalties escalate from a Class A misdemeanor for a first offense to a Class C felony for subsequent violations. Victims retain the right to seek civil damages and injunctive relief independent of criminal prosecution.

---

## Key Provisions

### Criminal Offense — Nonconsensual Deepfake Intimate Images
- It is a criminal offense to **create, distribute, publish, or transmit** AI-generated, digitally manipulated, or synthetically produced sexually explicit images depicting an identifiable individual without that individual's consent.
- The offense applies where:
  - The depicted person is identifiable;
  - The content was created or altered using artificial intelligence, deepfake technology, machine learning, or digital manipulation tools;
  - The depicted person did not consent to the creation or distribution of the content.

### Penalty Escalation
- **First offense:** Class A misdemeanor — maximum 1 year imprisonment and/or $3,000 fine (NDCC § 12.1-32-01).
- **Subsequent offense:** Class C felony — maximum 5 years imprisonment and/or $10,000 fine.

### Civil Cause of Action
- A victim depicted in nonconsensual deepfake intimate images may bring a **private civil action** against any person who created or distributed the content.
- Available civil remedies include:
  - **Actual damages** for economic harm, reputational injury, and emotional distress;
  - **Injunctive relief** to compel removal of the content and prevent further distribution;
  - **Statutory damages** in lieu of actual damages where available;
  - **Attorney fees and costs** for a prevailing plaintiff.

### Covered Content Types
- AI-generated sexually explicit images (generative AI / synthetic imagery);
- Deepfake video or images that superimpose an individual's likeness onto explicit content;
- Digitally altered images where an identifiable person's face, body, or voice is modified to create explicit content without consent.

### Scope
- Applies to living identifiable individuals.
- Covers creation, possession with intent to distribute, distribution, publication, and transmission.

---

## Enforcement

- Criminal enforcement: County State's Attorney.
- Civil enforcement: Private right of action in North Dakota district court.
