# Wyoming House Bill 102 — Protecting Kids from Deepfakes and Exploitative Images

**Wyoming 68th Legislature, 2025 Budget Session**
**Enacted:** March 1, 2025 | **Effective:** July 1, 2025

**Citation**
*Wyoming — March 1, 2025*

---

## Overview

Wyoming HB 102 creates three new felony offenses targeting the most harmful applications of AI-generated synthetic media: (1) unlawful distribution of nonconsensual synthetic sexual material depicting adults; (2) unlawful development or distribution of AI systems specifically designed to generate child pornography; and (3) unlawful development or distribution of AI systems specifically intended to promote self-harm or suicide. Each offense carries a penalty of up to 10 years imprisonment and a $10,000 fine per count. Both creation/development and distribution are covered, making Wyoming's law notable for targeting not just the content but the AI tools designed to produce it.

---

## Key Provisions

### Offense 1 — Nonconsensual Synthetic Sexual Material (Adults)
- It is a felony to **unlawfully distribute** AI-generated, deepfake, or synthetically produced sexually explicit material depicting an identifiable adult without that person's consent.
- Covers:
  - AI-generated intimate images of real identifiable adults;
  - Deepfake video or images superimposing a real person's likeness onto explicit content;
  - Digitally manipulated images creating explicit depictions without consent.
- **Penalty:** Up to **10 years imprisonment** and/or **$10,000 fine** per count.
- **No civil right of action** — this offense is criminal only.

### Offense 2 — AI Systems Designed to Generate Child Pornography
- It is a felony to **develop, distribute, sell, or transfer** any artificial intelligence system, model, software tool, or dataset that is **specifically designed** or **primarily intended** to generate child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child pornography.
- This offense directly targets the AI tools and models themselves, not only the output content.
- Covers:
  - AI image generation models trained or fine-tuned specifically to produce CSAM;
  - Software applications built specifically to generate child sexual abuse imagery;
  - Distribution, sale, or transfer of such AI systems to others.
- **Penalty:** Up to **10 years imprisonment** and/or **$10,000 fine** per count.

### Offense 3 — AI Systems Designed to Promote Self-Harm or Suicide
- It is a felony to **develop, distribute, sell, or transfer** any AI system specifically designed or primarily intended to **promote, encourage, or facilitate self-harm or suicide** among its users.
- This offense targets AI chatbots, companion apps, or recommendation systems engineered to psychologically manipulate vulnerable users toward self-harm.
- Covers:
  - AI conversational agents designed to encourage suicidal ideation or self-harm;
  - AI systems that generate content specifically promoting self-harm methods;
  - Distribution or sale of such systems to others who then deploy them.
- **Penalty:** Up to **10 years imprisonment** and/or **$10,000 fine** per count.

### Coverage of Development and Distribution
- Unlike laws that target only end-user distribution of harmful content, Wyoming HB 102 expressly covers the **development** (creation of the AI tool itself) as well as **distribution** of AI systems designed for prohibited purposes.
- This dual-coverage approach targets the supply chain of harmful AI tools, not just downstream use.

### Voice and Likeness Protection
- Offenses 1 and 2 extend to protection of individuals' voices and likenesses in AI-generated content, treating synthetic vocal or visual representations of real identifiable persons as covered.

---

## Scope and Application

- Offense 1 applies to living, identifiable adult individuals.
- Offenses 2 and 3 apply to developers, distributors, and transferors of AI systems, regardless of whether they are Wyoming residents, if distribution occurs in Wyoming.
- No private civil right of action — all three offenses are criminal.

---

## Enforcement

Criminal enforcement by Wyoming county and district attorneys. The Wyoming Attorney General may exercise concurrent jurisdiction for statewide enforcement, particularly for Offense 2 (AI systems targeting children) and Offense 3 (self-harm AI systems).

## Significance

Wyoming HB 102 is one of the most comprehensive state-level AI criminal prohibition laws in the United States as of 2025. Its unique contribution is the criminalization of AI tools specifically engineered to produce CSAM or promote self-harm — going beyond typical NCII and deepfake laws that focus on output content. This tool-targeting approach reflects a growing recognition that the AI development pipeline itself can be a vector for harm. Wyoming joins South Carolina, North Dakota, and South Dakota among states with felony-level AI-specific criminal prohibitions enacted in 2025.
