AN ACT

2 legal status of artificial intelligence.
Analysis by the Legislative Reference Bureau
This bill specifies that artificial intelligence is not a person; cannot own,
control, or hold title to property; and cannot be a spouse or a corporate officer or
director.
The people of the state of Wisconsin, represented in senate and assembly, do
enact as follows:
3 SECTION 1. 134.44 of the statutes is created to read:
4 134.44 Legal status of artificial intelligence. (1) In this section:
5 (a) “Artificial intelligence” means an engineered or machine-based system
6 that varies in its level of autonomy and that can, for explicit or implicit objectives,
7 infer from the input it receives how to generate outputs that can influence physical
8 or virtual environments.

9 (2) (a) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, artificial intelligence is not
10 a person for any purpose under the law of this state.
11 (b) Artificial intelligence may not be granted the status of person or any form
12 of legal personhood and may not be considered to possess consciousness, self-
13 awareness, sentience, or any similar traits of living beings.
14 (c) Artificial intelligence may not be a spouse or domestic partner and may not
15 hold any legal status analogous to marriage or union with a human or with other
16 artificial intelligence.
17 (d) Artificial intelligence may not be designated, be appointed, or serve as an
18 officer, director, partner, manager, or member, or any similar role, of an entity.
19 (e) Artificial intelligence may not own, control, or hold title to any form of
20 property, including real estate, intellectual property, financial accounts, and digital
21 assets. All assets and proprietary interests generated, managed, or otherwise
22 associated with artificial intelligence shall be attributed to the developer,
23 manufacturer, or owner of the artificial intelligence.
