# LAW OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN No. ZRU-1115 of 21 January 2026

## ON AMENDMENTS AND ADDITIONS TO CERTAIN LEGISLATIVE ACTS OF THE REPUBLIC OF UZBEKISTAN IN CONNECTION WITH THE REGULATION OF RELATIONS ARISING FROM THE APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

*Adopted by the Legislative Chamber on 12 August 2025*
*Approved by the Senate on 1 November 2025*

---

## Preamble

In recent years, large-scale work has been carried out in our country on the introduction and widespread application of artificial intelligence technologies, ensuring access to and high quality of digital data, and training qualified personnel in the field.

At the same time, there is a need to define the concept of artificial intelligence at the level of law, to establish general rules for the use of artificial intelligence in the creation of information resources and in the operation of information systems functioning on the basis of artificial intelligence technologies, and to establish administrative liability for the unlawful processing of personal data using artificial intelligence technologies.

This Law introduces additions and amendments to the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Informatization" aimed at establishing the general foundations for the use of artificial intelligence technologies and clarifying the powers of the competent state body in this field.

An addition is also made to the Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Administrative Liability, providing for the establishment of administrative liability for the unlawful processing of personal data using artificial intelligence.

---

## Article 1

The following additions and amendments are introduced into the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan of 11 December 2003 No. 560-II "On Informatization":

### 1) Article 3 is supplemented with a ninth paragraph as follows:

> **"artificial intelligence"** — a complex of technological solutions that makes it possible to imitate the cognitive functions of a human being (including self-learning and the search for solutions) and to obtain, when performing specific tasks, results comparable to the results of human intellectual activity;

### 2) In part two of Article 4:

The following eighth paragraph is added:

> "creation of the necessary conditions for the introduction and development of artificial intelligence, as well as determination of the legal, technological and economic foundations";

### 3) In Article 6:

The following ninth, tenth and eleventh paragraphs are added:

> "creates conditions for attracting investment in the area of artificial intelligence;
>
> forms the technical infrastructure for processing data of state bodies on the basis of artificial intelligence;
>
> organizes the training, retraining and professional development of personnel in the area of artificial intelligence";

### 4) The Law is supplemented with Article 7¹ as follows:

> **Article 7¹. Use of artificial intelligence in the creation of information resources and in the operation of information systems**
>
> Information resources created using artificial intelligence, as well as information systems functioning on the basis of artificial intelligence technologies, must not cause harm to a human being, to their life, health, freedom, honour, dignity, or violate their other inalienable rights.
>
> When making legally significant decisions affecting human rights and freedoms, it is not permitted to rely exclusively on the conclusions of information resources and information systems created on the basis of artificial intelligence technologies.

### 5) In part one of Article 12¹:

The following eighth paragraph is added:

> "unlawful processing of personal data using artificial intelligence technologies, their dissemination in the mass media, telecommunications networks, or the worldwide information network Internet";

---

## Article 2

Article 46² of the Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Administrative Liability is supplemented with a part two as follows:

> "Unlawful processing of personal data using artificial intelligence technologies, their dissemination in the mass media, telecommunications networks, or the worldwide information network Internet, —
>
> shall entail the imposition of a fine ranging from fifty to one hundred basic calculation units, with confiscation of the items constituting the administrative offence."

---

## Article 3

The Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan shall:

- bring government decrees into compliance with this Law;
- ensure the revision and repeal by the republican executive authorities of their normative legal acts that contradict this Law;
- ensure execution of this Law, communication of it to those responsible for its implementation, and explanation of its essence and significance to the population.

---

## Article 4

This Law shall enter into force on the day of its official publication.
