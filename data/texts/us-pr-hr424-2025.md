RESOLUTION
To order the Committees on Consumer Affairs; and on Banking, Insurance and Commerce of the House of Representatives of Puerto Rico to conduct a thorough investigation on how to prevent the modalities of financial fraud through the use of artificial intelligence; and for other related purposes.
STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
As artificial intelligence (AI) and available technologies advance, criminals use AI more frequently to commit identity theft. Scammers use AI to automate identity theft through phishing attacks, convincing emails, or impersonating real people.
For example, criminals use AI to create hyper-realistic videos and voice recordings that impersonate certain people. These "deepfakes" can be used to steal identities and gain access to personal or business accounts. Scammers use automated calls to trick people into revealing their personal information by imitating representatives of financial institutions, government agencies, or debt collectors.
Phishing schemes, malicious software, and data leaks provide scammers with personal credentials that they can then use to steal identities, gain access to bank account information, change account information, and initiate fraudulent transactions. For their part, social media is a popular tool for identity theft, as it allows thieves to gather publicly available personal data. Fraudsters could use names, photos, and personal information to create fraudulent accounts or commit scams with other identities.
These fraud schemes involve the use of financial products and applications such as PayPal, Venmo, ATH Móvil, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay. Therefore, it is urgent to examine the measures taken and available to prevent these scams that will continue to evolve with the advancement of AI.
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PUERTO RICO:
Section 1.- The Committees on Consumer Affairs; and on Banking, Insurance and Commerce of the House of Representatives are hereby ordered to conduct a thorough investigation on how to prevent the modalities of financial fraud through the use of artificial intelligence.
Section 2.- The Committees shall consider interagency collaboration with entities such as the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS), the Department of Consumer Affairs of Puerto Rico (DACO), the Office of the Commissioner of Financial Institutions (OCIF), and any other pertinent state, federal, or municipal agency; and shall consider collaboration with private individuals and entities that have expertise on the subject of financial fraud through the use of artificial intelligence.
Section 3.- The Committees shall submit a report with their findings, conclusions, and recommendations within a term not greater than one hundred eighty (180) days, counted from the approval of this Resolution.
    Section 4.- This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
