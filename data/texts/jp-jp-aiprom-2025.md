Law No. 53 of 2020
Act on the Promotion of Research and Development and Utilization of Artificial Intelligence-Related Technologies

Chapter 1 General Provisions

(the purpose)
Article 1　This Act, recognizing that artificial intelligence-related technologies are fundamental to the development of Japan's economy and society, establishes the basic principles and fundamental matters concerning the formulation of a basic plan for promoting the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, as well as other basic matters concerning such policies. Furthermore, by establishing the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters, this Act aims to promote comprehensive and systematic policies concerning the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies in conjunction with policies under the Basic Act on Science and Technology and Innovation (Act No. 130 of 1995) and the Basic Act on the Formation of a Digital Society (Act No. 35 of 2021) and other related laws, thereby contributing to the improvement of people's lives and the sound development of the national economy.

(definition)
Article 2　In this Act, "artificial intelligence-related technology" means technology necessary to realize functions that replace human cognitive, reasoning, and judgmental intellectual abilities through artificial means, as well as technology related to information processing systems that realize functions that process input information using said technology and output the results.

(Basic philosophy)
Article 3　The promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies shall be carried out in accordance with the basic principles set forth in this Article, in addition to the policies concerning the promotion of science and technology and innovation creation stipulated in Article 3 of the Basic Act on Science and Technology and Innovation and the basic principles stipulated in Chapter 2 of the Basic Act on the Formation of a Digital Society.

2　The promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies shall be carried out with the aim of maintaining Japan's capacity to conduct research and development of artificial intelligence-related technologies and improving the international competitiveness of industries related to artificial intelligence-related technologies, in light of the fact that artificial intelligence-related technologies are fundamental technologies for the development of the economy and society, as they will bring about significant efficiency and sophistication in administrative work and private business activities and the creation of new industries through their appropriate and effective utilization, and are also important technologies from the perspective of national security.

3　The promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies shall be carried out with the aim of comprehensively and systematically promoting these efforts, taking into consideration the close interrelationships between stakeholders at each stage, from basic research on artificial intelligence-related technologies to their application in people's lives and economic activities.

4　In light of the fact that research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, if conducted for fraudulent purposes or inappropriately, may contribute to criminal activity, leakage of personal information, copyright infringement, and other situations that harm the peace of public life and the rights and interests of citizens, necessary measures must be taken to ensure transparency in the research and development and utilization process of artificial intelligence-related technologies in order to promote their proper implementation.

5　The research and development and application of artificial intelligence-related technologies shall be promoted under international cooperation so as to contribute to the peace and development of Japan and the international community, and Japan shall endeavor to play a leading role in international cooperation concerning the research and development and application of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

(Responsibilities of the State)
Article 4　The State has the responsibility to comprehensively and systematically formulate and implement policies concerning the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, in accordance with the basic principles set forth in the preceding article (hereinafter referred to as the "basic principles").

2　The government shall promote the active use of artificial intelligence-related technologies in national administrative agencies in order to improve the efficiency and sophistication of administrative work.

(Responsibilities of local governments)
Article 5　Local governments, in accordance with the basic principles, have the responsibility to formulate and implement their own policies that take advantage of the characteristics of their respective areas, as measures that should be implemented by local governments, under an appropriate division of roles with the national government regarding the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

(Responsibilities of research and development institutions, etc.)
Article 6　Universities, research and development corporations as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 9 of the Act on the Promotion of Science and Technology and Innovation Creation (Act No. 63 of 2008), and other institutions that conduct research and development of artificial intelligence-related technologies (hereinafter referred to as "research and development institutions") shall, in accordance with the basic principles, actively endeavor to conduct research and development of artificial intelligence-related technologies, disseminate the results thereof, and cultivate human resources with specialized and broad knowledge, and shall endeavor to cooperate with the measures implemented by the national government pursuant to the provisions of Article 4 and the measures implemented by local governments pursuant to the provisions of the preceding article.

2　When the national and local governments formulate and implement policies related to universities concerning the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, they must endeavor to revitalize research activities at universities and give due consideration to the autonomy of researchers and other characteristics of research at universities.

3　In light of the need to comprehensively utilize knowledge from diverse fields of humanities and natural sciences in order to effectively advance research and development of artificial intelligence-related technologies, research and development institutions shall endeavor to conduct interdisciplinary or comprehensive research and development.

(Responsibilities of the user)
Article 7　Any person who intends to develop or provide products or services utilizing artificial intelligence-related technologies, or any other person who intends to utilize artificial intelligence-related technologies in their business activities (hereinafter referred to as "utilizing businesses"), shall, in accordance with the basic principles, endeavor to improve the efficiency and sophistication of their business activities and create new industries through the proactive utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, and shall cooperate with the measures implemented by the national government pursuant to the provisions of Article 4 and the measures implemented by local governments pursuant to the provisions of Article 5.

(The responsibility of citizens)
Article 8　The public shall, in accordance with the basic principles, deepen their understanding of and interest in artificial intelligence-related technologies, and endeavor to cooperate with the measures implemented by the national government pursuant to the provisions of Article 4 and the measures implemented by local governments pursuant to the provisions of Article 5.

(Strengthening cooperation)
Article 9　In light of the fact that the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies can be promoted through cooperation among the national government, local governments, research and development institutions, and businesses that utilize these technologies, the national government shall take necessary measures to strengthen cooperation among these parties.

(Legislative measures, etc.)
Article 10　The government shall take necessary legislative, financial, and other measures to implement policies concerning the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

Chapter 2 Basic measures

(Promotion of research and development, etc.)
Article 11　The government shall take measures to promote consistent research and development, from basic research to practical application of artificial intelligence-related technologies, to establish a system for transferring research and development results at research and development institutions, to provide information related to research and development results, and other such measures.

(Promotion of the development and sharing of facilities and equipment)
Article 12　The State shall take necessary measures to promote the development and sharing of facilities and equipment and intellectual infrastructure necessary for large-scale information processing, information communication, storage of electromagnetic records (records created by electronic, magnetic, or other methods that cannot be perceived by human senses and are used for information processing by computers), as well as datasets (collections of information collected for a specific purpose) and other intellectual infrastructure (intellectual infrastructure as defined in Article 24-4 of the Act on the Promotion of Science and Technology and Innovation Creation; hereinafter the same applies in this Article), which are required for research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

(Ensuring appropriateness)
Article 13　The government shall take necessary measures, including developing guidelines in accordance with the spirit of international norms, to ensure the proper implementation of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

(Securing personnel, etc.)
Article 14　The national government shall, in close cooperation with local governments, research and development institutions, and businesses utilizing artificial intelligence, take necessary measures to secure, train, and improve the qualifications of human resources from diverse fields who possess the specialized and broad knowledge required at each stage, from basic research on artificial intelligence-related technologies to their application in people's lives and economic activities.

(Promotion of education, etc.)
Article 15　The government shall take necessary measures, including promoting education and learning on artificial intelligence-related technologies and enhancing public relations activities, to deepen public understanding of and interest in artificial intelligence-related technologies among the general public.

(Research and studies, etc.)
Article 16　The State shall collect information on trends in research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies both domestically and internationally, analyze cases in which the rights and interests of citizens have been infringed as a result of research and development or utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies for fraudulent purposes or by inappropriate means, and consider countermeasures based on such analysis and other surveys and research that contribute to the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies. Based on the results of these surveys and research, the State shall provide guidance, advice, information, and other necessary measures to research and development institutions, utilization businesses, and other parties.

(International cooperation)
Article 17　The government shall promote international cooperation regarding the research and development and application of artificial intelligence-related technologies, and shall actively participate in the formulation of international norms.

Chapter 3: Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence

Article 18　The government shall, in accordance with the basic principles and based on the basic measures set forth in the preceding chapter, establish a basic plan for promoting research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies (hereinafter referred to as the "Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence").

2　The Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence shall stipulate the following matters:
one　Basic policy on measures to promote research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies
two　Measures that the government should take comprehensively and systematically to promote the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.
three　In addition to the matters listed in the preceding two items, other matters necessary for the government to comprehensively and systematically promote policies concerning the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

3　The Prime Minister shall seek Cabinet approval for the draft Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence prepared by the Headquarters for the Strategy of Artificial Intelligence.

4　When the Cabinet decision referred to in the preceding paragraph is made, the Prime Minister shall promptly publish the Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence.

5　The provisions of the preceding two paragraphs shall apply mutatis mutandis to amendments to the Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence.

Chapter 4: Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters

(Installation)
Article 19　In order to comprehensively and systematically promote policies concerning the research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, the Cabinet shall establish the Headquarters for the Strategy of Artificial Intelligence (hereinafter referred to as the "Headquarters").

(Duties)
Article 20　The headquarters is responsible for the following matters:
one　Matters concerning the creation and promotion of the draft and implementation of the Basic Plan for Artificial Intelligence.
two　In addition to the matters listed in the previous issue, this includes planning, formulating, and overall coordination of important policies concerning the promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies.

(organization)
Article 21　The headquarters will consist of the Director of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters, the Deputy Director of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters, and members of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters.

(Head of Artificial Intelligence Strategy)
Article 22　The head of the headquarters shall be the Director of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters (hereinafter referred to as "the Director"), and this position shall be filled by the Prime Minister.

2　The Director-General oversees the administrative affairs of the headquarters and directs and supervises the staff of the department.

(Deputy Head of Artificial Intelligence Strategy)
Article 23　The Headquarters shall have a Deputy Director-General for Artificial Intelligence Strategy (hereinafter referred to as "Deputy Director-General" in the following paragraph and Article 2, paragraph 2), who shall be the Chief Cabinet Secretary and the Minister in charge of Artificial Intelligence Strategy (a Minister of State whose duties are to assist the Prime Minister in the comprehensive and systematic promotion of research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, under the direction of the Prime Minister).

2　The deputy director assists the director in their duties.

(Member of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters)
Article 24　The headquarters will have members of the Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters (hereinafter referred to as "Headquarters Members" in the following section).

2　The headquarters shall consist of all Ministers of State other than the Director-General and Deputy Director-General.

(Submission of documents and other forms of cooperation)
Article 25　When the Headquarters deems it necessary to carry out its duties, it may request the heads of relevant administrative agencies, local governments, independent administrative agencies (meaning independent administrative agencies as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Act on General Rules for Independent Administrative Agencies (Act No. 103 of 1999)), and local independent administrative agencies (meaning local independent administrative agencies as defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 of the Local Independent Administrative Agency Act (Act No. 118 of 2003)), as well as representatives of special corporations (meaning corporations established directly by law or corporations established by special acts of incorporation under special laws, to which the provisions of Article 4, Paragraph 1, Item 8 of the Act on the Establishment of the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (Act No. 91 of 1999) apply), to submit materials, express opinions, provide explanations, and provide other necessary cooperation.

2　The headquarters may, when it deems it particularly necessary to carry out its duties, request necessary cooperation from persons other than those specified in the preceding paragraph.

(Administrative)
Article 26　Matters concerning the headquarters will be handled by the Cabinet Office.

(Minister in charge)
Article 27　With regard to matters concerning the headquarters, the minister in charge as referred to in the Cabinet Act (Act No. 5 of 1947) shall be the Prime Minister.

(Delegation to government ordinance)
Article 28　In addition to what is stipulated in this Act, necessary matters concerning the headquarters shall be prescribed by Cabinet Order.

Supplementary Provisions

(Effective date)
Article 1　This law shall come into effect from the date of its promulgation. However, the provisions of Chapters III and IV, and Articles III and IV of the Supplementary Provisions shall come into effect on a date to be determined by Cabinet Order within a period not exceeding three months from the date of promulgation.

(Consider)
Article 2　The government shall, taking into account international trends and other changes in socio-economic conditions concerning measures to promote research and development and utilization of artificial intelligence-related technologies, review the status of the implementation of this Act and, if deemed necessary, take the required measures based on the results of that review.
