GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO
  20th Legislative Assembly                                                                                                   2nd Regular Session
CHAMBER OF REPRESENTATIVES
H. R. 488
OCTOBER 16, 2025
Introduced by Representative Morey Noble
Referred to the Committee on Internal Affairs
RESOLUTION
To order the Committee on Government of the House of Representatives of the Government of Puerto Rico, to conduct an investigation on the current generation capacity of each electric power generation plant on the Island of Puerto Rico, as well as the estimates and projections of the demand for electric power expected for the next five (5) years, taking into consideration the possible impact of the growth of demand for electric power by data centers, linked to driving artificial intelligence (AI) applications, and for other related purposes.
STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
As is well known, the problems in the generation of electricity in Puerto Rico include dependence on fossil fuels, lack of investment in infrastructure, and an aging and vulnerable grid. Despite the transition objectives, delays in the reconstruction of the grid and continued dependence on fossil fuels have slowed progress toward cleaner and more stable energies. These factors cause frequent blackouts and a generation of energy that cannot meet demand, resulting in unstable and costly service for consumers. Generation plants operate with frequent breakdowns, which generates service interruptions.
In the next five years, the energy demand of data centers that sustain artificial intelligence (AI) is expected to increase significantly. The rapid growth of AI applications, from language models to image processing and complex simulations, requires an increasingly powerful computing infrastructure. These data centers, which house thousands of high-performance servers operating continuously, consume enormous amounts of electricity, both to operate the equipment and to maintain advanced cooling systems that prevent overheating. As the adoption of AI expands in sectors such as health, finance, logistics, and transportation, the pressure on the electrical grid and the planet's energy resources will be considerable.
Technology companies and governments will need to invest in more efficient and sustainable solutions, such as the use of renewable energies, innovative cooling systems, and computing architectures that optimize energy consumption. Without adequate planning, the exponential growth of AI could generate a disproportionate increase in electrical consumption, affecting energy sustainability and operating costs at a global level.
Puerto Rico is a growing technology destination, supported by a bilingual workforce, competitive tax incentives, and government backing through investment initiatives. Global and local companies are investing with the goal of establishing themselves as a center of innovation in the Caribbean and Latin America. These initiatives are expected to continue and to attract new AI technology companies in the coming years which would represent an increase in the consumption of electrical power affecting the sustainability of the energy grid.
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF PUERTO RICO:
Article 1.- The Committee on Government of the House of Representatives is hereby ordered to conduct a thorough investigation regarding the generation capacity of each electric power generation plant on the Island, including publicly owned, privately owned, and cooperative plants; and to determine the capacity of each plant and the amount of electric power effectively generated monthly and annually by each one. of the Government of Puerto Rico, to conduct an investigation on the current generation capacity of each generation plant in Puerto Rico, as well as the estimates and projections of the demand for electric power for the next five (5) years, taking into consideration the possible impact of the growth of data centers linked to artificial intelligence (AI) applications, and for other related purposes.
Article 2.- The Committee shall also request a thorough analysis from the Puerto Rico Electric Power Authority, LUMA Energy, Genera PR, and the Puerto Rico Energy Bureau on what their estimates or projections are of the demand for electric power expected in the next 5 years considering the possible increase in demand for electric power by data centers, that drive artificial intelligence (AI) applications.
Article 3.- The Committee, as provided by the Rules of the House of Representatives, H. R. 1, may subpoena any natural or juridical person, or governmental entity that has had or has any kind of relationship or connection with the matters specified in Sections 1 and 2 of this Resolution.
Article 4.- The Committee, in addition, may carry out all the studies, investigations, meetings, subpoenas, requests for production of documents, requests for information, requirements, and reports it deems necessary and may investigate any matter it deems pertinent to comply with the provisions of this Resolution.
Article 5.- The Committee shall submit a report with its findings, conclusions, and recommendations within a term of one hundred twenty (120) days, counted from the approval of this Resolution.
Article 6.- This Resolution shall take effect immediately after its approval.
