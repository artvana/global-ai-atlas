Understanding the UNC Commitment

Our Commitment

The United National Congress (UNC) recognizes that the economic
future of Trinidad and Tobago depends on strategic diversification
With the deciine of the Oil & Gas boom, itis imperative that we
identify and cultivate new revenue-generating industries. One such
industry, underutilized yet full of potential, is yachting and
maritime services.

The UNC proposes the development of a world-class Yacht
Service, Maintenance, and Boat-Building Hub in Chaguaramas—
an initiative that leverages our ideal geographic location, skilled
workforce, and existing maritime infrastructure to create thousands
of jobs and stimulate new economic activity.

Understanding the Future with Strategic Policies

Building A Stronger Future With
Strategic Policies

professionals and entrepreneurs. With specialized training
programs, certifications, and global partnerships, the workforce
will be equipped to meet industry demands and contribute to
national progress. innovation, expertise, and skill development will
drive the country towards a sustainable and competitive future.

NEW MEDIA AND SOCIAL MEDIA
AS CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

The rise of new media and social media has transformed the creative industries and digital
media sector, offering unprecedented opportunities for individuals to monetize their creativity
and upend traditional advertising. Content creators can now produce low-cost, high-efficiency
content using accessible tools like smartphones and leverage platforms like YouTube,
Instagram, TikTok, and others to generate income. The advent of Al further enhances this
potential, enabling creators to streamline production processes, improve content quality, and
expand their reach. This industry allows individuals to work remotely, build audiences through
followers, subscriptions, blogs, and podcasts, and earn revenue via advertising, brand
partnerships, and affiliate marketing. Influencers have become key players in driving product,
service, and messaging sales.

Establish clear guidelines for advertising transparency (¢.g., sponsored content disclosure).
Protect intellectual property rights for digital creators.

Create fair policies for legitimately recognizing earnings generated through online platforms
under our financial rules, thus enabling access to banking services and government incentives.

Create a Creative Content Development Fund to provide loans for aspiring content creators to
access equipment, training, and mentorship programs.

Partner with private sector companies to sponsor workshops on digital marketing strategies
and Al integration in content creation.

Offer courses on new media and social media management, video production, graphic design,
and Al tools.

Enhance affordable access to internet connectivity nationwide to ensure citizens can access
online platforms efficiently.

Establish co-working spaces equipped with tools like cameras, editing software, and
high-speed internet for content creators.

Youth Development: Reclaiming a Generation, Restoring Hope
OUR COMMITMENT

The United National Congress (UNC) recognizes that the future of Trinidad and Tobago
lies in the potential of our young people. Yet, under successive PNM administrations,
that potential has been systematically squandered. Our youth have been neglected,
underprepared, and left without opportunity or direction—pushed instead toward lives of
despair and, increasingly, crime.

The UNC will rebuild the broken foundation of youth development. We will invest in
programmes, policies, and pathways that equip our youth with skills, values, and hope.
We will confront the root causes of youth disenfranchisement and deliver a framework
that uplifts every young person in this country—no matter their background.

The Current Crisis
Trinidad and Tobago faces a youth emergency:

« Skills training programmes have collapsed or become irrelevant to the modern
economy.

+ Graduates leave school or university with no job prospects, disillusioned and
directionless.

« Values and attitudes have eroded, replaced by a survivalist culture of instant
gratification.

« Gangs have become the most accessible path to income and identity for too
many.

« Criminal activity has exploded—and it is not the elderly breaking into homes or
trafficking guns; it is our unattached, unsupported, and untrained youth.

The PNM government has abandoned an entire generation. The UNC will not.
