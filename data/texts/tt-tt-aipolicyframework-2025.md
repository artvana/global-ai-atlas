# Official Policy Framework for the Government of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago on Artificial Intelligence

Ministry of Public Administration and Artificial Intelligence (MPAAI)
Government of the Republic of Trinidad and Tobago

## Ministry Background

As of May 23, 2025, the Ministry of Public Administration and the Ministry of Digital Transformation merged to form the Ministry of Public Administration and Artificial Intelligence (MPAAI). This change is officially documented in Gazette No. 81. The Minister of MPAAI is Senator The Honourable Dominic Smith.

## Overview

Trinidad and Tobago's Official AI Policy Framework positions the nation as a regional pioneer in integrating advanced AI technologies into national development. The Ministry's approach to Artificial Intelligence is described as "thoughtful, ethical and people-first," achieved by developing national strategies to guide responsible AI adoption.

## National AI Assessment Initiative

The MPAAI, in collaboration with the UNESCO Regional Office for the Caribbean, is hosting national stakeholder consultations as part of the UNESCO Artificial Intelligence Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM) for Trinidad and Tobago. This process is designed to:
- Validate survey findings
- Inform the development of a comprehensive, ethical, and forward-looking national approach to artificial intelligence
- Assess AI preparedness across six critical dimensions

## Six Critical Dimensions of AI Readiness

Trinidad and Tobago's preparedness for AI adoption is being assessed across:
1. Governance
2. Policy
3. Digital infrastructure
4. Ethics
5. Institutional capacity
6. Inclusion

## Key National AI Initiatives

### Partnership with OpenAI

The MPAAI announced a landmark partnership with OpenAI. Trinidad and Tobago became a member of "50 in 5," a global initiative aimed at sharing learnings, best practices, and technologies for digital public infrastructure.

### T&T Government Partners with OpenAI

The T&T Government partnered with OpenAI to revolutionize national education and public service delivery through AI integration.

### UAE MoU on Digital Transformation

Trinidad and Tobago signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the United Arab Emirates on digital transformation, reflecting international partnerships in AI and digital governance.

## AI as National Development Strategy

The policy framework positions AI as potentially analogous to Trinidad and Tobago's traditional oil and gas sector — a transformative resource for national economic development. The government has described AI as a potential "new oil and gas frontier" for the country's economic future.

## National AI Readiness Drive

The MPAAI launched a National AI Readiness Drive with global partners to:
- Assess current AI capabilities and gaps
- Develop stakeholder understanding of AI opportunities and risks
- Build national capacity for responsible AI adoption
- Position T&T as a Caribbean AI hub

## Policy Principles

The ministry's AI approach is characterized as:
- **Thoughtful**: Deliberate and measured adoption
- **Ethical**: Grounded in ethical principles and human rights
- **People-first**: Prioritizing citizen benefits and societal welfare

## Budget and Planning

According to the MPAAI 2026 Budget Guide, the ministry has established dedicated resources for AI policy implementation, including the AI readiness assessment process and national strategy development.
