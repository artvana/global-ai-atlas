# South Dakota Senate Bill 41 — Prohibition on AI-Generated Nonconsensual Intimate Images

**South Dakota 101st Legislative Session, 2026**
**Enacted:** March 17, 2026 | **Effective:** July 1, 2026

**Citation**
*South Dakota — March 17, 2026*

---

## Overview

South Dakota SB 41 makes it a felony offense to create, possess with intent to distribute, or share AI-generated deepfake pornographic depictions of non-consenting adults. The law imposes criminal penalties of up to 5 years imprisonment and a $10,000 fine. In addition to criminal penalties, victims may seek civil damages through a private right of action. The law covers AI-generated, digitally manipulated, and synthetically generated intimate images of identifiable persons.

---

## Key Provisions

### Criminal Offense — Nonconsensual AI Intimate Images
- It is a felony offense to:
  - **Create** AI-generated, deepfake, or digitally synthesized intimate images of an identifiable real person without that person's consent;
  - **Possess with intent to distribute** such content;
  - **Share, distribute, transmit, or publish** such content.
- The offense applies where the depicted person:
  - Is an identifiable real adult individual;
  - Did not consent to the creation or distribution of the intimate image;
  - Would not reasonably have expected their likeness to be depicted in this manner.

### Covered Content
- AI-generated sexually explicit images created by generative AI, text-to-image models, or other machine learning systems;
- Deepfake video or images superimposing an identifiable person's likeness onto explicit content;
- Digitally manipulated images altering authentic images to create explicit content without consent;
- Synthetically generated audio depicting sexual content involving an identifiable person's voice without consent.

### Criminal Penalties
- **Felony offense** with penalties of:
  - Up to **5 years imprisonment**;
  - Fine of up to **$10,000**;
  - Penalties apply per count (each act of creation, possession with intent, or distribution).

### Civil Cause of Action — Private Right of Action
- A victim depicted in nonconsensual AI intimate images has a **private right of action** against any person who created or distributed the content.
- Civil remedies include:
  - **Compensatory damages** for economic harm, reputational injury, and emotional distress;
  - **Injunctive relief** to compel removal of content and prevent further distribution;
  - **Punitive damages** where the conduct was willful or malicious;
  - **Attorney fees and costs** for prevailing plaintiffs.

### Scope and Application
- Applies to living, identifiable adult individuals.
- Covers AI-generated, machine-learning-produced, digitally synthesized, and digitally manipulated content.
- Does not require proof that a real intimate image of the victim was used as source material — purely synthetic depictions are covered.

---

## Enforcement

- Criminal enforcement: County state's attorneys and South Dakota Attorney General.
- Civil enforcement: Private right of action in South Dakota circuit court.
