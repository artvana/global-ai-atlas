# Artificial Intelligence in Employment (Public Act 103-0804)

Legislative Update – Thursday, January 1, 2026

[Email](mailto:?subject=I%20thought%20you%20might%20find%20this%20interesting&body=https://dhr.illinois.gov/about-us/legislative-updates/artificial-intelligence-in-employment.html "Share by Email")

{ "@context": "http://schema.org", "@type": "NewsArticle", "url": "", "datePublished": "Thursday, January 1, 2026", "headline": "", "mainEntityOfPage": "" }

This law establishes requirements for employers using artificial intelligence and automated decision-making systems in hiring and employment. Employers must provide transparency and ensure these systems don't result in discrimination against protected classes. \[Note: IDHR is currently developing rules to implement this law.
