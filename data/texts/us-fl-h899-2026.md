 HB 899 
 2026 
 
 
 A bill to be entitled 
 An act relating to the Task Force on Artificial Intelligence in Public Postsecondary Education; creating the Task Force on Artificial Intelligence in Public Postsecondary Education; requiring the task force to convene by a specified date; providing the purpose of the task force; providing for the membership of the task force; providing duties of the task force; requiring the task force to provide a report to the Governor and Legislature by a specified date; providing for the future termination of the task force; providing an effective date. 
 
 
 
 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida: 
 
 
 Section 1. 
 (1) The Task Force on Artificial Intelligence in Public Postsecondary Education, a task force as defined in s. 20.03(5), Florida Statutes, is created adjunct to the Department of Education. The task force shall convene no later than August 1, 2026, to review and make recommendations on the opportunities, risks, and best practices presented by artificial intelligence in academic instruction, research, and student conduct within the State University System and Florida College System. 
 
 
 (2) The task force shall consist of the following members: 
 
 
 (a) Faculty members appointed by the presidents of state universities and Florida College System institutions, representing a mix of disciplines including humanities, sciences, and professional programs. 
 
 
 (b) Representatives of the Board of Governors of the State University System. 
 
 
 (c) Representatives of the State Board of Education. 
 
 
 (d) At least one expert in artificial intelligence, computer science, or data ethics appointed by the Governor. 
 
 
 (e) One representative from the Southern Regional Education Board. 
 
 
 (f) One representative from the Division of Colleges within the Department of Education. 
 
 
 (g) One student representative, who must be the President of the Florida student association or the equivalent who is serving on the Board of Governors of the State University System. 
 
 
 (3) The task force shall: 
 
 
 (a) Examine the impact of artificial intelligence on academic integrity, including plagiarism, contract cheating, and the verification of student work. 
 
 
 (b) Review potential applications of artificial intelligence in teaching, learning support, advising, grading, accessibility, and research. 
 
 
 (c) Assess implications for privacy, intellectual property, and data security when artificial intelligence tools are used by faculty and students. 
 
 
 (d) Consider safeguards to protect academic freedom while maintaining clear expectations for student conduct. 
 
 
 (e) Identify professional development, resources, and training needs for faculty and staff to address the responsible use of artificial intelligence in the classroom. 
 
 
 (f) Review national and international best practices in higher education regarding artificial intelligence governance and adoption. 
 
 
 (g) Recommend model policies for institutions to adopt regarding the ethical and effective use of artificial intelligence. 
 
 
 (h) Solicit input from faculty senates, student governments, accrediting bodies, and industry representatives with expertise in artificial intelligence and workforce development. 
 
 
 (4) By December 1, 2026, the task force shall submit a report to the Governor, the President of the Senate, and the Speaker of the House of Representatives with its findings and recommendations, including model policies and any proposed legislation. The task force shall terminate upon submission of the report. 
 
 
 
 Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
