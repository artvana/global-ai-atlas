 STATE OF OKLAHOMA 
 1st Session of the 60th Legislature (2025) 
 HOUSE BILL 1917 
 By: Alonso-Sandoval 
 AS INTRODUCED 
 
 
 An Act relating to artificial intelligence; creating the Artificial Intelligence Act of 2025; providing for noncodification; and providing an effective date. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA: 
 
 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law not to be codified in the Oklahoma Statutes reads as follows: 
 This act shall be known and may be cited as the &quot;Artificial Intelligence Act of 2025&quot;. 
 SECTION 2. This act shall become effective November 1, 2025.
