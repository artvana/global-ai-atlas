Namibia National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2025
National Commission on Research, Science and Technology (NCRST)
NAMIBIA | 2025

Source: https://cipit.strathmore.edu/national-ai-strategies-for-ai-governance-highlights-from-namibias-2025-ai-strategy/

Non-binding national AI strategy launched by Namibia's National Commission on Research, Science and Technology (NCRST) in February 2025. The strategy represents Namibia's first comprehensive AI policy framework and aims to harness AI for national development while managing associated risks.

Key Elements (based on publicly reported information):

1. Vision
   The strategy envisions Namibia as an AI-ready nation that leverages artificial intelligence to achieve its Vision 2030 development goals, promote inclusive growth, and improve public service delivery.

2. Governance Structure
   The strategy establishes a multi-stakeholder AI governance architecture:
   - National AI Council: High-level inter-ministerial body providing strategic direction
   - AI Secretariat: Operational body housed within NCRST for day-to-day coordination
   - Sector-specific Technical Working Groups: Expert bodies providing guidance for specific sectors

3. Priority Sectors
   The strategy identifies priority sectors for early AI adoption:
   - Healthcare: AI-assisted diagnostics, patient records management, disease surveillance
   - Agriculture: Precision agriculture, crop monitoring, weather prediction
   - Education: Personalized learning, administrative efficiency
   - Public administration: E-government services, fraud detection
   - Mining: Safety monitoring, predictive maintenance

4. Ethical AI Principles
   The strategy adopts a set of core AI ethics principles aligned with international frameworks:
   - Human-centered and inclusive development
   - Transparency and explainability
   - Accountability and responsibility
   - Safety and security
   - Privacy protection
   - Fairness and non-discrimination

5. Infrastructure and Data
   The strategy addresses the need for improved digital infrastructure and data governance, including connectivity expansion, data center development, and national data management policies.

6. Human Capital
   Significant emphasis on building AI skills across the population, including:
   - AI curriculum integration in schools and universities
   - Training programs for government officials
   - Support for AI research and development

7. Legal and Regulatory Framework
   The strategy calls for review and updating of existing laws to address AI-specific issues, including data protection, liability, and sector-specific AI use cases.

8. International Cooperation
   Engagement with African Union AI initiatives, SADC regional cooperation, and bilateral partnerships for capacity building and technology transfer.
