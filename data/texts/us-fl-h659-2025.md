 HB 659: Florida Museum of Black History 

 
 GENERAL BILL
 by 
 
Michael ; (CO-INTRODUCERS) 
Daniels ; Sapp 
 
 Florida Museum of Black History; Establishes Florida Museum of Black History Board of Directors; provides for membership of board; requires that appointments to board be made by specified date; prohibits specified members of board from holding state or local elective office while serving on board; provides for filling of vacancies; requires that board work jointly with Foundation for Museum of Black History, Inc.; requires St. Johns County Board of County Commissioners to provide administrative support &amp; staffing to board until specified actions are completed.
 
 
 
 
 
 Effective Date: 7/1/2025 
 Last Action: 
 6/16/2025
 House -
 Died in Government Operations Subcommittee

 Identical bill 
 Companion bills that are identical word-for-word, not including titles. However, Resolutions and Concurrent Resolutions are considered identical if the only difference is the word "House" or "Senate."
 
 
 Similar bill 
 Companion bills that are substantially similar in text or have substantial portions of text that are largely the same.
 
 
 Compare bill 
 Bills that have selected provisions that are similar in text.
 
 
 Linked bill 
 A bill that is contingent upon passage of another bill within the same chamber, e.g., a trust fund bill, a bill providing a public record exemption, or an implementing bill.
