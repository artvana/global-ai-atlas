 HOUSE BILL 1248 
 
 By: Delegates Woorman, Acevero, Embry, Grammer, Guyton, Kaufman, Martinez, McComas, and Wims 
 
 Introduced and read first time: February 7, 2025 
 Assigned to: Judiciary 
 
 
 A BILL ENTITLED 
 AN ACT concerning 
 
 Revenge Pornography and Intimate Visual Depictions - Prohibition and Removal 
 
 FOR the purpose of requiring certain online platforms to establish a process for an individual, or an individual&#39;s representative, to provide certain notice of and request the disabling of access to or removal of certain visual depictions; requiring a certain online platform to take certain actions after receiving a notice and request under this Act and to establish certain data logging requirements; altering the prohibition against revenge pornography; and generally relating to revenge pornography and intimate visual depictions. 
 BY adding to 
 Article - Commercial Law 
 Section 13-321 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2013 Replacement Volume and 2024 Supplement) 
 BY repealing and reenacting, with amendments, 
 Article - Criminal Law 
 Section 3-809 
 Annotated Code of Maryland 
 (2021 Replacement Volume and 2024 Supplement) 
 
 
 
 SECTION 1. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF MARYLAND, That the Laws of Maryland read as follows: 
 
 
 Article - Commercial Law 
 
 
 13-321. 
 
 
 (A) (1) IN THIS SECTION THE FOLLOWING WORDS HAVE THE MEANINGS INDICATED. 
 
 
 (2) (I) &quot;COVERED PLATFORM&quot; MEANS A PUBLIC-FACING WEBSITE OR DIGITAL APPLICATION THAT: 
 
 
 1. PRIMARILY PROVIDES A FORUM FOR USERS OF THE PLATFORM TO ACCESS USER-GENERATED CONTENT, INCLUDING MESSAGES, VIDEOS, IMAGES, GAMES, AND AUDIO FILES; AND 
 
 
 2. HAS AT LEAST 100,000 UNIQUE MONTHLY ACTIVE USERS IN THE UNITED STATES. 
 
 
 (II) &quot;COVERED PLATFORM&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE: 
 
 
 1. A PROVIDER OF BROADBAND INTERNET ACCESS SERVICE; 
 
 
 2. A PROVIDER OF ELECTRONIC MAIL; OR 
 
 
 3. A PUBLIC-FACING WEBSITE OR DIGITAL APPLICATION THAT CONSISTS PRIMARILY OF CONTENT THAT IS NOT USER GENERATED. 
 
 
 (3) &quot;INTIMATE PARTS&quot; HAS THE MEANING STATED IN &sect; 3-809 OF THE CRIMINAL LAW ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (4) &quot;INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION&quot; MEANS A VISUAL REPRESENTATION OF AN IDENTIFIABLE PERSON, OR A COMPUTER-GENERATED VISUAL REPRESENTATION THAT APPEARS TO BE OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON, THAT DISPLAYS THE PERSON WITH INTIMATE PARTS EXPOSED OR WHILE ENGAGED IN SEXUAL ACTIVITY. 
 
 
 (5) &quot;SEXUAL ACTIVITY&quot; HAS THE MEANING STATED IN &sect; 3-809 OF THE CRIMINAL LAW ARTICLE. 
 
 
 (B) (1) ON OR BEFORE OCTOBER 1, 2026, A COVERED PLATFORM SHALL ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN A PROCESS FOR AN INDIVIDUAL, OR AN INDIVIDUAL&#39;S REPRESENTATIVE, TO: 
 
 
 (I) NOTIFY THE COVERED PLATFORM THAT THE COVERED PLATFORM IS ALLOWING ACCESS TO OR OTHERWISE DISPLAYING AN INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION OF THE INDIVIDUAL WITHOUT THE INDIVIDUAL&#39;S CONSENT; AND 
 
 
 (II) SUBMIT A REQUEST THAT THE COVERED PLATFORM: 
 
 
 1. DISABLE ACCESS TO OR REMOVE THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION; 
 
 
 2. IDENTIFY AND DISABLE ACCESS TO OR REMOVE ANY COPIES OF THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION; AND 
 
 
 3. PREVENT NEW COPIES OF THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION FROM BEING ACCESSED OR DISPLAYED BY THE COVERED PLATFORM. 
 
 
 (2) A NOTIFICATION AND REQUEST FOR REMOVAL OF AN INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION SUBMITTED USING THE PROCESS ESTABLISHED UNDER PARAGRAPH (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION SHALL INCLUDE, IN WRITING: 
 
 
 (I) A PHYSICAL OR ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE OF THE INDIVIDUAL OR THE INDIVIDUAL&#39;S REPRESENTATIVE; 
 
 
 (II) AN IDENTIFICATION OF THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION OF THE INDIVIDUAL; 
 
 
 (III) A BRIEF STATEMENT THAT THE INDIVIDUAL HAS A GOOD FAITH BELIEF THAT THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION HAS BEEN MADE AVAILABLE FOR ACCESS OR OTHERWISE DISPLAYED WITHOUT THE INDIVIDUAL&#39;S CONSENT; AND 
 
 
 (IV) ANY RELEVANT INFORMATION TO ASSIST THE COVERED PLATFORM TO DETERMINE WHETHER THE INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION HAS BEEN MADE AVAILABLE FOR ACCESS OR OTHERWISE DISPLAYED WITHOUT THE INDIVIDUAL&#39;S CONSENT. 
 
 
 (C) A COVERED PLATFORM SHALL PROVIDE A CLEAR AND CONSPICUOUS NOTICE OF THE PROCESS ESTABLISHED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION THAT IS ACCESSIBLE BY THE PUBLIC THROUGH THE INTERNET. 
 
 
 (D) ON RECEIPT OF A REQUEST MADE USING THE PROCESS ESTABLISHED UNDER SUBSECTION (B) OF THIS SECTION, A COVERED PLATFORM SHALL COMPLY WITH THE REQUEST AS SOON AS POSSIBLE, BUT NOT LATER THAN 48 HOURS AFTER RECEIVING THE REQUEST. 
 
 
 (E) A COVERED PLATFORM SHALL ESTABLISH MINIMUM DATA LOGGING REQUIREMENTS THAT: 
 
 
 (1) PRESERVE DATA NECESSARY FOR LEGAL PROCEEDINGS RELATED TO AN INTIMATE PRIVACY VIOLATION; AND 
 
 
 (2) ENSURE THAT DATA PRESERVED UNDER ITEM (1) OF THIS SUBSECTION IS NOT TRANSFERRED OR OTHERWISE USED FOR A PURPOSE OTHER THAN A LEGAL PROCEEDING RELATED TO AN INTIMATE PRIVACY VIOLATION. 
 
 
 (F) A COVERED PLATFORM MAY NOT BE HELD LIABLE FOR A CLAIM ARISING IN TORT OR CONTRACT BASED ON THE COVERED PLATFORM&#39;S DISABLING OF ACCESS TO OR REMOVING OF AN INTIMATE VISUAL DEPICTION IN COMPLIANCE WITH, OR A GOOD FAITH ATTEMPT TO COMPLY WITH, THIS SECTION. 
 
 
 Article - Criminal Law 
 
 3-809. 
 (a) (1) In this section the following words have the meanings indicated. 
 (2) (I) &quot;APPEARS TO BE OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON&quot; MEANS THAT AN ORDINARY PERSON WOULD CONCLUDE THAT THE VISUAL REPRESENTATION IS OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON. 
 
 
 (II) &quot;APPEARS TO BE OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON&quot; INCLUDES A COMPUTER-GENERATED VISUAL REPRESENTATION THAT HAS BEEN CREATED, ADAPTED, OR MODIFIED TO APPEAR AS AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON. 
 
 
 (III) &quot;APPEARS TO BE OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON&quot; DOES NOT INCLUDE VISUAL REPRESENTATIONS OR ITEMS DEPICTING A PERSON THAT ARE: 
 
 
 1. DRAWINGS; 
 
 
 2. CARTOONS; 
 
 
 3. SCULPTURES; OR 
 
 
 4. PAINTINGS. 
 
 
 (3) &quot;Distribute&quot; means to give, sell, transfer, disseminate, publish, upload, circulate, broadcast, make available, allow access to, or engage in any other form of transmission, electronic or otherwise. 
 
 (3) 
 (4) &quot;Harm&quot; means: 
 (i) physical injury; 
 (ii) serious emotional distress; or 
 (iii) economic damages. 
 
 (4) 
 (5) &quot;Intimate parts&quot; means the naked genitals, pubic area, buttocks, or female nipple. 
 
 (5) 
 (6) &quot;Sexual activity&quot; means: 
 (i) sexual intercourse, including genital-genital, oral-genital, anal-genital, or oral-anal; 
 (ii) masturbation; or 
 (iii) sadomasochistic abuse. 
 
 (7) &quot;VISUAL REPRESENTATION OF ANOTHER IDENTIFIABLE PERSON&quot; INCLUDES A COMPUTER-GENERATED VISUAL REPRESENTATION THAT APPEARS TO BE OF AN ACTUAL AND IDENTIFIABLE PERSON. 
 
 (b) (1) This section does not apply to: 
 (i) lawful and common practices of law enforcement, the reporting of unlawful conduct, or legal proceedings; or 
 (ii) situations involving voluntary exposure in public or commercial settings. 
 (2) An interactive computer service, as defined in 47 U.S.C. &sect; 230(f)(2), is not liable under this section for content provided by another person. 
 (c) A person may not knowingly distribute a visual representation of another identifiable person that displays the other person with his or her intimate parts exposed or while engaged in an act of sexual activity: 
 (1) with the intent to harm, harass, intimidate, threaten, or coerce the other person; 
 (2) (i) under circumstances in which the person knew that the other person did not consent to the distribution; or 
 (ii) with reckless disregard as to whether the person consented to the distribution; and 
 (3) IF THE VISUAL REPRESENTATION WAS CREATED OR GENERATED WITH PERMISSION FROM THE OTHER PERSON, under circumstances in which the other person KNEW OR REASONABLY SHOULD HAVE KNOWN THAT THE OTHER PERSON had a reasonable expectation that the image would remain private. 
 (d) A person who violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor and on conviction is subject to imprisonment not exceeding 2 years or a fine not exceeding $5,000 or both. 
 (e) A person who violates this section is subject to &sect; 5-106(b) of the Courts Article. 
 (f) A visual representation , INCLUDING A COMPUTER-GENERATED VISUAL REPRESENTATION, of a victim that is part of a court record for a case arising from a prosecution under this section: 
 (1) subject to item (2) of this subsection, may not be made available for public inspection; and 
 (2) except as otherwise ordered by the court, may only be made available for inspection in relation to a criminal charge under this section to: 
 (i) court personnel; 
 (ii) a jury in a criminal case brought under this section; 
 (iii) the State&#39;s Attorney or the State&#39;s Attorney&#39;s designee; 
 (iv) the Attorney General or the Attorney General&#39;s designee; 
 (v) a law enforcement officer; 
 (vi) the defendant or the defendant&#39;s attorney; or 
 (vii) the victim or the victim&#39;s attorney. 
 SECTION 2. AND BE IT FURTHER ENACTED, That this Act shall take effect October 1, 2025.
