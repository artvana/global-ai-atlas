AN ACT TO ADD A NEW CHAPTER 16 TO TITLE 5,
GUAM CODE ANNOTATED, RELATIVE TO
ESTABLISHING THE GUAM ARTIFICIAL
INTELLIGENCE (AI) REGULATORY TASK FORCE TO
DEVELOP A COMPREHENSIVE FRAMEWORK FOR
THE REGULATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
(AI) IN THE GOVERNMENT OF GUAM AND PRIVATE
SECTOR WHILE PROTECTING CONSTITUTIONAL
AND ORGANIC ACT RIGHTS.
1 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF GUAM:
2 Section 1. Legislative Finding and Intent. I Liheslaturan Guåhan finds
3 that artificial intelligence (AI) technologies are rapidly advancing and being
4 deployed across both public and private sectors. While these technologies offer
5 significant benefits for efficiency, innovation, and economic growth, they also
6 present novel challenges to individual rights, privacy, due process, and equal
1

1 protection under the law. Guam must proactively develop a regulatory framework
2 that balances technological advancement with the protection of fundamental rights
3 guaranteed by the U.S. Constitution and the Organic Act of Guam. A collaborative
4 approach involving key stakeholders from government sectors and academia is
5 essential to create effective and balanced regulations.

21 §16100. Establishment. There is hereby established the “Guam
22 Artificial Intelligence Regulatory Taskforce”.
23 §16101. Definitions.
24 a. “Artificial Intelligence” (AI) means the simulation of human intelligence
25 processes by machines, especially computer systems, including learning,
26 reasoning, and self-correction.
2

1 b. “AI Technologies” means systems and applications developed using AI,
2 including machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and
3 computer vision.
4 c. “Algorithmic Bias” means systematic and repeatable errors in a computer
5 system that create unfair outcomes, such as privileging one arbitrary group
6 of users over others.
7 d. “Stakeholders” mean individuals or groups with an interest in the regulation
8 and deployment of AI technologies, including members of the government,
9 private sector, and academia.

8 §16103. Purpose and Duties.
9 (a) The Task Force shall:
10 (1) Study and evaluate current and emerging AI technologies and
11 their potential impacts on Guam;
12 (2) Assess existing laws and regulations related to AI technology;
13 (3) Identify potential risks and benefits of AI deployment in
14 government and private sector operations;
15 (4) Develop recommendations for a comprehensive regulatory
16 framework that:
17 (i) Protects individual rights under the U.S. Constitution and
18 Organic Act of Guam;
19 (ii) Ensures transparency and accountability in AI systems;
20 (iii) Promotes responsible AI innovation and economic
21 growth;
22 (iv) Prevents discriminatory outcomes and algorithmic bias;
23 (v) Safeguards privacy and data security;
24 (vi) Establishes clear guidelines for AI use in government
25 decision-making;
26 (vii) Creates mechanisms for oversight and enforcement.
27 §16104. Organization and Operations.
4

1 (a) The Task Force shall elect a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson
2 from among its members.
3 (b) Six (6) members shall constitute a quorum.
4 (c) The Task Force shall meet bi-monthly.
5 (d) Members shall serve without compensation but may be
6 reimbursed for reasonable expenses.
7 (e) The Office of Technology shall provide administrative support.
8 §16105. Working Groups.
9 The Task Force may establish working groups to address specific aspects of
10 AI regulation, including but not limited to:
11 (a) Constitutional Rights and Civil Liberties;
12 (b) Government Use and Procurement;
13 (c) Privacy and Data Protection;
14 (d) Economic Development and Innovation;
15 (e) Education and Workforce Development.
16 §16106. Reports and Recommendations.
17 (a) The Task Force shall submit:
18 (1) progress reports every four (4) months to I Liheslaturan
19 Guåhan;

21 meeting;
22 (3) A final report and recommended regulatory framework within
23 eighteen (18) months.
24 (b) The final report shall include:
25 (1) Proposed legislation and regulations;
26 (2) Implementation strategies;
27 (3) Resource requirements;
28 (4) Timeline recommendations;
