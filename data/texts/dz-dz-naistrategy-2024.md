Algeria National Artificial Intelligence Strategy (Stratégie Nationale pour l'Intelligence Artificielle, 2024)
ALGERIA | 2024

Source: https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/29116-algerian-government-announces-adoption-of-national-strategy-for-artificial-intelligence

Non-binding national AI strategy adopted by Algeria's National AI Council (Conseil National de l'Intelligence Artificielle) on 8 December 2024, under the authority of the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Statistics. The strategy was developed in response to the Presidential decree establishing the National AI Council earlier in 2024.

Key elements (based on publicly reported information):

1. Strategic Pillars
   The strategy is organized around six main pillars:
   - Scientific research and innovation: Strengthening AI research capabilities at universities and research centers
   - AI-enabling environment: Developing legal, regulatory, and infrastructure foundations
   - Local capabilities: Building national human capital and expertise in AI
   - National AI fund: Establishing dedicated financing mechanisms for AI development
   - Data systems: Creating national data infrastructure and data governance frameworks
   - Investment attraction: Drawing domestic and foreign investment into the AI sector

2. Economic Targets
   The strategy aims for AI to contribute 7% of GDP by 2027, with ambitions to position Algeria as an AI hub for the African continent and the Arab world.

3. Priority Sectors
   The strategy identifies priority application domains including agriculture, healthcare, education, public administration, security, and energy.

4. Governance
   Implementation will be overseen by the National AI Council (Conseil National de l'Intelligence Artificielle), with coordination across relevant ministries. A National AI Observatory (Observatoire National de l'IA) will be established to monitor progress.

5. International Cooperation
   The strategy emphasizes partnerships with regional and international partners including African Union member states, Arab League countries, and European partners.
