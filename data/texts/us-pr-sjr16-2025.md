(S. J. R. 16)
JOINT RESOLUTION
To order the Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) to create and implement an artificial intelligence-based application for monitoring public auction processes in Puerto Rico; ensure transparency, fairness, and prevention of dishonest practices in such processes; and for other related purposes.
STATEMENT OF MOTIVES
Public auction processes are fundamental to the State's acquisition of goods and services. The integrity of these processes is key to ensuring that public resources are used in a fair, efficient, and transparent manner. Despite the foregoing, we have witnessed how these processes are not exempt from risks of corruption, manipulation, or dishonest practices that may affect their transparency and fairness.
Artificial intelligence (AI) offers advanced tools that can revolutionize the monitoring and management of public auctions. AI-based technologies can analyze large volumes of data in real time, identify suspicious patterns, and alert about possible irregularities, thereby strengthening preventive controls. Technology undoubtedly constitutes an additional tool that would help ensure that contracts are awarded fairly and in accordance with the law, protecting public interests and optimizing the use of State resources.
For these reasons, it is necessary that the Government of Puerto Rico, through PRITS, lead the creation and implementation of this application to modernize public auction processes, ensure their transparency, and prevent dishonest practices.
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:
    Section 1.- The Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) is hereby ordered to develop and implement an artificial intelligence-based application for monitoring public auction processes in Puerto Rico. In addition, it shall be enabled for use by those municipal governments that choose to integrate it into their auction processes. The application shall include the following functions:
    a) Real-time monitoring of auction processes.
    b) Data analysis to identify suspicious patterns or irregular activities.
    c) Generation of automatic alerts upon possible irregularities.
    d) Reports accessible to system administrators and oversight entities.
    Section 2.- PRITS shall present a detailed development and implementation plan for the application to the Legislative Assembly within ninety (90) days following the approval of this Joint Resolution. This plan shall include:
    a) Work timeline.
    b) Resources necessary for its implementation.
    c) Strategies for the integration of the system with existing public auction platforms.
    Section 3.- PRITS shall work in coordination with the Office of the Inspector General, the General Services Administration, the Office of Government Ethics, and any other pertinent agency to ensure that the application complies with the necessary legal and transparency requirements.
    Section 4.- PRITS shall submit quarterly reports to the Legislative Assembly on the progress of the development and implementation of the application during the first year of effectiveness of this Joint Resolution.
    Section 5.- Effectiveness.
This Joint Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
