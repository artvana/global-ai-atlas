# Cybersecurity and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07), 2021 — Zimbabwe
**Chapter 12:07 of 2021**
*Zimbabwe — December 13, 2021 (effective January 1, 2022)*

---

## Overview

Zimbabwe's Cybersecurity and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07) is a landmark combined statute that addresses data protection, cybersecurity, and cybercrime in a single legislative instrument — a distinctive approach among African jurisdictions. Enacted in December 2021 and effective from January 1, 2022, the Act establishes POTRAZ (Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe) as the data protection supervisory authority and creates the Cybersecurity Centre for operational cybersecurity functions. The Act positions Zimbabwe within the broader African Union Data Policy Framework and aligns with the Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection.

---

## Key Provisions

### Part I — Data Protection Regime

**Eight Data Protection Principles**
- Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization and proportionality
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability of the data controller
- Cross-border transfer restrictions

**Data Subject Rights**
- Right to be informed of data collection and processing
- Right of access to personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to object to processing
- Right to erasure in certain circumstances
- Breach notification obligations to data subjects where risks are significant

**Sensitive Personal Data**
- Special categories: health, genetic, biometric, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade union membership, criminal record
- Processing of sensitive data requires explicit consent or specific statutory authority

**POTRAZ as Supervisory Authority**
- Registers data controllers
- Investigates complaints and data breaches
- Issues compliance notices and enforcement orders
- Cooperates with regional and international data protection authorities

### Part II — Cybersecurity

**Critical Infrastructure Protection**
- Designates critical national information infrastructure sectors
- Mandates security standards for operators of critical infrastructure
- Establishes incident reporting obligations

**Cybersecurity Centre**
- National Computer Incident Response Team (CIRT) functions
- Coordinates national cybersecurity response
- Issues cybersecurity guidelines and advisories

### Part III — Cybercrime Offenses
- Unauthorized access to computer systems
- Unlawful interception of data
- System interference and sabotage
- Computer fraud and forgery
- Child online protection offenses
- Cyberbullying and electronic harassment

## Enforcement & Penalties

- POTRAZ may issue compliance and enforcement notices for data protection violations
- Administrative fines for data protection breaches
- Criminal penalties: fines up to USD 15,000 and/or up to 10 years imprisonment for serious offenses including unlawful data processing and cybercrime offenses
- Cybercrime offenses carry varying penalties depending on severity, with aggravated offenses subject to higher penalties
- Civil liability: data subjects may seek compensation through the courts for damages caused by unlawful processing
