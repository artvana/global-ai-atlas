 AN ACT PROHIBITING THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO
 REPLACE TEACHERS.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to prohibit the use of artificial
2      intelligence to replace public school educators in providing instruction
3      to and regular interaction with students.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To prohibit the use of artificial intelligence to replace public school
       educators in providing instruction to and regular interaction with
       students.
