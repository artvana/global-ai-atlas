 Florida Senate - 2026 
 
 
 SB 1194 
 
 
 By Senator Jones 
 
 
 A bill to be entitled 
 An act relating to artificial intelligence in education; amending s. 1001.03, F.S.; requiring the State Board of Education to adopt statewide standards and policies for the use of artificial intelligence (AI); providing requirements for the policies; requiring the board to direct the Department of Education to collect specified metrics; requiring the board to direct the department to provide specified teacher training; requiring the board to direct the department to review school district compliance with AI policies; amending s. 1003.42, F.S.; requiring students in specified grade levels to receive instruction on digital literacy and the ethics of using AI in certain contexts; amending s. 1006.07, F.S.; requiring that student codes of conduct include AI policies; providing an effective date. 
 
 
 
 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Florida: 
 
 Section 1. Subsection (20) is added to section 1001.03, Florida Statutes, to read: 
 1001.03 Specific powers of State Board of Education. -- 
 
 (20) ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE POLICIES. --The State Board of Education shall adopt statewide standards and a policy for school use and student use of artificial intelligence (AI). 
 
 
 (a) The policies must: 
 
 
 1. Allow student use of AI only when a teacher has expressly permitted its use in certain applications. 
 
 
 2. Require a student to disclose how AI was used in his or her work. 
 
 
 3. Require AI-monitoring safeguards and proctored assessments for the Florida Virtual School. 
 
 
 (b) The board shall direct the Department of Education to: 
 
 
 1. Collect metrics on: 
 
 
 a. Student use of AI in academics. 
 
 
 b. Teacher-reported instances of academic dishonesty. 
 
 
 c. Performance data for statewide assessments in reading, writing, and mathematics. 
 
 
 d. Graduation readiness metrics from the Florida Virtual School. 
 
 
 2. Provide training for teachers on the use of AI, AI detection tools, and strategies for responsible use of AI. 
 
 
 3. Review school district compliance with AI policies and standards.

 Section 3. Paragraph (p) is added to subsection (2) of section 1006.07, Florida Statutes, to read: 
 1006.07 District school board duties relating to student discipline and school safety. --The district school board shall provide for the proper accounting for all students, for the attendance and control of students at school, and for proper attention to health, safety, and other matters relating to the welfare of students, including: 
 (2) CODE OF STUDENT CONDUCT. --Adopt a code of student conduct for elementary schools and a code of student conduct for middle and high schools and distribute the appropriate code to all teachers, school personnel, students, and parents, at the beginning of every school year. Each code shall include, but is not limited to:

 Section 4. This act shall take effect July 1, 2026.
