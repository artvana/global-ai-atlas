# Colombia Law 2502 of 2025 — AI-Facilitated Identity Fraud Criminal Aggravator

**Republic of Colombia | Enacted August 2025**

---

## Overview

Law 2502 of 2025 amends Colombia's Criminal Code (Law 599 of 2000) to establish artificial intelligence as an aggravating circumstance in specified criminal offenses, particularly identity fraud, electronic fraud, extortion, and sexual crimes involving deepfakes. The law increases criminal penalties when AI systems, generative AI tools, or automated digital processing are used to commit or facilitate the underlying offense.

---

## Amendments to the Criminal Code (Law 599 of 2000)

### Article 1 — General Aggravating Circumstance (Amendment to Article 58)

Article 58 of the Criminal Code, which lists general aggravating circumstances for criminal sentencing, is amended to add:

*"(18) The use of artificial intelligence systems, automated decision-making tools, generative AI models, machine learning algorithms, or similar technologies to plan, execute, facilitate, or conceal the criminal conduct."*

This aggravating circumstance increases the applicable imprisonment range by one-quarter to one-third of the base penalty.

### Article 2 — Identity Fraud and Electronic Document Falsification (Amendment to Articles 290–291)

The crimes of falsification of documents (Article 290) and use of falsified documents (Article 291) are aggravated when the falsification employs AI-generated imagery, voice synthesis, or biometric replication tools to create fraudulent identity documents, synthetic voice recordings, or digital likenesses.

**Additional penalty:** When AI tools are used to commit identity fraud:
- The imprisonment range increases by one-third
- The monetary fine increases by one-half
- The defendant is prohibited from holding public office for a period equal to twice the imprisonment sentence

### Article 3 — Non-Consensual Synthetic Intimate Imagery (New Article 218A)

A new criminal offense is created:

**Article 218A — Non-Consensual Dissemination of AI-Generated Intimate Imagery**

*"Any person who, without the consent of the depicted individual, creates using artificial intelligence, distributes, publishes, or enables access to synthetic sexually explicit images, videos, or audio recordings depicting an identifiable real person shall be punished with imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years and a monetary fine."*

**Aggravating circumstances for this offense include:**
- When the victim is a minor (imprisonment increases to 4–8 years)
- When the conduct causes severe psychological harm to the victim
- When the content is distributed commercially or for economic gain
- When the perpetrator uses the threat of distribution for extortion

### Article 4 — Electronic Fraud via AI-Generated Impersonation (Amendment to Article 269I)

The crime of electronic fraud (Article 269I) is specifically aggravated when AI-generated voice cloning, facial synthesis (deepfakes), or automated impersonation tools are used to deceive victims into financial transfers or disclosure of credentials.

**Enhanced penalties:**
- Imprisonment: 48 to 108 months (up from 36 to 72 months)
- Fine: 200 to 1500 minimum wages (up from 100 to 1000)

### Article 5 — Platform Liability

Digital platforms operating in Colombia that, upon actual notice, fail to remove non-consensual AI-generated intimate imagery within 48 hours may be subject to:
- Administrative fines by the Superintendent of Industry and Commerce (SIC) of up to 2,000 minimum wages per day of delay
- Suspension of the platform's authorization to operate in Colombia in cases of repeated violations

### Article 6 — Aggravated Extortion via AI Deepfakes (Amendment to Article 244)

Extortion committed by threatening to create or distribute AI-generated intimate or defamatory imagery is punished with imprisonment of 8 to 16 years (compared to the base range of 6 to 14 years).

---

## Implementation

The law entered into force 30 days after official publication in the Diario Oficial. The National Police and Fiscalía General de la Nación were directed to develop operational protocols for investigating AI-facilitated crimes within 90 days of enactment.

---

## Context

Law 2502 of 2025 was adopted in response to a significant increase in AI-facilitated fraud and deepfake-based extortion reported by Colombia's Fiscalía. It complements earlier Colombian legislation on data protection (Law 1581 of 2012) and cybercrime (Law 1273 of 2009) by addressing specifically the criminal dimensions of AI misuse.
