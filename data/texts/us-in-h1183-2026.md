 DIGEST OF INTRODUCED BILL 
 Citations Affected: IC 35-31.5-2; IC 35-45-4. 
 Synopsis: Digital sexual image abuse. Increases the penalty for the crime of distribution of an intimate image, from a Class A misdemeanor to a Level 6 felony, and increases the enhancement of this crime from a Level 6 felony to a Level 5 felony. Defines &quot;digital sexual image&quot; as a digital, computer generated image or video that is created or modified to depict: (1) sexual intercourse; (2) other sexual conduct; or (3) exhibition of certain body parts; of a quality, characteristic, or condition, such that it appears to depict an individual. Makes creation or possession of a digital sexual image a Class A misdemeanor and distribution of a digital sexual image a Level 6 felony. 
 Effective: July 1, 2026.

 (e) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-48, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-48-1.1-13. 
 (f) &quot;Distribute&quot;, for purposes of IC 35-49, has the meaning set forth in IC 35-49-1-2. 
 SECTION 3. IC 35-45-4-8, AS AMENDED BY P.L.79-2024, SECTION 4, IS AMENDED TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 8. (a) This section does not apply to: 
 (1) a photograph, digital image, or video that is distributed: 
 (A) to report a possible criminal act; 
 (B) in connection with a criminal investigation; 
 (C) under a court order; 
 (D) to a location that is: 
 (i) intended solely for the storage or backup of personal data, including photographs, digital images, and video; and 
 (ii) password protected; or 
 (E) by a news reporting or an entertainment medium (as defined in IC 32-36-1-4); 
 (2) a newspaper or news service that publishes news related information through a website; 
 (3) a cloud service provider; or 
 (4) an Internet provider, an affiliate or subsidiary of an Internet provider, or a search engine that: 
 (A) solely provides access or connection to a website or other Internet content that is not under the control of that Internet service provider, affiliate or subsidiary, or search engine; and 
 (B) is not responsible for creating or publishing the content that constitutes material harmful to minors. 
 (b) As used in this section, &quot;distribute&quot; means to transfer to another person in, or by means of, any medium, forum, telecommunications device or network, or website, including posting an image on a website or application. 
 (c) As used in this section, &quot;intimate image&quot; means a photograph, digital image, computer generated image, or video: 
 (1) that depicts: 
 (A) sexual intercourse; 
 (B) other sexual conduct (as defined in IC 35-31.5-2-221.5); or 
 (C) exhibition of the uncovered buttocks, genitals, or female breast; 
 of an individual; 
 (2) taken, captured, created, disseminated, or recorded by: 
 (A) an individual depicted in the photograph, digital image, or video and given or transmitted directly to the person described in subsection (d); 
 (B) the person described in subsection (d) in the physical presence of an individual depicted in the photograph, digital image, or video; or 
 (C) the person described in subsection (d) with respect to a photograph, digital image, computer generated image, or video of an individual created or modified by means of a computer software program, artificial intelligence, application, or other digital editing tools; and 
 (3) that is of a quality, characteristic, or condition such that it appears to depict the alleged victim. 
 (d) A person who: 
 (1) knows or reasonably should know that an individual depicted in an intimate image does not consent to the distribution of the intimate image; and 
 (2) distributes the intimate image; 
 commits distribution of an intimate image, a Class A misdemeanor 
 Level 6 felony. However, the offense is a Level 6 
 5 felony if the person has a prior unrelated conviction under this section. 
 (e) It is not a necessary element of the offense described in subsection (d) that the individual depicted in the intimate image actually sent the image. 
 SECTION 4. IC 35-45-4-9 IS ADDED TO THE INDIANA CODE AS A NEW SECTION TO READ AS FOLLOWS [EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026]: Sec. 9. (a) This section does not apply to: 
 
 
 (1) a digital image or video that is distributed: 
 
 
 (A) to report a possible criminal act; 
 
 
 (B) in connection with a criminal investigation; 
 
 
 (C) under a court order; or 
 
 
 (D) by a news reporting or an entertainment medium (as defined in IC 32-36-1-4); 
 
 
 (2) a newspaper or news service that publishes news related information through a website; 
 
 
 (3) a cloud service provider; or 
 
 
 (4) an Internet provider, an affiliate or subsidiary of an Internet provider, or a search engine that: 
 
 
 (A) solely provides access or connection to a website or other Internet content that is not under the control of that Internet service provider, affiliate or subsidiary, or search engine; and 
 
 
 (B) is not responsible for creating or publishing the content that constitutes material harmful to minors. 
 
 
 (b) As used in this section, &quot;digital sexual image&quot; means a digital, computer generated image or video that is created or modified by means of artificial intelligence, a computer software program, an application, or other editing tools that depicts: 
 
 
 (1) sexual intercourse; 
 
 
 (2) other sexual conduct (as defined in IC 35-31.5-2-221.5); or 
 
 
 (3) exhibition of the uncovered buttocks, genitals, or female breast; 
 
 
 of a quality, characteristic, or condition such that it appears to depict an individual. 
 
 
 (c) As used in this section, &quot;distribute&quot; has the meaning set forth in section 8(b) of this chapter. 
 
 
 (d) A person who: 
 
 
 (1) creates; or 
 
 
 (2) possesses; 
 
 
 a digital sexual image without the consent of the individual depicted in the digital sexual image commits digital sexual image abuse, a Class A misdemeanor. However, the offense is a Level 6 felony if the person has a prior unrelated conviction under this subsection. 
 
 
 (e) A person who distributes a digital sexual image without the consent of the individual depicted in the digital sexual image commits distribution of a digital sexual image, a Level 6 felony. 
 
 
 However, the offense is a Level 5 felony if the person has a prior unrelated conviction under this subsection.
