 HOUSE BILL NO. 4536 
 
 
 June 03, 2025, Introduced by Reps. Rheingans, Coffia, Price, Byrnes, Morgan, Wegela, Tsernoglou, Breen, Young, Wilson and MacDonell and referred to Committee on Communications and Technology. 
 
 
 
 A bill to amend 1956 PA 218, entitled 
 &quot;The insurance code of 1956,&quot; 
 (MCL 500.100 to 500.8302) by adding section 3406ss. 
 
 
 
 
 THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT: 
 
 
 
 Sec. 3406ss. An insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, or renews in this state a health insurance policy shall not deny, modify, or delay a claim based on a review using artificial intelligence.
