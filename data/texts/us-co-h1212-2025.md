 A BILL FOR AN ACT 
 CONCERNING PUBLIC SAFETY PROTECTION FROM THE RISKS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS. 
 
 
 Bill Summary 
 The bill prohibits a developer that has trained a foundation artificial intelligence model (foundation model) from preventing a worker from, or retaliating against a worker for, disclosing or threatening to disclose information to the developer, the attorney general, or appropriate state or federal authorities if the worker has reasonable cause to believe the information indicates one of the following: 
 ! The developer is out of compliance with law applicable to public safety or security; 
 ! The developer&#39;s activities pose a substantial risk to public safety or security, even if the developer is not out of compliance with any law; or 
 ! The developer has made false or misleading statements concerning public safety or security or concerning the developer&#39;s management of risks to public safety or security. 
 A developer must provide notice to all workers working on a foundation model of the workers&#39; rights and responsibilities under the bill. 
 The bill requires a developer to create and provide an internal process through which a worker may anonymously disclose information to the developer regarding a risk to public safety or security enabled by the developer&#39;s foundation model. The developer shall provide a monthly update to the worker who made the disclosure regarding the status of the developer&#39;s investigation of the disclosure and the actions taken by the developer in response to the disclosure. 
 An aggrieved worker may commence a civil action in district court against a developer for a violation of the bill. A court may order relief against a developer that is found to have violated the bill, including: 
 ! Reinstatement or rehiring of a worker, with or without back pay; 
 ! The greater of either $10,000 or any lost pay resulting from the violation; 
 ! Punitive damages; and 
 ! Reasonable attorney fees to an aggrieved worker. 
 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Colorado: 
 
 
 
 SECTION 1. In Colorado Revised Statutes, add article 13.6 to title 8 as follows: 
 
 
 ARTICLE 13.6 
 
 
 
 
 Whistleblower Protections for Workers Working in Artificial Intelligence 
 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-101. Definitions. AS USED IN THIS ARTICLE 13.6, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES: 
 
 
 (1) &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE&quot; HAS THE SAME MEANING AS &quot;ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM&quot; AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 6-1-1701(2). 
 
 
 (2) &quot;FOUNDATION MODEL&quot; MEANS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MODEL THAT: 
 
 
 (a) IS TRAINED ON BROAD DATA; AND 
 
 
 (b) IS APPLICABLE ACROSS A WIDE RANGE OF CONTEXTS. 
 
 
 (3) &quot;LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER&quot; MEANS AN ENTITY THAT: 
 
 
 (a) IS AN EMPLOYER AS DEFINED IN SECTION 8-4-101 (6) OR AN ENTITY THAT CONTRACTS WITH FIVE OR MORE INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS IN THE STATE EACH YEAR; 
 
 
 (b) HAS, WITHIN THE PRECEDING FIVE YEARS, TRAINED A FOUNDATION MODEL AT A COMPUTATIONAL COST OF AT LEAST TWENTY MILLION DOLLARS AS MEASURED BY THE AVERAGE COST OF AN EQUIVALENT AMOUNT OF CLOUD COMPUTING AT THE TIME THAT TRAINING COMMENCES; AND 
 
 
 (c) HAS, WITHIN ANY TWELVE-MONTH PERIOD DURING THE PRECEDING FIVE YEARS, TRAINED ONE OR MORE FOUNDATION MODELS AT A TOTAL COMPUTATIONAL COST OF AT LEAST ONE HUNDRED MILLION DOLLARS AS MEASURED BY THE AVERAGE COST OF AN EQUIVALENT AMOUNT OF CLOUD COMPUTING AT THE TIME THAT TRAINING COMMENCES. 
 
 
 (4) &quot;RISK TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY&quot; MEANS A RISK THAT A FOUNDATION MODEL COULD CAUSE OR MATERIALLY ENABLE DEATH, BODILY INJURY, OR DAMAGE TO PROPERTY. 
 
 
 (5) (a) &quot;WORKER&quot; MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO PERFORMS SERVICES FOR AND UNDER THE CONTROL AND DIRECTION OF A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER FOR WAGES OR OTHER REMUNERATION. 
 
 
 (b) &quot;WORKER&quot; INCLUDES: 
 
 
 (I) AN EMPLOYEE AS DEFINED IN SECTION 8-4-101 (5); 
 
 
 (II) AN INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR; OR 
 
 
 (III) A CORPORATE OFFICER. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-102. Prohibition against retaliatory action related to a substantial risk to public safety or security - limitation on worker contracts. (1) A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL NOT PREVENT A WORKER FROM, OR RETALIATE AGAINST A WORKER FOR, DISCLOSING OR THREATENING TO DISCLOSE INFORMATION TO THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL, OR APPROPRIATE STATE OR FEDERAL AUTHORITIES, INCLUDING THROUGH TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT OR SEEKING TO ENFORCE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT, IF THE WORKER HAS REASONABLE CAUSE TO BELIEVE THE INFORMATION INDICATES ONE OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 (a) THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER IS OUT OF COMPLIANCE WITH LAW APPLICABLE TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY; 
 
 
 (b) THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER&#39;S ACTIVITIES POSE A SUBSTANTIAL RISK TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY, EVEN IF THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER IS NOT OUT OF COMPLIANCE WITH ANY LAW; OR 
 
 
 (c) THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER HAS MADE FALSE OR MISLEADING STATEMENTS CONCERNING PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY OR THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER&#39;S MANAGEMENT OF RISKS TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY. 
 
 
 (2) THIS SECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO A WORKER WHO DISCLOSES INFORMATION: 
 
 
 (a) THAT THE WORKER KNOWS TO BE FALSE; OR 
 
 
 (b) WITH RECKLESS DISREGARD FOR THE TRUTH OR FALSITY OF THE INFORMATION. 
 
 
 (3) (a) A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL NOT REQUIRE OR ATTEMPT TO REQUIRE A WORKER TO SIGN A CONTRACT OR OTHER AGREEMENT THAT WOULD LIMIT OR PREVENT THE WORKER FROM DISCLOSING INFORMATION ABOUT RISKS TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY OR TO OTHERWISE ABIDE BY A WORKPLACE POLICY THAT WOULD LIMIT OR PREVENT SUCH DISCLOSURES. 
 
 
 (b) A CONTRACT OR AGREEMENT THAT VIOLATES SUBSECTION (3)(a) OF THIS SECTION IS VOID AND UNENFORCEABLE AS CONTRARY TO PUBLIC POLICY. A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER&#39;S ATTEMPT TO IMPOSE SUCH A CONTRACT OR AGREEMENT IS AN ADVERSE ACTION IN VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE 13.6. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-103. Written notice required. (1) A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL PROVIDE A CLEAR NOTICE TO ALL WORKERS WORKING ON A FOUNDATION MODEL OF THE WORKERS&#39; RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES UNDER THIS ARTICLE 13.6. A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER COMPLIES WITH THIS SECTION IF THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER DOES EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING: 
 
 
 (a) (I) AT ALL TIMES, DISPLAYS WITHIN ALL WORKPLACES MAINTAINED BY THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER A NOTICE TO ALL WORKERS OF THEIR RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES UNDER THIS ARTICLE 13.6; 
 
 
 (II) ENSURES THAT ALL NEW WORKERS RECEIVE THE NOTICE DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION (1)(a)(I) OF THIS SECTION; AND 
 
 
 (III) ENSURES THAT WORKERS WHO PERIODICALLY WORK REMOTELY RECEIVE THE NOTICE DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION (1)(a)(I) OF THIS SECTION; OR 
 
 
 (b) AT LEAST ONCE EVERY CALENDAR YEAR, PROVIDES WRITTEN NOTICE TO ALL WORKERS OF THEIR RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES UNDER THIS ARTICLE 13.6 AND ENSURES THAT THE NOTICE IS RECEIVED AND ACKNOWLEDGED BY ALL WORKERS. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-104. Internal reporting process. (1) (a) A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL PROVIDE A REASONABLE INTERNAL PROCESS THROUGH WHICH A WORKER MAY ANONYMOUSLY DISCLOSE INFORMATION TO THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER IF THE WORKER BELIEVES IN GOOD FAITH THAT THE INFORMATION INDICATES THE CONDITIONS DESCRIBED IN SECTION 8-13.6-102 (1). 
 
 
 (b) THE INTERNAL REPORTING PROCESS MUST INCLUDE A MONTHLY UPDATE TO THE WORKER WHO MADE THE DISCLOSURE REGARDING THE STATUS OF THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER&#39;S INVESTIGATION OF THE DISCLOSURE AND THE ACTIONS TAKEN BY THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER IN RESPONSE TO THE DISCLOSURE. THE MONTHLY UPDATES MUST CONTINUE UNTIL THE MATTER IS RESOLVED, AT WHICH TIME THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL PROVIDE A FINAL UPDATE TO THE WORKER. 
 
 
 (2) (a) THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL MAINTAIN A DISCLOSURE OR RESPONSE OF THE INTERNAL REPORTING PROCESS FOR A MINIMUM OF SEVEN YEARS AFTER THE DATE WHEN THE RESPONSE IS CREATED. 
 
 
 (b) AT LEAST ONCE PER QUARTER, THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER SHALL SHARE EACH DISCLOSURE AND RESPONSE WITH THE OFFICERS AND DIRECTORS OF THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER WHO DO NOT HAVE A CONFLICT OF INTEREST. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-105. Relief authorized. (1) AN AGGRIEVED WORKER MAY COMMENCE A CIVIL ACTION IN DISTRICT COURT AGAINST A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER FOR A VIOLATION OF THIS ARTICLE 13.6. 
 
 
 (2) IN A CIVIL ACTION COMMENCED BY AN AGGRIEVED WORKER UNDER THIS SECTION, THE COURT MAY ORDER AFFIRMATIVE RELIEF TO THE AGGRIEVED WORKER AGAINST A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER THAT IS FOUND TO HAVE VIOLATED THIS ARTICLE 13.6, INCLUDING: 
 
 
 (a) REINSTATEMENT OR REHIRING OF THE WORKER, WITH OR WITHOUT BACK PAY; 
 
 
 (b) THE GREATER OF EITHER: 
 
 
 (I) TEN THOUSAND DOLLARS; OR 
 
 
 (II) ANY LOST PAY RESULTING FROM THE VIOLATION, INCLUDING BACK PAY IF THE AGGRIEVED WORKER IS REINSTATED OR REHIRED AND FRONT PAY IF THE AGGRIEVED WORKER IS NOT REINSTATED OR REHIRED; AND 
 
 
 (c) ANY OTHER EQUITABLE RELIEF THE COURT DEEMS APPROPRIATE. 
 
 
 (3) (a) THE COURT MAY AWARD AN AGGRIEVED WORKER PUNITIVE DAMAGES AGAINST A LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER IF THE AGGRIEVED WORKER DEMONSTRATES BY CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER ENGAGED IN A DISCRIMINATORY, ADVERSE, OR RETALIATORY EMPLOYMENT PRACTICE WITH MALICE OR RECKLESS INDIFFERENCE TO THE RIGHTS OF THE AGGRIEVED WORKER. 
 
 
 (b) IN DETERMINING THE APPROPRIATE LEVEL OF DAMAGES TO AWARD AN AGGRIEVED WORKER, THE COURT SHALL CONSIDER THE GRAVITY OF THE RISK TO PUBLIC SAFETY OR SECURITY, THE SIZE AND ASSETS OF THE LARGE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE DEVELOPER, AND THE EGREGIOUSNESS OF THE DISCRIMINATORY, ADVERSE, OR RETALIATORY EMPLOYMENT PRACTICE. 
 
 
 (4) IF AN AGGRIEVED WORKER SEEKS PUNITIVE DAMAGES PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION (3) OF THIS SECTION, ANY PARTY TO THE CIVIL ACTION MAY DEMAND A TRIAL BY JURY. 
 
 
 (5) THE COURT SHALL AWARD REASONABLE ATTORNEY FEES TO AN AGGRIEVED WORKER IF THE AGGRIEVED WORKER PREVAILS IN AN ACTION BROUGHT PURSUANT TO THIS ARTICLE 13.6. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-106. No effect on other law. THIS ARTICLE 13.6 DOES NOT LIMIT PROTECTIONS PROVIDED TO WORKERS BY ANY OTHER LAW. 
 
 
 
 8-13.6-107. Trade secrets. THIS ARTICLE 13.6 DOES NOT PERMIT A WORKER TO DISCLOSE A TRADE SECRET, AS DEFINED IN 18 U.S.C. SEC. (3), WHEN SUCH DISCLOSURE IS PROHIBITED BY 18 U.S.C. SECS. 1831 TO 1839. 
 
 
 SECTION 2. Act subject to petition - effective date - applicability. (1) This act takes effect at 12:01 a.m. on the day following the expiration of the ninety-day period after final adjournment of the general assembly; except that, if a referendum petition is filed pursuant to section 1 (3) of article V of the state constitution against this act or an item, section, or part of this act within such period, then the act, item, section, or part will not take effect unless approved by the people at the general election to be held in November 2026 and, in such case, will take effect on the date of the official declaration of the vote thereon by the governor. 
 (2) This act applies to conduct occurring on or after the applicable effective date of this act.
