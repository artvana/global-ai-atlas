 AN ACT CREATING A TASK FORCE TO STUDY ARTIFICIAL
 INTELLIGENCE AND THE STATE WORKFORCE.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to establish a task force to study
2      the effect and fiscal impact of increasing the use of artificial intelligence
3      by the state and the automation of the size and scope of the state
4      workforce.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To study the practicality and use of increased artificial intelligence by
       the state and automation of the size and scope of the state workforce.
