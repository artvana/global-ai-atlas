 SENATE No. 48 
 The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 In the One Hundred and Ninety-Fourth General Court 
 (2025-2026) 
 
 
 AN ACT RELATIVE TO AI-GENERATED CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL. 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows: 
 
 SECTION 1. Section 29C of chapter 272 of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting after the words &quot;visual reproduction&quot; the following words:- &quot;, including visual material produced by digitization&quot;. 
 SECTION 2. Section 31 of said chapter of the General Laws is hereby amended by inserting before the definition of &ldquo;dissemination&rdquo; the following definition:- 
 &ldquo;Digitization&rdquo;, the creation or alteration of visual material including, but not limited to, through the use of computer-generated images, in a manner that would appear to a reasonable person to depict a minor. 
 SECTION 3. Said section of said chapter is hereby further amended by inserting, at the end of the definition of &quot;visual material&quot;, the following sentence:- The term &quot;visual material&quot; shall include visual material produced by digitization.
