# Security Management Measures for the Application of Facial Recognition Technology

**Promulgating Entities:** Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC); Ministry of Public Security of the People's Republic of China  
**Promulgation Date:** 2024-08-08  
**Effective Date:** 2024-09-01  
**Document Number:** Decree No. 12 of the Cyberspace Administration of China  

---

## Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1:** These Measures are formulated on the basis of the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and other relevant laws and administrative regulations, in order to regulate the application of facial recognition technology, protect the lawful rights and interests of individuals' personal information and other rights and interests, and safeguard national security and social public order.

**Article 2:** These Measures apply to the use of facial recognition technology within the territory of the People's Republic of China to process facial information for the purpose of identifying natural persons (hereinafter "facial recognition application activities"). Where laws and administrative regulations have other provisions, those provisions shall prevail.

**Article 3:** The handling of facial information in facial recognition application activities shall comply with the principles of legality, propriety, necessity, and integrity, and shall follow the purpose limitation principle. Facial information shall not be processed for purposes that are incompatible with the original collection purpose, and the processing of facial information shall be minimized in scope.

**Article 4:** The state encourages the research, development, and application of facial recognition technology on the premise of safety and reliability, and promotes the innovative development of facial recognition technology in lawful and orderly fields such as public security, transportation, and financial services.

**Article 5:** The Cyberspace Administration of China is responsible for the overall coordination of the supervision and administration work for facial recognition application activities. The relevant competent authorities under the State Council are responsible for the supervision and administration of facial recognition application activities within their respective scope of responsibilities in accordance with the law. Local cybersecurity and informatization departments, public security departments, and other relevant departments fulfill the supervision and administration responsibilities for facial recognition application activities within their respective administrative regions according to law and in accordance with their respective duties.

---

## Chapter II: General Provisions on the Use of Facial Recognition Technology

**Article 6:** Facial recognition technology shall not be used to process facial information where other non-biometric technical means can achieve the same purpose of identification or authentication. The processing of facial information shall be limited to the minimum necessary scope.

**Article 7:** Where it is truly necessary to use facial recognition technology to process facial information, the handler shall separately inform individuals of the purpose, method, and scope of the processing of facial information, the retention period for facial information, and the manner in which individuals may exercise their rights; where required by laws or administrative regulations, written consent shall be obtained from individuals. Where individuals refuse consent, the handler shall not refuse to provide products or services to individuals on those grounds, except where the processing of facial information is necessary for the provision of such products or services.

**Article 8:** Where facial recognition technology is used to process facial information, the handler shall inform individuals by way of notice signs or other prominent means:

(1) The name or personal name and contact method of the handler;

(2) The purpose of using facial recognition technology and the retention period for facial information;

(3) The channel and methods for individuals to exercise the rights provided under law;

(4) Other items that laws and administrative regulations require to be notified.

**Article 9:** Facial information collected through the application of facial recognition technology shall be stored in the territory of the People's Republic of China. Where it is truly necessary to provide it outside the territory of the People's Republic of China, the relevant security assessment procedures for outbound data transfer shall be completed in accordance with the law.

**Article 10:** Handlers applying facial recognition technology shall establish and maintain technical and management safeguards to ensure the security of facial information. The specific measures shall include:

(1) Establishing internal management rules and operating procedures for facial recognition activities;

(2) Implementing classified and hierarchical management of facial information;

(3) Adopting technical security measures such as encryption and de-identification for the storage of facial information;

(4) Regularly conducting security assessments and audits of facial recognition systems;

(5) Formulating and implementing emergency response plans for facial information security incidents;

(6) Adopting reasonable access control measures to prevent unauthorized access to facial information systems.

**Article 11:** Handlers of facial information shall not retain facial information beyond the period necessary to achieve the purpose of the processing. Where the purpose has been achieved or the individual withdraws consent, the facial information shall be deleted in a timely manner unless laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise.

**Article 12:** Handlers shall not provide facial information to third parties except under one of the following circumstances:

(1) Individuals have given their separate consent;

(2) It is necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract to which the individual is a party;

(3) It is necessary to fulfill the statutory duties and obligations of State organs;

(4) Other circumstances provided for in laws and administrative regulations.

## Chapter III: Requirements for Specific Facial Recognition Scenarios

**Article 13:** Where facial recognition technology is used in hotels, banking outlets, transportation hubs, commercial complexes, parks, exhibition venues, and other public or semi-public premises for the purposes of access control, visitor management, or identity verification, the handler shall:

(1) Set up prominent notice signs to inform persons entering the premises about the use of facial recognition technology;

(2) Provide alternative means of access or services for individuals who refuse to have their facial information processed, unless the use of facial recognition is mandated by laws or administrative regulations;

(3) Not use facial recognition technology as the sole or mandatory means of access control or service provision where other effective methods are available;

(4) Not use facial recognition technology for the purposes of inducing purchases, targeted marketing, or analyzing consumer behavior without the explicit separate consent of individuals.

**Article 14:** Where employers, schools, or other institutions use facial recognition technology for employee or student attendance management, the following requirements shall be met:

(1) Employees or students shall be informed in advance and their consent obtained;

(2) The processing of facial information shall be limited to attendance management purposes and shall not be used for other purposes;

(3) Effective alternative means for attendance management shall be provided to persons who refuse to provide facial information;

(4) Facial information shall not be shared with third parties or used for commercial purposes.

**Article 15:** Where facial recognition technology is used in residential communities or buildings for access control, the handler shall:

(1) Not set facial recognition as the only entry and exit method; other access methods must be provided and maintained;

(2) Not force residents to use facial recognition as a prerequisite for the conclusion, performance, modification, or termination of contracts related to property management or housing services;

(3) Comply with relevant regulations on residential community data governance and community information management.

**Article 16:** Where Internet platform service providers use facial recognition technology for user authentication, account security verification, or transaction authorization, the following requirements shall be met:

(1) The use of facial recognition technology shall be optional; alternative verification means shall be provided;

(2) The facial information collected for authentication purposes shall only be used for authentication and shall not be retained or used for other purposes after the authentication is completed;

(3) The security of authentication processes involving facial information shall be ensured to prevent unauthorized access or fraudulent use.

**Article 17:** Minors under the age of fourteen shall not have their facial information processed without the separate consent of their parents or other legal guardians. Handlers shall establish dedicated processing rules for the facial information of minors and shall take enhanced protective measures.

## Chapter IV: Prohibition on Certain Uses of Facial Recognition Technology

**Article 18:** The following uses of facial recognition technology are prohibited:

(1) Using facial recognition technology to illegally identify the racial origin, ethnicity, religious beliefs, health status, or other sensitive personal information of individuals from facial information;

(2) Using facial recognition technology to covertly collect, match, or track individuals in circumstances where individuals have not been informed or have not given consent, except where State organs perform law enforcement or judicial duties in accordance with the law;

(3) Using facial recognition technology to develop databases of individuals' facial information for the purpose of commercial profit without a lawful basis;

(4) Using facial recognition technology to discriminate against individuals based on their physical characteristics, or to restrict individuals' exercise of their lawful rights and interests based on analysis of facial information;

(5) Using facial recognition technology to infringe upon the personal dignity or reputation of individuals;

(6) Using facial recognition technology for activities that endanger national security, public order, or the lawful rights and interests of others.

**Article 19:** Where facial recognition technology is used to process the facial information of State employees or persons in positions of public authority in the course of their official duties, additional safeguards shall apply, and such processing shall be strictly limited to lawful and necessary purposes.

---

## Chapter V: Security Assessment and Registration Requirements

**Article 20:** Organizations and individuals that provide facial recognition technology products or services to others (hereinafter "providers") shall conduct security assessments of their products or services before they are put into use. The security assessment shall cover:

(1) The technical security of the facial recognition system, including resistance to spoofing attacks and the accuracy of recognition;

(2) The security protection measures for facial information, including data storage security, transmission security, and access control;

(3) Compliance with this Measures and relevant laws and administrative regulations;

(4) Risk assessment of the impact on individuals' rights and interests.

**Article 21:** Providers of facial recognition technology services that process facial information on a large scale shall register with the relevant cybersecurity and informatization department and submit the following information:

(1) The name of the provider, contact information, and registration details;

(2) The technical parameters and application scenarios of the facial recognition technology;

(3) The scope, volume, and nature of facial information processed;

(4) The security protection measures adopted for facial information;

(5) The results of security assessment.

**Article 22:** Providers that process facial information reaching the quantity thresholds determined by the Cyberspace Administration of China shall conduct personal information protection impact assessments before commencing or materially changing their facial recognition services, and shall retain assessment reports for not less than three years.

---

## Chapter VI: Rights of Individuals

**Article 23:** Individuals have the right to access, copy, correct, supplement, and delete their facial information held by handlers, and to withdraw consent for the processing of their facial information, except where laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise.

**Article 24:** Where individuals request handlers to delete their facial information, handlers shall comply within fifteen working days from receipt of the request, unless retention is required by laws or administrative regulations.

**Article 25:** Where individuals believe that a handler's processing of their facial information violates these Measures or other laws and administrative regulations, they have the right to complain and report to the relevant regulatory departments. The regulatory department receiving the complaint shall process it in a timely manner and notify the complainant of the outcome.

---

## Chapter VII: Supervision and Administration

**Article 26:** Cybersecurity and informatization departments and other relevant regulatory authorities shall conduct regular supervision and inspection of facial recognition application activities in accordance with the law, and may take the following measures:

(1) Request relevant organizations and individuals to provide information on facial recognition application activities;

(2) Conduct on-site inspections of the premises and equipment used for facial recognition activities;

(3) Review the security assessments, personal information protection impact assessments, and related technical documentation;

(4) Issue orders to rectify non-compliant facial recognition activities within a specified period.

**Article 27:** Where a facial information security incident occurs or is likely to occur, the handler shall immediately adopt remedial measures and report to the relevant regulatory department within the prescribed period, and shall notify the affected individuals of the incident and its potential impacts in accordance with the law.

---

## Chapter VIII: Legal Liability

**Article 28:** Where any organization or individual violates these Measures in the processing of facial information, the relevant regulatory departments shall order correction, confiscate unlawful income, and impose fines in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law, the Personal Information Protection Law, and other applicable laws and administrative regulations.

**Article 29:** Where providers of facial recognition technology products or services fail to conduct security assessments as required, or provide false or misleading information in their registrations or assessments, the relevant regulatory departments shall order corrections, and may impose fines of not more than five hundred thousand yuan; where the circumstances are serious, they may order the suspension of the relevant services.

**Article 30:** Where violations of these Measures constitute public security violations, public security administrative penalties shall be imposed in accordance with law; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability shall be pursued in accordance with law.

---

## Chapter IX: Supplementary Provisions

**Article 31:** The term "facial recognition technology" as used in these Measures refers to the technology of automatically identifying natural persons by analyzing and comparing facial features recorded in images, video, or other data, for the purpose of distinguishing or verifying individual identity.

The term "facial information" refers to the biometric information of natural persons extracted or generated through the application of facial recognition technology.

**Article 32:** These Measures apply to the processing of facial information in activities carried out by State organs, except where laws and administrative regulations provide otherwise.

**Article 33:** These Measures take effect on September 1, 2024.
