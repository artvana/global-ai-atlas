# Ley No. 6534/2020 — Ley de Protección de Datos Personales — Paraguay
**Ley No. 6534/2020**
*Paraguay — September 30, 2020 (effective March 1, 2021)*

---

## Overview

Paraguay's Law No. 6534/2020 on Personal Data Protection is the country's primary data protection statute, enacted on September 30, 2020 and entering into force on March 1, 2021. The law establishes the Secretaría Nacional de Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación (SENATIC) as the supervisory authority with powers of registration, investigation, and enforcement. The law builds on Paraguay's constitutional privacy protections and creates a comprehensive framework for the protection of personal data in both public and private sectors. It applies to all processing of personal data of Paraguayan residents carried out in Paraguay, and extends to non-Paraguayan processors targeting Paraguayan residents.

---

## Key Provisions

### SENATIC as Supervisory Authority
- Secretaría Nacional de Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación designated as competent authority
- Maintains a registry of personal databases
- Investigates complaints and may conduct ex officio investigations
- Issues compliance orders and enforcement notices
- Imposes administrative fines and refers criminal violations to prosecutors
- Issues technical guidelines and codes of conduct

### Data Protection Principles
- Lawfulness: processing requires a valid legal basis
- Consent: must be free, specific, informed, and unambiguous where consent is the basis
- Purpose specification: purposes must be specified prior to collection
- Proportionality: data limited to what is adequate, relevant, and not excessive
- Accuracy: data must be accurate and kept current
- Security: appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Transparency: data subjects must be informed of processing

### Lawful Processing Bases
- Explicit consent of the data subject
- Performance of a contract to which the data subject is party
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Protection of vital interests
- Legitimate interests of the controller

### Sensitive Personal Data Special Categories
- Categories requiring explicit consent or specific legal authority:
  - Health and medical data
  - Genetic data
  - Biometric data used for identification
  - Political opinions
  - Religious or philosophical beliefs
  - Trade union membership
  - Racial or ethnic origin
  - Criminal record and judicial measures

### Data Subject Rights (ARCO)
- Right of Access to personal data held by any controller
- Right of Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Right of Cancellation of data processed unlawfully or no longer necessary
- Right of Opposition to processing on legitimate grounds

### Mandatory Database Registration
- Data controllers must register personal databases with SENATIC before commencing processing
- Registration discloses data categories, purposes, data subjects, and security measures
- Sensitive data databases subject to additional requirements

### Cross-Border Data Transfers
- Transfers outside Paraguay prohibited unless recipient country provides adequate protection
- SENATIC may authorize transfers to non-adequate countries through approved safeguards

### Security Obligations
- Controllers must implement technical and organizational security measures proportionate to the risk
- Security measures must protect against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, and disclosure

## Enforcement & Penalties

- SENATIC may issue compliance notices, corrective orders, and formal warnings
- Administrative fines: up to PYG 500 million (approximately $67,000 USD) for serious violations
- Criminal penalties under Paraguay's criminal code for the most serious offenses
- Civil liability: data subjects may seek compensation through Paraguayan courts
- Processing suspension may be ordered as a remedy
