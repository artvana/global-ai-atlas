HOUSE BILL NO. 4536
June 03, 2025, Introduced by Reps. Rheingans, Coffia, Price, Byrnes, Morgan, Wegela,
Tsernoglou, Breen, Young, Wilson and MacDonell and referred to Committee on
Communications and Technology.
A bill to amend 1956 PA 218, entitled
"The insurance code of 1956,"
(MCL 500.100 to 500.8302) by adding section 3406ss.
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF MICHIGAN ENACT:
1 Sec. 3406ss. An insurer that delivers, issues for delivery, or
2 renews in this state a health insurance policy shall not deny,
3 modify, or delay a claim based on a review using artificial
4 intelligence.
