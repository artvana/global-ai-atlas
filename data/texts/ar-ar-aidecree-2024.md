# Decree 1182/2024 — Approving the National Artificial Intelligence Plan of Argentina and Establishing the Regulatory Framework for State Use of Artificial Intelligence

**Issued by:** The Executive Branch of the Argentine Nation (Poder Ejecutivo Nacional — PEN)  
**Date of Issue:** December 26, 2024  
**Published in:** Official Gazette (Boletín Oficial de la República Argentina), December 27, 2024  
**Authority:** The President of the Argentine Nation, in exercise of the powers conferred by Article 99, subsections 1 and 2, of the National Constitution  

---

## Preamble

WHEREAS:

That Argentina has a strategic interest in promoting the development and responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI) as a driver of economic growth, social development, scientific advancement, and modernization of the State;

That the national government has committed to a policy of technological modernization and the transformation of the Argentine public sector through digital innovation;

That artificial intelligence presents both significant opportunities — including improvements in the quality and efficiency of public services, support for scientific research, and the development of new economic sectors — and genuine risks to fundamental rights, social equity, and national security;

That a coordinated national approach is required to promote the responsible development and use of AI in Argentina, ensuring that AI benefits are broadly shared while risks are effectively managed;

That the Argentine State has an interest in being a responsible and leading user of AI in public administration, demonstrating ethical and effective AI governance;

That it is necessary to establish a national institutional framework and guiding principles for the development and use of AI in Argentina;

On the advice of the Chief of the Cabinet of Ministers;

THE PRESIDENT OF THE ARGENTINE NATION DECREES:

---

## Chapter I: National Artificial Intelligence Plan

### Article 1: Approval of the National AI Plan

The National Artificial Intelligence Plan of Argentina (hereinafter the "Plan"), which forms Annex I to this Decree and is an integral part thereof, is hereby approved.

### Article 2: Objectives of the National AI Plan

The National AI Plan aims to:

(a) Position Argentina as a regional leader in the development and responsible use of artificial intelligence;

(b) Promote the use of AI to drive the modernization of the State and improve the quality and accessibility of public services;

(c) Support the development of a competitive AI industry in Argentina, promoting innovation, investment, and the growth of AI enterprises;

(d) Promote the development of AI talent and strengthen AI research and development capabilities in Argentine universities and research institutions;

(e) Ensure that the development and use of AI in Argentina is responsible, ethical, and respectful of fundamental rights;

(f) Promote Argentina's active participation in international AI governance and standard-setting processes;

(g) Identify and address the potential negative impacts of AI on employment, social equity, and democracy.

### Article 3: Priority Areas of the National AI Plan

The National AI Plan identifies the following priority areas for AI development and application:

(a) **Modernization of the State**: Promoting the use of AI to improve the efficiency, quality, and transparency of public services, reduce administrative burdens, and strengthen evidence-based policy-making;

(b) **Health**: Promoting AI applications in medical research, diagnosis, treatment, and public health surveillance;

(c) **Agriculture and food security**: Promoting AI applications in precision agriculture, supply chain management, and food security monitoring, leveraging Argentina's position as a leading agricultural producer;

(d) **Natural resources and environment**: Promoting AI applications for environmental monitoring, natural disaster early warning, and management of natural resources;

(e) **Education**: Promoting AI applications in personalized learning, educational resource development, and education system management;

(f) **Scientific research**: Promoting AI as a tool for scientific discovery and supporting AI research in Argentine universities and national research institutions;

(g) **Financial inclusion**: Promoting AI applications that expand access to financial services for underserved populations;

(h) **Justice and security**: Promoting the responsible use of AI in judicial administration and public security, subject to strict safeguards for fundamental rights.

## Chapter II: Governing Principles for AI in Argentina

### Article 4: Principles Applicable to AI Development and Use in Argentina

The development and use of AI by State entities and, as a guide and reference for private sector actors, in Argentina shall be governed by the following principles:

(a) **Human-centered AI and human dignity**: AI shall be developed and used for the benefit of individuals and society, and shall respect the inherent dignity of all persons. AI shall not be used to undermine, manipulate, or instrumentalize human beings;

(b) **Respect for fundamental rights**: AI systems shall respect and shall not be used in ways that undermine or are incompatible with the fundamental rights guaranteed by the National Constitution, international human rights conventions ratified by Argentina, and applicable legislation;

(c) **Non-discrimination and equity**: AI systems shall not generate, perpetuate, or exacerbate discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, socioeconomic status, or other protected or analogous characteristics. AI applications shall be designed and deployed with attention to their potential for differential impact on vulnerable or historically marginalized groups;

(d) **Transparency**: The development, deployment, and use of AI systems, particularly in public administration, shall be transparent. The State shall be accountable for the AI systems it uses and shall provide accessible information about how AI is used in governmental functions;

(e) **Explainability**: AI systems used in high-stakes contexts — particularly in decisions that affect the rights or interests of individuals — shall produce outputs that can be explained in terms that are meaningful to affected persons;

(f) **Human oversight and control**: Significant decisions affecting individuals' rights and interests shall be subject to meaningful human oversight. Humans shall retain responsibility and accountability for AI-assisted decisions. The delegation of legally consequential decisions entirely to automated systems without human review is not compatible with these principles except where expressly authorized by law;

(g) **Safety and reliability**: AI systems shall function reliably, accurately, and safely, incorporating appropriate safeguards against errors, adversarial attacks, and unintended consequences. AI systems shall be tested and validated before deployment;

(h) **Privacy and data protection**: AI development and deployment shall comply with Law No. 25.326 on Personal Data Protection and applicable regulations. AI systems shall be designed with privacy-by-design principles, minimizing data collection and processing to what is strictly necessary;

(i) **Accountability**: Those responsible for developing and deploying AI systems — including State entities — bear responsibility for ensuring compliance with applicable legal requirements and ethical principles, and shall be accountable for the impacts of the systems they develop or deploy;

(j) **Sustainability**: AI development shall consider environmental impacts and contribute to Argentina's sustainable development commitments;

(k) **Inclusive access**: The benefits of AI shall be broadly accessible, and efforts shall be made to reduce rather than exacerbate the digital divide and socioeconomic inequalities in access to AI benefits.

## Chapter III: Governance Framework for AI in Argentina

### Article 5: Institutional Framework

The following institutional framework is established for the governance of AI in Argentina:

(a) **Lead Coordination**: The Chief of the Cabinet of Ministers (Jefatura de Gabinete de Ministros), through the Secretariat of Digital Transformation and Innovation of the State, shall be responsible for the overall coordination of the National AI Plan and for coordinating AI governance across the national public administration;

(b) **Inter-Ministerial AI Committee**: An inter-ministerial committee for AI governance is established, comprising representatives of the Secretariat of Digital Transformation and Innovation of the State, the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, the Ministry of Economy, the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Human Rights, and other relevant ministries. The Committee shall coordinate the implementation of the National AI Plan and develop national AI policy positions;

(c) **Sectoral Responsibility**: Each ministry and secretariat of the national executive shall be responsible for overseeing the development and use of AI within its respective sector, in accordance with the principles and requirements established in this Decree;

(d) **National AI Advisory Council (Consejo Asesor Nacional de Inteligencia Artificial — CANAI)**: A national advisory council is established comprising representatives of academia, the private sector, civil society organizations, and the scientific community, to provide recommendations on AI governance, ethics, and policy. The CANAI shall meet at least twice annually and shall publish its recommendations.

### Article 6: Functions of the Secretariat of Digital Transformation

The Secretariat of Digital Transformation and Innovation of the State shall, in its capacity as lead coordinator for the National AI Plan:

(a) Develop and maintain a national AI strategy roadmap and coordinate its implementation across government;

(b) Develop national guidelines, standards, and technical criteria for the responsible use of AI in the public sector;

(c) Maintain a registry of AI systems in use across the national public administration;

(d) Publish annual progress reports on the implementation of the National AI Plan;

(e) Represent Argentina in international forums on AI governance, in coordination with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

(f) Support the capacity building of public officials in AI governance and implementation;

(g) Promote collaboration between the public sector, private sector, and academia in AI research and development.

## Chapter IV: Obligations of State Entities in AI Use

### Article 7: General Obligations of National Public Administration Bodies

National public administration bodies that develop or use AI systems in the exercise of their functions shall:

(a) Comply with the principles established in Article 4 of this Decree;

(b) Maintain a register of the AI systems in use, to be shared with the Secretariat of Digital Transformation for inclusion in the national registry;

(c) Assess the potential impacts of AI systems on fundamental rights before deployment, and document their assessment;

(d) Implement meaningful human oversight mechanisms for AI-assisted decision-making that affects the rights and interests of individuals;

(e) Ensure that affected persons are informed when AI is used in decisions affecting their rights and interests;

(f) Provide accessible channels for affected persons to request explanations of AI-assisted decisions and to seek review of such decisions by a human official with authority to modify the decision;

(g) Comply with applicable data protection requirements;

(h) Conduct post-deployment monitoring of AI system performance and address risks and adverse outcomes identified through monitoring.

### Article 8: Transparency in Government AI

National public administration bodies shall publish on their official websites and through the government's transparency portal:

(a) A catalogue of the AI systems in use, in accessible language, including the purpose and the decisions they inform;

(b) Information on the human oversight arrangements for AI-assisted decisions;

(c) Contact information for persons who wish to request explanations or human review.

The Secretariat of Digital Transformation shall develop a uniform template for these disclosures.

### Article 9: High-Stakes Government AI Applications

For AI systems used in the following high-stakes contexts, the requirements of this Article shall apply in addition to the general requirements of Article 7:

(a) Decisions on eligibility for or exclusion from social benefit programs and public services;

(b) Decisions with consequences for criminal justice, including risk assessment tools used in the context of criminal investigations or proceedings;

(c) Decisions on immigration and asylum matters;

(d) Decisions in healthcare contexts, including AI diagnostic or treatment recommendation tools;

(e) Decisions on access to financial services provided by State-owned entities.

For these high-stakes contexts:

(i) A data protection impact assessment compliant with Law No. 25.326 shall be conducted before deployment;

(ii) A specific algorithmic impact assessment shall be conducted, evaluating potential for bias and discriminatory outcomes, and documenting mitigation measures;

(iii) The oversight body designated by the competent ministry shall review and approve the deployment of the AI system;

(iv) Results shall be subject to human review in every individual case where the AI output is used to support a consequential decision.

### Article 10: Obligations Regarding AI and Employment

National public administration bodies shall:

(a) Notify employees in advance of the planned deployment of AI systems that will materially affect their working conditions, tasks, or employment;

(b) Consult with employee representatives on AI deployment plans that may affect employment;

(c) Provide training and support to public employees whose roles are affected by AI deployment.

## Chapter V: Protection of Fundamental Rights

### Article 11: Right to Information

Individuals interacting with AI systems operated by national public administration bodies, or subject to AI-assisted decisions of such bodies, shall have the right to be clearly and promptly informed:

(a) That AI is being used in the relevant process;

(b) The purpose for which AI is being used;

(c) How to exercise their rights under this Decree and applicable law.

### Article 12: Right to Explanation

Individuals subject to AI-assisted decisions of national public administration bodies that affect their rights or interests shall have the right to request and receive, within fifteen (15) working days:

(a) A plain-language explanation of the principal factors that influenced the AI output used in the decision;

(b) Information on how the AI system reached its output, to the extent feasible given the technical characteristics of the system.

### Article 13: Right to Human Review

Individuals subject to AI-assisted decisions of national public administration bodies affecting their rights or interests shall have the right to request review of the decision by a competent human official. The relevant body shall complete the human review within the timeframe prescribed by applicable administrative procedure law.

### Article 14: Complaints and Remedies

Individuals who consider that an AI system operated by the national public administration has violated their rights may:

(a) File a complaint with the relevant body's internal complaints mechanism;

(b) File a complaint with the Access to Public Information Agency (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública — AAIP), which is authorized to investigate complaints related to AI systems' compliance with data protection and transparency obligations;

(c) Pursue remedies through administrative and judicial procedures as provided by applicable law.

---

## Chapter VI: Private Sector Guidelines and Incentives

### Article 15: AI Development Guidelines for the Private Sector

The principles established in Article 4 of this Decree are hereby adopted as national guidelines and a reference framework for private sector entities developing and using AI systems in Argentina, without prejudice to the binding obligations that apply to the private sector under existing legislation.

### Article 16: Promoting a Competitive AI Industry

To support the development of a competitive AI industry in Argentina, the Executive Branch shall:

(a) Promote investment in AI research and development through existing national science and technology funding mechanisms, including the CONICET, the ANPCyT, and the Polo Científico Tecnológico;

(b) Promote access to public datasets for AI research and development, through the government's open data portal and sector-specific data sharing initiatives, subject to data protection requirements;

(c) Support AI entrepreneurship through the existing start-up and innovation promotion programs, and develop AI-specific support programs;

(d) Promote international cooperation in AI, supporting Argentine enterprises in accessing international markets and participating in international AI standards development;

(e) Promote AI talent development through educational policy, promoting AI education from secondary school through graduate-level research.

### Article 17: Regulatory Sandbox

The Secretariat of Digital Transformation, in coordination with relevant sectoral regulators, is authorized to establish a regulatory sandbox program for AI innovation, enabling enterprises to test innovative AI applications under temporary, controlled regulatory flexibility, subject to appropriate safeguards and oversight.

---

## Chapter VII: International Cooperation and Participation

### Article 18: Argentina's International AI Agenda

Argentina shall pursue an active international AI agenda, including:

(a) Participating in international AI governance initiatives, including the OECD AI Policy Observatory, the Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence (GPAI), UNESCO's AI ethics implementation activities, the G20 AI working groups, and the ITU AI for Good platform;

(b) Promoting a human rights-centered approach to AI governance in multilateral forums;

(c) Advocating for approaches to international AI governance that reflect the perspectives and interests of middle-income and developing countries;

(d) Concluding bilateral and multilateral agreements on AI cooperation with key partner countries and regional organizations including MERCOSUR, CELAC, and the Ibero-American community.

---

## Chapter VIII: Personal Data Protection in AI

### Article 19: Application of Data Protection Law

The development and use of AI systems in Argentina shall comply with Law No. 25.326 on Personal Data Protection and all applicable regulations. The Access to Public Information Agency (AAIP) shall exercise its supervisory functions with respect to personal data processing by AI systems.

### Article 20: Automated Profiling and Decision-Making

In accordance with Law No. 25.326 and the right to human review established in Article 13 of this Decree, the processing of personal data for the purpose of automated profiling or automated decision-making that produces significant legal or material effects for the individual shall:

(a) Be based on a valid legal ground for data processing;

(b) Not produce discriminatory effects;

(c) Be subject to the individual's right to access information on the profiling;

(d) Be subject to the individual's right to contest automated decisions and to request human review.

---

## Chapter IX: Final Provisions

### Article 21: Regulatory Development

The Chief of the Cabinet of Ministers, through the Secretariat of Digital Transformation and Innovation of the State, shall issue the complementary administrative instruments necessary for the implementation of this Decree within one hundred and eighty (180) days of its publication.

### Article 22: Review

The National AI Plan approved by Article 1 of this Decree shall be reviewed and updated at least every two years by the Inter-Ministerial AI Committee, in consultation with the CANAI and relevant stakeholders, to reflect developments in AI technology, the results of implementation monitoring, and developments in international AI governance.

### Article 23: Entry into Force

This Decree shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Gazette.

### Article 24: Countersignature

This Decree shall be countersigned by the Chief of the Cabinet of Ministers and by the Ministers of Economy, Science Technology and Innovation, and Justice.

## Annex I: Summary of the National Artificial Intelligence Plan of Argentina (Key Elements)

### Vision

Argentina will be a regional leader in the responsible and transformative use of artificial intelligence by 2030, with a vibrant AI ecosystem, world-class AI researchers and professionals, a modernized and AI-enhanced public administration, and a governance framework that ensures AI serves the well-being of all Argentines.

### Thematic Priorities

**Axis 1 — Digital State**: Deployment of AI across national public administration bodies to improve service quality, reduce costs, and strengthen evidence-based governance.

**Axis 2 — AI for Social Development**: AI applications in health, education, and social services targeting improved outcomes for vulnerable populations and reduction of inequalities.

**Axis 3 — AI for Economic Competitiveness**: AI applications in agriculture, mining, energy, and financial services to enhance Argentina's competitive position in global markets.

**Axis 4 — AI Talent and Research**: Strengthening Argentina's AI research ecosystem and developing the AI talent pipeline from secondary education through doctoral research.

**Axis 5 — Responsible AI Governance**: Developing and implementing the governance frameworks, standards, and legal instruments needed to ensure AI is developed and used responsibly.

**Axis 6 — International Leadership**: Positioning Argentina as a leading voice for responsible AI in Latin America and in global governance forums.

### Key Actions and Milestones (2025–2027)

(1) Establishment of the Inter-Ministerial AI Committee and CANAI within sixty days of Decree promulgation;

(2) Publication of the national Government AI Register within one hundred and eighty days;

(3) Development and publication of sectoral AI guidelines for health, justice, and social services within twelve months;

(4) Launch of the AI regulatory sandbox within twelve months;

(5) Development of a national AI education and training strategy within eighteen months;

(6) Completion of a review of Law No. 25.326 (Personal Data Protection) for AI-readiness within eighteen months, with legislative recommendations as appropriate;

(7) Publication of the first annual progress report on implementation of the National AI Plan within twenty-four months.
