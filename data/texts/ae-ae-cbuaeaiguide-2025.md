Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) — Guidance Note on Consumer Protection and Responsible Adoption and Use of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning by Licensed Financial Institutions (2025)

UNITED ARAB EMIRATES | 2025

Source: https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-central-bank-responsible-ai-guidance

Non-binding supervisory guidance from the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) issued 11 February 2025 establishing expectations for responsible adoption and use of AI and machine learning by all UAE-licensed financial institutions. The guidance applies to banks, insurance companies, finance companies, exchange houses, payment service providers, and other CBUAE-licensed entities.

Key provisions (based on publicly reported information):

1. Governance and Accountability
   - Institutions must establish clear accountability structures for AI systems, including board-level oversight
   - Senior management must be responsible for AI strategy, risk appetite, and compliance
   - Institutions must designate a responsible officer or function for AI governance

2. Fairness and Consumer Protection
   - AI systems must not result in discriminatory or unfair outcomes for consumers
   - Institutions must monitor AI systems for bias and unintended discriminatory effects
   - Consumer rights must be protected in AI-driven decision-making processes

3. Transparency and Explainability
   - Institutions must be able to explain AI-driven decisions to affected consumers in plain language
   - Material decisions made by AI must be explainable to regulators and auditors
   - Black-box AI models used in high-risk applications must be subject to additional scrutiny

4. Human Oversight
   - Meaningful human oversight must be maintained over material AI-driven decisions
   - Escalation procedures must exist for cases where AI recommendations are challenged
   - Institutions must not delegate morally or legally significant decisions entirely to AI systems

5. Data Quality and Model Risk Management
   - Training data must be accurate, complete, relevant, and free from material biases
   - Models must undergo rigorous pre-deployment validation and ongoing performance monitoring
   - Model risk management frameworks must cover AI-specific risks

6. Third-Party AI and Vendor Management
   - Institutions using third-party AI solutions remain accountable for compliance
   - Due diligence requirements apply to AI vendor selection and ongoing oversight
   - Contractual protections must ensure access to model documentation and audit rights

7. Cybersecurity and Operational Resilience
   - AI systems must incorporate cybersecurity controls proportionate to their risk
   - Institutions must assess and mitigate adversarial AI attack risks

The CBUAE indicated that compliance with the guidance will be assessed during supervisory examinations.
