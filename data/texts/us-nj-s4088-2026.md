42
43 This bill establishes the New Jersey State Board of General
44 Contractors to regulate the general contracting profession by
45 requiring licensure by the board. “General contractor” is defined in
46 the bill to mean an individual or business that is to be or can be
47 classified in trade 006, 007, 008, or 009 by the Division of Property

1 Treasury. The board is to consist of nine members, six of whom are
2 required to have been practicing general contracting in the State for
3 at least 10 years. The board is to: establish the standards required for
4 licensure; review the qualifications of individuals who apply for
5 licensure; ensure the proper conduct and standards of examination;
6 renew, suspend, or revoke licenses; prescribe or change fees for
7 examination or licensure; and maintain a record of all licensed
8 general contractors. No person may hold themselves out as a general
9 contractor without being licensed.
10 Under the bill, to be eligible as a general contractor, a person is
11 required to: (1) be at least 18 years of age; (2) have completed a
12 program accredited by a regional accreditation agency recognized by
13 the United States Department of Education leading to a degree in
14 construction or project management or an apprenticeship program in
15 a building or construction trade accredited and approved by the United
16 States Department of Labor, the New Jersey Department of Labor and
17 Workforce Development, or affiliated unions; (3) possess certain field
18 experience; and (4) have successfully passed an examination in
19 general contracting prepared by or approved by the board. The
20 examination is to be held in the State at least two times a year at a
21 time and place determined by the board. The board may also arrange
22 for reasonably required physical accommodations and facilities to
23 conduct examinations. The board is also permitted to establish
24 standards and procedures to provide that the examination be offered
25 via the Internet. Additionally, a study guide for the examination is
26 to be created by the board.
27 Individuals who have at least 10 years of experience performing
28 general contracting services prior to the effective date of this bill and
29 who, during that time, have not declared bankruptcy or altered the
30 name of a business to deceive other parties regarding an interest in or
31 ownership of a previous business, are exempt from these education and
32 examination requirements, as are individuals with a license,
33 certification, or registration issued by the State in a closely allied
34 profession, which includes commercial general contracting.
35 Additionally, individuals who can demonstrate, in a manner as
36 determined by the board, completion of three years of field experience
37 prior to the effective date of the bill and that was performed under the
38 supervision of a general contractor with at least 10 years of experience
39 completed prior to the effective date of the bill will also qualify for
40 licensure upon passage of the examination required of the bill.
41 Licenses issued by the board are to be renewed every three years.
42 Fees for licensure are to be determined by the board and established
43 by regulation. The board is required to promulgate rules and
44 regulations for implementing continuing education requirements as a
45 condition of licensure renewal, which are to include the subject
46 matter and content of courses of study. Once a license is issued, the
47 licensee is to have the license readily available at the work site where
48 general contracting services are being performed.
