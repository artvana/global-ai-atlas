 AN ACT CONCERNING HEALTH DATA.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

 1         That the general statutes be amended to (1) recognize patients and
 2      users as the primary owners of their personal health data and enable
 3      them to access, control and transfer such data, (2) ensure that all parties
 4      involved in health data collection and storage provide transparency, (3)
 5      establish consent standards for health technologies, including, but not
 6      limited to, artificial-intelligence-enabled health technologies, (4)
 7      implement standardized digital consent processes to ensure that
 8      patients and users understand how their health data will be used,
 9      shared and retained, (5) enable patients and users to revoke consent by,
10      among other things, requiring implementation of transparent and
11      readily understandable opt-in and opt-out mechanisms, (6) establish an
12      ethics review board for health technology companies, health care
13      providers and research institutions that develop or use predictive
14      algorithms to ensure fair and unbiased application of such algorithms,
15      and (7) require continuous security audits by, and disclosure of findings
16      to, an oversight body to ensure health data integrity.
