GOVERNMENT OF PUERTO RICO

20th Legislative Assembly                                          2nd Regular Session

HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

H.B. 968

NOVEMBER 7, 2025

Introduced by Representative Aponte Hernández

Referred to the Committee on Education

LAW

To create the "Act on the Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence in Educational Institutions of Puerto Rico"; to require the Department of Education of the Government of Puerto Rico, as well as every private educational institution, to establish policies that incorporate ethical norms on the use of artificial intelligence; and for other related purposes.

STATEMENT OF MOTIVES

Artificial intelligence (AI) is making its way at great speed into our society. It is a concept that is revolutionizing the world. According to Title 15 of the United States Code, Section 9401(3), AI is defined as:

"The term 'artificial intelligence' means a machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing real or virtual environments. Artificial intelligence systems use machine and human-based inputs to— (A) perceive real and virtual environments; (B) abstract such perceptions into models through analysis in an automated manner; and (C) use model inference to formulate options for information or action."

Its use is increasingly frequent, and the field of education is not exempt from this innovation. It represents a technology that can streamline and improve the teaching and learning processes. Virtual assistants, tutoring systems, automated assessments, and educational data analysis are some examples of the advantages of artificial intelligence as a working tool.

However, this technology also entails a series of challenges that need to be addressed, especially when it concerns more vulnerable populations such as our children and youth. For that reason, it is urgent to establish ethical norms that guide its responsible implementation in educational contexts, since its use, without adequate ethical regulation, can generate significant risks.

The use of AI in education must be guided by ethical principles that protect privacy and sensitive information from misuse and unauthorized access, ensure equity, and foster responsible learning. Ethical frameworks must specify responsibility and accountability in AI. They must also promote transparency and autonomy.

Harnessing artificial intelligence in the educational realm represents an opportunity to transform the teaching and learning processes. However, it must be integrated within a robust ethical framework, capable of providing guidance on its responsible use. Such frameworks are indispensable for facing the challenges and complexities inherent to these technologies.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY OF PUERTO RICO:

Article 1. — Title.

This Act shall be known and may be cited as the "Act on the Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence in Educational Institutions."

Article 2. — Applicability.

This Act shall be applicable to the Department of Education of the Government of Puerto Rico and to private educational institutions, as defined in Act No. 212-2018, as amended, known as the "Educational Institutions Registration and Licensing Act."

Article 3. — Department of Education.

The Department of Education shall promulgate and implement policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence in public schools and shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with the same. The Puerto Rico Innovation and Technology Service (PRITS) shall provide the necessary technical advice for the development and implementation of said policies. The Department of Education shall publish the policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence in public schools on the agency's website.

Article 4. — Educational Institutions.

Educational institutions, as defined in subsection (i) of Section 4 of Act No. 212-2018, as amended, known as the "Educational Institutions Registration and Licensing Act," shall promulgate and implement similar policies that shall be applicable in their respective academic entities. Notwithstanding, each educational institution has the prerogative to decide the level of use of AI on its campus, how it implements it, and how it integrates it into its curriculum and into non-teaching duties, as well as the academic level from which its use will be recommended.

The policies must be filed with the Department of State of Puerto Rico as evidence of compliance with this Act. The private educational institution must include in its bylaws the policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence corresponding to its entity.

Article 5. — Policies on the Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence.

The policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence (AI) to be developed and implemented, in accordance with the provisions of Articles 3 and 4 of this Act, must include the following general principles:

(A) Safety and Protection. The AI technologies used must be robust, containing security standards that minimize risks to the systems. Control and supervision mechanisms shall be established, and shall comply with applicable laws and norms on data protection.

(B) Autonomy. AI is a support tool in the learning process without reducing cognitive capacities. Academic autonomy, critical thinking, and independent learning shall be fostered and respected.

(C) Privacy. Adequate safeguards must be established to protect users' right to privacy and to ensure the protection of personal and sensitive data. The implementation of the user's informed consent shall be guaranteed, maintaining the confidentiality of the information. AI systems shall be evaluated regularly, in accordance with the policies established by PRITS, to verify compliance with privacy policies.

(D) Transparency and Explainability. Clear and understandable information shall be provided to the user regarding the use of AI systems, allowing the reasoning used to reach a result to be traced.

(E) Diversity and Inclusion. Fair and equitable access to the benefits of AI shall be promoted, ensuring equity and equality among the different groups of users, taking into account specific needs. AI systems shall be inclusive and accessible, thereby avoiding an increase in the digital divide.

(F) Responsibility and Accountability. Establish clear controls in which all AI users assume responsibilities regarding its use and are aware of the ethical implications and the impact of their decisions. In addition, detection and verification mechanisms shall be established.

Article 6. — Guidance and Training Related to the Policies on the Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence.

Prior to the integration of artificial intelligence systems in public schools and private educational institutions, and subsequently each school year, students and teaching and non-teaching personnel shall be oriented and trained on ethics and the responsible use of AI technologies in teaching, including the policies adopted. For such purpose, collaborative agreements may be established between public and private entities.

Article 7. — Compliance with Federal and State Legal Norms.

The process of incorporating the policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence shall comply with all applicable federal and state legal norms.

Article 8. — Severability Clause.

If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional or null by a Court of competent jurisdiction, the judgment rendered to such effect shall not affect, prejudice, or invalidate the remainder of this Act. The effect of said judgment shall be limited to the paragraph, subsection, or article that has been so declared unconstitutional.

Article 9. — Effectiveness.

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval. Notwithstanding, the Department of Education and the educational institutions shall have a term of no more than one hundred eighty (180) days to promulgate and implement the policies on the ethical use of artificial intelligence, as established in this Act.
