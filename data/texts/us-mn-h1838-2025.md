 HF 1838 
 as introduced - 94th Legislature (2025 - 2026) Posted on 03/03/2025 
 
 
 A bill for an act relating to health insurance; prohibiting the use of artificial intelligence in the utilization review process; amending Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 62M.02, by adding a subdivision; proposing coding for new law in Minnesota Statutes, chapter 62M. 
 
 
 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF MINNESOTA: 
 
 Section 1. Minnesota Statutes 2024, section 62M.02, is amended by adding a subdivision to read: 
 
 Subd. 2a. Artificial intelligence. &quot;Artificial intelligence&quot; has the meaning given in United States Code, title 15, section 9401. 
 
 Sec. 2. 
 [62M.20] PROHIBITION ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE. 
 
 
 
 The use of artificial intelligence is prohibited in utilization review. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, a utilization review organization is prohibited from using artificial intelligence in any part of its review, evaluation, determination, or appeals processes.
