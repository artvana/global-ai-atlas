Introduction

## What is the AI Basic Act?

_Basic Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Establishment of a Foundation for Trustworthiness_ is groundbreaking legislation that balances AI innovation with safety and ethical protections. Enacted in January 2025 and effective January 2026, it positions South Korea as a global leader in comprehensive AI governance.

### Comprehensive Framework

The law consolidates 19 separate AI bills into a unified framework that covers everything from research funding to safety requirements.

### Innovation-First Approach

Unlike purely restrictive regulations, the Act actively promotes AI development through startup support, talent programs, and industry clustering while maintaining essential guardrails for safety and ethics.

### Global Leadership

South Korea is one of the first countries to implement a complete law on AI, making it a key player in the responsible development of AI.
