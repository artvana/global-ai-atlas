 AN ACT PROHIBITING HEALTH INSURERS FROM USING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TO DENY HEALTH INSURANCE CLAIMS. 
 
 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Assembly convened: 
 
 That the general statutes be amended to prohibit any health insurer from using artificial intelligence to deny health insurance claims. 
 
 Statement of Purpose: 
 
 To prohibit any health insurer from using artificial intelligence to deny health insurance claims.
