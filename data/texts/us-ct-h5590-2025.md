    Introduced by:

 AN ACT PROHIBITING HEALTH INSURERS FROM USING ARTIFICIAL
 INTELLIGENCE TO DENY HEALTH INSURANCE CLAIMS.

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General
 Assembly convened:

1         That the general statutes be amended to prohibit any health insurer
2      from using artificial intelligence to deny health insurance claims.

       Statement of Purpose:
       To prohibit any health insurer from using artificial intelligence to deny
       health insurance claims.
