## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Peru's first comprehensive AI implementing regulation under Law 31814. Establishes a risk classification framework (minimal, limited, high, unacceptable), defines prohibited AI practices, mandates transparency and human oversight for high-risk systems, extends obligations to the private sector, and creates a national AI registry. Covers both public institutions and private sector actors deploying AI systems that affect individuals' rights.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Classify AI systems according to four-tier risk framework
- Register high-risk AI systems in national AI registry
- Conduct impact assessments for high-risk AI before deployment
- Prohibit AI systems categorised as unacceptable risk
- Disclose AI use and provide human oversight for high-risk applications

**Published in:** Official Gazette El Peruano, September 10, 2025

## Preamble

That Law No. 31814, the Law Promoting the Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Peru, was promulgated on July 20, 2023, and established the foundational principles and framework for the governance of artificial intelligence in Peru;

That Article 26 of Law No. 31814 mandated the Executive Branch to issue implementing regulations within 180 calendar days of promulgation;

That the National Secretariat of Digital Government of the Presidency of the Council of Ministers, in coordination with relevant ministries and following a public consultation process, has developed these Regulations to operationalize the requirements of Law No. 31814;
