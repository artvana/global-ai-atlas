# Rhode Island H5872 / S0816 — Prohibition on Deceptive AI Campaign Communications

**Enacted July 2, 2025 | R.I. Gen. Laws Chapter 17-23.2**

---

## Overview

Rhode Island H5872 (companion: S0816) prohibits the creation and distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated media in campaign communications within a specified period before elections. The law requires clear and conspicuous disclosures when AI-generated content is included in political advertising, and creates civil and criminal penalties for violations.

---

## Definitions

**"Materially deceptive AI-generated media"** means any image, video, audio recording, or digital content that:
(a) was created or substantially altered using artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar computational methods;
(b) depicts a clearly identified candidate or officeholder appearing to do or say something they did not in fact do or say; and
(c) would be believed by a reasonable person to authentically depict the candidate or officeholder.
