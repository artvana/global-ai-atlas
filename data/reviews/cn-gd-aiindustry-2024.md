## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Guangdong's AI Industry Development Regulations, building on Shanghai's 2022 model, establish a provincial framework for AI development and governance. Key provisions include: provincial AI innovation corridors (Shenzhen-Hong Kong, Guangzhou), safe harbour certification for compliant AI products, training data governance requirements, risk assessment for AI in critical applications, and provincial AI standard-setting in manufacturing and industrial AI.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Risk assessment for AI in critical manufacturing and public safety applications
- Training data governance policy required for AI enterprises
- Provincial AI product certification for safe harbour
- Disclosure for AI used in government public services
- AI product registration for cross-border Greater Bay Area deployment
