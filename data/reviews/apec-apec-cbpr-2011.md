## Summary (verify for accuracy)

APEC's voluntary certification framework enabling free personal data flows between participating economies (US, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Canada, Mexico, Philippines, Chinese Taipei, Australia). Organizations certified against APEC Privacy Framework principles by accredited Accountability Agents can transfer data across participating economies. In 2022 the Global CBPR Forum was established to expand the system beyond APEC. Increasingly relevant for AI training data flows in Asia-Pacific.

## Overview

The APEC Cross-Border Privacy Rules (CBPR) System is a voluntary certification and interoperability framework established by the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum to facilitate the free flow of personal data across APEC member economies while ensuring meaningful privacy protections. Adopted at the APEC Leaders' Summit in November 2011 and operational from January 2013, the CBPR system provides a mechanism for organizations certified against APEC Privacy Framework principles to transfer personal data between participating economies without needing to satisfy additional data localization or transfer restriction requirements. Participating economies include the United States (with the FTC as accountability agent), Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Canada, Mexico, Philippines, Chinese Taipei, and Australia.
