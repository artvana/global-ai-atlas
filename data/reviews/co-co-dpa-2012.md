## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Colombia's foundational data protection law establishing rights and obligations for personal data processing. Applied to AI systems by the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) through regulatory guidance on algorithmic systems and automated profiling. Requires consent for personal data processing, provides access and rectification rights, and protects sensitive categories including biometric data. The SIC has issued specific guidance on AI systems under this law.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain prior, express, and informed consent for personal data processing
- Provide data subjects with access, correction, and deletion rights
- Register databases containing personal data with the SIC
- Classify sensitive data (health, biometric, political) and apply heightened protections
- Notify data subjects and SIC of data breaches

### Purpose and Scope

Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012, enacted as an Ley Estatutaria (a category of legislation requiring supermajority congressional approval and mandatory constitutional court review, reflecting its status as fundamental rights legislation), is the cornerstone of Colombia's data protection framework. It was promulgated on 17 October 2012 and regulated in detail by Decree 1377 of 2013 (later incorporated into Decree 1074 of 2015 as the Sole Regulatory Decree for Commerce). The Law is grounded in the constitutional right of habeas data recognized in Article 15 of the 1991 Colombian Constitution, which guarantees every person the right to know, update, and rectify information gathered about them in databases or files.
