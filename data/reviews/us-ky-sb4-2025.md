## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Establishes AI governance for Kentucky state government with risk-based oversight by Commonwealth Office of Technology. Requires AI inventory and pre-implementation approval for consequential government AI systems. Adds election integrity provisions requiring disclosure of AI-generated political content.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Establishes AI governance framework for state government AI use
- State agencies must inventory and assess AI systems for risk
- Requires AI policies for high-risk government AI deployment
- Creates AI advisory body within state government
