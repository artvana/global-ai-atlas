## Summary (verify for accuracy)

India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued this advisory requiring all intermediaries and platforms to ensure AI models, LLMs, and generative AI tools do not produce unlawful content, do not enable bias or discrimination or threaten electoral integrity, and that under-tested AI is labelled with fallibility warnings. It also requires deepfake/synthetic media to be embedded with permanent unique metadata for traceability. Compliance required with immediate effect.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI/LLM outputs must not contain unlawful content under IT Rules 3(1)(b)
- AI must not enable bias, discrimination or threaten electoral integrity
- Under-tested AI models must be labelled with fallibility warnings; consent popup required
- Synthetic/deepfake media must be embedded with permanent unique metadata identifying the source

*Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India. Advisory No. 2(4)/2023-CyberLaws-3 (supersession). Issued 15 March 2024.*

## Subject

Due diligence by Intermediaries / Platforms under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 with respect to Artificial Intelligence, LLMs, and Generative AI.

## Background

This advisory is issued in supersession of advisory No. 2(4)/2023-CyberLaws-3 dated 1st March 2024, and is supplementary to advisory No. 2(4)/2023-CyberLaws-2 dated 26th December 2023.

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has noted that intermediaries and platforms are often negligent in undertaking due-diligence obligations outlined under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 ("IT Rules").
