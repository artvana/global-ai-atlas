## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Developed through a multi-stakeholder process convened by the EU AI Office under Article 56 of the EU AI Act, this Code of Practice sets out the specific obligations for General-Purpose AI (GPAI) model providers. It covers transparency, training data copyright compliance, systemic risk assessment, cybersecurity, and content watermarking for frontier models. Compliance creates a presumption of conformity with the EU AI Act's GPAI provisions.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Publish technical documentation and model card
- Copyright compliance policy for training data
- Systemic risk assessment for high-capability models
- Implement adversarial testing (red-teaming)
- Watermark AI-generated content
- Report serious incidents to EU AI Office
