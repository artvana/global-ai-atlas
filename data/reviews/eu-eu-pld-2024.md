## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Modernises EU product liability rules to cover AI systems and software. Clarifies that AI systems are "products" under EU law; manufacturers and importers are strictly liable for damage caused by defective AI products. Introduces disclosure obligations for defendants to share technical documentation, alleviating the burden of proof problem for victims of AI harms.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI systems are "products" — manufacturers strictly liable for defective AI causing damage
- Defendants must disclose technical documentation when requested by plaintiff courts
- Presumption of causation where defect is plausible and damage consistent with type
- Member states must implement by December 9, 2026
