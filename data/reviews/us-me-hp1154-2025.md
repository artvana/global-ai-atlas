## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits AI chatbots from misleading consumers into believing they are interacting with a human without clear conspicuous notice. Violations treated as Unfair Trade Practices Act violations. Separate from LD 2082, which covers mental health specifically.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Chatbot operators must disclose AI identity when sincerely asked
- Prohibition on claiming to be human when sincerely questioned
- AG enforcement; civil penalties per violation
