## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Quebec's landmark privacy modernization law is the most AI-relevant enacted privacy law in Canada. Section 12.1 requires organizations to inform individuals when decisions are made exclusively through automated processing, disclose the logic used, and allow human review. Privacy Impact Assessments are mandatory for high-risk systems before deployment.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Inform individuals when decisions are made exclusively through automated processing; disclose logic and factors involved
- Allow individuals to have exclusively automated decisions reviewed by a human
- Conduct and publish Privacy Impact Assessments for high-risk AI systems before deployment
- 72-hour breach notification to the Commission d'accès à l'information
- Appoint Privacy Officer; implement privacy-by-default
