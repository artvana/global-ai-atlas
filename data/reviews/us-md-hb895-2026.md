## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits food retailers and grocery delivery services from using personal consumer data to set individualized (surveillance-based) pricing. First state law banning algorithmic price discrimination at grocery stores. Applies to retailers with $100M+ annual revenue.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Food retailers ($100M+ revenue) prohibited from using personal consumer data to set individualized prices
- Prohibit using purchase history or location data for price discrimination
- AG enforcement; consumer civil penalties
