## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Wisconsin Act 34/2025 makes it a misdemeanor to create AI-generated sexually explicit imagery of a person without consent; makes it a felony to use such content to coerce, harass, or intimidate. Covers 'synthetic intimate representations' realistic enough to be believed real.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Misdemeanor: creating AI-generated sexually explicit imagery without consent
- Felony: using such content to coerce, harass, or intimidate
