## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Russia's draft comprehensive AI governance framework law, submitted by the Government. Establishes foundational principles for AI development and deployment including risk-based regulation, disclosure obligations, data localisation requirements for trusted AI models, a register of certified trusted AI models for use in state systems, and liability provisions. Emphasises technological sovereignty and Russian value alignment. A sovereign AI model development programme supports domestic large foundation models. Mandatory human-in-the-loop rights for autonomous decisions affecting citizens. Watermarking/labelling requirements for AI-generated content. Enters into force 1 September 2027.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclose to users when AI is used in service delivery (Article 9)
- Notify citizens when autonomous AI decisions affect their rights (Article 9)
- Provide opt-out from AI for service delivery where feasible (Article 9)
- Allow pre-trial appeal of AI-assisted government decisions (Article 9)
- Developers must document model architecture and conduct risk modelling (Article 10)
- Operators must appoint AI safety officers and record incidents (Article 10)
- Service owners must label all AI-generated content in human- and machine-readable formats (Article 12)
- Only certified trusted AI models permitted in state information systems (Article 8)
- Data processing for trusted AI models must remain within Russian territory (Article 8)
- Training data text/data mining permitted without IP rights holder consent if lawfully acquired (Article 13)

---

### Article 1. Purpose and Subject of Regulation of this Federal Law

1. This Federal Law regulates relations arising in connection with the development, deployment, use, and other application of artificial intelligence technologies (hereinafter also referred to as the application of artificial intelligence) in the Russian Federation.
