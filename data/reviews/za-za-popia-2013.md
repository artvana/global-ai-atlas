## Summary (verify for accuracy)

South Africa's POPIA establishes 8 conditions for lawful personal data processing and has been fully enforced since July 2021. Section 71 explicitly addresses automated decision-making, granting data subjects rights to request human review of decisions based solely on automated processing. The Information Regulator has issued enforcement notices and fines.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- 8 lawful processing conditions apply to all personal data including AI training data
- Section 71: data subjects may request human review of solely automated decisions with significant effects
- Cross-border data transfer restrictions (Section 72)
- Responsible parties must notify Information Regulator and data subjects of breaches
- Data subject rights: access, correction, deletion, objection
