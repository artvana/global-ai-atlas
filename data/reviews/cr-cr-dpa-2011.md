## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Costa Rica's primary data protection law and Central America's most developed data protection framework. Establishes PRODHAB as a powerful independent agency. Provides ARCO rights, lawful processing bases, sensitive data categories, habeas data judicial remedy, mandatory database registration, cross-border transfer restrictions, and significant administrative fines up to CRC 172 million.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Register all personal data databases with PRODHAB
- Implement lawful processing basis
- Protect sensitive data categories with explicit consent or legal authority
- Provide data subject ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition)
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with adequate protection or PRODHAB-approved safeguards
- Cooperate with PRODHAB audits and investigations

## Overview

Costa Rica's Law No. 8968 of July 5, 2011, on the Protection of Persons with Regard to the Treatment of Their Personal Data is the country's primary personal data protection statute, entering into force on March 5, 2012. The law establishes the Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes (PRODHAB) as an independent supervisory authority with strong investigative and enforcement powers. PRODHAB is widely regarded as one of Central America's most active and effective data protection supervisory authorities. The law applies to all processing of personal data of Costa Rican residents by public and private sector organizations, and it provides both administrative remedies through PRODHAB and judicial remedies through the habeas data mechanism. The law is the most comprehensive and developed data protection framework in Central America.
