## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends India's IT Intermediary Guidelines to regulate Synthetically Generated Information (SGI — the Indian legal term for AI-generated content). Requires platforms to label AI-generated audio, video, and images with a mandatory metadata identifier visible across at least 10% of the display area. Platforms must remove unlabeled synthetic content within three hours of notice. Defines SGI broadly to cover deepfakes, voice cloning, and AI-generated text in certain contexts.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Label AI-generated content with metadata identifier covering 10% of display area
- Remove unlabeled synthetic content within three hours of notice
- Maintain audit logs of synthetic content removal actions
- Apply SGI labeling to audio, video, images, and specified text content
- Notify users when AI-generated content is served to them
