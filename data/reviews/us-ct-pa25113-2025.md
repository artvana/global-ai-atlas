## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends Connecticut Data Privacy Act to require controllers to disclose in privacy notices whether they collect, use, or sell personal data to train large language models. First US state law making LLM training data use a mandatory privacy disclosure element.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- LLM developers above compute threshold must publish training data summary
- Disclosure must include categories of training data, sources, and whether copyrighted material was used
- Content generated by covered LLMs must be labeled as AI-generated

*Connecticut General Assembly — 2025 Session*
*Effective: October 1, 2025*

---

## Overview

Connecticut Public Act 25-113 amends existing Connecticut law to require certain disclosures regarding artificial intelligence systems, with a particular focus on large language model (LLM) training data transparency. The Act establishes non-binding disclosure frameworks applicable to developers and deployers of AI systems sold or offered in Connecticut.

---

## Key Provisions

### Training Data Disclosure (Section 1)

- Developers of large language models and generative AI systems that are sold or offered for use in Connecticut must make available, upon request, a general description of the categories of data used to train the model
- Disclosure must include whether the training data included copyrighted works, personal data, or data from Connecticut residents
- Disclosures are required to be published on the developer's website or provided in product documentation
