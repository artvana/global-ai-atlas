## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Arizona's SB 1359 requires clear disclosure on AI-generated synthetic media used in political advertisements about candidates within 90 days of an election. Civil penalties of $10/day (first 15 days) and $25/day thereafter for non-disclosure. Parody and satire are exempt.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Clear disclosure required on AI-generated synthetic media in political advertisements
- Applies within 90 days of an election
- Civil penalties: $10/day first 15 days; $25/day thereafter for non-disclosure
- Parody and satire exempt
