## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Creates a federal civil cause of action for victims of non-consensual intimate digital forgeries (AI-generated NCII). Plaintiffs may sue any person who knowingly produces or possesses NCII with intent to distribute when they know or recklessly disregard that the depicted person did not consent. Allows injunctive relief, actual damages, and attorney fees. Also creates criminal liability under 18 U.S.C.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Civil cause of action for victims of NCII deepfakes
- Defendant liable if they knowingly produce or possess NCII with intent to distribute
- Injunctive relief and actual damages available
- Criminal penalties under amended federal statute
