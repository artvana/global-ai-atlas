## Summary (verify for accuracy)

New York's A3008 amends deceptive practices law to require clear disclosure ('THIS PRICE WAS SET BY AN ALGORITHM USING YOUR PERSONAL DATA') when personalized algorithmic pricing is used. Also regulates AI companion operators: requires safety protocols and disclosure that the companion is AI. First-of-its-kind algorithmic pricing disclosure law.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Clear disclosure required when personalized algorithmic pricing is used based on personal data
- AI companion operators must disclose that the companion is AI
- AI companion operators must implement safety protocols
