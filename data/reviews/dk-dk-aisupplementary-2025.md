## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Denmark's Law 467/2025 is among the first national EU AI Act implementation laws, designating the Danish Agency for Digitisation (Digitaliseringsstyrelsen) as the primary national competent authority and market surveillance authority. Passed unanimously by the Folketing, it grants inspection powers and establishes the administrative penalty framework aligned to EU AI Act maximums.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Designates Digitaliseringsstyrelsen as national competent authority and market surveillance authority
- Grants inspection powers: on-site visits, document requests, source code access
- Establishes national penalty regime: up to €35M or 7% global turnover
- Creates administrative enforcement framework: injunctions, recalls, temporary prohibitions

## Lov om supplerende bestemmelser til forordningen om kunstig intelligens

**Law No.:** 467 of 14 May 2025
**Adopted by:** Folketing (Danish Parliament)
**Parliamentary Adoption Date:** 8 May 2025

**Entry into Force:** 2 August 2025 (mainland Denmark)
**Geographic Scope:** Applies to Denmark proper; does not apply to the Faroe Islands or Greenland
**Enabling instrument for:** EU Artificial Intelligence Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the "EU AI Act")

---

## Overview and Significance

Law No. 467 of 14 May 2025 (the "Danish AI Supplementary Act" or "Supplerende AI-lov") is Denmark's national implementing legislation for the EU Artificial Intelligence Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), which entered into force on 1 August 2024 and is directly applicable across all EU Member States. Denmark was one of the first EU Member States to enact national supplementary legislation, completing the designation of national competent authorities required by Article 70 of the EU AI Act before the August 2, 2025 deadline.
