## Overview

Slovakia enacted Act No. 318/2025 Z.z. on 1 November 2025, amending the Act on Conformity Assessment of Products (zákon o posudzovaní zhody výrobkov) to align the national conformity assessment framework with the requirements of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) for notified bodies. The Act entered into force on 1 January 2026.

This statute is the first component of Slovakia's two-part EU AI Act implementation legislative package. It addresses the notified body (conformity assessment body) side of AI Act implementation — specifically the organizations authorized to conduct third-party conformity assessments of high-risk AI systems. A companion governance bill (LP/2025/401), designating market surveillance authorities and establishing enforcement procedures, remained pending in the Slovak National Council as of May 2026.
