## Overview

North Dakota HB 1351 creates both a criminal offense and a civil cause of action for the creation or distribution of nonconsensual AI-generated or digitally altered sexually explicit images of identifiable persons. The law expressly covers deepfake technology, generative AI, and other digital manipulation tools used to produce intimate images without the depicted person's consent. Criminal penalties escalate from a Class A misdemeanor for a first offense to a Class C felony for subsequent violations. Victims retain the right to seek civil damages and injunctive relief independent of criminal prosecution.
