## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Issued pursuant to the Biden AI Executive Order (EO 14110), DHS published this Roles and Responsibilities Framework assigning accountability across the AI supply chain for critical infrastructure sectors. It defines responsibilities for cloud and compute providers, AI developers, critical infrastructure owners/operators, and civil society. The framework addresses AI-specific risks to the 16 critical infrastructure sectors and provides sector-specific guidance.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Cloud and compute providers: implement baseline AI safety practices for infrastructure they provide
- AI developers: design AI with safety-by-design principles and provide documentation to downstream deployers
- Critical infrastructure owners/operators: conduct AI risk assessments before deployment
- All actors: report AI-related safety incidents to CISA
