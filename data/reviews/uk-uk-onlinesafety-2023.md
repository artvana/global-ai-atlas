## Summary (verify for accuracy)

UK Online Safety Act imposes platform duties to prevent illegal content, protect children, and ensure user safety. AI chatbots and generative AI systems are in scope as of Ofcom guidance from 2026. Requires risk assessments, proactive content moderation technology, and Ofcom registration for regulated services. UK has no dedicated AI Act.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Platforms must proactively assess and mitigate AI-generated harmful content risks
- Criminal offence to share non-consensual intimate deepfake images
- Category 1 services must conduct annual transparency reports on AI content moderation
- Ofcom to produce codes of practice for AI content harms
- Age verification requirements for platforms with AI-generated adult content
