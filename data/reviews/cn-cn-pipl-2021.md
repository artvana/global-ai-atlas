## Summary (verify for accuracy)

China's comprehensive personal data law directly applicable to AI: requires lawful basis for processing personal data in AI training, mandates consent for sensitive data, grants individuals rights to access, correct, and delete data, and prohibits differential treatment based on personal information analysis. AI recommendation algorithms must provide opt-out mechanisms.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Individuals have right to explanation of automated decision-making affecting them
- Right to refuse to be subject to automated decisions in commercial marketing and credit
- Explicit consent for processing sensitive personal information
- Data localization requirements for critical personal information handlers
