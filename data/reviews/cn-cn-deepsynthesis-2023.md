## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Regulates deep synthesis (deepfake/AI-generated) technology for video, voice, images, and text. Service providers must register users, implement ethics reviews, monitor content for illegal/disinformation use, and add visible labels to all synthetic content. Prohibits deepfakes implicating national security or reputational harm.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Providers must obtain consent before using individual likeness or voice for deep synthesis
- Label AI-generated or significantly altered content with visible and non-visible markers
- Security assessment and algorithm filing for public deep synthesis services
- No creation of false news or content harmful to national security using deep synthesis
