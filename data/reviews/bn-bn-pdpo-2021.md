## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Brunei's first comprehensive personal data protection law, the PDPO establishes nine data protection principles (purpose limitation, consent, accuracy, retention, transfer, access, correction, openness, security) governing all private-sector organizations processing personal data. Administered by AITI, the PDPO provides data subjects with access and correction rights, restricts cross-border transfers, and classifies biometric and health data as sensitive categories requiring explicit consent. Implementation was phased through 2024.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain consent before collecting personal data unless a specific exception applies
- Limit data collection and use to the stated purpose (purpose limitation)
- Implement reasonable security measures to protect personal data
- Respond to data subject access and correction requests within 30 days
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with equivalent protection or under contractual safeguards

## Overview / Background

The Personal Data Protection Order 2021 (PDPO) is Brunei Darussalam's first comprehensive personal data protection statute, enacted by His Majesty the Sultan and Yang Di-Pertuan of Brunei Darussalam under the Emergency (Essential Powers) Act. The Order was gazetted in May 2021 and entered into force on January 1, 2022, with full implementation phased through 2024.

The PDPO was developed by the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry of Brunei Darussalam (AITI), which serves as the primary enforcement authority. AITI has statutory powers to investigate complaints, issue enforcement notices, and impose financial penalties. The PDPO brings Brunei into alignment with ASEAN's Model AI Governance Framework and broader regional data protection norms, positioning Brunei as a credible digital economy participant in the context of ASEAN frameworks.

The PDPO applies to any organization (private sector entity) that controls the collection, holding, processing, or use of personal data in Brunei
