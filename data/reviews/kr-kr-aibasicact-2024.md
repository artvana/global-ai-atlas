## Summary (verify for accuracy)

South Korea's comprehensive AI framework consolidates 19 bills, requiring developers and deployers of 'high-impact AI' (healthcare, finance, transportation, employment) to meet transparency and safety obligations. Mandates AI use disclosure to users. Low penalties reflect innovation-first design; 1-year enforcement grace period means effective enforcement begins January 2027.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Government to establish national AI basic plan every 3 years
- High-impact AI systems subject to conformity assessment
- Providers of high-impact AI must disclose information to users
- AI providers must implement measures to prevent discriminatory outcomes
- National AI Commission to coordinate AI governance across ministries
