## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Sweeping reform of Israel's 1981 Privacy Protection Law, one of the first national laws to explicitly address AI training data and automated decision-making. Prohibits unauthorized scraping of personal data from the internet for AI model training. Mandates transparency and accountability for AI-based automated decisions affecting individuals. Imposes data minimization, purpose limitation, and security obligations on AI developers.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibit unauthorized scraping of personal data for AI training
- Conduct impact assessments for automated decision-making
- Disclose AI-based processing to data subjects
