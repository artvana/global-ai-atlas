## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Tunisia's National AI Strategy 2023–2025 focuses on four pillars: AI for economic competitiveness, AI infrastructure development, AI skills and talent, and regulatory and ethical framework. The strategy commits Tunisia to developing AI-specific legislation by 2025, adopting the UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation, and strengthening the INPDP's capacity to regulate AI data processing.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Develop AI-specific legislation by 2025
- Implement UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation
- Build AI regulatory capacity at INPDP
- Invest in AI infrastructure and skills

### 1.1 Introduction and Context

The Strategy builds upon Tunisia's established strengths in information and communication technology (ICT) education, a well-educated technical workforce, and a growing ecosystem of technology start-ups and digital enterprises.
