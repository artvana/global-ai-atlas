## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Japan's joint METI/MIC AI Business Guidelines are the country's primary operational governance document for AI, establishing ten guiding principles (human-centric AI, safety, security, fairness, accountability, transparency, privacy, education, innovation, social contribution) and tier-specific guidance for AI developers, providers, and users. Voluntary compliance with sector-specific appendices covering healthcare, financial services, legal services, and employment.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI developers should conduct safety testing and provide documentation (model cards) for foundation models
- AI providers should disclose to users when interacting with AI systems and implement human oversight for high-stakes decisions
- AI users should conduct risk assessments before deploying AI in business operations
- All actors should implement privacy protections consistent with the APPI
- AI actors should promote AI literacy among employees and users

## Overview / Background

The AI Business Guidelines (AI事業者ガイドライン) were jointly published by Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) on April 19, 2024. They represent Japan's primary operational governance document for AI systems and serve as the government's principal guidance instrument for AI developers, operators, and users prior to the enactment of any dedicated AI statute.

The guidelines were developed as part of Japan's broader AI governance strategy, building on the Hiroshima AI Process — the G7-led international framework Japan led during its 2023 G7 Presidency — as well as Japan's long-standing AI principles. The document consolidates earlier guidelines from METI and MIC, aligning them with evolving international standards including the OECD AI Principles and the Hiroshima Process International Guiding Principles for Advanced AI.
