## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Louisiana's Act 142/2024 criminalizes dissemination or sale of AI-generated intimate images of another person without consent and with intent to coerce, harass, or intimidate. Creates RS 14:73.14. Platforms are not liable for user-generated content under this provision.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Criminal prohibition on disseminating AI-generated intimate images without consent with intent to harm
- Up to 6 months imprisonment / $750 fine
