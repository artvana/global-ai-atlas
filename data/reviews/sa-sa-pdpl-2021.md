## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Saudi Arabia's first comprehensive personal data protection law, administered by SDAIA (the Saudi Data and AI Authority). Amendments in 2022 (Royal Decree M/148) and implementing regulations in September 2023 triggered full enforcement. The law includes provisions on automated profiling and sensitive data directly applicable to AI systems.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Legal basis required for all personal data processing including AI training
- Data subjects may withdraw consent and object to automated profiling
- Biometric data classified as sensitive; heightened protection requirements
- Data breach notification to SDAIA
- Controller and processor accountability requirements

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope

Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), issued by Royal Decree M/19 of 9 Sha'ban 1442H (corresponding to 16 March 2021) and amended by Royal Decree M/148 of 5 Muharram 1444H (corresponding to 3 August 2022, with the amendments effective 14 September 2023), is Saudi Arabia's first comprehensive, standalone data protection statute. The Law and its implementing Regulations issued by the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) establish a modern framework for the protection of personal data in the Kingdom, balancing privacy rights with the Kingdom's Vision 2030 digital transformation objectives and its aspiration to develop Saudi Arabia as a leading data economy in the region.

The Law applies to any processing of personal data of individuals residing in Saudi Arabia, where: the processing is carried out in Saudi Arabia; or the processing is carried out outside Saudi Arabia by a controller established in Saudi Arabia or by a controller processing data about individuals residing in Saudi Arabia.
