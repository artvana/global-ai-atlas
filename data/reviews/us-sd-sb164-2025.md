## Overview

South Dakota SB 164 prohibits the use of unlabeled AI-generated deepfakes to influence an election within 90 days before an election. The law requires a clear and conspicuous disclaimer when AI-generated content depicts real persons in a political context intended to influence voters. Violation of the labeling requirement is a Class 1 misdemeanor. The law exempts satire, parody, and media broadcasters accurately reporting on political events. It applies to both digital and traditional media distributed in South Dakota.

---
