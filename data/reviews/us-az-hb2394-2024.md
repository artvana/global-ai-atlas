## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Arizona's HB 2394 creates a civil cause of action for victims depicted in AI-generated deepfakes without consent. Covers political candidates (within 180 days pre-election) and any citizen. Requires actual knowledge by publisher; civil remedies include injunctions and damages. No criminal penalties.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Persons depicted in deepfakes without consent have civil cause of action
- Candidates: prohibition on AI-generated false depictions within 180 days of election
- Any citizen: civil remedy for non-consensual AI-generated depictions
- Publishers with actual knowledge of falsity are liable
