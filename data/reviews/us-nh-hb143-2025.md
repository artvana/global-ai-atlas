## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits AI chatbot operators from facilitating or encouraging harmful conduct toward children, including sexual exploitation, illegal drug use, self-harm, and violence. Provides private right of action for minors and parents with $1,000 statutory damages. AG enforcement available after 90-day cure notice.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Chatbot operators must disclose AI identity to users under 18
- Criminal prohibition on AI-generated CSAM
- Crisis protocol required when minors express suicidal ideation to chatbot
- AG enforcement
