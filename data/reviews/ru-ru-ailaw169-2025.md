## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Extends and updates Russia's experimental legal regime (regulatory sandbox) framework for AI development under Federal Law No. 258-FZ. Introduces mandatory civil liability insurance for AI developers operating within regulatory sandboxes. Expands the ELR framework to cover generative AI systems, adding content moderation and user disclosure requirements during testing. Updates governance procedures for sandbox applications. Builds on the original 2020 framework that created the Moscow AI digital sandbox.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Mandatory civil liability insurance for AI developers in regulatory sandboxes
- Generative AI ELR participants must implement content moderation during testing
- Generative AI ELR participants must disclose ELR status to users
- Generative AI ELR participants must report content-related incidents
- Extended statutory authority for ELRs for five additional years

## Overview

Federal Law No. 169-FZ, signed by the President of the Russian Federation on 4 January 2025, amends Federal Law No. 258-FZ of 31 July 2020 "On Experimental Legal Regimes in the Field of Digital Innovations in the Russian Federation." This Law extends and updates Russia's experimental legal regime (ELR) framework — sometimes referred to as the "regulatory sandbox" system — for AI development and testing.

The amendments build on the foundational ELR framework established in 2020, which created the original Moscow digital sandbox for AI technologies (supplemented by Federal Law No. 123-FZ of 2020 specifically addressing Moscow's AI sandbox). Law No. 169-FZ expands the ELR mechanism nationally, adds new requirements including mandatory liability insurance for participants, and extends the framework to cover generative AI systems that had not been addressed in the original 2020 legislation.
