## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Ethiopia's foundational personal data protection law, modeled on the GDPR. AI-relevant provisions include: right to object to automated decision-making affecting individuals' legal interests, data protection impact assessments required for high-risk processing including AI profiling, explicit consent required for biometric data processing, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain explicit consent for processing biometric and sensitive personal data
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk AI processing
- Allow individuals to object to purely automated decisions with significant effects
- Appoint a Data Protection Officer for systematic high-risk processing
- Register with the Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Commission

### Purpose and Scope

The Proclamation was developed against the backdrop of Ethiopia's expanding digital economy, the growth of mobile financial services, the deployment of digital identity systems, and the government's broader digital transformation agenda.
