## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Pennsylvania's Digital Forgery Law creates the crime of 'digital forgery' — using AI to generate fake voices, images, or videos to injure, exploit, or scam. Misdemeanor for digital impersonation; Felony for fraud. Satire and parody are protected.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Crime of digital forgery: AI-generated fake voices/images/videos to injure or scam
- Misdemeanor 1st degree for digital impersonation
- Felony 3rd degree for fraud using AI-generated content
- Satire and parody explicitly exempt
