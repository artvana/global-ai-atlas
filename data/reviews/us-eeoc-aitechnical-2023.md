## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The EEOC's Technical Assistance document clarifies that employers who use AI, algorithmic decision-making, or automated systems in employment decisions remain fully liable under Title VII, ADA, ADEA, and other employment discrimination statutes. The guidance confirms that: disparate impact liability applies to AI hiring tools, employers are responsible for their vendors' AI tools, the ADA reasonable accommodation requirement extends to AI assessment formats, and the 4/5ths adverse impact rule applies to AI screening.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Employers liable for AI hiring tool discrimination regardless of vendor responsibility
- 4/5ths adverse impact rule applies to AI screening tools
- ADA reasonable accommodation must extend to AI assessment formats
- Disparate impact analysis required for AI selection procedures
