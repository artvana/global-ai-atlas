## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Turkey's GDPR-like personal data protection law (KVKK), significantly amended in 2024 to align cross-border data transfer rules with GDPR adequacy mechanisms. Data subjects have the right to object to solely automated processing affecting their rights. No standalone Turkish AI law enacted; draft AI law submitted to Parliament June 2024, still pending.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Data subjects have right to object to solely automated decisions affecting their rights
- Cross-border data transfers via adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or binding corporate rules (post-2024)
- Explicit consent required for special category data (biometrics, health) used in AI
- Organizations must conduct DPIAs for high-risk AI processing (KVKK guidance)
- DPO appointment recommended for large-scale AI data processors
