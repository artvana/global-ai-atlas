## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends Australia's Privacy Act to require APP entities to disclose automated decision-making processes in privacy policies where AI could significantly affect individual rights or interests. Transparency obligation only — no prohibition or assessment requirement. Effective December 2026.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Organizations using ADM for decisions significantly affecting individuals must update privacy policies to disclose
- Right to seek explanation of ADM decisions affecting individuals
- Mandatory privacy impact assessments for high-privacy-risk activities including ADM
- OAIC to develop guidance on ADM transparency requirements
