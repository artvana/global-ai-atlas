## Summary (verify for accuracy)

South Korea's landmark 2023 PIPA amendments introduce Asia-Pacific's strongest statutory right to explanation for automated AI decisions and the right to object to and demand human review of purely automated decisions that legally or significantly affect individuals. The amendments also modernize cross-border transfer mechanisms (SCCs, BCRs, adequacy decisions), introduce 72-hour breach notification aligned with GDPR, expand PIPC enforcement powers, and clarify extraterritorial scope covering foreign online service providers processing Korean residents' data.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Controllers using automated decision-making must notify individuals of their right to explanation and to request human review
- Provide explanation of automated decision logic, significance, and consequences upon individual request
- Pause automated decision effects pending human review when objection is filed
- Notify PIPC of data breaches within 72 hours
- Foreign online service providers processing Korean residents' data must designate a domestic representative

## Overview / Background

South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA, 개인정보 보호법) is the country's primary data protection statute, administered by the Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC). Originally enacted in 2011 and significantly revised in 2020, the 2023 amendments (Act No. 19234, enacted March 14, 2023, effective September 15, 2023) represent the most substantial overhaul of PIPA since its inception, introducing rights specifically addressing automated decision-making and AI systems, strengthening the cross-border transfer framework, and substantially expanding PIPC's enforcement powers.

South Korea's PIPA framework has long been characterized by its GDPR-adjacent structure — emphasizing lawful basis, data subject rights, and independent supervisory authority — while incorporating Korean-specific elements such as the "pseudonymous information" regime enabling research and AI development uses.
