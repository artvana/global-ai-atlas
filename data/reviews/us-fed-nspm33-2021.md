## Summary (verify for accuracy)

NSPM-33, issued January 14, 2021 (final days of Trump administration), establishes national security policy for federally-funded R&D. It addresses the risk of foreign governments — particularly China — accessing US research through talent recruitment programs, undisclosed affiliations, and research theft. Key requirements: disclosure of foreign funding/affiliations for all federally-funded researchers, standardized disclosure forms across agencies, export controls alignment, and agency-specific research security programs. The memo was implemented through NSPM-33 Implementation Guidance (January 2022) issued by the Biden OSTP.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Federally-funded researchers must disclose all foreign affiliations and funding sources
- Federal agencies must standardize disclosure and certification requirements for research grants
- Agencies must develop research security programs covering cybersecurity, export control, and foreign travel
- Institutions receiving federal R&D funding must implement research security training
