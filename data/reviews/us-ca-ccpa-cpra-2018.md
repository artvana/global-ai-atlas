## Summary (verify for accuracy)

CCPA/CPRA grants Californians rights to opt out of automated decision-making technology (ADMT), request human review of significant AI decisions, and access data used in profiling. CPRA's 2023 ADMT regulations required by CPPA are among the most expansive in the US for limiting algorithmic profiling.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Right to opt out of automated decision-making using sensitive personal information
- Right to know when profiling is used to make consequential decisions
- Right to limit use of sensitive personal information in AI systems
- CPPA to issue regulations on automated decision-making technology access and correction rights
