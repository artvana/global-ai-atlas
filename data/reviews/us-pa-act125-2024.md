## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Pennsylvania Act 125/2024 criminalizes creation and distribution of intimate/sexual deepfakes and classifies explicit AI-generated depictions of minors as CSAM. Makes it a crime to disseminate AI sexual depictions of current/former intimate partners with intent to harass. The PA OAG has already charged individuals under this law.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Criminal prohibition on creation and distribution of AI-generated sexual deepfakes
- AI-generated depictions of minors classified as CSAM
- Criminal for disseminating AI sexual depictions of intimate partners with intent to harass
