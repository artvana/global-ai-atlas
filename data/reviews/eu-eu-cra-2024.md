## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Establishes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for all products with digital elements placed on the EU market, including AI systems. Manufacturers must conduct security risk assessments, implement security-by-design, provide security updates throughout product lifecycle, and report actively exploited vulnerabilities within 24 hours. AI systems that are "products with digital elements" must comply alongside AI Act requirements.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Security risk assessment required before market placement
- Implement security-by-design and security-by-default
- Provide security updates throughout product lifecycle (minimum 5 years)
- Report exploited vulnerabilities to ENISA within 24 hours
- Critical products require third-party conformity assessment
