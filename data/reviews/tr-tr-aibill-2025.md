## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Turkish omnibus bill amending multiple laws to address AI: introduces statutory AI definition; mandates deepfake content labelling; creates 6-hour takedown obligation for AI-generated content violating personal rights; grants BTK emergency blocking powers for harmful AI-generated content; establishes joint liability for AI developers and platform operators; amends Internet Law (No. 5651), Turkish Penal Code, KVKK (Law 6698), Electronic Commerce Law, and Cybersecurity Law. Submitted to Grand National Assembly November 2025; passage timeline uncertain.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI-generated content depicting real persons must be labelled
- Platform operators must remove personal-rights-violating AI content within 6 hours
- BTK empowered to issue emergency access blocking orders without prior judicial authorization
- Joint liability for AI developers and content providers for harmful AI-generated content
- Criminal penalties for deliberate deepfake creation to harm reputation
- Critical AI system operators must conduct periodic cybersecurity assessments

## Overview

The Bill on Amendments to Certain Laws Regarding Artificial Intelligence (Yapay Zeka Alanında Bazı Kanunlarda Değişiklik Yapılması Hakkında Kanun Teklifi, Esas No. 2/3358) was submitted to the Turkish Grand National Assembly (Türkiye Büyük Millet Meclisi) on 7 November 2025. The bill is an omnibus instrument amending multiple existing Turkish laws to address AI-related challenges, particularly around AI-generated content, deepfakes, and digital platform liability.

The bill does not establish a comprehensive AI regulatory framework modeled on the EU AI Act; instead, it targets specific harms identified by Turkish regulators — particularly deepfake content, AI-generated disinformation, and unauthorized use of personal likenesses — through amendments to existing legislation governing internet content, telecommunications, criminal law, and data protection.
