## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The AU Continental AI Strategy, adopted by the African Union Assembly in February 2024, sets a continent-wide framework for AI development and governance across 55 member states. It is structured around five strategic objectives: developing African AI capabilities; ensuring responsible and trustworthy AI; creating AI-enabling infrastructure; building human capacity; and establishing an African AI governance architecture. The strategy mandates development of an AU AI Policy Framework by 2025.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Member states to develop national AI strategies aligned with AU framework
- Establish AU AI governance architecture and regulatory coordination
- Promote African AI research and data centres
- Ensure AI benefits reach marginalised and rural populations
- Develop AU AI Policy Framework by 2025
