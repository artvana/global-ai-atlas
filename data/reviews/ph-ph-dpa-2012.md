## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The Philippines' comprehensive data protection law prohibits solely automated decisions with legal effects without legal authorization or data subject consent (Section 21). The NPC issued Advisory No. 2024-04 explicitly applying DPA principles to the full AI lifecycle. No AI-specific enacted law exists in the Philippines; the DPA is the primary binding framework.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Section 21: no solely automated decisions with legal/significant effects without consent or legal basis
- Data subjects have right to access personal data used by AI systems
- NPC registration for personal information controllers
- DPOs required for government and large processors
- NPC Advisory 2024-04: transparency, legitimate purpose, proportionality throughout AI lifecycle

## Law Text (excerpt)

```
# Data Privacy Act of 2012

**Republic Act No. 10173**
*Republic of the Philippines, Fifteenth Congress, Second Regular Session*
*Approved: 15 August 2012*

---

## CHAPTER I — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Section 1 — Short Title

This Act shall be known as the **"Data Privacy Act of 2012"**.

### Section 2 — Declaration of Policy

It is the policy of the State to protect the fundamental human right of privacy of communication while ensuring free flow of information to promote innovation and growth. The State recognizes the vital role of information and communications technology in nation-building and its inherent obligation to ensure that personal information in information and communications systems in the government and in the private sector are secured and protected.

### Section 3 — Definitions

- **(a) Commission** — the National Privacy Commission created by this Act.
- **(b) Consent** — any freely given, specific, informed indication of will whereby the data subject agrees to the collection and processing of personal information. May be written, electronic or recorded, or given by an authorized agent.
- **(c) Data subject** — an individual whose personal information is processed
```
