## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Ukraine's Draft Law No. 11175 on Artificial Intelligence, registered in the Verkhovna Rada in March 2024, closely mirrors the EU AI Act framework as part of Ukraine's EU accession obligations. It establishes a risk-based classification system, impact assessment requirements for high-risk AI, prohibited AI practices, content labeling, and human oversight rights. Legislative progress has been slow due to the ongoing war.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Risk-based AI classification aligned with EU AI Act
- Impact assessments for high-risk AI systems
- Prohibited AI practices (social scoring, subliminal manipulation)
- Content labeling and transparency requirements
- Human oversight for consequential AI decisions
