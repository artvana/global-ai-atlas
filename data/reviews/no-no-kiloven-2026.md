*Norway — Consultation closed September 2025; introduction to Storting expected 2026*

---

## Overview

Norway is in the process of enacting KI-loven (the AI Act), implementing the EU's Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) into Norwegian law through the European Economic Area (EEA) Agreement. As an EEA EFTA state, Norway is not an EU member but is obligated to incorporate EU Single Market legislation — including the EU AI Act — into its national legal order following a formal EEA Agreement amendment process.

The Norwegian Ministry of Digitalisation and Public Governance published a consultation package on the implementing legislation on 30 June 2025. The consultation closed on 30 September 2025. Following review of consultation responses, the government planned to introduce a formal bill (Odelstingsproposisjon) to the Storting (Norwegian parliament) during the 2026 legislative session. Formal adoption and EEA entry into force are expected in 2026–2027, subject to the EEA Joint Committee decision incorporating the EU AI Act into Annex XX of the EEA A
