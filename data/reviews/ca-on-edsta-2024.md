## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Canada's first AI-specific regulatory requirements for public institutions. Applies to Ontario public sector entities (government, agencies, police, school boards). Requires AI governance policies, AI risk assessments, and public disclosure of AI use. Amends FIPPA with privacy impact assessments.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Public sector entities must develop and publish AI governance policies
- Risk assessments required before deploying AI in public services
- Transparency reports on significant AI system use required
- Privacy Impact Assessments for personal information processing
