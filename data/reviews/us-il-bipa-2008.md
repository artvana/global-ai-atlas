## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires private entities collecting biometric identifiers (fingerprints, retinal scans, facial geometry, voiceprints) to obtain written consent, establish retention policies, and prohibits selling biometric data. Provides private right of action with statutory damages per violation. Highly relevant to AI facial recognition and voice AI systems.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prior written consent required before collecting biometric identifiers
- Publicly available written policy with retention schedule and destruction guidelines
- No sale or profit from biometric data
- Destroy data when purpose is fulfilled or 3 years, whichever first
- Private right of action: $1,000–$5,000 per violation; class actions permitted
