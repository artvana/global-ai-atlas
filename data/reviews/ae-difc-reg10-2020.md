## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The Dubai International Financial Centre's dedicated regulation for AI and automated decision-making. Controllers using autonomous or semi-autonomous systems must conduct data protection impact assessments, notify individuals when significant decisions are made automatically, provide human review rights, and register high-risk autonomous systems with the DIFC Commissioner. One of the earliest standalone AI/automated-systems regulations in the Middle East.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Conduct data protection impact assessment before deploying autonomous systems
- Notify individuals when significant decisions are made autonomously
- Provide human review option for autonomous decisions affecting rights or interests
- Register high-risk autonomous systems with DIFC Commissioner
- Maintain audit trails for all autonomous decisions
