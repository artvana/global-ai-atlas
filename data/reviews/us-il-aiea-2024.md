## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Extends Illinois AI employment obligations to include AI-assisted resume screening and candidate evaluation tools, beyond the earlier video interview AI law. Employers must notify candidates when AI analytics are used in the screening process and share race/ethnicity data of candidates reviewed by AI with the state annually.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Notify candidates when AI analytics tools are used in screening
- Annual report to state on race/ethnicity demographics of candidates reviewed by AI
- Comply with Illinois Human Rights Act anti-discrimination requirements
