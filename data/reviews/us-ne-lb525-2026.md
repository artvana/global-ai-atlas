## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires conversational AI chatbot operators to disclose AI identity to users, prohibit CSAM generation, mandate crisis referral protocols, and restrict romantic/sexual interactions with minors. Mirrors Idaho's approach with identical penalty structure.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclose AI identity at start of user interaction
- Prohibit CSAM generation
- Mandate crisis referral protocols
- Restrict romantic/sexual interactions with minors
- Penalty: $1,000 per violation, up to $500,000 per operator
