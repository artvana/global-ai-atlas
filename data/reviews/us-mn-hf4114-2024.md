## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits the creation or distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated audio or visual media depicting a political candidate within 90 days of an election without a disclosure label. Requires prominent disclosure that content is AI-generated. Provides private right of action for candidates.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclosure label required on AI-generated political media within 90 days of election
- Prohibition on materially deceptive AI-generated candidate depictions without disclosure
- Private right of action for affected candidates
- Platform liability for knowing distribution of non-disclosed AI election content
