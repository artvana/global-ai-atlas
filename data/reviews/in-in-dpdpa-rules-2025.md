## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Implementing rules under India's 2023 DPDPA establishing specific compliance requirements for significant data fiduciaries (large-scale data processors). Rules specify consent notice formats, data protection officer qualifications, data impact assessment requirements, and Data Protection Board procedure. AI systems used by significant data fiduciaries must comply with assessment requirements.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Significant data fiduciaries must appoint data protection officer
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing
- Specified consent notice format and language requirements
- Audit and algorithmic assessment by independent data auditor annually
- Implement privacy by design mechanisms in AI systems
