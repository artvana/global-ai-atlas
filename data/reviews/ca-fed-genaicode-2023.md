## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Canada's Voluntary Code of Conduct on Generative AI was published by ISED in September 2023 as an interim governance instrument following the failure of Bill C-27 (AIDA). It applies to organisations developing and deploying advanced generative AI systems and covers: safety testing, fairness and bias assessment, transparency to users, content labeling, human oversight, and reporting of significant harms. Major Canadian tech companies and multinational AI labs signed the code.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Safety testing before deployment
- Bias and fairness assessment
- Transparency disclosures to users about AI use
- Content labeling of AI-generated material
- Human oversight mechanisms for high-stakes decisions
- Reporting significant AI harms to ISED

These advanced systems may be used to perform many different kinds of tasks — such as writing emails, answering complex questions, generating realistic images or videos, or writing software code.

While they have many benefits, advanced generative AI systems also carry a distinctly broad risk profile, due to the broad scope of data on which they are trained, their wide range of potential uses, and the scale of their deployment.
