## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Wisconsin requires disclosure when AI-generated audio or video (deepfakes) is used in political advertisements. Covers synthetic media in political communications; does not ban use, only mandates disclosure. Note: does not cover AI-generated photos.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclosure required when AI-generated audio or video used in political advertisements
- Covers synthetic media in political communications
