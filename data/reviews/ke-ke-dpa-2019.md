## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Kenya's comprehensive data protection law establishes the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) with provisions on automated processing applicable to AI systems. The Act prohibits subjecting data subjects to automated processing decisions with legal effects without adequate safeguards and disclosure.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Right not to be subject to automated processing decisions with legal effects without safeguards
- Registration of data controllers and processors with ODPC
- Data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing
- 72-hour breach notification
- Cross-border transfer restrictions
