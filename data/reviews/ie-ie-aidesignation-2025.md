## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Ireland's S.I. 366/2025 implements Article 70 of the EU AI Act by designating 15 national competent authorities across sectors. The Data Protection Commission (DPC) is designated as market surveillance authority for fundamental rights AI obligations. The Minister for Enterprise is Ireland's single point of contact with the EU AI Office.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Minister for Enterprise designated as Single Point of Contact with EU AI Office
- DPC designated as market surveillance authority for fundamental rights-related AI obligations
- Central Bank and 13 sector regulators designated as sectoral market surveillance authorities
- National AI Office to be established as coordinating body by August 2026
