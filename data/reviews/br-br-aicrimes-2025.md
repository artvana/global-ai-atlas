## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Criminalizes the creation, distribution, or use of AI-generated deepfakes and synthetic media to harass, intimidate, or commit crimes against women. Imposes criminal penalties of up to 8 years imprisonment for creating or sharing non-consensual AI-generated intimate images targeting women. Platforms must remove flagged content within 24 hours. Signed by President Lula in response to high-profile deepfake abuse cases.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibition on creating or distributing non-consensual AI-generated intimate imagery targeting women
- Platforms must remove flagged deepfake content within 24 hours
- Criminal penalties up to 8 years imprisonment for AI-enabled gender-based crimes
- Victim right of action for civil damages
