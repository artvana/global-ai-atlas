## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires mental health chatbots — AI that simulates licensed mental health professional conversations — to disclose their AI nature at multiple touchpoints: before access, if 7+ days since last use, and when asked by user. Applies to apps and platforms that simulate confidential therapy conversations.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Mental health chatbots must disclose AI identity clearly
- Must provide access to licensed professional resources
- Cannot represent as licensed mental health provider
