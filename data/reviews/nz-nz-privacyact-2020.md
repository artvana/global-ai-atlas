## Summary (verify for accuracy)

New Zealand's principal privacy statute establishing 13 Information Privacy Principles applicable to collection, use, disclosure, and storage of personal data. No express automated decision or AI-specific provisions, but IPP 8 (data accuracy) and IPP 11 (disclosure limits) apply to AI systems. The Privacy Commissioner issued non-binding AI guidance in September 2023.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- 13 Information Privacy Principles govern collection, use, disclosure, and storage of personal data
- Mandatory breach notification to Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals
- Privacy Commissioner may issue compliance notices and access directions
- Overseas transfer restrictions: comparable safeguards required
