*Florida — June 1, 2025*

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

HB 757 criminalizes the willful creation, solicitation, or possession with intent to promote nonconsensual AI-generated sexually explicit imagery of an identifiable person, and provides a civil cause of action for victims.

### Criminal Offense

The bill makes it a third-degree felony (punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment) to willfully:
- Create nonconsensual AI-generated sexually explicit imagery of an identifiable real person
- Solicit another person to create such imagery
- Possess such imagery with the intent to promote, distribute, or disseminate it

The offense applies to both wholly AI-generated imagery (content in which no actual person was depicted in the source material) and AI-manipulated imagery (content that superimposes or digitally alters a person's likeness onto existing material). Lack of consent from the depicted person is an element of the offense.
