## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Utah was an early mover on AI disclosure, requiring companies using generative AI to disclose AI use upon consumer request. Regulated professionals must provide advance notice of AI use. Creates Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy to develop risk-tiered AI governance frameworks.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Regulated entities must disclose AI use to consumers upon request
- Licensed professionals must provide advance notice of AI use
- Establishes Utah Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy
- Prohibits falsely representing AI as human
