## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires explicit free and informed consent before AI systems use a person's voice or image in media. Mandates remuneration for consented use. Covers voice cloning, deepfakes, and generative models. Passed 335-129 in Chamber of Deputies. First Latin American law directly regulating AI voice/image rights.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Employers cannot use AI to replicate worker voice or image without explicit written consent
- Workers have right to refuse AI likeness reproduction for commercial use
- Labour law remedies apply for unauthorised AI use of worker identity
