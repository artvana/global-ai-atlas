## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Biden's landmark 2023 AI executive order directed federal agencies to develop safety standards, required developers of powerful AI systems to share safety test results with the government, and tasked NIST with creating AI risk benchmarks. It addressed everything from AI in hiring to biosecurity risks and was the most comprehensive US federal AI governance action prior to its revocation.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Frontier AI developers with training compute above threshold must share safety test results with NIST
- NIST to develop AI risk management benchmarks and red-teaming standards
- Federal agencies must conduct AI risk assessments before procurement
- OMB to issue binding guidance on government AI use and procurement
