## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework (Second Edition) is a detailed voluntary guide for private-sector organisations on responsible AI deployment, published by PDPC at the World Economic Forum in January 2020. Its most distinctive contribution is a risk-calibrated human oversight matrix — distinguishing human-in-the-loop, human-over-the-loop, and human-out-of-the-loop oversight models based on the probability and severity of harm — along with practical guidance on training data governance, algorithmic explainability, and customer transparency. Widely cited internationally and influential on ASEAN, OECD, and UK regulatory thinking.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Principle 1: Establish internal governance structures with clear accountability for AI systems at board/senior management level
- Principle 2: Calibrate human oversight (human-in-loop / over-loop / out-of-loop) based on probability and severity of harm matrix
- Principle 3: Ensure training data is representative, bias-tested, and documented; monitor for model drift
- Principle 3: Provide explainability for AI decisions affecting individuals (technical and process-level)
- Principle 4: Inform individuals when they are interacting with an AI system
- Principle 4: Provide accessible mechanisms for individuals to raise concerns about AI-based decisions
- Self-Assessment Guide: Complete 61-question self-assessment to identify governance gaps and prioritise improvements

## Overview

The Singapore Model AI Governance Framework (MAIGF), Second Edition, is a voluntary, non-binding guidance document published by Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC). It provides detailed, practical guidance for private-sector organisations on how to deploy AI responsibly and in a manner that is explainable, transparent, and human-centric.

The Framework was first published in January 2019 (First Edition) at the World Economic Forum. The Second Edition, released in January 2020, incorporates feedback from industry consultation, expands coverage to include new technology developments, and adds a self-assessment guide and a compendium of use cases illustrating implementation across industries including financial services, healthcare, and human resources.
