## Summary (verify for accuracy)

EO 13960, signed by President Trump on December 3, 2020, established nine principles to guide all federal agencies in the design, development, acquisition, and use of artificial intelligence. The principles — lawful, purposeful and performance-driven, accurate and reliable, safe and secure, understandable, responsible and traceable, regularly monitored, transparent, and accountable — were intended to promote trustworthy AI across the federal government and foster public trust. Agencies were required to submit plans documenting how their existing AI use cases align with these principles. Implemented in conjunction with the AI in Government Act of 2020 and the NIST AI standards effort.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Federal agencies must design, develop, acquire, and use AI consistently with nine enumerated trustworthy AI principles
- Agencies must designate officials responsible for AI governance and submit consistency plans to OMB within 60 days
- Each agency must inventory AI use cases and document alignment with the nine principles
- NIST to develop standards and guidelines for trustworthy AI consistent with the principles
- Agencies must ensure AI applications are regularly tested and monitored against the nine principles

## Overview

Executive Order 13960 directed all federal agencies to design, develop, acquire, and use artificial intelligence in a manner consistent with nine enumerated principles of "trustworthy AI." The order was aimed at fostering public trust in government use of AI, ensuring accountability and transparency, and positioning the federal government as a model for responsible AI adoption. It was issued in the final weeks of Trump's first term alongside the **National AI Initiative Act of 2020** (enacted January 1, 2021) and complemented the earlier **Executive Order 13859** (February 2019), which had established the American AI Initiative and directed federal investment in AI research and development.
