## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Extends California right of publicity protections to deceased performers by requiring prior consent from the estate before creating or using an AI-generated digital replica of a deceased performer in an audiovisual work, sound recording, or live performance. Applies to performances and recordings made during the performer's life.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prior consent from estate required to create AI digital replica of deceased performer
- Applies to audiovisual works, sound recordings, and live performances
- Civil cause of action with actual damages or $75,000 per violation
- Estate holds rights for 70 years post-death
