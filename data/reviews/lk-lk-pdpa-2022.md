## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Sri Lanka became the first South Asian country to enact comprehensive privacy legislation. Data subjects may object to automated processing creating irreversible and continuous impacts on rights and freedoms. The Data Protection Authority was established August 2023; core compliance obligations delayed by 2025 amendments.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Data subjects may object to automated processing creating irreversible and continuous impact on rights and freedoms
- Data controllers must implement privacy by design
- Data Protection Authority established to regulate compliance
- Data breach notification and security requirements
- Cross-border transfer restrictions
