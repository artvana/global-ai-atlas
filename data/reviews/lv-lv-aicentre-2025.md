## Overview

The Law on the Latvian Artificial Intelligence Centre (Mākslīgā intelekta centra likums) was adopted by the Saeima (Latvian parliament) on 6 March 2025 and entered into force on 20 March 2025. It establishes the Latvian Artificial Intelligence Centre (AI Centre) as a public-private-academic foundation under Latvian law, making Latvia the first European Union member state to create a dedicated national AI institution by statute.

The Law is primarily an institutional and governance instrument — it creates and defines the mandate, structure, and financing of the AI Centre — rather than a substantive regulation of AI systems or providers. Its significance lies in establishing Latvia's national infrastructure for AI policy, research coordination, regulatory sandbox operation, and international engagement under the EU AI Act framework.
