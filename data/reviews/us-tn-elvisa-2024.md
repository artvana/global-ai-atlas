## Summary (verify for accuracy)

First US state law to specifically address AI voice cloning. Expands Tennessee's right-of-publicity law to protect voice as an attribute of personality alongside name, image, and likeness. Prohibits using AI to mimic a person's voice without consent. Provides both criminal misdemeanor and civil action remedies.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibition on using AI to mimic individual voice without consent
- Civil cause of action for unauthorized AI voice cloning
- Criminal misdemeanor for commercial unauthorized voice cloning
- Applies to AI sound recordings distributed in commerce
