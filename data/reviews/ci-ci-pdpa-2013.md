## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Côte d'Ivoire's comprehensive personal data protection law designating ARTCI as the supervisory body for data protection in addition to its telecommunications regulatory role. Provides data protection principles, lawful processing bases, special category data protections, mandatory ARTCI declaration, data subject rights, cross-border transfer restrictions, and criminal penalties.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Declare processing activities to ARTCI before commencing
- Obtain prior ARTCI authorization for sensitive or high-risk processing
- Comply with data protection principles (proportionality, accuracy, purpose limitation)
- Protect special category data with explicit consent or legal authority
- Provide data subject rights (information, access, rectification, opposition)
- Implement technical and organizational security measures
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with adequate protection

## Overview

Côte d'Ivoire's Law 2013-450 of June 19, 2013, on the protection of personal data is the country's comprehensive data protection framework. The law designates the Autorité de Régulation des Télécommunications / TIC de Côte d'Ivoire (ARTCI) — the telecommunications and ICT regulatory authority — as the supervisory body for personal data protection in addition to its existing telecom regulatory mandate. The law applies to all processing of personal data carried out in Ivory Coast, covering both automated and manual processing. It follows the French civil law tradition and draws on the ECOWAS data protection framework, reflecting regional harmonization efforts in West Africa.
