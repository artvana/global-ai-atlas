## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Thailand's AI Act establishes a three-tier risk system: prohibited unacceptable-risk AI, high-risk systems requiring registration and risk management under ISO/IEC 42001:2023, and lower-risk systems with lighter obligations. High-risk providers must register, implement risk management, report serious incidents, and ensure human oversight.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- High-risk AI operators must obtain certification from National AI Committee
- AI developers must register AI systems with regulatory authority
- Disclosure obligations for AI use in public-facing applications
- Establish AI Supervisory Committee with cross-ministry membership
