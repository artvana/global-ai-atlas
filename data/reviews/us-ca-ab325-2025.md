## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends the Cartwright Act to prohibit anticompetitive use of common pricing algorithms. Makes it unlawful to coerce competitors into adopting algorithm-recommended prices and lowers pleading standards for California antitrust claims involving algorithmic coordination.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibition on using common pricing algorithm with competitor data to coordinate prices
- Cannot coerce competitors into adopting algorithm-recommended prices
- Private right of action under Cartwright Act; treble damages available
