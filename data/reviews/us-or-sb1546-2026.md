## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires conversational AI operators to clearly disclose to users that they are interacting with an AI system. Includes a private right of action with statutory damages of $1,000 per violation, making it notable for providing a private enforcement mechanism.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Conversational AI operators must clearly disclose that users are interacting with AI
- Disclosure required at start of interaction and upon sincere user request
- Private right of action: $1,000 per violation, class actions permitted
