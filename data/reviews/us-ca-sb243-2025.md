## Summary (verify for accuracy)

First US law regulating AI companion chatbots. Requires disclosure that the companion is an AI, mandates safety protocols for minors including suicide/self-harm prevention, and prohibits romantic or sexual interactions with minors. Includes private right of action and AG enforcement.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclose AI identity when user sincerely asks if interacting with human
- Prohibit romantic or sexual interactions with minors
- Implement suicide/self-harm safe messaging protocols
- Private right of action: $1,000 per violation
