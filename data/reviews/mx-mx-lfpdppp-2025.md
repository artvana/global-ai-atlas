## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Mexico's 2025 comprehensive revision of its data protection framework, replacing the 2010 LFPDPPP. Introduces AI-specific provisions including: mandatory disclosure of AI and automated decision-making in privacy notices, algorithmic logic transparency requirements, right to object to automated decision-making for high-risk decisions, human-in-the-loop requirements, and data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing including AI profiling.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclose AI and automated decision-making use in privacy notices
- Provide algorithmic logic transparency to data subjects
- Implement human-in-the-loop for high-risk automated decisions
- Allow individuals to object to automated decision-making
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for AI profiling

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope

Mexico's Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares (LFPDPPP), originally enacted in 2010, was substantially amended in 2025 to introduce AI-specific provisions, modernize the consent framework, expand data subject rights in the context of automated processing, and align the Law more closely with contemporary international standards including the GDPR and the Latin American data protection modernization trends exemplified by Chile's Ley 21.719.
