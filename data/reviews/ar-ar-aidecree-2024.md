## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Decree 1182/2024 establishes Argentina's National AI Plan and creates an AI Ethics Committee within the national public administration. It requires impact assessments for AI systems used in government decisions, adopts a risk-based approach aligned with OECD principles, and mandates transparency for AI systems affecting citizens. The decree applies to national executive branch agencies and sets principles for future legislation.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Impact assessment for AI in government decisions
- AI Ethics Committee review of high-risk applications
- Transparency to citizens of AI use in public services
- Adherence to OECD AI Principles in government procurement
