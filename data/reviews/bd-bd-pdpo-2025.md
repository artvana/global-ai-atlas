## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Bangladesh's first personal data protection law. AI-relevant provisions include: data protection impact assessments required for high-risk processing (including profiling and automated decision-making), individuals have the right to object to purely automated decisions affecting their legal rights, and biometric and health data are classified as sensitive categories requiring explicit consent.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain explicit consent for processing sensitive personal data including biometrics
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk AI processing
- Provide individuals the right to object to purely automated decisions
- Appoint a Data Protection Officer for high-risk processing
- Report data breaches to the Data Protection Authority within prescribed timeframes

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope

The Bangladesh Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 is Bangladesh's first comprehensive, standalone statute governing the collection, processing, storage, and transfer of personal data. Promulgated as an Ordinance in 2025 — reflecting the legislative mechanism available under the interim government that followed the political transition of 2024 — the Ordinance draws heavily on the GDPR model and on the data protection frameworks of neighboring countries, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Its primary purpose is to protect the fundamental right to privacy of natural persons in Bangladesh while enabling the lawful use of personal data for legitimate commercial, governmental, and social purposes.

The Ordinance applies to the processing of personal data where: the data fiduciary (controller) or data processor is established in Bangladesh; the processing relates to offering goods or services to data principals (data subjects) located in Bangladesh; or the processing involves the monitoring of behavior of data principals in Bangladesh insofar as that behavior takes place within Bangladesh.
