## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Colorado's landmark risk-based AI law requires developers and deployers of 'high-risk AI' systems to use reasonable care to prevent algorithmic discrimination. Mandates impact assessments, consumer notifications of AI use, documentation of decision-making, and annual reporting. Covers high-stakes domains: housing, lending, employment, education, healthcare, insurance.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Deployers of high-risk AI must use reasonable care to prevent algorithmic discrimination
- Conduct and document algorithmic impact assessment before deployment
- Notify consumers when high-risk AI is used in consequential decisions
- Provide opportunity for consumers to appeal AI-assisted decisions
- Developers must provide deployers with impact assessment documentation
