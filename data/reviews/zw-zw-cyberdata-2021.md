## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Zimbabwe's combined cybersecurity and data protection framework in a single statute. Establishes POTRAZ as the data protection supervisory authority and the Cybersecurity Centre for operational cybersecurity functions. Provides eight data protection principles, data subject rights, sensitive data protections, breach notification obligations, critical infrastructure protection requirements, and cybercrime offenses.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Register data controllers with POTRAZ
- Comply with eight data protection principles
- Implement appropriate security measures
- Provide data subject rights (access, rectification, objection, erasure)
- Protect sensitive data categories including biometric data
- Notify POTRAZ and data subjects of significant breaches
- Comply with critical infrastructure cybersecurity standards

## Overview

Zimbabwe's Cybersecurity and Data Protection Act (Chapter 12:07) is a landmark combined statute that addresses data protection, cybersecurity, and cybercrime in a single legislative instrument — a distinctive approach among African jurisdictions. Enacted in December 2021 and effective from January 1, 2022, the Act establishes POTRAZ (Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe) as the data protection supervisory authority and creates the Cybersecurity Centre for operational cybersecurity functions. The Act positions Zimbabwe within the broader African Union Data Policy Framework and aligns with the Malabo Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection.
