## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The TAKE IT DOWN Act requires online platforms to remove non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), including AI-generated deepfake sexual images, within 48 hours of a valid takedown request. It criminalizes the publication of such content and tasks the FTC with enforcement. The first federal law specifically targeting AI-generated deepfake pornography.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Platforms must remove non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) including AI-generated within 48 hours of valid request
- Criminal prohibition on publishing NCII
- FTC to enforce against non-compliant platforms under Section 5
