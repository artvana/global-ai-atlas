## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The most debated AI safety bill of 2024. Would have required California-based developers of frontier AI models (trained with ≥10²⁶ FLOPs or costing over $100M) to implement safety and security programs, conduct pre-deployment hazardous capability evaluations, maintain kill-switch capability, and submit to annual third-party audits. Also proposed a new Frontier Model Division within the California Department of Technology. Passed the California Legislature on August 28, 2024. Vetoed by Governor Newsom on September 29, 2024 as technically flawed and harmful to California AI innovation.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Implement written safety and security program before training any covered model
- Conduct pre-deployment hazardous capability evaluations (CBRN, critical infrastructure, cyberweapons)
- Maintain ability to fully shut down all covered models (kill switch)
- Protect model weights against unauthorized access with NIST-standard cybersecurity
- Submit to annual third-party audits by AG-certified auditors
- Retain safety testing records for at least three years
- Report critical safety incidents to new Frontier Model Division
