## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Bill C-63 (first reading February 26, 2024) proposed creating a new Online Harms Act establishing a Digital Safety Commission with powers to regulate online platforms. It imposed duties of care on platforms to mitigate harmful content (CSAM, non-consensual intimate images, hate speech, incitement to violence), required age-appropriate design, restricted algorithmic recommendation of harmful content, and created a Digital Safety Ombudsperson. The bill died when Parliament was prorogued in January 2025.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Regulated platforms must implement duty of care to protect users from harmful content
- Platforms must not algorithmically recommend content that constitutes child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, or incitement to violence
- Platforms must implement age verification for adult content
- Annual digital safety plans and transparency reports required
- Emergency removal orders for CSAM within 24 hours
