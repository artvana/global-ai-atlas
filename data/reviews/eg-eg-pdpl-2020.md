## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Egypt's first comprehensive data protection law covers automated processing with provisions (Article 34) applicable to AI-driven decisions. Implementing regulations remained in draft as of mid-2025, limiting full enforcement; the law is in force but enforcement capacity is developing.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Controller registration with PDPC required
- Lawful basis required for all personal data processing
- Data subject rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction
- Cross-border transfer restrictions
- Breach notification requirements

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope

Egypt's Law No. 151 of 2020 on Personal Data Protection, promulgated on 15 July 2020 and accompanied by Executive Regulations issued in 2021, represents Egypt's first comprehensive legislative framework governing the collection, processing, storage, transfer, and use of personal data. Prior to Law 151/2020, data protection in Egypt was addressed only through scattered sector-specific provisions in laws governing banking, telecommunications, and electronic transactions, and through general constitutional provisions recognizing privacy as a fundamental right. Law 151/2020 fills that gap with a unified, modern framework influenced by international standards including the GDPR, though adapted to Egypt's legal traditions and administrative context.

The Law applies to all processing of personal data carried out wholly or partly within Egypt, to any controller established in Egypt regardless of where the processing occurs, and to any controller established outside Egypt that processes personal data of persons residing in Egypt in connection with offering goods or services to those persons or monitoring their behavior within Egypt.
