## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Broadly criminalizes forged digital likenesses — AI-manipulated audio or video indistinguishable from genuine and likely to deceive. Applies to creation and distribution. Provides both criminal (gross misdemeanor) and civil ($3,000/violation) remedies. Carve-outs for satire, public interest, and fair use.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Extends Washington right of publicity to cover digital replicas and AI-generated likenesses
- Consent required for commercial use of digital replica
- Prohibition on non-consensual AI replicas for sexual content
- Civil action: actual damages plus up to $10,000 per violation
