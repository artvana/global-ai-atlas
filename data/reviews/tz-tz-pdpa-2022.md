## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Tanzania's first comprehensive personal data protection law establishing the Personal Data Protection Commission. Incorporates modern data protection concepts including nine principles, data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection), DPO requirements, DPIA obligations for high-risk processing, sensitive data categories, mandatory breach notification, and cross-border transfer rules.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Register with PDPC as data controller or processor
- Comply with nine data protection principles
- Appoint a DPO for public bodies and high-risk processing
- Conduct DPIAs for high-risk processing activities
- Provide data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection)
- Protect sensitive data categories with explicit consent or legal authority
- Notify PDPC and affected individuals of significant breaches
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with adequate protection or approved safeguards

## Overview

Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act 2022 is the country's first comprehensive personal data protection law, establishing a modern data protection framework aligned with global standards. The Act establishes the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) as the independent supervisory authority responsible for overseeing and enforcing data protection requirements. The Act applies to any organization processing personal data of Tanzania residents, whether the processing takes place in Tanzania or elsewhere. The law draws on international best practices including the GDPR and African Union Data Policy Framework, and incorporates contemporary concepts such as data portability and data protection officers. Note: Commission establishment and implementing regulations were in progress as of 2026; some provisions may have staggered implementation.
