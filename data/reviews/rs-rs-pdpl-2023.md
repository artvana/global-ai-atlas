## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection (Law No. 87/2018), modelled closely on GDPR, provides the operative AI governance framework for Serbia pending specific AI legislation. It covers: data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing, right not to be subject to solely automated decisions, explicit consent for biometric data, and data subject access and rectification rights applicable to AI-processed data.
