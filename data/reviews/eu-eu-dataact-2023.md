## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The EU Data Act establishes harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data generated by connected products (IoT) and related services across the EU. Key AI implications include: users gain access to data generated by AI-enabled products; rules restrict cloud-switching lock-in affecting AI infrastructure; data sharing with public bodies in emergencies is mandated; and AI model vendors cannot exploit IoT device data without user consent.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Connected product manufacturers must give users access to device-generated data
- Data holders must share data with users and authorised third parties on request
- Cloud service providers must enable data portability and switching within 30 days
- Prohibit AI model vendors from using non-personal IoT data without explicit agreement
- Public bodies may request emergency data access in declared crises

**Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 December 2023 on harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data**

*Applies from 12 September 2025*

---

## Chapter I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Subject matter and scope

Establishes harmonised rules on:
(a) making data from connected products and related services accessible to users;
(b) data sharing between data holders and data recipients under Union law;
(c) unfair contractual terms in B2B data sharing;
(d) public sector access to private-sector data in exceptional need;
(e) switching between data processing services;
(f) interoperability standards for data spaces.

Applies to both personal and non-personal data.
