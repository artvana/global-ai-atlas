## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Vermont Act 161/2024 explicitly criminalizes nonconsensual deepfake pornography by amending Vermont's Revenge Porn Statute. AI-generated sexual imagery of identifiable persons without consent is treated equivalently to non-AI intimate image abuse.
