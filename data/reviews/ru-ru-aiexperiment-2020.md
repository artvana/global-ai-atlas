## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Russia's experimental legal regime framework for AI innovation in Moscow. Grants participating companies waivers from specified general laws (data protection, transport, medical) to develop AI, autonomous vehicles, medical diagnostics, and fintech applications. Establishes a regulatory sandbox with oversight by the Ministry of Economic Development and sector regulators. Participants must disclose AI use and conduct safety assessments for high-risk deployments. The sandbox model has expanded to additional sectors and cities.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Participants must obtain sandbox designation from Ministry of Economic Development
- Disclose AI use to users in participating deployments
- Conduct safety assessments for high-risk AI applications
- Submit periodic compliance reports to oversight authority
- Maintain participant status — violations result in sandbox exclusion and reinstatement of general law obligations
