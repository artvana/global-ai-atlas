## Overview

North Dakota HB 1167 requires political communications and advertisements that are created wholly or in part by artificial intelligence to carry a prominent disclaimer disclosing AI involvement. The law applies to paid political ads and mass communications distributed in North Dakota. Violations constitute a Class B misdemeanor. The law is part of a broader national trend of state-level AI political disclosure mandates, consistent with similar laws enacted in Wisconsin (2023 Act 123), California (AB 2839), and Minnesota (HF 4114).
