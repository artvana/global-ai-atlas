## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The SEC's proposed rule would require broker-dealers and investment advisers to evaluate and eliminate or neutralise conflicts of interest when using predictive data analytics (including AI and ML) in investor interactions. The rule targets AI systems that optimise for firm interests at investors' expense. It requires: conflict identification through testing, documented conflict elimination or neutralisation, disclosure to investors, and compliance program requirements.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Test AI/ML tools for conflicts of interest
- Eliminate or neutralise identified conflicts
- Disclose use of predictive analytics to investors
- Maintain compliance programs for AI investment tools

## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC or Commission) is proposing new rules under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 and the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to address conflicts of interest associated with broker-dealers' and investment advisers' use of predictive data analytics (PDA), including artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and related technologies, in investor interactions.

The proposed rules would require broker-dealers and investment advisers (collectively, "covered firms") that use covered technologies in investor interactions to:

1. Evaluate whether such use involves or results in a conflict of interest that places the firm's or an associated person's interest ahead of the investor's interest;
