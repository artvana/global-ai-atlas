# Tennessee SB 1580 — Prohibition on Healthcare AI Chatbots Claiming Professional Status

**Enacted April 1, 2026 | Amends T.C.A. Title 63 (Professions of the Healing Arts)**

---

## Overview

Tennessee SB 1580 prohibits healthcare-related AI chatbots and automated systems from representing themselves as licensed healthcare professionals, and requires clear disclosures when AI systems provide health-related advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. The Act amends Tennessee's existing healthcare professional licensing laws to address AI impersonation of licensed practitioners.

---

## Definitions

**"Healthcare AI chatbot"** means any automated conversational system, virtual assistant, or artificial intelligence application that:
(a) interacts with users through natural language interfaces, whether voice or text;
(b) provides, generates, or facilitates information about health conditions, symptoms, medications, diagnoses, or treatments; and
(c) is deployed by or on behalf of a healthcare entity, or is made publicly available to individuals seeking healthcare information.
