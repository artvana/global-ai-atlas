## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires AI developers with 1M+ monthly users to embed provenance data (watermarks/metadata) in AI-generated or materially altered images, video, and audio. Providers must offer free detection tools for identifying their AI-generated content. Effective Feb 1, 2027.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI developers with 1M+ monthly users must embed provenance data in AI-generated or materially altered images, video, audio
- Provide free public detection tool for verifying AI-generated content
