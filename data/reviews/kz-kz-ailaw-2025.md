## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Central Asia's first comprehensive AI law establishes a risk-based framework classifying AI systems by impact on citizens' safety and rights. Prohibits behavioral manipulation, emotion recognition without consent, social scoring, and illegal data collection. Mandates labeling of AI-generated synthetic content. Creates a national AI platform supporting domestic model development.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI system owners must classify systems by risk level and manage lifecycle risks
- Prohibited uses: behavioral manipulation, social scoring, emotion recognition without consent
- AI-generated synthetic content must be clearly labeled
- High-risk AI systems require formal approval process
- Documentation, safety, and reliability requirements for AI system operators
