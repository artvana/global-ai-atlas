## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The Algorithm Charter for Aotearoa New Zealand, launched in July 2020, is a voluntary commitment by New Zealand government agencies to use algorithms transparently and accountably. Signatory agencies commit to: explaining how algorithms affect decisions, considering Māori data sovereignty (Te Tiriti o Waitangi obligations), assessing and managing risks, engaging with affected communities, and maintaining human oversight. By the Year 1 Review (December 2021), 26 agencies had signed.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Signatory agencies must explain how algorithms affect decisions that affect people
- Engage with people affected by algorithms before and during development
- Consider impacts on Māori and Te Tiriti obligations
- Maintain human oversight and ability to override algorithmic decisions
- Annually report on charter compliance to Stats NZ
