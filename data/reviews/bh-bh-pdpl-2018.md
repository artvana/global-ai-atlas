## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Bahrain's PDPL was one of the first comprehensive data protection statutes in the GCC, establishing the Personal Data Protection Authority and a consent-based framework covering all organizations processing personal data in Bahrain or about Bahrain residents. Key provisions include sensitive data categories (health, genetic, biometric, racial, financial), data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, objection), cross-border transfer restrictions, mandatory breach notification, DPO requirements for large-scale or sensitive processing, and a licensing regime for high-risk processing activities.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain consent or establish another lawful basis before processing personal data
- Classify and apply heightened protections for sensitive data categories including biometric, health, and financial data
- Respond to data subject access, correction, deletion, and objection requests within statutory timeframes
- Notify PDPA and affected individuals of personal data breaches without undue delay
- Obtain a PDPA license before commencing large-scale processing of sensitive personal data

## Overview / Background

Law No. 30 of 2018 on the Protection of Personal Data (the Bahrain PDPL) is the Kingdom of Bahrain's comprehensive personal data protection statute, enacted on July 12, 2018 and entering into force on August 1, 2019 (following a one-year implementation period). Bahrain was one of the first Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) states to enact comprehensive data protection legislation, establishing a framework that has influenced subsequent regional laws in Oman, Jordan, and other Middle Eastern jurisdictions.

The PDPL established the Personal Data Protection Authority (PDPA) as Bahrain's independent data protection supervisory authority. The PDPA has powers to issue licenses, receive and investigate complaints, conduct audits, issue guidance, and impose administrative penalties. The Minister responsible for information and communications technology oversees the PDPA's operations.
