## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The first UN General Assembly resolution on AI governance, adopted by consensus in March 2024. It calls on member states to develop safe, secure, and trustworthy AI; emphasises the need to bridge the digital divide; highlights AI risks to human rights; and requests the Secretary-General to establish a High-Level Advisory Body on AI.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- States to promote safe, secure, trustworthy AI for SDGs
- Bridge the AI divide between developed and developing nations
- Respect human rights in AI development and use
- Support the UN Secretary-General's AI governance initiatives

## Background and Significance

Resolution A/RES/78/265 is the first standalone resolution on artificial intelligence adopted by the United Nations General Assembly. It marks a watershed moment in global AI governance, establishing that the international community — including states with divergent approaches to technology regulation such as China and Russia — can reach consensus on foundational principles for the responsible development and deployment of AI systems.

The resolution was introduced as document A/78/L.49 and adopted at the 61st plenary meeting of the 78th session of the General Assembly on 21 March 2024.
