## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Oman's first comprehensive data protection law establishes a consent-based framework covering all organizations processing personal data in Oman or about Oman residents, administered by the Information Technology Authority. Key provisions include six core processing principles, sensitive data categories (health, genetic, biometric, racial, religious), data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal), DPO requirements for large-scale processing, mandatory breach notification, and cross-border transfer restrictions. Implementing regulations issued in 2023 provide procedural detail.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Establish a valid lawful basis before processing personal data
- Apply heightened protections for sensitive data categories including biometric, genetic, health, and religious data
- Respond to data subject access, correction, deletion, restriction, and objection requests within 30 days
- Notify ITA and affected individuals of personal data breaches without undue delay
- Appoint a DPO for large-scale processing of personal or sensitive data or systematic monitoring activities

## Overview / Background

The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), promulgated by Royal Decree No. 6/2022, is the Sultanate of Oman's first comprehensive personal data protection statute. Enacted on February 9, 2022, and entering into force one year later on February 9, 2023 (following a mandatory transition period), the PDPL establishes the legal framework for the protection of personal data in Oman and is administered by the Information Technology Authority (ITA).

Oman's PDPL reflects the influence of regional and international data protection frameworks, including Bahrain's PDPL 2018 and elements of the EU GDPR. Its enactment positions Oman as a credible digital economy participant in the Gulf Cooperation Council and enables cross-border data flows with jurisdictions requiring equivalent protection standards. The PDPL is supplemented by implementing regulations issued by the ITA in 2023.
