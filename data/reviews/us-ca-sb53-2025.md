## Summary (verify for accuracy)

California's first frontier AI safety law requires developers of large AI models (training compute above threshold) to conduct pre-deployment safety evaluations and publish safety policies. Developers must share critical safety information with regulators and implement incident response protocols. The law focuses on catastrophic risk prevention for frontier models.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Developers of covered large AI models must conduct pre-deployment safety evaluations
- Publish safety policy and incident response plan publicly
- Report critical safety incidents to California AG within 72 hours
- Implement capability-based safeguards for models above training compute threshold
