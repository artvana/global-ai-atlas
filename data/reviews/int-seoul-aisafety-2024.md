*International — May 21-22, 2024*

---

## Overview

The AI Seoul Summit of May 21-22, 2024, produced two significant outputs that together form the Seoul AI Safety package: (1) the Seoul Ministerial Statement on AI Safety — signed by governments; and (2) the Frontier AI Safety Commitments — voluntary commitments signed by major AI companies. Building directly on the Bletchley Declaration, the Seoul Summit deepened the international AI safety agenda by establishing concrete company-level commitments on safety evaluations, dangerous capability thresholds, and information sharing. Participating AI companies included Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, xAI, Amazon, Samsung, and others. The summit also formally established the international AI Safety Institute network, connecting national institutes from the UK, United States, Japan, Canada, Australia, South Korea, France, and others. The Seoul outputs represent the most operationally specific international AI safety agreements concluded to that point.
