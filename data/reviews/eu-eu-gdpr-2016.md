## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Article 22 GDPR grants individuals the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions (including profiling) that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Requires human intervention, explanation, and contestation rights when exceptions apply. First major binding regulation of AI decision-making globally.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or significant effects
- Data subject may request human review of automated decision
- Controller must explain logic of automated decision upon request
- Explicit consent required for profiling producing legal effects
- Data protection impact assessment required for systematic automated processing
