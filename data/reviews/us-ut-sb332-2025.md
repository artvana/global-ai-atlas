## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends the Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act (UAIPA) to further refine mental health chatbot regulation and generative AI disclosure requirements for consumer transactions, and extends the UAIPA's sunset date by two years to allow time for development of a permanent governance framework. Includes updated definitions and scope clarifications addressing interpretive ambiguities from the law's first year.

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 332 amends the Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act (UAIPA), originally established by SB 149 (2024) and previously amended by HB 452 (2024), making further refinements to mental health chatbot regulation, updating generative AI disclosure requirements, and extending the UAIPA's sunset date.

### Mental Health Chatbot Amendments

The bill further amends UAIPA provisions governing conversational AI systems used in mental health contexts. These amendments refine the definition of covered mental health AI services and clarify obligations for operators of AI chatbots that provide mental health support or guidance, including disclosure requirements and safety protocol mandates.
