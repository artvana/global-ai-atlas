## Overview

Lithuania's parliament (Seimas) enacted amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications (Elektroninių ryšių įstatymas) on 14 January 2025, designating national supervisory authorities for the EU AI Act ahead of the February 2025 deadline for member states to establish such authorities. Lithuania was among the first EU member states to complete this formal designation.

The amendments establish the regulatory structure for Lithuanian implementation of the EU AI Act, distributing supervisory competences across two existing institutions rather than creating a new dedicated body. Lithuania also launched the EU's first national AI regulatory sandbox pilot program in parallel with these legislative changes, allocating €3 million for its operation.
