## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Finland's AI supervision legislation package (HE 46/2025) made Finland the first EU member state with fully operational AI Act enforcement from January 1, 2026. It designates the Finnish Safety and Chemicals Agency (Tukes) as the primary market surveillance authority, with the Data Protection Ombudsman handling fundamental rights AI cases.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Designates Tukes as primary market surveillance authority for most AI systems
- Data Protection Ombudsman designated for fundamental rights AI enforcement
- Authorities empowered: inspections, document/source code access, enforcement orders
- National AI regulatory sandbox to be operational by August 2026
