## Summary (verify for accuracy)

TRAIGA prohibits developing AI systems designed to incite self-harm, create CSAM, generate deepfake pornography, or implement social credit scoring. Requires state agencies and healthcare providers to disclose AI use in consumer-facing interactions. Exclusive AG enforcement with tiered penalties.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibit AI designed to induce self-harm or generate CSAM
- Prohibit AI-generated deepfake pornography
- Prohibit social credit scoring AI systems
- State agencies and healthcare providers must disclose AI use in consumer interactions
- AG enforcement with tiered civil penalties
