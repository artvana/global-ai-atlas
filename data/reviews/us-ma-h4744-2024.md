## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Massachusetts' first deepfake-specific law criminalizes nonconsensual distribution of intimate images including AI-generated deepfakes, amending the criminal harassment statute. Also establishes coercive control as domestic abuse element and creates teen diversion program for sexting.
