## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Four-bill package criminalizing AI-generated deepfakes in elections. Requires disclosure of AI use in political ads. Criminalizes knowingly distributing materially deceptive AI media within 90 days of election with intent to harm a candidate or deceive voters. Establishes Class E felony penalties. One of the earliest state election deepfake laws.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibition on creating or distributing deepfake political ads without disclosure
- Criminal prohibition on materially deceptive synthetic media about candidates
- Disclosure required on AI-generated election communications
- AG and election officials may seek injunctive relief
