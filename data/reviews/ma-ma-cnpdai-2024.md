## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Morocco's CNDP issued guidelines in 2024 on the application of Law 09-08 to AI systems processing personal data. The guidelines address: lawful basis for AI training data collection, transparency obligations for automated processing, DPIA requirements for high-risk AI, right to human review of algorithmic decisions, and restrictions on biometric AI use. Morocco's CNDP is one of the most active data protection authorities in Africa and Francophone world.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Lawful basis required for AI training data collection
- DPIA for high-risk automated processing
- Transparency to individuals about automated decisions
- Right to contest automated decisions affecting individual rights
- Prior authorisation for biometric AI systems
