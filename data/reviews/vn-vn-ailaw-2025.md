## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Vietnam's first standalone comprehensive AI law establishes risk-based classification (high, medium, low risk) for AI systems. High-risk applications in finance, healthcare, and education have an 18-month grace period (until September 2027); other sectors have 12 months. Applies to both domestic and foreign AI providers affecting Vietnam.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI developers and deployers of high-risk AI must register with competent authorities
- High-risk AI must undergo conformity assessment
- AI systems must meet safety and reliability standards set by Ministry of Science and Technology
- Foreign AI service providers must comply with data localization requirements

## Law Text (excerpt)

```
# LAW ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Law No. 134/2025/QH15**
*Hanoi, 10 December 2025*
*Takes effect: 1 March 2026*

---

## CHAPTER I — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1 — Scope of regulation

Regulates the research, development, provision, deployment and use of AI systems; the rights and obligations of related organizations and individuals; and State management of AI activities in Vietnam. AI activities serving solely national defence, security or cryptography are excluded.

### Article 2 — Subjects of application

Applies to Vietnamese agencies, organizations and individuals, and to foreign organizations and individuals participating in AI activities in Vietnam.

### Article 3 — Definitions

1. **Artificial intelligence (AI)** — the electronic implementation of human cognitive capacities, including learning, reasoning, perception, judgement and understanding of natural language.
```
