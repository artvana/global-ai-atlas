## Law Text (excerpt)

```
# Maryland Senate Bill 360 — Nonconsensual Deepfake Intimate Imagery Act

**Senate Bill No. 360**

*Maryland — April 22, 2025*

---

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

SB 360 expands Maryland's existing nonconsensual pornography (revenge pornography) law to expressly cover AI-generated and digitally manipulated deepfake intimate images, and adds a civil cause of action for victims.

### Expansion of Criminal Law

The bill amends Maryland's existing criminal statute prohibiting nonconsensual distribution of private sexual images to expressly include:
- Wholly AI-generated intimate imagery depicting an identifiable person without their consent
- Digitally manipulated (deepfake) images that use a real person's likeness to create intimate content

The criminal offense remains a misdemeanor, punishable by up to 2 years imprisonment and a fine of up to $5,000.

### Civil Cause of Action

SB 360 adds an express civil cause of action for victims of nonconsensual deepfake intimate imagery, permitting victims to sue for:
- Compensatory damages
- Attorney's fees and costs
- Injunctive relief
```
