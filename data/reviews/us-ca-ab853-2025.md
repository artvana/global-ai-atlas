## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends the California AI Transparency Act (SB 942) to delay the compliance deadline for AI content provenance and origin disclosure requirements from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026, while making technical corrections to disclosure obligations. The core requirement that large AI providers offer a free, publicly accessible AI detection tool is maintained without substantive change.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Delays SB 942 compliance date from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026
- Large AI providers (1M+ monthly users) must still embed watermarks in AI-generated images, video, audio
- Large AI providers must still provide free public AI detection tool
- Technical corrections to manifest and latent disclosure requirements

*California — October 13, 2025*

## Key Provisions

### Purpose and Scope

AB 853 amends the California AI Transparency Act (SB 942, enacted 2024) to delay the compliance date for content provenance and AI origin disclosure requirements from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026, and to make technical corrections to the disclosure framework.

### Implementation Delay

The bill pushes back the operative date of SB 942's AI detection tool and manifest/latent disclosure requirements from January 1, 2026 to August 2, 2026. This delay was intended to provide large AI providers (those with 1 million or more monthly users) additional time to implement the required technical infrastructure for watermarking and detection tools.

### Technical Corrections

AB 853 makes technical corrections to SB 942's disclosure requirements, clarifying definitions and procedural obligations for covered providers.
