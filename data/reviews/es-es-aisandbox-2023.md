## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Spain's Royal Decree 817/2023 established the first regulatory sandbox for AI systems in the EU, creating a controlled legal testing environment for high-risk AI systems in advance of the EU AI Act. Participants can develop, test, and validate AI systems with regulatory flexibility for up to 36 months, supervised by AESIA.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Applicants must demonstrate AI system meets EU AI Act high-risk criteria
- Participants operate under supervised deviation from applicable rules for limited periods
- AESIA issues conditions of participation and monitors compliance
- Maximum participation: 36 months or until EU AI Act full applicability
