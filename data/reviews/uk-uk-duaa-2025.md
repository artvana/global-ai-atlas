## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The UK's Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 fundamentally restructures the UK approach to AI automated decision-making under UK GDPR. It replaces the near-prohibition on sole automated decisions with a broader permission framework tied to legitimate interests, while mandating transparency, right to contest, and human review options. This represents a deliberate post-Brexit divergence from EU GDPR Article 22.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Permits automated decisions with legal/significant effects on lawful bases including legitimate interests (non-special-category data)
- Must provide: information about the decision; mechanism for representations; option for human intervention; ability to contest
- Special category data (health, etc.) automated decisions still require explicit consent or substantial public interest
- ICO to issue statutory codes of practice on automated decision-making
