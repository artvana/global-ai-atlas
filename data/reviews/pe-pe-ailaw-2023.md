## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Peru's AI governance law promotes responsible development and use of AI while protecting fundamental rights. Establishes principles of non-discrimination, privacy, human oversight, transparency, sustainability, accountability, and intellectual property respect. Public institutions must register AI systems and conduct impact assessments. AI systems used in high-stakes decisions affecting citizens must provide human review options.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Register AI systems used in public sector decisions with PCM
- Conduct impact assessments for AI systems affecting citizens rights
- Provide human review for automated decisions in high-stakes contexts
- Disclose AI use to affected individuals
- Ensure non-discrimination in AI system design and deployment

# Law No. 31814 — Law Promoting the Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Peru

**Adopted by:** Congress of the Republic of Peru  
**Promulgation Date:** July 20, 2023  
**Published in:** Official Gazette El Peruano, July 22, 2023  
**Regulatory Decree:** Supreme Decree No. 115-2025-PCM (implementing regulations, 2025)  

---

## Title I: General Provisions

### Article 1: Purpose

This Law aims to promote the responsible, ethical, and human-centered development and use of artificial intelligence (AI) in Peru, establishing a framework of principles and obligations to ensure that AI systems are designed, developed, deployed, and used in a manner that is safe, transparent, fair, and respectful of the fundamental rights of persons.

The Law also establishes the basis for the State's use of AI in public administration and for the promotion of AI as a strategic technology for Peru's economic and social development.

### Article 2: Scope of Application

This Law applies to:

(a) Natural persons and legal entities, whether public or private, domiciled in Peru that develop, market, distribute, deploy, or use AI systems;
