## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Japan's first binding AI law establishes a national AI promotion framework with cabinet-level AI Strategy Headquarters. Emphasizes research investment, talent development, and international AI leadership rather than restrictions. No direct monetary penalties — relies on sector-specific laws and guidance for enforcement. Deliberate contrast to EU's risk-based prohibitive approach.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Government to develop basic AI policy and implementation guidelines
- Establish AI Safety Institute with authority to conduct safety evaluations
- Promote AI development while ensuring human-centric safety principles
- Develop technical standards for AI safety assessment in consultation with international bodies

ActGovernance and OversightInternational Alignment



The Act on Promotion of Research, Development and Utilization of Artificial Intelligence‑Related Technologies (AI Promotion Act) establishes a national framework to promote AI research, development and practical use while addressing risks through government coordination, guidelines and oversight bodies rather than heavy-handed prescriptive penalties. It creates an AI Strategic Headquarters, requires an AI Basic Plan and instructs ministries to publish voluntary guidance and investigation mechanisms to protect public safety and trust. See the Cabinet Office overview and provisional English outline for primary details: Cabinet Office — AI Act and AI Act (Overview) (Provisional Translation).
