## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires large AI providers (1M+ monthly users) to implement watermarking tools for AI-generated images, video, and audio — both visible labels and imperceptible digital watermarks. Providers must offer free detection tools so users can verify whether content is AI-generated.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Large AI providers (1M+ monthly users) must embed watermarks in AI-generated images, video, audio
- Provide free public detection tool for users to verify AI-generated content
- Maintain manifest of watermarked content
- Label AI-generated political content prominently
