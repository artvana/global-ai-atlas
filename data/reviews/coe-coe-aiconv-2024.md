## Summary (verify for accuracy)

First binding international AI treaty, opened for signature September 2024. Requires signatory states to ensure AI systems respect human rights, democratic principles, and rule of law. Mandates impact assessments, transparency, accountability, and remedies. 37 signatories as of April 2026; no ratifications yet — not yet in force.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Parties must ensure AI lifecycle activities respect human rights
- Prohibition on AI that undermines integrity of democratic institutions
- Right to seek review of AI-assisted decisions by public authorities
- Parties must establish independent oversight bodies for AI
- Transparency obligations for AI systems used by or on behalf of public authorities
