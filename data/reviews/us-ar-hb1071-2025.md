## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Arkansas extends its Publicity Rights Protection Act to explicitly cover AI-generated images, voice replicas, and digital likenesses. Prohibits commercial use of a person's AI-generated likeness without consent. Part of Arkansas's 2025 legislative expansion addressing AI-generated identity exploitation.

## Overview

Arkansas HB 1071 (2025) amends Arkansas's existing Publicity Rights Protection Act to explicitly extend its protections to AI-generated and AI-manipulated representations of individuals' names, voices, images, photographs, and likenesses. The Act prohibits the unauthorized commercial use of a person's identity — including digital replicas created by generative AI — without prior written consent.

HB 1071 is part of a wave of five AI-related identity-protection measures enacted by Arkansas in its 2025 regular session, positioning the state among the most active in the country on digital likeness rights legislation.
