## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Extends Georgia's right of publicity statute to prohibit the creation of AI-generated synthetic depictions of individuals' likeness or voice without consent for commercial purposes. Provides civil remedies including actual damages and injunctive relief. Focuses on performers and artists in the entertainment industry.

## Law Text (excerpt)

```
# Georgia SB 396 — Artificial Intelligence in Creative Content Act

**Enacted April 18, 2024 | O.C.G.A. §§ 51-5-10 through 51-5-13**

---

## Purpose

The Act creates a civil cause of action for the unauthorized use of an individual's likeness, voice, or image in AI-generated content for commercial purposes or to defame, harass, or harm the individual. It extends Georgia's existing right of publicity protections to AI-generated replicas.

---

## Definitions

**"AI-generated replica"** (O.C.G.A. § 51-5-10): means a video, audio recording, photograph, or digital image of an individual's likeness, voice, or image that:
(a) did not occur in reality;
(b) was created or substantially altered using artificial intelligence, machine learning, or other digital technology; and
(c) appears to be a realistic portrayal of that individual.

**"Individual"** means a natural person, living or deceased (within the protections applying post-mortem for a period of 10 years after death, as under Georgia's right of publicity statute).

**"Commercial purpose"** means use for advertising, trade, fundraising, or any purpose involving the direct or indirect sale of goods or services.
```
