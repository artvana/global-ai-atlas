## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires generative AI developers to publicly disclose information about the datasets used to train their models, including data types, sources, whether it included personal information, and whether synthetic data was used. Disclosure must be published on the developer's website.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- GenAI developers must publicly post training data summary on website
- Disclosure must include data types, sources, whether personal information was used
- Disclose whether synthetic data was used in training
- Update disclosures when model is significantly retrained
