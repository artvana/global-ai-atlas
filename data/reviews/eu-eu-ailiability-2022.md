## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The European Commission proposed the AI Liability Directive (COM/2022/496) in September 2022 as a companion to the AI Act, adapting civil liability rules to AI systems. It introduced a rebuttable presumption of causation for high-risk AI systems (shifting burden of proof to defendants) and a disclosure mechanism allowing claimants to request AI-related evidence from developers. The Commission withdrew the proposal in February 2025 following the AI Act's adoption, citing the AI Act's enforcement mechanisms and the Omnibus simplification package.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- High-risk AI systems: rebuttable presumption of causal link between fault and damage
- Developers and deployers must disclose relevant AI documentation to claimants upon court order
- National courts to apply EU AI Act risk classification in liability proceedings
- Claimants harmed by AI entitled to request evidence disclosure from AI operators
