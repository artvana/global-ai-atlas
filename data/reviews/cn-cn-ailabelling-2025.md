## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Mandates visible and embedded (metadata/watermark) labels for all AI-generated and synthetic content distributed publicly in China, covering text, images, audio, video, and virtual scenes. Technical standard GB45438-2025 specifies metadata fields including provider code, content ID, and timestamp. Labels must persist when content is downloaded.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Standardised technical requirements for labelling AI-generated content
- Explicit and implicit labelling methods specified
- Applies to text, images, audio, and video AI-generated content
- Platforms must implement labeling infrastructure meeting GB 45438 specifications
