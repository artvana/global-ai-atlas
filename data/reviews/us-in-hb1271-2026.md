## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits health insurers from using fully automated AI processes to submit or deny health benefit claims without human review by a qualified provider or claims professional. Ensures human accountability in AI-assisted health insurance claim adjudication.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Health insurers may not use fully automated AI to adjudicate claims without human review
- Claims must be reviewed by qualified provider or licensed claims professional
- Human override required before issuing AI-generated claim denials
