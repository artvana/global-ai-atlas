## Summary (verify for accuracy)

OMB M-24-10, issued March 28, 2024 under the Biden administration, was the primary binding governance framework for federal agency use of AI. It required each CFO Act agency to designate a Chief AI Officer (CAIO) within 60 days, establish an AI governance board, maintain an annual public inventory of AI use cases, and develop enterprise AI strategies. The memo established mandatory minimum risk-management practices for 'rights-impacting AI' and 'safety-impacting AI' — requiring AI impact assessments, ongoing monitoring, and human oversight before such systems could be used. Agencies that could not comply by December 1, 2024 were required to cease using non-compliant systems. M-24-10 was rescinded and replaced by OMB M-25-21 on April 3, 2025, by the Trump administration.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Each CFO Act agency must designate a Chief AI Officer (CAIO) within 60 days
- Agencies must establish an AI governance board including senior legal, privacy, IT, procurement, and mission officials
- Agencies must maintain an annual public-facing inventory of AI use cases
- Each agency must develop an enterprise AI strategy covering responsible AI adoption
- Before using rights-impacting or safety-impacting AI, agencies must complete an AI impact assessment documenting purpose, expected benefits, risks, data quality, and mitigation measures
- Agencies must conduct ongoing human monitoring of rights-impacting and safety-impacting AI post-deployment
- Agencies must regularly evaluate risks and mitigate emerging harms from safety- and rights-impacting AI
- Agencies unable to comply with minimum practices by December 1, 2024 must cease using the non-compliant AI system
- Agencies must develop and publish compliance plans within 180 days, updated biennially
