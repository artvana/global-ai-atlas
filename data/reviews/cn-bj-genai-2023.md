## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Beijing's Measures for Administering Generative AI Services implement the national GenAI Interim Measures at the municipal level, adding Beijing-specific requirements. Key additions: mandatory security assessment filing with Beijing Cyberspace Administration for GenAI services, content labeling requirements stricter than national standard, AI product registration for public-facing services, and prohibited content categories aligned with national CAC regulations but with additional local restrictions.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Security assessment filing with Beijing Cyberspace Administration
- Content labeling of all AI-generated content
- Training data compliance with national content standards
- Prohibited content detection and filtering system
- Real-name registration of users for high-risk GenAI services
