## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Uzbekistan's first AI-specific law amends the Law on Informatization and the Code on Administrative Liability. Key provisions: (1) statutory definition of AI as a complex of technological solutions imitating human cognitive functions; (2) prohibition on causing harm to life, health, freedom, honour, or dignity through AI-based information resources and systems; (3) prohibition on exclusive reliance on AI conclusions for legally significant decisions affecting human rights and freedoms; (4) administrative liability (fine of 50-100 basic calculation units plus confiscation) for unlawful processing of personal data using AI. Adopted by the Legislative Chamber on 12 August 2025 and approved by the Senate on 1 November 2025.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI-based systems must not cause harm to human life, health, freedom, honour, or dignity
- Exclusive reliance on AI conclusions for legally significant decisions affecting human rights is prohibited
- Unlawful processing of personal data using AI technologies is an administrative offense

## Preamble

In recent years, large-scale work has been carried out in our country on the introduction and widespread application of artificial intelligence technologies, ensuring access to and high quality of digital data, and training qualified personnel in the field.
