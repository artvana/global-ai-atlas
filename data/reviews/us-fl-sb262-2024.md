## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Requires political advertisements containing AI-generated content to include a clear and conspicuous disclosure stating the content was "created in whole or in part with the use of generative artificial intelligence." Applies to television, radio, digital, and print political advertising in Florida.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Political ads containing AI-generated content must include prominent disclosure label
- Disclosure must state content was created using generative AI
- Applies to all political advertising formats: TV, radio, digital, print
- Division of Elections enforcement
