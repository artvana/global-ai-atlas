## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The AI in Government Act of 2020 (enacted as part of the Consolidated Appropriations Act) directed GSA to create an AI Center of Excellence to facilitate AI adoption across federal agencies, and directed OMB to produce guidance ensuring federal AI use is effective, ethical, and accountable. It established the interagency AI coordination working group.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- GSA to establish AI Center of Excellence to facilitate agency AI adoption
- OMB to issue guidance within 270 days on ethical and accountable federal AI use
- Interagency AI coordination working group established
- Agencies must report AI use cases to OMB
