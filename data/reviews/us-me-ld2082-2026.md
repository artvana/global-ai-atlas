## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits unlicensed entities from offering therapy services using AI and restricts licensed practitioners to administrative AI use only with client consent. AI systems may not be deployed to provide psychotherapy or mental health counseling services without licensed professional supervision.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibit unlicensed entities from offering therapy using AI
- Licensed practitioners may only use AI for administrative tasks with client consent
- AI may not provide psychotherapy without licensed professional supervision
- Penalty: $10,000 per violation
