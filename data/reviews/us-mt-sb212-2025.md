## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Establishes a fundamental right to privately own and use computational resources in Montana. Any government restriction on computing resources must be narrowly tailored and demonstrably necessary. Requires AI-controlled critical infrastructure operators to develop NIST-based risk management policies and annual safety reviews.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Government agencies prohibited from using AI to deny individual rights based on protected class
- Residents have right to access and operate AI computing without government interference
- Prohibition on social credit scoring by government entities
