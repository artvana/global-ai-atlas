## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Malaysia's 2024 amendment substantially updates the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 to align with GDPR standards. Mandates DPO appointment, 72-hour breach notification, expands sensitive personal data to include biometric data, and imposes direct obligations on data processors. AI systems processing personal data must comply with enhanced consent and DPO oversight requirements.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Data controllers and processors must appoint a DPO
- Mandatory breach notification within 72 hours
- Biometric data now classified as sensitive personal data requiring explicit consent
- Data processors directly liable for compliance obligations
- Data portability rights introduced

**Laws of Malaysia — Act A1727**

*An Act to amend the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 [Act 709].*

- **Date of Royal Assent**: 9 October 2024
- **Date of publication in the Gazette**: 17 October 2024
- **Commencement**: Dates to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette; different dates may be appointed for different provisions.

---

## Section 1 — Short title and commencement

This Act may be cited as the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024**.

---

## Section 2 — General amendment

The principal Act is amended by substituting **"data user"** and **"data users"** wherever appearing with **"data controller"** and **"data controllers"**, except in the definition of "register" under section 4 and in section 9.
