## Summary (verify for accuracy)

China's National Technical Committee 260 on Cybersecurity (SAC NTC260) released the AI Safety Governance Framework in September 2024. The framework establishes principles for AI safety governance, classifies AI safety risks into inherent and systemic categories, and provides guidance on risk identification, assessment, and mitigation across the AI lifecycle. It complements China's binding CAC regulations on generative AI and algorithmic recommendations.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI developers and operators should implement AI safety governance aligned with the framework's principles
- Classify AI risks into inherent risks (data, model, application) and systemic risks
- Conduct safety assessments across the AI lifecycle
- Implement technical and management controls for identified risks
