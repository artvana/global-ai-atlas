*Alabama — Enacted April 24, 2024 | Effective October 1, 2024*

---

## Overview

Alabama House Bill 161 extends Alabama's existing nonconsensual intimate image (NCII) laws to explicitly cover images, videos, and other media that are generated or substantially altered using artificial intelligence. The law prohibits the creation and distribution of sexually explicit AI-generated depictions of identifiable real persons without their consent, and provides both criminal penalties for violators and a civil cause of action for victims.

The law is part of a national trend of state legislation targeting AI-generated nonconsensual intimate imagery — sometimes referred to as "AI deepfake porn" — which has proliferated with the advent of widely accessible generative AI image tools. Alabama's law applies to both fully AI-generated images (where the depicted person never posed) and AI-manipulated images (where real images of a person are altered to appear sexually explicit).
