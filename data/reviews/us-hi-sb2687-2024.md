## Overview

Hawaii Senate Bill 2687, enacted as Act 191 of 2024, prohibited the creation and distribution of materially deceptive AI-generated media (deepfakes) in connection with political campaigns without a prominent disclaimer identifying the content as AI-generated. The law applied within 90 days of a covered election and provided for civil and criminal enforcement.

However, the law's practical effect has been significantly curtailed: a federal district court issued a preliminary injunction against enforcement in 2024, finding that the law likely violated the First Amendment, and subsequently issued a permanent injunction against enforcement in early 2026. The law remains on the books but is unenforceable. Hawaii's experience has become a reference point in national debates about the constitutionality of state AI deepfake election laws.
