## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Expands California's data broker registration law to require disclosure when consumer data is shared with generative AI developers for training purposes. Data brokers must update privacy disclosures and provide opt-out mechanisms for GenAI training use.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Data brokers must disclose when consumer data is shared with GenAI developers for training
- Update privacy disclosures to reflect AI training use
- Provide opt-out mechanism for GenAI training use of personal data
