## Summary (verify for accuracy)

New Jersey's deepfake law establishes criminal penalties (3rd degree crime, up to $30,000 fine) for making or distributing deceptive AI deepfakes to further additional criminal activity. Creates civil cause of action for victims. Passed unanimously in both chambers.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Criminal penalty (3rd degree crime, up to $30,000) for deepfakes used to further criminal activity
- Civil cause of action for victims
