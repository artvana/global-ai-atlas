## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Indonesia's first comprehensive personal data protection law, modeled on GDPR. Prohibits automated decision-making that produces legal effects without human oversight mechanisms. Creates a Personal Data Protection Agency (still being constituted). Compliance transition ended October 2024.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Legitimate basis required for all personal data processing including AI training
- Data subject right to withdraw consent and object to automated processing with legal effects
- DPO required for large-scale or high-risk personal data processing
- Data breach notification within 14 days
- Cross-border data transfers require adequate protection

### Purpose and Scope

Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection, "UU PDP") is Indonesia's first comprehensive personal data protection statute, enacted on 17 October 2022 after years of legislative development. Prior to UU PDP, Indonesia's data protection framework consisted of scattered provisions in sectoral laws and Government Regulation No. 71 of 2019 on Electronic Systems and Transactions (PP 71/2019), which provided only basic data protection principles. UU PDP establishes a modern, comprehensive framework drawing on the GDPR and the personal data protection laws of neighboring ASEAN countries including Singapore and Thailand.
