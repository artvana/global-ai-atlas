## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Prohibits creating or distributing materially deceptive AI-generated media depicting a candidate within 60 days before an election without a clear disclosure label. Criminal misdemeanor for knowing violations. The disclosure must be conspicuous and state that the content contains AI-generated material.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibition on deceptive AI political media within 60 days of election without disclosure
- Disclosure must state content is AI-generated
- Class 2 misdemeanor for knowing violations

## Law Text (excerpt)

```
# North Carolina Protecting Elections from Deceptive AI Act (SB 815)

**Enacted October 3, 2024 | Codified at G.S. Chapter 163, Article 22F**

---

## Purpose

The Act prohibits the knowing use of artificial intelligence to generate or distribute deceptive political advertisements that falsely depict a candidate or elected official doing or saying something they did not do or say, without required disclosures.

---

## Definitions

**"Deceptive AI-generated media"** means images, audio, or video that:
(a) depicts a candidate, elected official, or political party chair doing or saying something they did not actually do or say; and
(b) was created or substantially altered using AI or other digital technology in a manner designed to mislead a reasonable person.

**"Clearly false content"** means synthetic media that a reasonable person would believe to be authentic but is fabricated or materially altered.

**"Candidate"** has the same meaning as in G.S. 163-278.6.

**"Election communication"** means any message distributed for the purpose of influencing North Carolina voters in connection with any election.
```
