## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The foundational US federal AI law, enacted as Division E of the FY2021 NDAA. Establishes the National AI Initiative to coordinate federal AI R&D, creates the National AI Initiative Office within OSTP, requires a National AI Research Resource, and mandates an AI technical standards program at NIST. Sets the institutional framework for subsequent federal AI governance.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Establishes National AI Initiative Office in OSTP to coordinate federal AI activities
- NIST to develop voluntary AI standards and frameworks
- NSF and DOE to expand AI research investments
- National AI Research Resource to democratize access to AI compute and data
- Annual report to Congress on federal AI R&D spending
