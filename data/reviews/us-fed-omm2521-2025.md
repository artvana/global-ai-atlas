## Summary (verify for accuracy)

OMB Memorandum M-25-21, issued by OMB Director Russell Vought, replaces the Biden-era M-24-10 and reframes federal AI governance around innovation acceleration while maintaining risk management. It retains the Chief AI Officer (CAIO) structure, requires agencies to designate high-impact AI use cases, mandates risk management for rights-and-safety-impacting AI, and emphasizes domestic AI leadership. Unlike M-24-10, it removes requirements for equity-focused AI impact assessments and deemphasizes civil rights considerations.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Each Executive Branch agency must designate a Chief AI Officer (CAIO)
- Agencies must maintain AI use case inventories
- High-impact AI uses (rights or safety affecting) require documented risk management
- Agencies must report annually on AI use and governance progress to OMB
