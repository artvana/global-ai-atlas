## Summary (verify for accuracy)

New York's frontier AI safety law requires large AI developers to establish and publish safety protocols, report critical incidents to the state within 72 hours, and maintain documented safety practices. Applies to frontier models developed, deployed, or operating in New York. Creates a new state AI oversight office within DFS.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Employers must notify workers before deploying high-risk AI in employment decisions
- Conduct algorithmic impact assessments annually
- Workers may request explanation of AI decision affecting them
- Right to request human review of high-risk AI employment decision
- Independent audit required for high-risk AI used in employment
