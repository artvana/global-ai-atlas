## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Amends Illinois Human Rights Act to prohibit employers from using AI that produces a discriminatory effect on protected classes in hiring, recruitment, and employment decisions. Employers face strict liability for discriminatory AI outcomes. Requires employers to notify workers when AI is used for employment decisions.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Employers prohibited from using AI that produces discriminatory effect on protected classes in hiring
- Strict liability for discriminatory AI employment outcomes
- Notify employees and job applicants when AI is used in employment decisions
- Comply with Illinois Human Rights Act remedies for violations
