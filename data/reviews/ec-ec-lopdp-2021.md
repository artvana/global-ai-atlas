## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Ecuador's first comprehensive data protection law with GDPR-inspired structure. Establishes the SNPDP as supervisory authority. Provides seven principles, ARCO+ rights (including portability and non-discrimination), automated decision-making notification and human review rights, DPO requirements, DPIA obligations, sensitive data categories, 72-hour breach notification, and significant administrative fines.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Implement lawful processing basis for all personal data processing
- Protect sensitive data categories with explicit consent or specific legal authority
- Provide ARCO+ rights (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition, portability, non-discrimination)
- Notify data subjects of automated decision-making and provide human review right
- Appoint DPO for public bodies and high-risk processing
- Conduct DPIAs for high-risk processing activities
- Notify SNPDP of breaches within 72 hours
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with adequacy determination or approved safeguards

## Overview

Ecuador's Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales (LOPDP) is the country's first comprehensive data protection law, enacted on May 26, 2021. The LOPDP establishes a GDPR-inspired framework for the protection of personal data, creating the Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales (SNPDP) as the independent supervisory authority. Implementing regulations were issued in 2023, providing detailed operational guidance. The law applies to any processing of personal data carried out in Ecuador or by controllers/processors established in Ecuador, and also applies to processing by non-Ecuadorian controllers targeting Ecuadorian residents (extraterritorial reach). The LOPDP is recognized as one of the most modern data protection frameworks in Latin America, drawing heavily on GDPR principles and structure.
