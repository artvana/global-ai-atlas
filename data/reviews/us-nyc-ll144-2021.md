## Summary (verify for accuracy)

First city-level law in the US regulating AI hiring tools. Requires NYC employers and employment agencies using automated employment decision tools to conduct annual independent bias audits, publish audit results publicly, and notify job candidates that AI is being used in the hiring process.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Employers must conduct annual independent bias audit of automated employment decision tools
- Publish bias audit results publicly on company website
- Notify job candidates in advance that AEDT is being used in hiring process
- Provide alternative selection procedure upon candidate request

The Department of Consumer and Worker Protection is proposing to add rules to implement new legislation related to the use of automated employment decision tools. The proposed rules would clarify the requirements for the use of automated employment decision tools within New York City, the notices to employees and candidates for employment regarding the use of the tool, the bias audit for the tool, and the required published results of the bias audit.
