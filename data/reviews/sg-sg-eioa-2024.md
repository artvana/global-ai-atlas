## Summary (verify for accuracy)

First Singapore legislation to explicitly mention artificial intelligence. Amends elections law to prohibit AI-generated or AI-manipulated online election advertising. Bans manipulated political content including deepfakes and AI-synthesized images, audio or video of candidates. Extends existing online election advertising regulations to cover AI-generated content.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Prohibit AI-manipulated or AI-generated election advertising
- Ban deepfake images, audio and video of candidates during elections

## Overview

The Elections (Integrity of Online Advertising) (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act 34 of 2024) amends Singapore's Presidential Elections Act 1991 and Parliamentary Elections Act 1954 to prohibit AI-generated or AI-manipulated political content in online election advertising. It is the **first Singapore legislation to explicitly mention and regulate artificial intelligence**, specifically targeting deepfakes and synthetic media depicting electoral candidates.
