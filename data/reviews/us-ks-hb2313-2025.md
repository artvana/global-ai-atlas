## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Kansas prohibits state agencies from using AI platforms owned by foreign adversaries (China, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Venezuela) on state networks or devices. Specifically names DeepSeek. Also bars state-funded medical/research facilities from using genetic sequencers from foreign adversaries.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- State agencies must not use AI platforms from foreign adversary nations on state networks
- Agencies must deactivate and delete DeepSeek and similar adversary AI accounts
- State-funded medical/research facilities barred from using foreign adversary genetic sequencers
