## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Taiwan's foundational AI governance law establishes seven core AI principles (sustainability, human autonomy, privacy, cybersecurity, transparency, fairness, accountability) and designates the National Science and Technology Council as central AI authority. MODA must develop a risk classification framework within 2 years. Primarily a framework/promotional law with limited immediate private-sector binding obligations.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Government must formulate national AI development guidelines every 4 years
- National AI Strategy Committee to meet at least annually
- MODA to develop internationally aligned risk classification within 2 years
- Government AI use must comply with 7 AI principles
- AI providers must cooperate with government oversight mechanisms

**Republic of China (Taiwan)**
**Presidential Order Hua-Zong-Yi-Yi-Zi No. 11400001461**
**Promulgated and effective: 8 January 2025**

---

## Article 1 — Legislative Purpose

This Act is enacted to build a smart nation; promote the human-centered research, development, and industrial advancement of artificial intelligence (AI); construct a safe environment for AI applications; realize digital equity; protect the fundamental rights of the people; enhance social well-being; improve the quality of life for citizens; foster the sustainable development of society and the nation; preserve national cultural values; and elevate international competitiveness, while ensuring that technological applications comply with social ethics.
