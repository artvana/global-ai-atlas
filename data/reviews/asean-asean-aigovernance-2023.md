## Summary (verify for accuracy)

The ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics (2nd edition, 2023) provides a voluntary framework for AI governance across the 10 ASEAN member states. It is organised around seven principles: transparency and explainability; fairness and equity; security and safety; human-centricity; privacy and data governance; accountability and integrity; and robustness and reliability. The 2nd edition adds a chapter on generative AI and updates guidance on foundation models. It is accompanied by case studies from Singapore, Indonesia, and Thailand.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Transparency and explainability for significant AI decisions
- Fairness and non-discrimination assessment
- Human oversight mechanisms for high-stakes AI
- Privacy and data governance aligned with national frameworks
- Safety and security testing before deployment

## Foreword

The ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics (2nd Edition) builds on the first edition published in 2021, incorporating feedback from industry, government, and academia across the region. The guide provides practical guidance for organizations in both the public and private sectors on implementing responsible AI, aligned with the ASEAN AI Principles adopted in 2019. This second edition expands coverage to include new use cases in healthcare, financial services, and smart cities, and introduces guidance on generative AI and foundation models.
