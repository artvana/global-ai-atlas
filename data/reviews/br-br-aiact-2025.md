## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Brazil's comprehensive AI framework law, passed after three years of parliamentary debate. Establishes a risk-based regulatory regime modeled partly on the EU AI Act. Creates prohibited AI categories, mandates impact assessments for high-risk AI, requires AI interaction disclosure, and grants individuals rights to contest AI decisions. ANPD is the primary enforcement authority alongside sector regulators.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclosure required when interacting with AI system
- High-risk AI systems require prior algorithmic impact assessment
- Prohibited AI categories include social scoring and manipulative AI
- Human review right for AI decisions with significant effects
- Private right of action for individuals harmed by prohibited AI
- AI providers must register high-risk systems with ANPD

## Chapter I — General Provisions

### Article 1 — Purpose and Scope
This Law establishes principles, rights, duties, and rules for the development, implementation, and use of artificial intelligence systems in Brazil, aimed at ensuring the protection of human rights, democracy, and fundamental freedoms.

This Law applies to any natural person or legal entity, public or private, that develops, offers, or uses artificial intelligence systems that produce effects in Brazil, regardless of where the developer or operator is established.

### Article 2 — Definitions
For purposes of this Law:

**Artificial Intelligence System**: a machine-based system that processes inputs and generates outputs such as predictions, recommendations, content, or decisions that influence real or virtual environments, capable of operating with varying degrees of autonomy.
