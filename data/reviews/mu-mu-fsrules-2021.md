## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Mauritius rules governing robo-advisory and AI-enabled financial advisory services. Requires licensed financial advisors deploying AI-powered advisory systems to disclose the automated nature of advice, ensure the system is fit for purpose, maintain human escalation options for clients, and conduct ongoing performance monitoring. One of the first sector-specific AI financial services regulations in Africa.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Disclose automated nature of advice to clients before onboarding
- Maintain a human escalation pathway for complex advisory needs
- Conduct regular assessments of AI advisory system performance
- Ensure AI system aligns with client risk profiles
- Obtain FSC approval before deploying robo-advisory services

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope

The Financial Services (Robotic and AI Enabled Advisory Services) Rules 2021 (the "Robo-Advisory Rules" or "the Rules") were issued by the Financial Services Commission (FSC) of Mauritius pursuant to the Financial Services Act 2007. They came into effect in June 2021 and represent a pioneering regulatory instrument in the African and Indian Ocean region specifically targeting the provision of financial advisory services through automated, algorithmic, and AI-driven systems — commonly known as robo-advisors.

The Rules apply to any holder of a license under the Financial Services Act 2007 who provides or proposes to provide investment advice, financial planning, portfolio management, or other regulated financial advisory services through an automated or AI-enabled system — whether fully automated (robo-advisory) or partially automated (hybrid advisory combining algorithmic outputs with human adviser oversight). The Rules do not apply to the use of algorithmic tools solely for back-office processing, risk management, or regulatory compliance where no direct client-facing advice is generated.
