## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Voids provisions in entertainment contracts that grant employers the right to use an AI replica of a performer to replace work the performer would otherwise perform, unless the performer receives specific, individualized notice and representation by a qualified representative. Applies to contracts for audiovisual and sound recording work.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- AI replica contract provisions unenforceable unless performer receives individual notice
- Performer must be represented by qualified representative before signing AI replica rights away
- Applies to contracts for audiovisual and recorded performance work
