## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Pakistan's first comprehensive data protection law, establishing rights over personal data processing. AI-relevant provisions include: right to object to automated decision-making with legal or significant effects, data protection impact assessments required for high-risk processing including AI profiling, biometric and health data classified as sensitive requiring explicit consent, and rights of access, rectification, and erasure applicable to AI-processed data.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Obtain explicit consent for processing sensitive personal data including biometrics
- Conduct data protection impact assessments for high-risk AI processing
- Provide right to object to automated decisions with significant effects
- Register high-risk processing with NCPDP
- Appoint Data Protection Officer for systematic high-risk processing

## Summary and Analysis

### Purpose and Scope
