## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Comprehensive Washington AI law requiring developers and deployers of high-risk AI to conduct impact assessments, provide consumer disclosures, and allow humans to review AI decisions. Effective Jan 1, 2027. Includes consumer private right of action and safe harbor for documented compliance.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Deployers of high-risk AI must conduct algorithmic impact assessments
- Provide consumer disclosure of AI use in consequential decisions
- Allow consumers to opt out of AI decisions and request human review
- Private right of action under UDAP statutes
- Safe harbor for documented compliance programs
