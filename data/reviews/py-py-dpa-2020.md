## Summary (verify for accuracy)

Paraguay's primary data protection law establishing SENATIC as the supervisory authority. Provides data protection principles, lawful processing bases, sensitive data special categories (health, genetic, biometric, political, criminal), ARCO data subject rights, mandatory database registration, cross-border transfer restrictions, and administrative and criminal penalties.

## Key Obligations (verify completeness and accuracy)

- Register personal databases with SENATIC before commencing processing
- Comply with data protection principles
- Implement lawful processing basis
- Protect sensitive data categories with explicit consent or legal authority
- Provide ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation, opposition)
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Restrict cross-border transfers to countries with adequate protection

## Overview

Paraguay's Law No. 6534/2020 on Personal Data Protection is the country's primary data protection statute, enacted on September 30, 2020 and entering into force on March 1, 2021. The law establishes the Secretaría Nacional de Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación (SENATIC) as the supervisory authority with powers of registration, investigation, and enforcement. The law builds on Paraguay's constitutional privacy protections and creates a comprehensive framework for the protection of personal data in both public and private sectors. It applies to all processing of personal data of Paraguayan residents carried out in Paraguay, and extends to non-Paraguayan processors targeting Paraguayan residents.
